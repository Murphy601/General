## PAGE 1: Listening carefully
Listening is more than hearing. To hear is to catch sounds with your ears. To listen is to pay attention so that you understand. Good listeners get the information they need, enjoy stories and learn from other people.

### Habits of a good listener
• Look at the speaker.
• Sit quietly and keep your hands still.
• Do not interrupt. Wait until the speaker has finished.
• Think about what you hear and picture it in your mind.
• Listen for key words such as who, where, when and what.
• Take short notes if you may need to remember details.
• Ask a question when the speaker has finished if you did not understand.

### Why listen carefully?
If we listen well we can answer questions correctly, follow instructions and tell the story again to someone else. We can also learn a lesson from a story.

### Words for our theme: nutrition
• nutrition: the food a body needs to grow and stay healthy.
• diet: the kinds of food a person usually eats.
• balanced diet: a diet with the right amounts of all types of food.
• vitamin: a substance in food that keeps the body healthy.
• protein: a food substance that helps the body grow and repair itself, found in meat, fish, eggs and beans.
• carbohydrates: foods that give the body energy, such as ugali, rice and potatoes.
• fat: a food substance that stores energy, found in cooking oil and butter.
• plump: fat in a pleasant way, round and healthy.
• thin: with little flesh on the body.

## PAGE 2: The sounds /ʊ/, /uː/ and /æ/
### /ʊ/: a short sound
Round your lips slightly and make a short sound, as in "book". Words: book, good, foot, full, put, cook, wood, pull, sugar.

### /uː/: a long sound
Round your lips more tightly and hold the sound, as in "food". Words: food, fruit, soup, spoon, moon, juice, blue, too, school.

### Contrast pairs
The short and long sounds change the meaning of a word.
• full, fool
• pull, pool
Say "full" quickly and "fool" slowly. Hold /uː/ for a longer time.

### /æ/
Open your mouth wide and pull the corners back, as in "apple". Words: apple, fat, jam, cabbage, sandwich, snack, back, black.

### Sentences
• The cook put good food in the full pot.
• Fruit juice is good for you.
• The black cat sat on a sack.
Read each one twice. Listen for the target sounds.

### Sort
Put these words under the correct sound: book, food, jam, spoon, cook, apple, moon, full.
/ʊ/: book, cook, full.
/uː/: food, spoon, moon.
/æ/: jam, apple.

## PAGE 3: The sounds /f/ and /v/ again
Remember how to make these sounds. Touch your top teeth to your lower lip and blow. For /f/ the sound is whispered. For /v/ your voice buzzes.

### Words with /f/
food, fruit, fat, fish, flour, fresh, beef, leaf, half

### Words with /v/
vitamin, vegetables, very, five, liver, leaves, give, have, save, seven

### Minimal pairs
• fan, van
• fine, vine
• leaf, leave
• safe, save
• half, halve

### Say the sentences
• Fresh vegetables and fruit have many vitamins.
• Five fish and seven vegetables are for lunch.
• Vera loves fruit and fish.
• I have a full plate of food.

### Tongue twister
Five fat fish fried in fresh oil for Vera's very fine feast.
Say it slowly three times, then say it faster with clear /f/ and /v/ sounds.

### Listen and choose
Your partner says one word from each pair: fan/van, leaf/leave, safe/save. You say which one you heard, and then you say it back. Swap roles.

## PAGE 4: Listening passage and questions
Your teacher or a friend reads this passage aloud. Listen twice. Do not look at the text the first time.

### A Healthy Plate
Baraka was a thin boy who felt tired at school. His sister Vera was plump and cheerful. One day their grandmother visited. "What do you eat every day?" she asked Baraka. "Ugali and tea," he said. "That is not enough," said Grandmother.

She took the children to the kitchen. "A balanced diet has different kinds of food," she said. "Ugali, rice and potatoes give us energy. Beans, eggs, fish and meat help us grow. Fruit and vegetables such as spinach, oranges and mangoes protect us from illness. A little oil gives extra energy. And we must drink clean water every day."

That week the family cooked ugali with sukuma wiki and beans. They ate fruit after each meal. Baraka drank water at school. After a month he ran faster and felt much stronger. "I would like to be a footballer," he said. "I need a balanced diet to be strong."

### Questions
1. Why did Baraka feel tired at school?
2. Which foods give us energy?
3. What do fruit and vegetables do for the body?
4. What did Baraka say he would like to be?
5. What lesson does the story teach?

Answers: 1. He ate only ugali and tea, which is not enough. 2. Ugali, rice and potatoes. 3. They protect the body from illness. 4. A footballer. 5. We need a balanced diet to be healthy and strong.

### Retell
Tell the story to a partner in the correct order. Use first, then, after that and finally.

## PAGE 5: The pattern "noun phrase + would like to be"
We use "would like to be" to say what we wish or hope to become. The words after "be" are a noun phrase. A noun phrase is a noun with the small words that go with it, such as "a footballer" or "a good farmer".

### Pattern
subject + would like to be + noun phrase
• I would like to be a doctor.
• She would like to be a nurse.
• We would like to be healthy people. (noun phrase: healthy people)
• Baraka would like to be a strong footballer.
• They would like to be good cooks.

### Notes
• The verb "be" stays the same after "to".
• We can shorten "I would" to "I'd": I'd like to be a teacher.
• We can also ask: What would you like to be? I would like to be a farmer.
• Add a reason with because: I would like to be a farmer because I love plants.

### Connect to nutrition
• I would like to be a strong runner, so I eat a balanced diet.
• My sister would like to be a nutritionist. She would like to help people eat well.
• We would like to be healthy children. We drink water and eat fruit.

### Talk and act
Work in a group. Ask each other, "What would you like to be?" Then dramatise the story "A Healthy Plate" with roles: Baraka, Vera and Grandmother. Discuss the lesson you learnt.

### Speak in full sentences
Use this frame: "I would like to be ______ because ______." Complete it in three ways.

## QUIZ
1. Which word contains the long sound /uː/?
   A) book
   B) good
   C) food
   D) foot
   Answer: C
   Why: Food has the long /uː/. Book, good and foot have the short /ʊ/.
2. Which pair are a minimal pair for /f/ and /v/?
   A) leaf, leave
   B) book, look
   C) jam, ham
   D) food, spoon
   Answer: A
   Why: Leaf and leave differ only in the last sound.
3. Which sentence is correct?
   A) I would like be a nurse.
   B) I would like to be a nurse.
   C) I would like to being a nurse.
   D) I would liking to be a nurse.
   Answer: B
   Why: The pattern is would like to be + noun phrase.
4. What does a balanced diet mean?
   A) Eating only meat
   B) Eating the right amounts of all types of food
   C) Eating once a day
   D) Eating only sweets
   Answer: B
   Why: A balanced diet includes energy foods, body-building foods, protective foods and water.
5. In the story, which foods give us energy?
   A) Ugali, rice and potatoes
   B) Fruit and vegetables
   C) Water only
   D) Salt and pepper
   Answer: A
   Why: Grandmother said ugali, rice and potatoes give us energy.
6. Write two words with the sound /æ/. (2 marks)
   Answer: For example: apple and jam.
   Why: 1 mark for each correct word.
7. Write two sentences beginning "I would like to be" and give a reason in each. (3 marks)
   Answer: For example: I would like to be a doctor because I want to help sick people. I would like to be a footballer because I enjoy playing.
   Why: 1 mark for each correct pattern and 1 mark for good reasons.
8. Explain what Baraka learnt from Grandmother. (3 marks)
   Answer: He learnt that ugali and tea are not enough, and that a balanced diet with energy foods, body-building foods, fruit and vegetables and water keeps him strong.
   Why: 1 mark for the wrong diet, 1 mark for the balanced diet and 1 mark for the result.
9. State three habits of a good listener. (3 marks)
   Answer: Any three: look at the speaker, do not interrupt, think about what you hear, take notes, ask questions after.
   Why: 1 mark for each correct habit.
