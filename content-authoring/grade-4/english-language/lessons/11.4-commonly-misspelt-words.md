## PAGE 1: Why some words are hard to spell
English spelling is not always regular. Some words do not look the way they sound. The word "Wednesday" is said "Wenz-day". The word "friend" is said "frend". These words are commonly misspelt because they have letters we do not hear, or sounds spelt in unusual ways.

### Why spelling matters
Correct spelling makes your writing easy to read and shows care. Wrong spelling can even change meaning: "form" and "from", "quiet" and "quite", "there" and "their".

### How to become a better speller
1. Look at the word closely. Notice the shape and letters.
2. Say the word slowly in syllables: Wed-nes-day.
3. Cover, write and check: cover the word, write it from memory, then check.
4. Use memory tricks (mnemonics).
5. Write the word in a sentence.
6. Keep a personal list of words you find hard.
7. Read a lot. The more you see words, the better you remember them.
8. Use a dictionary to check.

### Dictation
Your teacher reads a word or sentence, and you write it. Check with the correct spelling and correct any errors.

### Try it
Write these words from memory, then check: because, friend, people, school. Which did you get right?

## PAGE 2: Frequently misspelt words, list 1
Study these words. The tricky part is shown after each word.

• because: b-e-c-a-u-s-e. Memory trick: Big Elephants Can Always Understand Small Elephants.
• friend: f-r-i-e-n-d. Trick: a friend stays until the END: fri-END.
• people: p-e-o-p-l-e. Trick: People Eat Orange Peels Like Elephants.
• school: s-c-h-o-o-l. The "ch" says /k/.
• said: s-a-i-d. It is not spelt "sed".
• they: t-h-e-y. It is not "thay".
• where: w-h-e-r-e. Trick: it has "here" inside: w-HERE.
• there: t-h-e-r-e. Also has "here" inside.
• which: w-h-i-c-h.
• answer: a-n-s-w-e-r. The w is silent.
• laugh: l-a-u-g-h. The gh says /f/.
• again: a-g-a-i-n.
• once: o-n-c-e.
• little: l-i-t-t-l-e.
• mother, brother: o says /ʌ/.

### Practice sentences
1. "My friend said she would help because she was kind."
2. "The people asked where the school was."
3. "Which answer is right?"

### Cover-write-check
Use a piece of paper to cover each word. Write it, then check. Repeat for the ones you missed.

## PAGE 3: Frequently misspelt words, list 2
### Words with double letters or unusual patterns
• beautiful: b-e-a-u-t-i-f-u-l. Trick: say it in parts, beau-ti-ful, and write each part.
• different: d-i-f-f-e-r-e-n-t. Two f's.
• necessary: n-e-c-e-s-s-a-r-y. Trick: one collar, two sleeves: one c, two s's.
• separate: s-e-p-a-r-a-t-e. Trick: there is "a rat" in separate.
• tomorrow: t-o-m-o-r-r-o-w. One m, two r's.
• address: a-d-d-r-e-s-s. Two d's and two s's.
• favourite: f-a-v-o-u-r-i-t-e. (In British spelling, "our".)
• neighbour: n-e-i-g-h-b-o-u-r.
• library: l-i-b-r-a-r-y. Remember the first r: "li-BRA-ry".
• government: g-o-v-e-r-n-m-e-n-t. The n after "r" is often left out.
• environment: e-n-v-i-r-o-n-m-e-n-t.
• interesting: i-n-t-e-r-e-s-t-i-n-g.

### Days and months
• Wednesday: Wed-nes-day.
• February: Feb-ru-ary.
• Saturday: Sat-ur-day.
• Also check: January, August, December.

### Tip: "i before e except after c"
When the sound is /iː/, we usually write "ie", except after c, then "ei".
• believe, field, piece, chief (ie)
• receive, ceiling (ei after c)
Exceptions: neighbour, weigh (ei but not after c).

### Practice sentence
"Tomorrow my neighbour will visit the library to borrow a beautiful book."

## PAGE 4: Homophones and near-words
Some words sound the same or almost the same, but are spelt differently and have different meanings. Learn these.

• their (belonging to them) / there (a place) / they're (they are)
• two (2) / too (also, or more than needed) / to (towards)
• hear (with your ears) / here (in this place)
• to, too and two: "I went to the shop too. It was two miles away."
• write (with a pen) / right (correct, or the opposite of left)
• know (to have learnt) / no (the opposite of yes)
• see (with your eyes) / sea (a big body of water)
• one (1) / won (past tense of win)
• buy (to get by paying) / by (near or through)
• sun / son
• meet (to come together) / meat (food from animals)
• wear (to have clothes on) / where (in what place)
• whole (all) / hole (an opening)

### Sentences
1. "I can hear the crowd from here."
2. "They're putting their kit over there."
3. "Our team won the match."
4. "I know the answer, but I will not say no."

### Watch out for these mistakes
• "alot" is wrong. Write "a lot" (two words).
• "wich" is wrong. Write "which".
• "freind" is wrong. Write "friend".
• "becuase" is wrong. Write "because".

### Try it
Choose the right word: "I (know / no) the (write / right) answer." (know, right)

## PAGE 5: Games and activities to help spelling
### Jumbled letters
Unscramble the letters to form a word:
1. n-e-i-d-f-r → friend
2. u-c-a-b-e-s-e → because
3. p-e-l-o-e-p → people
4. t-a-b-i-e-u-l-f-u → beautiful
5. r-r-o-m-o-w-t-o → tomorrow
6. d-r-e-s-s-a-d → address (or: a-d-d-r-e-s-s)

### Word puzzle
Make a word search or a crossword with your partner. Choose ten commonly misspelt words. Write clues for the crossword.
Example clue: "The day after Tuesday." (Wednesday)

### Vocabulary book
Keep a book with a page for each letter. Write the words that trouble you. Copy each correctly, write its meaning, and write it in a sentence.

### Sentences with misspelt words
Write a sentence for each: because, people, different, address, favourite.
• "I like maths because it is interesting."
• "People from different places came to the match."
• "Please write your address here."
• "My favourite game is netball."

### Spelling bee
Stand in a line. A teacher reads a word. Spell it aloud. If you are correct, stay standing. If not, sit down. The last learner standing wins. Encourage everyone, and clap for correct spellers.

### Dictation
Ask a friend or adult to dictate: "My favourite team played different games. Their neighbour said the library is beautiful."

## PAGE 6: Checking your writing
### Proofreading steps
1. Read your work slowly, word by word.
2. Underline any word you are unsure of.
3. Check it in a dictionary.
4. Correct it.
5. Add it to your personal list.

### Use a dictionary
Dictionary words are in alphabetical order. To find "necessary", go to the n section, then look at the second letter, e, then the third, c.

### Spelling and speaking
Say the word aloud to hear its syllables. But remember: some letters are silent, so do not rely on hearing alone.

### Correct these sentences
1. "My freind said she wil come tomorow." → My friend said she will come tomorrow.
2. "The peple in the libary were quiet." → The people in the library were quiet.
3. "It was a beutiful day, becuase the sun was out." → It was a beautiful day, because the sun was out.

### Summary
• Say the word in syllables and notice tricky letters.
• Use memory tricks and cover-write-check.
• Learn homophones such as their/there and hear/here.
• Keep a personal spelling list and use a dictionary.

## QUIZ
1. Which spelling is correct?
   A) freind
   B) friend
   C) frend
   D) frind
   Answer: B
   Why: The correct spelling is f-r-i-e-n-d.
2. Which spelling is correct?
   A) becuase
   B) becase
   C) because
   D) becouse
   Answer: C
   Why: "Because" is spelt b-e-c-a-u-s-e.
3. Which word has a double r (rr)?
   A) tomorrow
   B) library
   C) address
   D) beautiful
   Answer: A
   Why: "Tomorrow" is spelt t-o-m-o-r-r-o-w, with a double r. The other words have no double r.
4. Choose the correct word: "I can ____ the music from here."
   A) here
   B) hear
   C) heer
   D) hair
   Answer: B
   Why: "Hear" is what we do with our ears.
5. Write the correct spellings: peple, libary, beutiful. (3 marks)
   Answer: people, library, beautiful.
   Why: One mark for each correct spelling.
6. Give a memory trick for spelling "necessary" or "separate". (2 marks)
   Answer: For example: "necessary" has one collar (c) and two sleeves (ss); "separate" has "a rat" in it.
   Why: One mark for a trick and one mark for a correct explanation.
7. Choose the right word in each sentence: (a) I (know / no) the answer. (b) They put (their / there) bags (their / there). (3 marks)
   Answer: (a) know (b) their, there
   Why: One mark for each correct choice.
8. Write three sentences using three of these words: because, different, address, favourite, tomorrow. (3 marks)
   Answer: Any three correct sentences using the words with correct spelling.
   Why: One mark for each correct sentence.
