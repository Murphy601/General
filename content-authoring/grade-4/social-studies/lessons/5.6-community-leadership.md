## PAGE 1: Who are community leaders?
A community is a group of people who live in the same area and share things such as a market, a water point, a school and customs. Every community needs people to guide it, settle problems and speak for it. These people are called community leaders.

A leader is a person who guides, helps and influences others to do something good. Community leaders are respected because they are wise, experienced or trusted.

### Kinds of leaders you may find in your community
• Religious leaders: pastors, priests, imams, sheikhs, elders of a temple and others who lead people in prayer and worship.
• Clan leaders: elders who lead a clan, which is a large family group that shares a common ancestor.
• Village leaders: elders who look after the affairs of a village.
• Government administrators: the chief and assistant chief, who work for the national government in your location and sub-location.
• School leaders: headteachers, teachers and pupil leaders.

These leaders may be chosen in different ways. Religious leaders are chosen or appointed by their faith groups. Clan elders are respected by the clan for their age and wisdom. Village elders are chosen or appointed in different ways, depending on the community. Government administrators are appointed by the government.

## PAGE 2: Religious leaders and their duties
Religious leaders guide people in matters of faith and good living. They can be found in churches, mosques, temples and other places of worship. People of every faith have leaders who are respected by their communities.

### Duties of religious leaders
• Leading prayers and worship services.
• Teaching about God and about good values such as honesty, love and forgiveness.
• Counselling people who are sad, worried or in conflict.
• Visiting the sick and comforting the bereaved.
• Conducting ceremonies such as weddings, naming, blessings and funerals.
• Promoting peace and unity among people, including people of different faiths.
• Helping the poor, for example by collecting food or clothes.
• Teaching children and young people.

### Why they are important
Many people turn first to a religious leader when they have a problem, because they trust them. A good religious leader helps the community to live in peace.

### Example
After a dispute between two neighbours, a local pastor and an imam invited both families to talk. They listened to each side, prayed together and helped them to agree. This is peace-building leadership.

## PAGE 3: Clan and village leaders
### Clan leaders
A clan is a large family group whose members trace their descent to a common ancestor. In many Kenyan communities, clans have elders who lead them. A group of elders is called a council of elders.

Clan elders:
• Keep the customs and history of the clan and teach them to young people.
• Settle disputes between families, for example about land or animals.
• Advise the community during ceremonies such as weddings and initiation.
• Help to keep peace, and sometimes help people who have quarrelled to reconcile.
• Bless and guide people at important events.

### Village leaders
A village is a small settlement of homes. A village elder knows the families and their problems. Village elders:
• Welcome visitors to the village.
• Settle small quarrels among neighbours.
• Call village meetings and tell people about decisions and government messages.
• Help the community to work together on projects, such as fixing a path or a water point.
• Report problems such as insecurity, hunger or illness to the chief or county officials.

### Chief and assistant chief
The chief leads a location and an assistant chief leads a sub-location. They are appointed by the national government. They help to keep law and order, pass on government information and work with elders to solve problems.

## PAGE 4: Qualities of a good leader
Not every leader is a good leader. A good leader has qualities that make people trust and follow them.

• Honesty: tells the truth and does not steal from the community.
• Fairness: treats everyone equally, rich or poor, whatever tribe or religion.
• Good listening: hears everyone before deciding.
• Wisdom: makes good decisions using knowledge and experience.
• Kindness and care: notices and helps those in need.
• Courage: does what is right even when it is hard.
• Humility: does not boast or look down on others.
• Hard work: works to improve the community.
• Accountability: explains what they have done with the community's resources.
• Good example: behaves in the way they ask others to behave.
• Good communication: speaks clearly and respectfully.

### Why should leaders have good qualities?
When leaders are honest and fair, people live in peace, resources are used well, and the community develops. If leaders are dishonest or unfair, people quarrel, resources are wasted and trust is lost.

### Example
An elder was asked to share food aid among families. He gave equal shares to all, including his enemies, and wrote down what he gave. Everyone trusted him. This shows fairness and honesty.

## PAGE 5: Practising and celebrating good leadership
Leaders are not only adults. You can practise leadership now at home, in class and on the playground.

### Activities
• Invite a resource person: ask a religious leader, village elder or chief to visit and explain their work. Prepare questions such as: What are your duties? What qualities do you need? What is difficult about your job?
• Role-play: act out a village elder settling a quarrel between two neighbours. The elder listens to both, is fair, and helps them to agree.
• Design a poster: write a title, such as "A Good Leader Serves", and list four qualities with a picture.
• Compose a song about good leadership. For example, choose a simple tune and sing: "A good leader listens, a good leader shares, a good leader works for all who care."

## PAGE 6: Respecting leaders and summary
Leaders need the support of their community. Respect does not mean agreeing with everything they say. It means listening politely, following fair instructions and giving advice respectfully.

### How to show appreciation for good leaders
• Greet them politely and thank them for their service.
• Attend meetings and take part in community projects.
• Obey good rules and laws.
• Give honest opinions and suggestions.
• Tell an adult if a leader misuses power or hurts a child.
• Learn from them and pray for them.

### Summary
Community leaders guide and serve the people. They include religious leaders, clan leaders and village leaders, together with government administrators such as chiefs. Their duties include leading worship, keeping customs, settling disputes, welcoming visitors and keeping peace. A good leader is honest, fair, wise, kind, humble, hard working and a good example.

### Common mistakes
• Thinking that leaders are important only because of their age. Their character matters most.
• Thinking that leaders can do whatever they want. Leaders must serve and follow the law.
• Forgetting that you can be a leader too, in your class and family.

## QUIZ
1. Who is a religious leader?
   A) A person who leads people in prayer and worship
   B) A market trader
   C) A bus driver
   D) A sports coach
   Answer: A
   Why: Religious leaders lead worship and teach values.
2. Which leader settles disputes between families and keeps the customs of the clan?
   A) Clan elder
   B) Cook
   C) Gatekeeper
   D) Driver
   Answer: A
   Why: Clan elders keep customs and settle disputes.
3. Which quality is most important for a leader who shares food aid?
   A) Boasting
   B) Fairness
   C) Laziness
   D) Selfishness
   Answer: B
   Why: Fairness means giving everyone an equal share.
4. Who leads a location and works for the national government?
   A) Chief
   B) Clan elder
   C) Pastor
   D) Village poet
   Answer: A
   Why: A chief is appointed by the government to lead a location. An assistant chief leads a sub-location.
5. Which action shows respect for community leaders?
   A) Insulting them
   B) Listening politely and following fair rules
   C) Refusing to attend any meetings
   D) Spreading rumours
   Answer: B
   Why: Respect includes polite listening and obeying good rules.
6. State three duties of a religious leader. (3 marks)
   Answer: Lead prayers, teach values and counsel people. Accept visiting the sick or conducting weddings and funerals.
   Why: 1 mark for each correct duty.
7. Give three qualities of a good leader. (3 marks)
   Answer: Honesty, fairness and listening. Accept kindness, courage or humility.
   Why: 1 mark for each correct quality.
8. Explain why leaders should have good qualities. (2 marks)
   Answer: When leaders are honest and fair, people trust them, there is peace and resources are well used.
   Why: 1 mark for trust or peace, 1 mark for good use of resources.
9. Describe how a village elder can help two neighbours who are quarrelling. (2 marks)
   Answer: The elder listens to both people, treats them fairly and helps them to agree and forgive each other.
   Why: 1 mark for listening to both, 1 mark for helping them make peace.
