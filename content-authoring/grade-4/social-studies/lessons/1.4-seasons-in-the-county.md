## PAGE 1: What is a season?
Have you noticed that some months are very hot and dusty, and others are wet with mud on the road? Some early mornings in July make you shiver, while some afternoons in February make you look for shade. These changes come in a pattern each year. A period of the year with a particular kind of weather is called a season.

Weather is what the air outside is like today: sunny, cloudy, windy, rainy, hot or cold. A season is the weather that is typical during several months.

In some countries far from the equator, the four seasons are spring, summer, autumn and winter. Kenya lies on the equator, so the temperature does not change as much through the year. Our seasons depend mainly on rain and on how high the land is above sea level. In Kenya we talk about four seasons:
• Hot season
• Cold season
• Dry season
• Rainy season

### Key words
• Weather: how the air is on a particular day.
• Season: a part of the year with a typical kind of weather.
• Rainfall: water that falls from the clouds.

### Think about it
1. What was the weather like yesterday?
2. Which season is it now in your county?
3. Which season do you like most and why?

This is only a general guide. The exact months are different from county to county.
• Hot season: Very hot days, especially in low areas and at the coast. It comes after the short rains, usually January to March.
• Rainy season: The long rains come in March to May and the short rains in October to December in many places. Parts of western Kenya get rain over more months.
• Cold season: Cool, cloudy weather with cold mornings, mostly June to August. It is coldest in high places such as the slopes of Mount Kenya.
• Dry season: Little or no rain, dusty air and dry grass.

## PAGE 3: How seasons affect what people do
Most farmers plan their year using the seasons. Other people's work also changes.

### Rainy season
• Farmers plant maize, beans and vegetables when the rains begin.
• Grass grows green and animals have plenty to eat.
• People collect rainwater in tanks and jerrycans.
• Roads can become muddy and rivers can flood.

### Dry season
• Farmers harvest and dry their crops, and prepare the land.
• Fishermen dry fish in the sun.
• In dry places, herders move their cattle, goats and camels to find water and grass.
• Water may become scarce and dust makes some people cough.

### Hot season
• People wear light clothes and drink more water.
• More people buy cold drinks and go to swim.
• The risk of bush fires is higher.

### Cold season
• People wear sweaters, jackets and closed shoes.
• Vendors sell more hot tea, roasted maize and blankets.
• Colds and flu are more common, so people must keep warm.

### Think about it
Which season is best for planting? Which is best for drying crops? Why?

## PAGE 5: Rainy weather and road safety
Rainy weather can be dangerous for people on roads and paths.

### Why it is dangerous
• Roads are slippery. Vehicles need longer to stop and pedestrians can fall.
• It is harder to see, because rain and mist blur the view, and drivers may not see people.
• Water can hide holes in the road.
• Floods can carry people and vehicles away.
• Lightning can strike people who stand under a tall tree.

### How to stay safe
• Never try to cross a flooded road, drain or river. Wait or go another way.
• Wear bright clothes or a reflective jacket so that drivers see you.
• If there is no pavement, walk on the side facing the traffic, as far from the road as you can.
• Cross the road only at a crossing or where you can see far in both directions. Look right, left, right again.
• Do not play in drains, puddles near the road or fast water.
• Do not take shelter under a tree during a thunderstorm. Go into a strong building.
• Be careful with umbrellas. Do not let them cover your eyes and ears.
• Ask a trusted adult to help you if the road is flooded.

### For drivers and boda boda riders
Slow down, keep a big distance from the vehicle in front, and switch on lights when it is raining or misty.

## PAGE 6: Caring for ourselves in every season
Each season has its own problems and its own benefits. We can plan ahead.

• Rainy season: keep drinking water clean, sleep under a mosquito net, and wear a raincoat.
• Dry season: save water, cover food, drink enough water, and plant trees.
• Hot season: wear a hat, stay in the shade in the middle of the day and drink plenty of clean water.
• Cold season: dress warmly, eat hot food and wash hands to avoid catching colds.

### A song idea
You can make up your own song about the seasons. Choose a tune you know, such as a rhythm you clap. For example: "When the rain comes down, the maize will grow, planting time is here, plant and hoe."

### Summary
Kenya has four seasons: hot, cold, dry and rainy. They depend on rainfall and how high the land is. Seasons affect farming, clothing, health and travel. A weather chart records the weather. Rain makes roads dangerous, so we must follow the safety rules.

### Common mistakes
• Saying that Kenya has summer and winter. We have hot, cold, dry and rainy seasons.
• Thinking that every county has the same months of rain. It differs from place to place.
• Sheltering under trees during a storm. That is dangerous.

## QUIZ
1. Which of these is a season in Kenya?
   A) Winter
   B) Autumn
   C) Rainy season
   D) Spring
   Answer: C
   Why: Kenya's four seasons are hot, cold, dry and rainy.
2. Which months usually have the long rains in many parts of Kenya?
   A) January to February
   B) March to May
   C) June to August
   D) September only
   Answer: B
   Why: The long rains come in about March to May, and the short rains in October to December.
3. In the dry season, herders in dry areas often do what?
   A) Plant maize
   B) Move animals to find water and grass
   C) Sell umbrellas
   D) Collect rainwater from the roof
   Answer: B
   Why: Grass and water are scarce, so animals are moved to where these can be found.
4. On the weather chart in Figure 2, how many days were rainy?
   A) 1
   B) 2
   C) 3
   D) 4
   Answer: B
   Why: Wednesday and Thursday show rain symbols, so there were 2 rainy days.
5. What should you do when you find a flooded road?
   A) Run through it quickly
   B) Cross carefully with friends
   C) Wait or take another way
   D) Swim across
   Answer: C
   Why: Floodwater can be deep, fast and hide holes. It is not safe to cross.
6. State three ways in which rainy weather makes roads dangerous. (3 marks)
   Answer: Roads become slippery; it is hard to see; water hides holes; floods can carry vehicles and people away.
   Why: 1 mark for each correct point, up to 3.
7. Explain how the rainy season helps farmers. (2 marks)
   Answer: The rain gives water to the crops so farmers can plant, and it makes grass grow for animals.
   Why: 1 mark for planting or water for crops, 1 mark for grass or pasture.
8. Give three things people do in the cold season. (3 marks)
   Answer: Wear warm clothes, drink hot tea or eat hot food, and use blankets.
   Why: 1 mark each for three correct activities.
9. Draw a weather chart showing sunny on Monday, rainy on Tuesday and cloudy on Wednesday, and add a key. (3 marks)
   Answer: Three boxes labelled Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday with a sun, a raincloud and a cloud, and a key that names each symbol.
   Why: 1 mark for correct days, 1 mark for correct symbols, 1 mark for the key.
