# Grade 4 Social Studies: notes for human checking

Coverage: 19 lessons (1.1-1.5, 2.1-2.2, 3.1-3.2, 4.1-4.3, 5.1-5.7). 5.1 and 5.5 were missing from manifest.json but are real sub-strands in the design, so they have lessons. No retire.json needed (every manifest topic has a lesson).

County-specific content is taught as concepts. Pupils are asked to apply them to their own county. Sample schools, timelines, dot maps and county maps are imaginary and labelled as examples.

Facts worth a quick human check:
- Turkana = largest county, Mombasa = smallest county by area (1.2).
- Kenya census 2019 count of about 47.6 million (2.2).
- Mount Kenya 5,199 m, second highest in Africa (1.3).
- Seasons (1.4): months given as a general pattern only (long rains Mar-May, short rains Oct-Dec, cool Jun-Aug, hot Jan-Mar). Real months differ by county. Also "walk facing the traffic" when there is no pavement.
- Historic sites (1.5): Fort Jesus (Portuguese, over 400 years old, UNESCO), Lamu Old Town, Gede Ruins near Watamu, Thimlich Ohinga in Migori, Uhuru Gardens, Bomas of Kenya, Kisumu and Kitale museums.
- Economic examples (4.1, 4.2): tea Kericho; coffee Nyeri and Kiambu; sugarcane Kakamega/Bungoma/Kisumu; Olkaria geothermal; wind power in Marsabit; soda ash Magadi; titanium Kwale; cement at Mombasa and Athi River.
- Traditional culture (3.1): examples of dress, food and games by community (shuka, kanga, kikoi, mursik, githeri, Isukuti, Maasai jumping dance, Kisii soapstone). Kept general and light.
- Child rights (5.3): age of marriage 18; child helpline 116 (lesson tells pupils to confirm with a teacher); FGM illegal; Day of the African Child 16 June. Sexual abuse is taught in a very brief, age-appropriate way with "No, Go, Tell".
- Children's Government (5.5): structure varies by school; lesson says so and describes a typical one.
- County government (5.7): governor, deputy governor, executive committee members, assembly with Speaker and MCAs (one per ward plus nominated members), first county elections 2013. Term limits deliberately left out. County vs national service lists are simplified.
- Corporal punishment: lesson says teachers are not allowed to beat pupils (3.2).

Figures: 53 inline SVGs (compass roses, sketch maps with keys, dot map, bar graphs, charts, clock at 8:00, tally chart, org charts). All well-formed XML; numbers checked against the text.
