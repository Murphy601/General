## PAGE 1: Time and the week
A week has seven days. Every day has its own name and its own work. On Monday you may go to school, on Saturday you may help at home or play football, and on Sunday you may go to church or mosque with your family. Days tell us when to do things, so knowing them helps us to plan our time.

Phonological awareness means noticing the sounds inside words. When you say a word, you can hear separate sounds. The word bayt has the sounds b, ay, t. Being aware of sounds helps you to pronounce each word correctly.

In this lesson you will learn the seven days in Arabic, practise pronouncing them, and learn simple instructions that you can act out, like "stand up" or "sit down".

### Talk about it
• What do you do on Monday?
• What do you do on Saturday?
• Which day do you like best, and why?

Share with a partner in English or Kiswahili. Then we will learn how to say the days in Arabic.

## PAGE 2: The seven days in Arabic
In Arabic-speaking countries the week starts on Sunday. The Arabic word for a day is yawm (يَوْمٌ) and for a week it is usbuuʿ (أُسْبُوعٌ). Here are the days in order.

### Days of the week
• al-ahad = الْأَحَدُ = Sunday
• al-ithnayn = الِاثْنَيْنِ = Monday
• ath-thulaathaa' = الثُّلَاثَاءُ = Tuesday
• al-arbiʿaa' = الْأَرْبِعَاءُ = Wednesday
• al-khamiis = الْخَمِيسُ = Thursday
• al-jumuʿa = الْجُمُعَةُ = Friday
• as-sabt = السَّبْتُ = Saturday

To say "on Monday", say yawma l-ithnayn (يَوْمَ الِاثْنَيْنِ), meaning "on the day of Monday".

## PAGE 3: Pronouncing the days
Some days have sounds that are new to you. Practise these parts carefully.

### Sound tips
• al-ithnayn begins with a short i. The sound th here is like th in "think". Say ith-nayn.
• ath-thulaathaa' has th twice. Hold the sound: ath-thu-laa-thaa'. In Arabic, the l of al- turns into th here, so we say ath-thulaathaa', not al-thulaathaa'. The same happens in as-sabt.
• al-khamiis has kh, the throat sound. It is like clearing your throat softly, not like k.
• al-jumuʿa has ʿ, a deep sound from the throat. Listen to your teacher and copy.
• al-arbiʿaa' begins with a small stop for the hamza. Say a-r-bi-ʿaa'.

### Say it faster
Say the days as a chain, in order, without stopping. Then say them backwards, starting with Saturday. This trains your mouth to be flexible.

### Make a song
With your group, choose a tune you all know, such as a clapping game. Sing the seven days in order. Clap on each day. Add a movement for each day so that you remember the order. You can perform your song in class.

## PAGE 4: What we do on different days
Listen to a short presentation. Your teacher reads:

adhhabu ilaa l-madrasa yawma l-ithnayn = أَذْهَبُ إِلَى الْمَدْرَسَةِ يَوْمَ الِاثْنَيْنِ = I go to school on Monday.
alʿabu yawma s-sabt = أَلْعَبُ يَوْمَ السَّبْتِ = I play on Saturday.

Notice madrasa (مَدْرَسَةٌ), which means school, and adhhabu (أَذْهَبُ), which means "I go".

To ask what day it is today, say: ayyu yawmin al-yawm? (أَيُّ يَوْمٍ الْيَوْمَ؟) The answer looks like this: al-yawmu yawmu l-khamiis (الْيَوْمُ يَوْمُ الْخَمِيسِ), "Today is Thursday."

Another useful sentence is fii l-usbuuʿi sabʿatu ayyaam (فِي الْأُسْبُوعِ سَبْعَةُ أَيَّامٍ), which means "There are seven days in a week."

### Answer orally
Your teacher asks: which day do you go to school? Which day do you play? Which day is your favourite? Answer with a full sentence using yawm and the day name. You may give the English words for the parts you have not yet learnt.

## PAGE 5: Simple instructions
An instruction tells someone to do something. Instructions help everyone to know what to do in class and at home. Here are some Arabic instructions. They are spoken to one boy. When speaking to a girl, add -ii at the end.

### Class instructions
• qif = قِفْ = stand up
• ijlis = اجْلِسْ = sit down
• ukhruj = اُخْرُجْ = go out
• udkhul = ادْخُلْ = come in
• iftahi l-baab = افْتَحِ الْبَابَ = open the door
• aghliqi l-baab = أَغْلِقِ الْبَابَ = close the door
• uktub = اُكْتُبْ = write
• iqraʾ = اِقْرَأْ = read
• ismaʿ = اِسْمَعْ = listen

To a girl, qif becomes qifii (قِفِي) and ijlis becomes ijlisii (اجْلِسِي).

### Polite words
Add min fadlika (مِنْ فَضْلِكَ), which means "please" to a boy, and thank the person with shukran (شُكْرًا).

### Game: act it out
In pairs, one learner gives an instruction, for example qif, min fadlika. The other learner acts it out. If they act correctly, they say shukran. Then swap. Try to give five different instructions each.

## PAGE 6: Summary and practice
### The value of time
Knowing the days of the week helps us plan school, homework, prayer, sport and rest. When you use time well, you have time for everything that matters. Being on time is a way of showing respect for other people.

### Summary
• The seven days are al-ahad, al-ithnayn, ath-thulaathaa', al-arbiʿaa', al-khamiis, al-jumuʿa, as-sabt.
• Say yawm before the day to mean "on the day of".
• Instructions to one boy: qif, ijlis, iftahi l-baab, ukhruj.
• Add -ii when speaking to a girl.
• Phonological awareness means listening to the sounds in words.

### Common mistakes
• Saying kh like k in al-khamiis.
• Saying al-thulaathaa' instead of ath-thulaathaa'.
• Forgetting that the week starts on Sunday in Arabic lists.
• Forgetting the polite word min fadlika.

### Practice
Say all seven days without looking. Give your partner two instructions and check that they follow them.

## QUIZ
1. Which Arabic word means "week"?
   A) يَوْمٌ
   B) أُسْبُوعٌ
   C) مَدْرَسَةٌ
   D) بَيْتٌ
   Answer: B
   Why: أُسْبُوعٌ (usbuuʿ) means week. يَوْمٌ (yawm) means day.
2. Which day is الْجُمُعَةُ (al-jumuʿa)?
   A) Sunday
   B) Wednesday
   C) Friday
   D) Saturday
   Answer: C
   Why: al-jumuʿa is Friday.
3. What does qif mean?
   A) Sit down
   B) Stand up
   C) Open the door
   D) Read
   Answer: B
   Why: qif (قِفْ) is the instruction to stand up. ijlis means sit down.
4. What is the first day of the week in the Arabic list?
   A) al-ahad
   B) al-jumuʿa
   C) as-sabt
   D) al-ithnayn
   Answer: A
   Why: The Arabic week begins with al-ahad, Sunday.
5. Which sound do you need for al-khamiis?
   A) A soft s
   B) The throat sound kh
   C) A hard k
   D) A long m
   Answer: B
   Why: The letters kh make a throat sound, like a soft clearing of the throat.
6. Write the seven days of the week in Arabic sounds (English letters), in order. (4 marks)
   Answer: al-ahad, al-ithnayn, ath-thulaathaa', al-arbiʿaa', al-khamiis, al-jumuʿa, as-sabt.
   Why: 4 marks for all seven in the correct order; 3 marks for five or six correct; 2 marks for three or four; 1 mark for one or two.
7. Give the instruction "close the door" in Arabic sounds and say what you would do if you were told this. (2 marks)
   Answer: aghliqi l-baab. I would shut the door.
   Why: 1 mark for the Arabic sounds, 1 mark for the action.
8. Explain why it is important to give and follow instructions politely. (2 marks)
   Answer: Polite instructions show respect. They help everyone to understand what to do and to work well together.
   Why: 1 mark for respect, 1 mark for understanding or teamwork.
9. Translate: adhhabu ilaa l-madrasa yawma l-ithnayn. (2 marks)
   Answer: I go to school on Monday.
   Why: 1 mark for "I go to school" and 1 mark for "on Monday".
