## PAGE 1: My family
A family is a group of people who love and care for one another. The smallest family group is called the nuclear family. It is made of parents and their children. In many homes in Kenya, grandparents, aunts, uncles and cousins also live close by or in the same compound. Together they are the extended family.

In this lesson you will listen for the main idea, which is called the gist. When you listen for gist, you do not need every word. You listen for who is being talked about and what is being said about them. You will also learn Arabic words for members of the family.

### How to listen attentively
• Sit still and look at the speaker.
• Do not talk while another person is speaking.
• Think about the topic. Here the topic is family.
• Listen for words you know, then guess the rest.
• Ask a question when the speaker has finished.

### Try it
Your teacher will say the names of family members in Kiswahili. Put up your hand when you hear the word for mother or father. Practise listening carefully before we meet the Arabic words.

## PAGE 2: Members of the nuclear family
Here are the Arabic words for the members of the nuclear family. Say each one aloud after your teacher.

### Family words
• ab = أَبٌ = father
• umm = أُمٌّ = mother
• akh = أَخٌ = brother
• ukht = أُخْتٌ = sister
• ibn = ابْنٌ = son
• bint = بِنْتٌ = daughter or girl
• usra = أُسْرَةٌ = family

The word umm has a small sign over the letter mim that looks like a tiny w. It is called shadda and it means the letter is said twice, so we say um-m, not um.

To say "my father" we add the sound -ii to the end. So my father is abii (أَبِي), my mother is ummii (أُمِّي), my brother is akhii (أَخِي) and my sister is ukhtii (أُخْتِي). The little -ii ending always means "my".

### Chant
Say this chant in a group, clapping the beat:
abii, ummii, akhii, ukhtii
usratii, usratii (أُسْرَتِي means "my family").

## PAGE 4: Aunts, uncles and grandparents
The extended family has more words. In Arabic, your father's brother and your mother's brother have different names. The same is true for aunts.

### Extended family words
• jadd = جَدٌّ = grandfather
• jadda = جَدَّةٌ = grandmother
• ʿamm = عَمٌّ = father's brother (uncle)
• ʿamma = عَمَّةٌ = father's sister (aunt)
• khaal = خَالٌ = mother's brother (uncle)
• khaala = خَالَةٌ = mother's sister (aunt)

Add -ii to say "my": jaddii (جَدِّي) is my grandfather, and jaddatii (جَدَّتِي) is my grandmother.

### Question and answer
A friend may ask: man haadhaa? (مَنْ هَذَا؟) meaning "Who is this?" when pointing to a boy or man. When pointing to a girl or woman, ask man haadhihi? (مَنْ هَذِهِ؟)

You answer: haadhaa abii (هَذَا أَبِي), "This is my father." Or: haadhihi ummii (هَذِهِ أُمِّي), "This is my mother."

You can also say: ʿindii akh wa ukht (عِنْدِي أَخٌ وَأُخْتٌ), which means "I have a brother and a sister."

## PAGE 5: What family members do
Every person in the family has a role. Parents care for the children, work, cook and pay for food and school. Children help at home, study and respect the elders. Grandparents share stories and advice.

Listen to these short sentences. Your teacher will read them and you will hear the family words you learnt.

### Sentences to listen to
• abii yaʿmalu = أَبِي يَعْمَلُ = My father works.
• ummii tatbukhu = أُمِّي تَطْبُخُ = My mother cooks.
• akhii yalʿabu = أَخِي يَلْعَبُ = My brother plays.
• ukhtii taqraʾu = أُخْتِي تَقْرَأُ = My sister reads.
• jaddatii tuhibbunii = جَدَّتِي تُحِبُّنِي = My grandmother loves me.

### Listen for the gist
After hearing each sentence, answer with one word: who is being talked about? If you hear yaʿmalu, the person works. Listening for the family word tells you the main idea even if you did not catch every word.

### Appreciate your family
Talk with a partner. Say one thing that your mother, your father or your grandparent does for you. Say thank you in Arabic: shukran (شُكْرًا).

## PAGE 6: Practice and summary
### Pronounce carefully
Words with shadda (ّ) are said with a little pause on the doubled letter: um-m, jad-d, ʿam-m. Words with the letter kh, such as akh and khaal, need the throat sound. Words with ʿ, such as ʿamm, need the deep throat sound. Practise in groups and give each other kind feedback.

### Summary
• The nuclear family is parents and children.
• ab is father, umm is mother, akh is brother, ukht is sister.
• Add -ii to mean "my": abii, ummii.
• Use man haadhaa? for a boy or man, and man haadhihi? for a girl or woman.

### Common mistakes
• Saying the two-letter word akh with a soft k instead of the throat sound kh.
• Mixing up ʿamm (father's brother) and khaal (mother's brother).
• Forgetting that -ii means "my".

## QUIZ
1. What is the Arabic word for "mother"?
   A) أَبٌ
   B) أُمٌّ
   C) أَخٌ
   D) جَدٌّ
   Answer: B
   Why: أُمٌّ (umm) means mother. أَبٌ is father, أَخٌ is brother and جَدٌّ is grandfather.
2. What does ukhtii mean?
   A) my brother
   B) my father
   C) my sister
   D) my grandmother
   Answer: C
   Why: ukht is sister and the ending -ii means "my", so ukhtii is "my sister".
3. Which question do you ask when you point to a girl and want to know who she is?
   A) man haadhaa?
   B) man haadhihi?
   C) kayfa haaluka?
   D) maa ismuka?
   Answer: B
   Why: haadhihi is used for a girl or woman. haadhaa is for a boy or man.
4. Which word means "father's brother"?
   A) ʿamm
   B) khaal
   C) ibn
   D) jadd
   Answer: A
   Why: ʿamm is the father's brother. khaal is the mother's brother.
5. What is the meaning of haadhihi ummii?
   A) This is my mother.
   B) This is my sister.
   C) This is my grandfather.
   D) I have a sister.
   Answer: A
   Why: haadhihi means "this (girl or woman)" and ummii means "my mother".
6. List four members of the nuclear family in Arabic (sounds in English letters) and give their meanings. (4 marks)
   Answer: For example ab (father), umm (mother), akh (brother), ukht (sister).
   Why: 1 mark for each correct word with its meaning.
7. Translate into English: ʿindii akh wa ukht. (2 marks)
   Answer: I have a brother and a sister.
   Why: 1 mark for "I have" and 1 mark for "a brother and a sister".
8. Explain what it means to listen for the gist. (2 marks)
   Answer: It means listening to find the main idea of what is said, without needing to understand every word.
   Why: 1 mark for the main idea and 1 mark for saying you do not need every word.
9. Write two things you can do to show that you are listening attentively. (2 marks)
   Answer: For example look at the speaker and do not interrupt.
   Why: 1 mark for each sensible listening behaviour.
