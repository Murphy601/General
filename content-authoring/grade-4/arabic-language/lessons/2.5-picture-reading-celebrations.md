## PAGE 1: Pictures tell stories
A picture can tell a whole story without a single word. There is a saying that a picture is worth a thousand words. When you look carefully, you can see who is in the picture, what they are doing, where they are, and how they feel.

Picture reading is an important skill for beginners in a new language. Even if you do not know all the Arabic words, the picture helps you to understand the text next to it. Pictures are cues, or clues, that help you to guess new words.

In this lesson we read pictures about family celebrations. You will describe the events, pick out people and things, answer questions and make predictions.

### How to read a picture
• Look at the whole picture first.
• Then look at the details: people, things, colours and places.
• Ask yourself: who, what, where, when and how?
• Say what you see in short sentences.
• Think about what may happen next.

### Warm up
Think about a celebration that you know, such as a birthday. Describe to your partner what you would see in a picture of it. Say the words you know in Arabic, such as hafla, kaʿka, baaluun and hadiyya.

### Describe it
Say what you can see. Use your own words. For example: "There are two children. There is a cake on the table. There is a balloon above the table. There is a gift on the floor."

### Words for the picture
• hafla = حَفْلَةٌ = party
• kaʿka = كَعْكَةٌ = cake
• baaluun = بَالُونٌ = balloon
• hadiyya = هَدِيَّةٌ = gift
• sadiiq = صَدِيقٌ = friend

## PAGE 3: Reading sentences about the picture
Now read Arabic sentences that describe a party picture. Look at Figure 1 as you read.

### Sentences
• هَذِهِ حَفْلَةٌ = haadhihi hafla = This is a party.
• عَلَى الطَّاوِلَةِ كَعْكَةٌ = ʿalaa t-taawilati kaʿka = On the table there is a cake.
• فَوْقَ الطَّاوِلَةِ بَالُونٌ = fawqa t-taawilati baaluun = Above the table there is a balloon.
• الْأَصْدِقَاءُ فِي الْحَفْلَةِ = al-asdiqaa'u fii l-hafla = The friends are at the party.
• هَدِيَّةٌ لِأَمِينَةَ = hadiyya li-Aminata = A gift for Amina.

The small word li (لِ) before a name means "for". So li-Aminata means "for Amina".

### Pick out people and things
Read the sentences again and find:
• two things (cake, balloon or gift)
• one place word (fii or ʿalaa or fawqa)
• one group of people (al-asdiqaa', the friends)

Write each in your book with its meaning.

### Work in pairs
Take turns to read one sentence and point to the part of the picture that it describes.

## PAGE 4: Asking and answering questions
You can ask questions about a picture. In Arabic, question words come at the front of the sentence.

### Question words
• man? = مَنْ؟ = who?
• maadhaa? = مَاذَا؟ = what?
• ayna? = أَيْنَ؟ = where?
• hal? = هَلْ؟ = is it true that? (starts a yes or no question)
• limaadhaa? = لِمَاذَا؟ = why?
• kam? = كَمْ؟ = how many?

### Sample questions and answers
• ayna l-kaʿka? = أَيْنَ الْكَعْكَةُ؟ = Where is the cake?
   ʿalaa t-taawila = عَلَى الطَّاوِلَةِ = On the table.
• hal fii l-hafla asdiqaa'? = هَلْ فِي الْحَفْلَةِ أَصْدِقَاءُ؟ = Are there friends at the party?
   naʿam = نَعَمْ = Yes.
• maadhaa fawqa t-taawila? = مَاذَا فَوْقَ الطَّاوِلَةِ؟ = What is above the table?
   baaluun = بَالُونٌ = A balloon.

### Ask each other
In pairs, ask three questions about Figure 1 and answer them. You can also ask in English or Kiswahili for the parts you do not yet know in Arabic.

## PAGE 5: Making predictions
A prediction is a sensible guess about what will happen next. When you read a picture, look for clues.

### Clues in the picture
• The candle is on the cake, so the friends may sing.
• There is a gift on the floor, so the birthday child may open it.
• The friends are standing beside the table, so they will probably eat the cake.

### Answer these questions
• What will the friends do next? Give your reason.
• Who is the party for? What clue tells you?
• How do you think the friends feel? Why?

There is no single correct answer to a prediction. A good answer gives a reason based on the picture.

### Ask "why?"
Practise asking limaadhaa? A friend says "The friends will sing." You ask limaadhaa? and the friend answers: "Because there is a cake with a candle."

### Value of special days
Talk about why we celebrate special days. They bring family and friends together, teach us to share and show gratitude. Say shukran (شُكْرًا) to the people who prepare celebrations for you.

## PAGE 6: A class picture book
Working in a group, make a picture book about a family celebration. The pages will show a celebration in a simple way.

### Steps
1. Collect pictures or draw your own of things at a celebration: cake, balloons, gifts, food, friends.
2. Stick or draw one picture on each page.
3. Under each picture, write an Arabic word or a simple sentence, such as haadhihi hafla (هَذِهِ حَفْلَةٌ) or kaʿka (كَعْكَةٌ).
4. Add the English meaning in small letters beside it.
5. Make a cover with a title, such as حَفْلَتِي (haflatii, my party).
6. Put the book in the reading corner so that others can read it for enjoyment.

### Summary
• Read a picture by looking at the whole, then the details.
• Ask who, what, where and why.
• A prediction is a sensible guess with a reason.
• Use li- for "for", fii for "in" and ʿalaa for "on".
• A picture book helps you to read for enjoyment.

### Common mistakes
• Guessing without looking at the picture carefully.
• Forgetting to give a reason for a prediction.
• Mixing up man (who) and maadhaa (what).

## QUIZ
1. What does the word maadhaa mean?
   A) Who
   B) What
   C) Where
   D) Why
   Answer: B
   Why: مَاذَا (maadhaa) means what. مَنْ is who, أَيْنَ is where and لِمَاذَا is why.
2. What does ayna l-kaʿka? ask?
   A) Who is the cake?
   B) Where is the cake?
   C) What is the cake?
   D) How many cakes?
   Answer: B
   Why: ayna means where and al-kaʿka means the cake.
3. Which sentence means "On the table there is a cake"?
   A) عَلَى الطَّاوِلَةِ كَعْكَةٌ
   B) فَوْقَ الطَّاوِلَةِ بَالُونٌ
   C) هَذِهِ حَفْلَةٌ
   D) هَدِيَّةٌ لِأَمِينَةَ
   Answer: A
   Why: عَلَى means on, الطَّاوِلَةِ means the table and كَعْكَةٌ means a cake.
4. What does the small word li- mean in li-Aminata?
   A) in
   B) on
   C) for
   D) and
   Answer: C
   Why: The prefix li- means for, so li-Aminata means "for Amina".
5. What is a prediction?
   A) A sensible guess about what will happen next
   B) A copy of a word
   C) A list of foods
   D) A spelling test
   Answer: A
   Why: We use clues to guess what will happen next.
6. Name three things you can see at a birthday party in Arabic sounds (English letters) and give their meanings. (3 marks)
   Answer: For example kaʿka (cake), baaluun (balloon), hadiyya (gift).
   Why: 1 mark for each correct item with meaning.
7. Write one question in Arabic sounds that you can ask about a picture of a party, and give the meaning. (2 marks)
   Answer: For example ayna l-kaʿka? Where is the cake?
   Why: 1 mark for a correct question word and 1 mark for the meaning.
8. Look at Figure 1. Make a prediction about what the friends will do next and give a reason. (2 marks)
   Answer: They will sing and eat the cake, because the cake has a candle and the friends are standing beside the table.
   Why: 1 mark for a sensible prediction and 1 mark for a reason based on the picture.
9. Describe the steps of making a picture book on family celebrations. (3 marks)
   Answer: Collect or draw pictures, stick one picture on each page, write an Arabic word or sentence under each with the meaning, and make a cover.
   Why: 1 mark for pictures, 1 mark for writing the words or sentences and 1 mark for the cover or sharing.
