## PAGE 1: The food we eat
Food gives us energy to learn, play and grow. In Kenya we eat many foods: maize, beans, rice, chapati, sukuma wiki, fish, milk, bananas, mangoes and much more. Different counties have different favourite foods, and many families enjoy sharing them at meals and on special days.

In this lesson you will listen to short oral texts about food and drinks. You will answer questions about what you heard. There are two kinds of questions.

### Direct questions
The answer is said clearly in the text. Example: "What does Amina eat in the morning?" You can find the answer in the words you heard.

### Inferential questions
The answer is not said, but you can work it out from clues. Example: "Is it morning or evening?" The speaker may say "I eat breakfast", so you know it is morning.

Good listeners think about both kinds of questions. Before you listen, remember to sit still, look at the speaker, and keep your ears open for the names of foods.

### Talk about it
Tell your partner what you ate today. Which food do you like most? Why?

## PAGE 2: Names of foods
Here are Arabic words for common foods. Repeat after your teacher.

### Foods
• khubz = خُبْزٌ = bread
• aruzz = أَرُزٌّ = rice
• dhura = ذُرَةٌ = maize
• laHm = لَحْمٌ = meat
• samak = سَمَكٌ = fish
• dajaaj = دَجَاجٌ = chicken
• bayd = بَيْضٌ = eggs
• jazar = جَزَرٌ = carrots
• Tamaatim = طَمَاطِمُ = tomatoes
• bataatis = بَطَاطِسُ = potatoes
• khudaar = خُضَارٌ = vegetables
• faakiha = فَاكِهَةٌ = fruit
• tuffaaH = تُفَّاحٌ = apples
• mawz = مَوْزٌ = bananas
• burtuqaal = بُرْتُقَالٌ = oranges
• milH = مِلْحٌ = salt
• sukkar = سُكَّرٌ = sugar

### Notice
The word for mango, manjuu (مَانْجُو), is borrowed from another language. Words that come from other languages are also written in Arabic letters.

## PAGE 3: Names of drinks and useful verbs
### Drinks
• maa' = مَاءٌ = water
• Haliib = حَلِيبٌ = milk
• shaay = شَايٌ = tea
• ʿasiir = عَصِيرٌ = juice
• sharaab = شَرَابٌ = drink

### Verbs for eating and drinking
• aakulu = آكُلُ = I eat
• ashrabu = أَشْرَبُ = I drink
• uHibbu = أُحِبُّ = I like

### Model sentences
• aakulu l-khubz = آكُلُ الْخُبْزَ = I eat bread.
• ashrabu l-maa' = أَشْرَبُ الْمَاءَ = I drink water.
• uHibbu l-mawz = أُحِبُّ الْمَوْزَ = I like bananas.
• uHibbu l-ʿasiir = أُحِبُّ الْعَصِيرَ = I like juice.

To ask a friend, say hal tuHibbu l-mawz? (هَلْ تُحِبُّ الْمَوْزَ؟) which means "Do you like bananas?" The answer can be naʿam, uHibbu l-mawz (yes, I like bananas) or laa (no).

### Talk with a partner
Ask your partner three questions using hal tuHibbu. Answer with naʿam or laa.

## PAGE 4: Listening to a short text
Listen while your teacher reads this text slowly. Do not look at the page the first time.

### Amina's breakfast
fii s-Sabaah aakulu khubzan wa bayDan wa ashrabu shaayan. uHibbu l-mawz.

In Arabic script:
فِي الصَّبَاحِ آكُلُ خُبْزًا وَبَيْضًا وَأَشْرَبُ شَايًا. أُحِبُّ الْمَوْزَ.

In English: In the morning I eat bread and eggs and I drink tea. I like bananas.

The new word wa (وَ) means "and". The word fii means "in". Sabaah (صَبَاحٌ) means morning.

### Direct questions
• What does Amina eat in the morning? (bread and eggs)
• What does she drink? (tea)
• Which fruit does she like? (bananas)

### Inferential questions
• Is this a breakfast or a supper? Clue: fii s-Sabaah, "in the morning".
• Does Amina like a healthy breakfast? Clue: she eats eggs and a fruit she likes.

Now work in pairs. One learner reads the text aloud while the other listens. Then swap. Try to make up one direct and one inferential question of your own.

## PAGE 5: Talking about food and singing
Now you can have a small conversation. Use the words you learnt.

### A conversation
A: hal tuHibbu l-ʿasiir?
B: naʿam, uHibbu l-ʿasiir. wa anta?
A: uHibbu l-Haliib. shukran.

The word anta (أَنْتَ) means "you" (to a boy) and anti (أَنْتِ) means "you" (to a girl). So wa anta? (وَأَنْتَ؟) means "And you?"

### Sing about foods
Make a song using foods in your area. For example, repeat this simple line with different foods:
uHibbu l-mawz, uHibbu l-mawz
uHibbu l-khubz, uHibbu l-khubz

Add clapping. Groups may perform their songs at an inter-class festival.

### Food is life
Food is important for good health. A meal with fruit, vegetables, protein and energy foods helps your body to grow strong. Sharing food with friends is also a sign of love.

## PAGE 6: Do not waste food
Millions of people go hungry every day. When we waste food, we waste the work of farmers and the money of our parents. Here is how we can reduce food waste at home and at school.

• Take only what you can eat.
• Store leftovers safely and eat them later.
• Share extra food with neighbours in need.
• Use vegetable peels for compost or animal feed.
• Grow some vegetables in a small garden.

### Summary
• Food words: khubz, aruzz, laHm, samak, bayd, mawz, maa', Haliib.
• uHibbu means I like. aakulu means I eat. ashrabu means I drink.
• hal makes a yes or no question.
• Direct questions have answers in the text. Inferential questions need clues.

### Common mistakes
• Mixing up aakulu (I eat) and ashrabu (I drink).
• Saying an answer that is not in the text for a direct question.
• Not looking at the speaker while listening.

## QUIZ
1. What does khubz mean?
   A) Rice
   B) Bread
   C) Milk
   D) Fish
   Answer: B
   Why: خُبْزٌ (khubz) means bread.
2. Which Arabic sentence means "I drink water"?
   A) آكُلُ الْخُبْزَ
   B) أُحِبُّ الْمَوْزَ
   C) أَشْرَبُ الْمَاءَ
   D) أُحِبُّ الْعَصِيرَ
   Answer: C
   Why: أَشْرَبُ (ashrabu) means I drink and الْمَاءَ means the water.
3. What does hal tuHibbu l-mawz? mean?
   A) Where is the banana?
   B) I like bananas.
   C) Do you like bananas?
   D) I eat bananas.
   Answer: C
   Why: hal begins a yes or no question and tuHibbu means "you like".
4. Which is a direct question about the text "Amina's breakfast"?
   A) What does Amina drink?
   B) Why does Amina like tea?
   C) Is Amina a healthy girl?
   D) What will she eat tomorrow?
   Answer: A
   Why: The text says that she drinks tea, so the answer is stated directly.
5. In the text, which words tell you it is morning?
   A) khubzan wa bayDan
   B) fii s-Sabaah
   C) uHibbu l-mawz
   D) ashrabu shaayan
   Answer: B
   Why: Sabaah means morning, so fii s-Sabaah, "in the morning", tells us the time.
6. Write three foods and two drinks in Arabic sounds (English letters) with meanings. (5 marks)
   Answer: For example khubz (bread), samak (fish), mawz (bananas); maa' (water), Haliib (milk).
   Why: 1 mark for each correct word with its meaning.
7. Translate into English: uHibbu l-ʿasiir. (2 marks)
   Answer: I like juice.
   Why: 1 mark for "I like" and 1 mark for "juice".
8. Explain the difference between a direct question and an inferential question. (2 marks)
   Answer: The answer to a direct question is stated in the text. For an inferential question you use clues to work out the answer.
   Why: 1 mark for each correct description.
9. Give two ways of reducing food waste at home. (2 marks)
   Answer: For example take only what you can eat; store leftovers and eat them later; share extra food.
   Why: 1 mark for each sensible way.
