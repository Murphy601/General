## PAGE 1: Talking about weather
Every day we notice the weather. Is the sun hot? Are there clouds in the sky? Is the wind blowing? Will it rain? Farmers watch the weather to plan when to plant. Children look at the sky to decide whether to carry an umbrella or a jersey. In Kenya, the weather is different from place to place. The coast is warm and humid, the highlands around Mount Kenya are cool, and the north is often dry.

Fluency means speaking smoothly and clearly, without long stops. To speak fluently you need good pronunciation, good speed and good intonation. Intonation is the way your voice goes up and down when you speak. Your voice goes down at the end of a statement, and it goes up at the end of a question.

In this lesson you will learn Arabic words for weather and the environment and practise saying them fluently. You will also learn how to check a friend's speech and give kind advice.

### Talk about it
Look out of the window. Describe the weather in English or Kiswahili to your partner. Then listen to your partner and say whether you agree.

## PAGE 2: Weather words
Repeat these words after your teacher. Say each word three times.

### Weather vocabulary
• Taqs = الطَّقْسُ = weather
• jaww = الْجَوُّ = the weather, the air (used to speak about the weather)
• shams = شَمْسٌ = sun
• saHaab = سَحَابٌ = clouds
• maTar = مَطَرٌ = rain
• riiH = رِيحٌ = wind
• samaa' = سَمَاءٌ = sky
• Haarr = حَارٌّ = hot
• baarid = بَارِدٌ = cold
• jamiil = جَمِيلٌ = beautiful, nice

### Describing the weather
To say what the weather is like, use al-jawwu and an adjective.
• al-jawwu Haarr = الْجَوُّ حَارٌّ = The weather is hot.
• al-jawwu baarid = الْجَوُّ بَارِدٌ = The weather is cold.
• al-jawwu jamiil = الْجَوُّ جَمِيلٌ = The weather is nice.
• al-jawwu mumTir = الْجَوُّ مُمْطِرٌ = The weather is rainy.
• al-jawwu mushmis = الْجَوُّ مُشْمِسٌ = The weather is sunny.
• al-jawwu ghaa'im = الْجَوُّ غَائِمٌ = The weather is cloudy.

## PAGE 3: Pronunciation and intonation
Fluent speakers do three things. They pronounce every sound clearly, they speak at a steady pace, and they use their voice well.

### Pronunciation practice
• Taqs starts with a heavy T, made with the tongue flat and the sound deep. It is different from a light t.
• maTar also has the heavy T.
• Haarr begins with H, a breathy sound like blowing on your glasses to clean them. Do not say it like the English h in hat.
• Haarr has a doubled r (a shadda). Roll the r twice: Haar-r.

### Intonation practice
Say the same words in two ways.
• A statement: al-jawwu Haarr. Let your voice fall at the end.
• A question: hal al-jawwu Haarr? Let your voice rise at the end. The question means "Is the weather hot?"

You can also ask kayfa l-jaww al-yawm? (كَيْفَ الْجَوُّ الْيَوْمَ؟) which means "How is the weather today?" The answer is al-jawwu Haarr al-yawm (الْجَوُّ حَارٌّ الْيَوْمَ), "The weather is hot today."

### Excitement and feeling
Say al-jawwu jamiil with a happy voice. Say al-jawwu baarid with a shivering voice. Intonation helps a listener to feel what you feel.

## PAGE 4: Peer review for fluency
Peer review means listening to your friend and telling them how they did. It should always be kind and helpful. Use this checklist.

### Fluency checklist
1. Did my friend say each word clearly?
2. Was the speed steady, not too fast and not too slow?
3. Did the voice go down for statements and up for questions?
4. Did my friend look at the listeners?
5. What is one thing to improve next time?

### How to give feedback
• Start with something good: "You said maTar very well."
• Give one tip: "Try to make the H in Haarr more breathy."
• End kindly: "Let us practise together."

### Activity
Work in threes. One learner describes today's weather in three Arabic sentences. The second learner listens and fills in the checklist. The third learner watches the time. Change roles so that everybody has every job.

Example description:
al-jawwu jamiil al-yawm. ash-shamsu Haarra. as-samaa'u zarqaa'.

In Arabic script: الْجَوُّ جَمِيلٌ الْيَوْمَ. الشَّمْسُ حَارَّةٌ. Here Haarra (حَارَّةٌ) is used with shams because shams is a feminine word. Practise the first two sentences.

## PAGE 5: Taking care of our environment
The environment is everything around us: land, water, air, plants, animals and weather. When we look after it, it looks after us. Trees give shade, clean the air and hold the soil. Clean water keeps us healthy.

### Environment words
• bii'a = بِيئَةٌ = environment
• shajara = شَجَرَةٌ = tree
• zahra = زَهْرَةٌ = flower
• jabal = جَبَلٌ = mountain
• nahr = نَهْرٌ = river
• naDhiif = نَظِيفٌ = clean

### Sentences
• nuHaafiDhu ʿalaa l-bii'a = نُحَافِظُ عَلَى الْبِيئَةِ = We look after the environment.
• azraʿu shajara = أَزْرَعُ شَجَرَةً = I plant a tree.
• laa narmii l-qumaama = لَا نَرْمِي الْقُمَامَةَ = We do not throw rubbish.
• al-maa'u naDhiif = الْمَاءُ نَظِيفٌ = The water is clean.

### Class discussion
How can we look after our immediate environment? Think about the school compound, your home, the river or the road you use. Use pronunciation and intonation as you share your ideas. Give each person a fair chance to speak.

## PAGE 6: A short talk and summary
Prepare to say three sentences about the weather and the environment to the class. For example:

al-jawwu jamiil al-yawm. azraʿu shajara. nuHaafiDhu ʿalaa l-bii'a.

الْجَوُّ جَمِيلٌ الْيَوْمَ. أَزْرَعُ شَجَرَةً. نُحَافِظُ عَلَى الْبِيئَةِ.

The meaning is: "The weather is nice today. I plant a tree. We look after the environment." Read it aloud until it sounds smooth. Practise with a friend, then say it to the class.

### Summary
• Fluency means clear sounds, steady pace and good intonation.
• Weather words: shams, saHaab, maTar, riiH, Haarr, baarid.
• Voice falls at the end of a statement and rises at the end of a question.
• Peer review should be kind and helpful.
• We look after the environment by planting trees and keeping things clean.

### Common mistakes
• Saying the heavy T like a light t.
• Speaking in a flat voice with no intonation.
• Speaking so fast that the words run together.

## QUIZ
1. What does the word مَطَرٌ (maTar) mean?
   A) Sun
   B) Wind
   C) Rain
   D) Cloud
   Answer: C
   Why: مَطَرٌ means rain.
2. Which sentence means "The weather is cold"?
   A) الْجَوُّ حَارٌّ
   B) الْجَوُّ بَارِدٌ
   C) الْجَوُّ جَمِيلٌ
   D) الْجَوُّ مُشْمِسٌ
   Answer: B
   Why: بَارِدٌ (baarid) means cold. حَارٌّ is hot, جَمِيلٌ is nice and مُشْمِسٌ is sunny.
3. What happens to your voice at the end of a question?
   A) It goes up
   B) It goes down
   C) It stops
   D) It stays the same for every word
   Answer: A
   Why: In a question the voice rises at the end. In a statement it falls.
4. Which of these is the Arabic question "How is the weather today?"
   A) كَيْفَ الْجَوُّ الْيَوْمَ؟
   B) مَا اسْمُكَ؟
   C) أَيْنَ الْكِتَابُ؟
   D) هَلْ عِنْدَكَ حَفْلَةٌ؟
   Answer: A
   Why: kayfa means how, al-jaww means the weather and al-yawm means today.
5. What is fluency?
   A) Reading only very slowly
   B) Speaking smoothly and clearly with good pronunciation
   C) Speaking as loudly as possible
   D) Speaking without meaning
   Answer: B
   Why: Fluency is smooth, clear speech using correct sounds, steady pace and intonation.
6. Write three weather words in Arabic sounds (English letters) with their meanings. (3 marks)
   Answer: For example shams (sun), maTar (rain), riiH (wind).
   Why: 1 mark for each correct word with meaning.
7. Say and translate one Arabic sentence about the weather today. (2 marks)
   Answer: For example al-jawwu Haarr al-yawm: The weather is hot today.
   Why: 1 mark for a correct Arabic sentence, 1 mark for the correct translation.
8. Give three questions you can ask in a fluency checklist when you review a friend's speech. (3 marks)
   Answer: Were the words clear? Was the speed steady? Did the voice fall for statements and rise for questions?
   Why: 1 mark for each suitable question.
9. Explain two ways to look after the environment near your school. (2 marks)
   Answer: For example plant trees, and put rubbish in a bin.
   Why: 1 mark for each sensible action.
