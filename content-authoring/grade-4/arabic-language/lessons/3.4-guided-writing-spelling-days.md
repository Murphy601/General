## PAGE 1: Why spelling matters
Spelling means writing the letters of a word in the right order. When you spell correctly, other people can read what you wrote. If you leave out a letter or put it in the wrong place, the word may change or become impossible to understand.

In Arabic, spelling is very important, because the words are made from a small number of letters and small dots. A missing letter or a dot in the wrong place can turn one word into another. For example, if you write ب instead of ت, you write a different letter and often a different word.

In this lesson we practise spelling the days of the week in Arabic. You already know their names and how to read them. Now you will list them, spell them, complete them and use them in sentences.

### The days of the week
الْأَحَدُ • الِاثْنَيْنِ • الثُّلَاثَاءُ • الْأَرْبِعَاءُ • الْخَمِيسُ • الْجُمُعَةُ • السَّبْتُ

### Talk about it
Why is it important to spell correctly? Think about a text message with a wrong word. Was it confusing? Share with your partner.

### Spell aloud
Say each day, then spell it in Arabic letter names. For example, السَّبْتُ is spelled: alif, laam, siin, baa, taa. We spell from right to left. The vowel marks are not spelled.

## PAGE 2: The spelling of each day
Here is each day written without vowel marks, as it usually appears in books, and with the letters named in order.

### Day spellings
• الأحد = al-ahad = alif, laam, alif (with hamza), Haa, daal
• الاثنين = al-ithnayn = alif, laam, alif, thaa, nuun, yaa, nuun
• الثلاثاء = ath-thulaathaa' = alif, laam, thaa, laam, alif, thaa, alif, hamza
• الأربعاء = al-arbiʿaa' = alif, laam, alif (with hamza), raa, baa, ʿayn, alif, hamza
• الخميس = al-khamiis = alif, laam, khaa, miim, yaa, siin
• الجمعة = al-jumuʿa = alif, laam, jiim, miim, ʿayn, taa marbuta
• السبت = as-sabt = alif, laam, siin, baa, taa

### Notice
• Every day starts with alif and laam. This is the word "the" (al-).
• In ath-thulaathaa' and as-sabt the laam is not pronounced, but it is still written.
• Friday ends with taa marbuta (ة), a special taa that appears at the end of many feminine words.
• Wednesday and Tuesday end in alif and hamza (اء).

### Practise
Write each day three times, then cover it and write it again from memory.

### Jumbled letter exercises
Put the letters in order to make a day (without al-).
1. د ح أ  → أحد (ahad, Sunday)
2. س ي م خ → خميس (khamiis, Thursday)
3. ة ع م ج → جمعة (jumuʿa, Friday)
4. ت ب س → سبت (sabt, Saturday)

Answer each puzzle by writing the letters in the order of the day, from right to left, and joining them.

### Fishing for letters
Put a mixed heap of letter cards on the floor. Your teacher calls a day. Two learners race to "fish out" the right cards and lay them in order. The class checks the spelling by reading each letter name aloud.

### Tip
Look for the letter that starts the day. Then find the letter that ends it. The middle letters are then easier to find.

## PAGE 4: Complete the missing letter
Puzzles with a missing letter train your memory for spelling. Fill in each missing letter.

### Puzzle 1
الخ_يس → the missing letter is miim (م). The word is الخميس (Thursday).

### Puzzle 2
الجم_ة → the missing letter is ʿayn (ع). The word is الجمعة (Friday).

### Puzzle 3
السب_ → the missing letter is taa (ت). The word is السبت (Saturday).

### Puzzle 4
الأح_ → the missing letter is daal (د). The word is الأحد (Sunday).

### Your turn
Here are four more for you to solve. Write the complete word.
1. الاثن_ن (missing yaa)
2. الأرب_اء (missing ʿayn)
3. الثلا_اء (missing thaa)
4. ا_سبت (missing laam)

Answers: 1 الاثنين, 2 الأربعاء, 3 الثلاثاء, 4 السبت. Read each aloud and say the day in English.

### Check
Compare your answers with your partner. If you disagree, look at the list of spellings on Page 2 and decide together.

## PAGE 5: Dictation and word puzzles
In a dictation, your teacher says a word and you write it. You must listen carefully to each sound, then write the letters in order.

### How to do a dictation
1. Your teacher says the day, for example al-khamiis.
2. Repeat it quietly to yourself.
3. Say the sounds one by one: al, kha, miis.
4. Write the letters from right to left.
5. Your teacher repeats the word so that you can check.
6. Swap books with a partner and correct together.

### Word matching puzzle
Match each Arabic day with its English name. Draw lines in your book.
• الجمعة
• الأحد
• السبت
• الخميس
Names: Sunday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday.

Answers: الجمعة is Friday, الأحد is Sunday, السبت is Saturday, الخميس is Thursday.

### Correct the spelling
A learner wrote الخميس as الحميس. What is the mistake? The first letter after al- should be khaa (خ) with a dot above. The learner wrote Haa (ح) with no dot. Correct spelling needs the correct dot.

### Language puzzle
Find the day with the letter jiim. Find the day with the letter thaa twice. (Answers: al-jumuʿa, ath-thulaathaa'.)

## PAGE 6: Making sentences with days
Words in a sentence must be in the right order. In these puzzles, the words of a sentence are jumbled. Put them in order.

### Jumbled sentences
1. الْخَمِيسِ / يَوْمُ / الْيَوْمُ
Answer: الْيَوْمُ يَوْمُ الْخَمِيسِ = al-yawmu yawmu l-khamiis = Today is Thursday.

2. الْمَدْرَسَةِ / إِلَى / أَذْهَبُ
Answer: أَذْهَبُ إِلَى الْمَدْرَسَةِ = adhhabu ilaa l-madrasa = I go to school.

3. السَّبْتِ / يَوْمَ / أَلْعَبُ
Answer: أَلْعَبُ يَوْمَ السَّبْتِ = alʿabu yawma s-sabt = I play on Saturday.

### Work in groups
Write each word of a sentence on a separate card. Shuffle the cards. Another group puts them in order and reads the sentence aloud.

### Summary
• Spelling means writing the letters in the correct order.
• The days start with al- (alif and laam).
• Friday ends with taa marbuta (ة).
• Jumbled letters, missing letters and dictation are good ways to practise.
• Correct spelling includes the correct dots.

### Common mistakes
• Leaving out the alif and laam at the start of a day.
• Missing or misplacing a dot, as in ح and خ.
• Spelling from left to right.

## QUIZ
1. How many letters are in the word السبت (as-sabt)?
   A) 3
   B) 4
   C) 5
   D) 6
   Answer: C
   Why: The letters are alif, laam, siin, baa, taa, so there are five.
2. Which letter is missing in الجم_ة (Friday)?
   A) ع
   B) غ
   C) ح
   D) ف
   Answer: A
   Why: The word is الجمعة, so the missing letter is ʿayn (ع).
3. Which day ends with taa marbuta (ة)?
   A) الأحد
   B) الخميس
   C) الجمعة
   D) السبت
   Answer: C
   Why: الجمعة (Friday) ends with ة.
4. Which is the correct spelling of Thursday?
   A) الحميس
   B) الخميس
   C) الجميس
   D) الخمبس
   Answer: B
   Why: Thursday is الخميس with khaa (خ), which has one dot above.
5. What is a dictation?
   A) The teacher says a word and you write it
   B) You draw a picture
   C) You sing a song
   D) You copy a word from the board
   Answer: A
   Why: In a dictation you listen and write what you hear.
6. Put the letters in order to make a day: ت ب س. Write the word and its meaning. (2 marks)
   Answer: سبت (sabt), Saturday.
   Why: 1 mark for the correct word and 1 mark for the meaning.
7. Rearrange to make a sentence and give the meaning: أَلْعَبُ / يَوْمَ / السَّبْتِ. (3 marks)
   Answer: أَلْعَبُ يَوْمَ السَّبْتِ. I play on Saturday.
   Why: 1 mark for the first word, 1 mark for the correct order and 1 mark for the meaning.
8. Name the seven days in Arabic sounds and choose one to spell with letter names. (4 marks)
   Answer: al-ahad, al-ithnayn, ath-thulaathaa', al-arbiʿaa', al-khamiis, al-jumuʿa, as-sabt. For as-sabt: alif, laam, siin, baa, taa.
   Why: 3 marks for all the days (2 for five or six, 1 for three or four) and 1 mark for a correct spelling.
9. Explain why correct spelling is important in Arabic. (2 marks)
   Answer: A missing letter or a dot in the wrong place can change the word, so the reader may not understand the message.
   Why: 1 mark for a change in the word and 1 mark for communication or understanding.
