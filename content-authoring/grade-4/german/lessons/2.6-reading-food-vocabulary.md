## PAGE 1: Reading a food chart
A food chart shows pictures with their names. Reading charts helps you learn words quickly. Look at each word, say it aloud, then cover it and try to remember.

### Fruit (das Obst) and vegetables (das Gemüse)
der Apfel = apple
die Banane = banana
die Orange = orange
die Mango = mango
die Tomate = tomato
die Kartoffel = potato
die Karotte = carrot
die Zwiebel = onion
der Kohl = cabbage or kale
die Bohne = bean

### Main foods
das Brot = bread
der Reis = rice
der Mais = maize
das Ei = egg
der Fisch = fish
das Fleisch = meat
das Huhn = chicken
der Käse = cheese

### Say it
Käse = KAY-ze. Huhn = hoon. Say the u long, like "oo".

### Sound check
Read these aloud: Kartoffel, Zwiebel, Käse, Fleisch. Which words contain ei, ie, ä?

## PAGE 2: Drinks and meals on the chart
### Drinks (die Getränke)
das Wasser = water
die Milch = milk
der Tee = tea
der Saft = juice
der Orangensaft = orange juice
der Kaffee = coffee
der Kakao = cocoa
die Limonade = lemonade, fizzy drink

### Meals (die Mahlzeiten)
das Frühstück = breakfast
das Mittagessen = lunch
das Abendessen = supper

### Sentences to read
Zum Frühstück esse ich Brot und trinke Tee. = For breakfast I eat bread and drink tea.
Zum Mittagessen esse ich Reis und Bohnen. = For lunch I eat rice and beans.
Zum Abendessen esse ich Ugali und Kohl. = For supper I eat ugali and kale.

### Note
The word zum means "for the" here. It is used with meals. Ugali is a Kenyan word which German speakers use as it is.

## PAGE 3: Reading a text on food
Read this text aloud, then answer the questions.

### Text: Essen bei uns
Ich heiße Aisha. Zum Frühstück trinke ich Tee und esse ein Mandazi. Zum Mittagessen esse ich Reis mit Bohnen. Ich mag Bananen und Mangos. Ich trinke gern Wasser. Ich mag keine Cola, denn Cola ist nicht gesund. Meine Mutter kocht Ugali mit Fisch. Das schmeckt gut!

### New words
denn = because. gesund = healthy. schmeckt gut = tastes good. ich mag keine = I do not like (any). mit = with.

### Questions
1. Was trinkt Aisha zum Frühstück? = What does Aisha drink for breakfast? Tea.
2. Was isst sie zum Mittagessen? = What does she eat for lunch? Rice with beans.
3. Welche Früchte mag sie? = Which fruits does she like? Bananas and mangoes.
4. Warum mag sie keine Cola? = Why does she not like cola? Because it is not healthy.
5. Was kocht die Mutter? = What does the mother cook? Ugali with fish.

Say: warum = va-ROOM (why). welche = VEL-khe (which).

## PAGE 4: Healthy and unhealthy foods
Reading lists helps us sort foods. Read the two lists aloud. Then discuss in English which of these foods you eat often.

### Gesund (healthy)
Obst, Gemüse, Wasser, Bohnen, Fisch, Milch, Mais

### Ungesund (unhealthy if eaten too often)
Süßigkeiten (sweets), Limonade, Chips, Zucker (sugar)

### Say: Chips = chips. Süßigkeiten = ZÜ-sikh-ky-ten. Zucker = TSOO-ker.

### Sentences to read
Obst ist gesund. = Fruit is healthy.
Zucker ist nicht sehr gesund. = Sugar is not very healthy.
Ich esse gern Gemüse. = I like eating vegetables.

### Group task
In a group, create two lists on a chart: gesund and ungesund. Write only the German words. Read the lists aloud to the class.

### Reading tip
Words that begin with the same sound help you remember: Wasser, Wassermelone. Words that end the same: Apfelsaft, Orangensaft. The little word for a joined noun always comes from the last part.

## PAGE 5: Playing with words: food blends
In the curriculum we can also make up funny food names by joining the start of one food word with the end of another. These are playful words, not real German words, but they help reading.

### Examples
Banane + Reis = Banareis (a banana and rice mash).
Orange + Apfel = Orapf (an orange and apple salad).
Cola + Milch = Colmil (a cola and milk drink).

### Try it
1. Choose two foods that you like.
2. Take the start of one and the end of the other.
3. Write the new word and read it aloud to a friend.
4. Say what is in it: "Das ist Banane und Reis."

### Remember
• A chart with pictures helps you to learn words fast.
• Food words are nouns, so they start with a capital letter.
• Use zum with meals: Zum Frühstück esse ich Brot.
• Say aloud each word you read.

Common mistake: writing "das Reis". Reis is a der word, so it is der Reis.

## QUIZ
1. What is "das Brot"?
   A) Egg
   B) Bread
   C) Rice
   D) Fish
   Answer: B
   Why: das Brot is bread.
2. What does "Ich mag keine Cola" mean?
   A) I like cola
   B) I do not like cola
   C) I am buying cola
   D) I drink cola every day
   Answer: B
   Why: keine shows that the person has no liking for cola.
3. Which word means "healthy"?
   A) lecker
   B) gesund
   C) kalt
   D) groß
   Answer: B
   Why: gesund means healthy.
4. Which is a drink?
   A) der Reis
   B) das Ei
   C) der Saft
   D) der Kohl
   Answer: C
   Why: der Saft is juice, a drink.
5. Write the German words with der, die or das for: rice, milk, egg, banana. (4 marks)
   Answer: der Reis, die Milch, das Ei, die Banane.
   Why: 1 mark for each noun with its correct article.
6. Read: "Zum Frühstück trinke ich Tee." Explain in English what it says. (2 marks)
   Answer: For breakfast I drink tea.
   Why: 1 mark for breakfast, 1 mark for tea.
7. List three healthy foods and two unhealthy foods in German. (5 marks)
   Answer: Healthy, for example: Obst, Gemüse, Bohnen. Unhealthy, for example: Süßigkeiten, Limonade.
   Why: 1 mark for each correct food in the right group.
8. Create a food blend from Mango and Milch. Write it and say what it is. (2 marks)
   Answer: Example: Mangmil, a mango and milk drink.
   Why: 1 mark for a sensible blend, 1 mark for the explanation.
