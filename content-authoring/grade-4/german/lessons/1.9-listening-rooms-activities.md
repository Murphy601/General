## PAGE 1: What happens in which room?
Every room in a home has a job. We cook in the kitchen and sleep in the bedroom. If you hear an activity, you can often guess the room. This is how we find the gist of a listening text about the home.

### Room words (revision)
das Wohnzimmer = living room.
das Schlafzimmer = bedroom.
die Küche = kitchen.
das Badezimmer = bathroom.
der Garten = garden.

### Room and place words
Look at the little word "in". It is used to show the place: in der Küche (in the kitchen), im Wohnzimmer (in the living room), im Schlafzimmer (in the bedroom), im Bad (in the bathroom), im Garten (in the garden).
Im is short for in dem. It is used with der and das words. In der is used with die words.

### Try it
Look at the words above. Which are die words? Which are der or das words? The ones with "in der" are die words: die Küche. The ones with im are der and das words.

## PAGE 2: Activity words
Here are verbs that tell what people do at home. The verb for ich (I) ends in -e. Say each one aloud.

ich koche = I cook. Say: ikh KOKH-e.
ich schlafe = I sleep. Say: ikh SHLAH-fe.
ich lese = I read. Say: ikh LAY-ze.
ich spiele = I play. Say: ikh SHPEE-le.
ich dusche = I have a shower. Say: ikh DOO-she.
ich esse = I eat. Say: ikh ES-se.
ich sehe fern = I watch TV. Say: ikh ZAY-e fern.

### Full sentences
Mutter kocht in der Küche. = Mother cooks in the kitchen.
Ich schlafe im Schlafzimmer. = I sleep in the bedroom.
Ich lese im Wohnzimmer. = I read in the living room.
Ich spiele im Garten. = I play in the garden.
Ich dusche im Badezimmer. = I shower in the bathroom.

Notice that the verb is the second idea in the sentence. Mutter kocht (Mother cooks). The word kocht has -t because it is about someone else (she cooks), not I.

## PAGE 3: Guess the room game
Play this game to practise listening and speaking.

### Rules
1. One player says an activity: "Ich schlafe."
2. The others guess: "Das ist im Schlafzimmer!"
3. If the guess is right, they say "Richtig!" The guesser goes next.
4. Wait for your turn and never shout over another person.

### More activity ideas
ich lerne = I learn or study. Say: ikh LER-ne.
ich helfe = I help. Say: ikh HEL-fe.
ich wasche = I wash. Say: ikh VA-she.
ich singe = I sing. Say: ikh ZIN-ge.

### Example round
Player A: Ich koche.
Player B: Das ist in der Küche!
Player A: Richtig! Jetzt du.

The word jetzt means now, and du means you.

## PAGE 4: Listening text and gist questions
Read or listen to this text twice. The first time, listen for the main idea. The second time, listen for details.

### Text: Ein Samstag zu Hause
Heute ist Samstag. Ich bin zu Hause. Mutter kocht in der Küche. Vater liest im Wohnzimmer. Mein Bruder spielt im Garten. Ich helfe Mutter. Wir essen zusammen. Das Essen ist lecker!

### New words
zusammen = together. Say: tsu-ZAM-men. das Essen = the food, the meal.

### Gist questions
1. Which day is it? Saturday.
2. Where is Mother? In the kitchen.
3. What is Father doing? He is reading in the living room.
4. Where is the brother? In the garden.
5. What is the main idea? A family spends a happy Saturday at home.

### Note
The gist is not about each word. It is about what the whole text tells us: a family is at home, each person does something, and they eat together.

## PAGE 5: Staying safe at home (discussion in English)
Some of our lessons on the home also talk about safety. This part of the lesson is in English, but you can use the German room names.

### Talking points
• Kitchen (die Küche): Keep matches, hot pots and sharp knives out of reach of small children. Make sure the cooking fire or stove is not left alone.
• Bathroom (das Badezimmer): Keep the floor dry so no one slips.
• Living room (das Wohnzimmer): Do not overload sockets with too many plugs. Keep electric wires away from water.
• Garden (der Garten): Keep chemicals and tools in a safe place.
• All rooms: Know the exit, the door (die Tür), so you can get out quickly in an emergency.

### Discussion
Talk with a partner: Which room in your home is most risky? What can you do to make it safe?

### Remember
• im Wohnzimmer, im Garten, in der Küche.
• Listen to the activity word to guess the room.
• Take turns: do not interrupt someone who is speaking.

Common mistake: saying "in dem Küche". Küche is a die word, so say in der Küche.

## QUIZ
1. Which sentence means "Mother cooks in the kitchen"?
   A) Mutter schläft im Garten
   B) Mutter kocht in der Küche
   C) Mutter liest im Wohnzimmer
   D) Mutter spielt im Bad
   Answer: B
   Why: kocht means cooks and in der Küche means in the kitchen.
2. Where do you sleep?
   A) in der Küche
   B) im Garten
   C) im Schlafzimmer
   D) im Badezimmer
   Answer: C
   Why: Schlafzimmer is the bedroom, where we sleep.
3. Which is correct?
   A) in der Wohnzimmer
   B) im Küche
   C) in der Küche
   D) in dem Küche
   Answer: C
   Why: die Küche uses in der.
4. You hear "Ich lese." Which room is most likely?
   A) Im Wohnzimmer
   B) In der Toilette
   C) In der Dusche
   D) Im Regen
   Answer: A
   Why: Reading is often done in the living room. It is the only sensible room among the options.
5. Write the German for: I cook, I sleep, I read, I play. (4 marks)
   Answer: Ich koche, ich schlafe, ich lese, ich spiele.
   Why: 1 mark for each correct sentence.
6. Write one German sentence that tells where someone plays. (2 marks)
   Answer: Ich spiele im Garten.
   Why: 1 mark for Ich spiele, 1 mark for im Garten.
7. Read: "Heute ist Samstag. Vater liest im Wohnzimmer. Mutter kocht in der Küche." What is the gist? (3 marks)
   Answer: It is Saturday and the parents are busy at home. Father is reading in the living room and Mother is cooking in the kitchen.
   Why: 1 mark for Saturday, 1 mark for father, 1 mark for mother.
8. Name two ways to make the kitchen safer at home. (2 marks)
   Answer: Keep hot pots and matches out of reach of small children; never leave the cooker alone.
   Why: 1 mark for each sensible safety idea.
