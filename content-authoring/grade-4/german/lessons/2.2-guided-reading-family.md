## PAGE 1: Punctuation marks help us read
When we speak, our voice goes up, goes down and stops. In writing, punctuation marks show us how to do this. If you ignore them, your reading sounds flat and can be confusing. Compare: "Let's eat, Grandma" and "Let's eat Grandma". A comma changes the meaning!

German uses the same basic marks as English. Learn their German names.

### The marks
der Punkt (.) = full stop. Your voice goes down and you stop. Say: poonkt.
das Komma (,) = comma. You take a very short pause. Say: KOM-ma.
das Fragezeichen (?) = question mark. Your voice goes up at the end. Say: FRAH-ge-tsy-khen.
das Ausrufezeichen (!) = exclamation mark. You show surprise, joy or a strong request. Say: OWS-roo-fe-tsy-khen.

### Example
Wer ist das?  (voice goes up)
Das ist mein Vater.  (voice goes down)
Hallo, Papa!  (short pause after Hallo, then joy)

### Try it
Read the three lines above aloud. Notice how your voice changes with each mark.

## PAGE 2: Reading a text on the family
Read this text aloud in a whisper first. Then read it again clearly. Pay attention to the full stops and the question mark.

### Text: Meine Familie
Ich heiße Zawadi. Ich bin zehn Jahre alt. Das ist meine Familie. Mein Vater heißt Juma. Er ist groß und nett. Meine Mutter heißt Neema. Sie ist jung und lustig. Ich habe einen Bruder. Er heißt Baraka. Er ist klein. Wer ist das? Das ist meine Oma. Sie ist alt, aber sehr nett!

### Words to learn from the text
Jahre alt = years old. Say: YAH-re alt.
groß = big, tall. Say: grohs.
klein = small. Say: kline.
nett = nice, kind. Say: net.
jung = young. Say: yoong.
alt = old. Say: alt.
lustig = funny, jolly. Say: LOOS-tikh.
aber = but. Say: AH-ber.
sehr = very. Say: zair.

### Reading tip
In German, every noun starts with a capital letter: Familie, Vater, Mutter, Bruder, Oma. Names also begin with capital letters. That helps you find the nouns as you read.

## PAGE 3: Answering questions about the text
Reading for the answer means finding it in the text. Look back at Zawadi's text.

### Questions and answers
1. Wie heißt das Mädchen? = What is the girl called? Zawadi.
2. Wie alt ist Zawadi? = How old is Zawadi? She is ten.
3. Wie heißt der Vater? = What is the father called? Juma.
4. Ist die Mutter alt? = Is the mother old? No, she is young and funny.
5. Wer ist alt, aber sehr nett? = Who is old but very nice? The grandmother (die Oma).

Say: Wie = vee (how/what). Wer = vair (who). Ist = ist (is).

### How to find an answer
1. Read the question.
2. Find the key word: for example Vater or Mutter.
3. Look at the sentence around that word.
4. Write the answer in a full sentence, with a capital letter and a full stop.

### Pair reading
Read the questions aloud to your partner. Your partner finds the answer in the text. Then swap. Remember to change your voice at the question mark.

## PAGE 4: Reading descriptions and playing the guessing game
The guessing game "Wer ist das?" is a good way to read descriptions.

### Read the descriptions and guess who it is
1. Er ist groß und nett. Er ist mein Vater. Wer ist das? Das ist der Vater.
2. Sie ist alt und nett. Sie ist meine Oma. Wer ist das? Das ist die Oma.
3. Er ist klein und lustig. Er ist mein Bruder.

### Sentence pattern
Das ist mein/meine ... = This is my ...
Er ist ... = He is ...
Sie ist ... = She is ...

### Family poem to read aloud
Meine Mama ist nett.
Mein Papa ist lustig.
Meine Oma ist alt.
Mein Opa ist groß.
Ich habe sie alle gern!

Meaning: My mum is kind. My dad is funny. My grandmother is old. My grandfather is tall. I like them all!

New words: sie alle = them all. ich habe gern = I like.

Read this poem aloud. Stop at each full stop, and use joy in your voice at the exclamation mark.

## PAGE 5: Reading tongue twisters and checking fluency
### Tongue twister to read
Montags macht mir meine muntere Mutter mittags meistens Mus.

Read the line slowly and count: it has seven m-words. The comma and full stops guide your breath. Because this line has only one full stop, take one big breath and read it as one sentence.

### Fluency checklist
• Did I read every word correctly?
• Did I stop at full stops and take a short pause at commas?
• Did my voice go up at question marks?
• Did I show feelings at exclamation marks?
• Did I read at a natural speed, not too fast or slow?

### Remember
• Punkt (.) stop, Komma (,) short pause, Fragezeichen (?) voice up, Ausrufezeichen (!) strong feeling.
• All nouns and all names have capital letters.
• Read for meaning, not just sounds.

Common mistake: reading a question with a flat voice. Look for the question mark before you read.

## QUIZ
1. What does the mark "?" show?
   A) The sentence is over
   B) A question
   C) A short pause
   D) Surprise
   Answer: B
   Why: ? is the Fragezeichen, the question mark.
2. What is the German name for a full stop?
   A) das Komma
   B) das Fragezeichen
   C) der Punkt
   D) das Ausrufezeichen
   Answer: C
   Why: der Punkt is the full stop.
3. What does "Er ist groß und nett" mean?
   A) He is small and old
   B) He is big and nice
   C) She is big and nice
   D) He is young and funny
   Answer: B
   Why: er = he, ist = is, groß = big, nett = nice.
4. Which word must start with a capital letter in German?
   A) ist
   B) und
   C) Mutter
   D) nett
   Answer: C
   Why: Mutter is a noun, and every German noun starts with a capital letter.
5. Read: "Meine Mutter heißt Neema. Sie ist jung und lustig." What is the mother like? (2 marks)
   Answer: She is young and funny.
   Why: 1 mark for young (jung) and 1 mark for funny (lustig).
6. Write the four punctuation marks with their German names. (4 marks)
   Answer: . der Punkt, , das Komma, ? das Fragezeichen, ! das Ausrufezeichen.
   Why: 1 mark for each mark with its name.
7. Write a short description in German of your father or mother using er/sie ist. (3 marks)
   Answer: For example: Meine Mutter heißt Neema. Sie ist jung und nett.
   Why: 1 mark for a correct name sentence, 1 mark for er/sie ist with an adjective, 1 mark for capital letters and full stops.
8. Explain why punctuation marks are important for reading aloud. (2 marks)
   Answer: They show where to stop, pause, raise the voice or show feeling, so the meaning is clear.
   Why: 1 mark for showing how to read, 1 mark for meaning.
