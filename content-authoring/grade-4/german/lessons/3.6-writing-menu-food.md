## PAGE 1: Sorting food into groups
Functional writing means writing that has a job to do, such as a list, a menu or a shopping list. To write these, we first sort foods into categories. In German, a category is die Kategorie.

### Groups of food
das Obst = fruit: der Apfel, die Banane, die Orange, die Mango, die Ananas
das Gemüse = vegetables: die Tomate, die Karotte, die Zwiebel, der Kohl, die Kartoffel
die Getränke = drinks: das Wasser, der Tee, die Milch, der Saft, der Kakao
das Fleisch = meat: das Huhn (chicken), das Rindfleisch (beef)
der Fisch = fish
das Getreide = grains: der Reis, der Mais, das Brot

Say: die Ananas = AH-nah-nas. das Getreide = geh-TRY-de. das Rindfleisch = RINT-flysh.

### A tip on spelling
Look closely at Obst, the word for fruit. It is spelled O-b-s-t. In the curriculum some people write "Öbst", which is not correct. The correct spelling is das Obst.

### Try it
Take a sheet of paper. Make four columns: Obst, Gemüse, Getränke, Fleisch/Fisch. Write five foods in the correct columns with their little word.

## PAGE 2: Healthy and unhealthy food
Write sentences about healthy and unhealthy foods.

### Useful words
gesund = healthy
ungesund = unhealthy
lecker = tasty
süß = sweet
Obst und Gemüse sind gesund. = Fruit and vegetables are healthy.
Zu viel Zucker ist ungesund. = Too much sugar is unhealthy.
Wasser ist gesund. = Water is healthy.
Limonade ist süß. = Lemonade is sweet.

Say: süß = züs, with ü. zu viel = tsoo feel.

### Make two lists
Gesund: Obst, Gemüse, Bohnen, Fisch, Wasser
Nicht so gesund: Süßigkeiten, Limonade, Chips

### Writing a sentence
Use this pattern: [Food] ist gesund. Or [Foods] sind gesund.
Notice: use ist with one thing and sind with many: Der Apfel ist gesund. Äpfel sind gesund.

### Plural hint
The plural of der Apfel is die Äpfel. The a changes to ä. The plural of die Banane is die Bananen. The plural of die Bohne is die Bohnen.

### Responsibility
Writing lists of healthy and unhealthy foods reminds us to take care of our health. Choose fruit and water more often than sweets and fizzy drinks.

## PAGE 3: Word puzzles with food
### Unscramble
1. FELPA → Apfel (der Apfel)
2. NANABE → Banane (die Banane)
3. TORB → Brot
4. SERI → Reis
5. LIMCH → Milch
6. RAFFTOKLE → Kartoffel

### Word search grid idea
Choose eight food words. Write them in a grid 10 by 10, across and down. Fill other squares with letters. Swap with a friend.

### Crossword clues in English
1. A white drink from cows: Milch.
2. Food from chickens: Ei.
3. Used to make ugali: Mais.

### Tips
Write in capital letters in a crossword and skip the little words der, die, das. Umlauts: write ä as ä, or AE if the puzzle needs one letter per square.

### Practice
Make a crossword with four food words. Give each word a clue in English and swap.

## PAGE 4: Writing a menu
A menu is a plan of what you will eat and drink at different times of the day. Here is an example from the curriculum.

### Menu example
Frühstück: Kakao und Mandazi
Mittagessen: Githeri und Orangensaft
Abendessen: Ugali und Sukuma wiki

### Meaning
Breakfast: cocoa and mandazi. Lunch: githeri and orange juice. Supper: ugali and sukuma wiki.

### Rules for writing a menu
1. Write the name of each meal followed by a colon.
2. Then write the food and drink.
3. Join two foods with und.
4. Use capital letters for nouns.
5. No verb is needed in a menu list.

### Your menu
Write your own menu for one day. Choose something for Frühstück, Mittagessen and Abendessen. You can add a drink and a fruit as well.

Beispiel: Frühstück: Tee und Brot. Mittagessen: Reis und Bohnen. Abendessen: Ugali und Fisch. Obst: eine Banane.

### Sharing
Read your menu aloud to your partner. Say if your partner's menu is healthy: Das ist gesund! Or ask: Ist das gesund?

## PAGE 5: Sentences about food I like
Now write full sentences. Use ich esse and ich trinke and gern for things you like.

### Pattern
Ich esse gern ... = I like eating ...
Ich trinke gern ... = I like drinking ...
Ich mag ... = I like ...
Ich mag keine Cola. = I do not like cola. (Copy this pattern for now.)

### A model text
Ich heiße Neema. Zum Frühstück esse ich Brot. Ich trinke gern Tee. Zum Mittagessen esse ich Reis und Bohnen. Ich mag Mangos. Zum Abendessen esse ich Ugali und Kohl. Das ist lecker und gesund!

### Checklist
• Are foods written with capital letters?
• Did I use esse for foods and trinke for drinks?
• Did I write ü in Frühstück and süß?
• Did I add full stops?

### Remember
• Sort foods: Obst, Gemüse, Getränke, Fleisch/Fisch, Getreide.
• Use und to join foods in a menu.
• Frühstück has ü; Mittagessen has double t and double s.

Common mistake: writing "Öbst". Correct: Obst.

## QUIZ
1. Which German word means "vegetables"?
   A) das Obst
   B) das Gemüse
   C) die Getränke
   D) das Getreide
   Answer: B
   Why: das Gemüse is vegetables.
2. Which food group does "der Apfel" belong to?
   A) Getränke
   B) Gemüse
   C) Obst
   D) Fleisch
   Answer: C
   Why: An apple is a fruit, das Obst.
3. What does "Zum Frühstück esse ich Brot" mean?
   A) For lunch I eat bread
   B) For breakfast I eat bread
   C) I do not like bread
   D) I bake bread
   Answer: B
   Why: Frühstück is breakfast.
4. Which verb goes with a drink?
   A) esse
   B) trinke
   C) koche
   D) lese
   Answer: B
   Why: trinken means to drink.
5. Unscramble and write with the article: FELPA and SERI. (4 marks)
   Answer: der Apfel and der Reis.
   Why: 1 mark for each correct word and 1 mark for each correct article.
6. Write a menu for one day in German with three meals. (3 marks)
   Answer: For example: Frühstück: Tee und Brot. Mittagessen: Reis und Bohnen. Abendessen: Ugali und Fisch.
   Why: 1 mark for each meal correctly written with a food.
7. Write two sentences about healthy food and one about unhealthy food. (3 marks)
   Answer: For example: Obst ist gesund. Wasser ist gesund. Limonade ist ungesund.
   Why: 1 mark for each correct sentence.
8. Explain why a menu needs a colon after the meal name. (2 marks)
   Answer: The colon announces the list of foods that follows.
   Why: 1 mark for saying it announces the list and 1 mark for the example.
