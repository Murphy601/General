## PAGE 1: Numbers and ages
To read about birthdays, you need numbers. Numbers tell the age and the date. Learn the numbers from zero to twelve first.

### Numbers 0 to 12
0 null (nool)   1 eins (ines)   2 zwei (tsvy)   3 drei (dry)
4 vier (feer)   5 fünf (fünf)   6 sechs (zeks)   7 sieben (ZEE-ben)
8 acht (akht)   9 neun (noyn)   10 zehn (tsayn)   11 elf (elf)   12 zwölf (tsvölf)

### Sound notes
• z = ts (zwei, zehn, zwölf).
• w = v (zwei, zwölf).
• ie = ee (vier, sieben).
• eu = oy (neun).
• ü and ö are round-lip sounds (fünf, zwölf).

### Ages
Ich bin acht Jahre alt. = I am eight years old.
Ich bin neun Jahre alt. = I am nine years old.
Ich bin zehn Jahre alt. = I am ten years old.
Ich bin heute zehn. = Today I am ten.

### Try it
Read the numbers aloud in order. Then say your age in German.

## PAGE 2: Numbers 13 to 20 and the months
Numbers from 13 to 19 are built from the small number plus zehn. Say them.

13 dreizehn   14 vierzehn   15 fünfzehn   16 sechzehn
17 siebzehn   18 achtzehn   19 neunzehn   20 zwanzig
Say: zwanzig = TSVAN-tsikh.

Notice the spelling: sechs becomes sech- and sieben becomes sieb- in sechzehn and siebzehn.

### The months (die Monate)
Januar, Februar, März, April, Mai, Juni,
Juli, August, September, Oktober, November, Dezember.

Say: Januar = YAN-oo-ar; Februar = FAY-broo-ar; März = merts; Juli = YOO-lee; Juni = YOO-nee.
In German, j sounds like English y.

### Saying when your birthday is
Wann hast du Geburtstag? = When is your birthday? Say: van hast doo geh-BOORTS-tahk.
Ich habe im Mai Geburtstag. = My birthday is in May.
Ich habe im Dezember Geburtstag. = My birthday is in December.

Im = in (the). Say your own month: Ich habe im ... Geburtstag.

## PAGE 3: A birthday text
Read the text together, one sentence at a time. Your teacher will guide you.

### Text: Kamaus Geburtstag
Heute ist Samstag. Kamau hat Geburtstag. Er ist heute zehn Jahre alt. Kamau hat einen Kuchen mit zehn Kerzen. Seine Freunde kommen. Sie singen ein Lied. Sie sagen: "Alles Gute zum Geburtstag!" Kamau bekommt Geschenke. Er sagt: "Danke!" Alle sind froh.

### New words
hat Geburtstag = has a birthday. seine Freunde = his friends. kommen = come. sagen = say. bekommt = receives, gets. Geschenke = presents. alle = everyone. froh = happy.

### Punctuation in the text
Look at the quotation marks. They show the words someone says. Notice the colon before the quotation: Sie sagen: "Alles Gute zum Geburtstag!" The exclamation mark inside the quotation shows the friends are cheerful.

### Read aloud
Read the text aloud in pairs. Partner A reads two sentences, then partner B reads two sentences. Remember to read the quotation with a happy voice.

## PAGE 4: Answering questions on the text
Read the questions and find the answers in the text.

### Questions
1. Welcher Tag ist heute? = Which day is it today? Samstag (Saturday).
2. Wie alt ist Kamau? = How old is Kamau? He is ten.
3. Wie viele Kerzen hat der Kuchen? = How many candles does the cake have? Ten (zehn).
4. Wer kommt? = Who comes? His friends (seine Freunde).
5. Was sagen die Freunde? = What do the friends say? "Alles Gute zum Geburtstag!"
6. Was sagt Kamau? = What does Kamau say? "Danke!"

### Question words
wer = who. was = what. wie = how. wie viele = how many. welcher = which. wann = when. wo = where.

### Tips for full answers
Answer with a full sentence, like "Kamau ist zehn Jahre alt." Start with a capital letter and end with a full stop.

### Number link with mathematics
Birthdays link to numbers and dates in maths. If Kamau is ten now, how old will he be next year? Eleven, which is elf in German.

## PAGE 5: Reading a poem and a tongue twister
### Poem: Geburtstag
Heute ist mein Tag.
Ich bin nicht mehr neun.
Ich bin jetzt zehn.
Kuchen, Kerzen, Lieder,
alle singen mit!

Meaning: Today is my day. I am not nine any more. I am ten now. Cake, candles, songs, everyone sings along!

New words: nicht mehr = no longer. jetzt = now. mitsingen = sing along.

### Reading with turns
Read the poem in a group: each learner reads one line. Listen to the reader and follow the rules of turn-taking: only one person reads at a time.

### Tongue twister
Bei Bens Geburtstag backt Bella bunten Butterkuchen.

Read this slowly, then faster. Count the b sounds!

### Remember
• Numbers: eins, zwei, drei... zwanzig.
• Months start with capital letters.
• The colon and quotation marks show speech.
• Punctuation tells you where to pause and how to read with feeling.

Common mistake: reading "zwei" as "zwey" with a Kiswahili z. Say tsvy.

## QUIZ
1. What is "zehn"?
   A) 6
   B) 8
   C) 10
   D) 12
   Answer: C
   Why: zehn is the number 10.
2. How do you say "I am nine years old"?
   A) Ich bin acht Jahre alt
   B) Ich bin neun Jahre alt
   C) Ich bin zehn Jahre alt
   D) Ich habe neun Kuchen
   Answer: B
   Why: neun is nine.
3. What does "Wann hast du Geburtstag?" ask?
   A) Who is your friend?
   B) When is your birthday?
   C) How old are you?
   D) What is your name?
   Answer: B
   Why: wann means when and Geburtstag is birthday.
4. Which month is "Mai"?
   A) March
   B) May
   C) June
   D) July
   Answer: B
   Why: Mai is May. März is March.
5. Write the numbers 5, 8 and 12 in German. (3 marks)
   Answer: fünf, acht, zwölf.
   Why: 1 mark for each correct number with correct spelling.
6. Read the text: "Kamau hat Geburtstag. Er ist zehn Jahre alt. Seine Freunde singen ein Lied." Answer: How old is Kamau and what do his friends do? (2 marks)
   Answer: He is ten. His friends sing a song.
   Why: 1 mark for age, 1 mark for singing.
7. Write a full sentence in German that says when your birthday is. (2 marks)
   Answer: For example: Ich habe im Mai Geburtstag.
   Why: 1 mark for the pattern Ich habe im ... Geburtstag, 1 mark for a correctly spelled month.
8. Explain why quotation marks are used in the text "Sie sagen: "Alles Gute zum Geburtstag!"". (2 marks)
   Answer: They show the exact words that the friends say.
   Why: 1 mark for speech and 1 mark for exact words.
