## PAGE 1: Reading sentences smoothly
Fluent readers read like they speak: smoothly, with the right speed and feeling. In this lesson we read about the home aloud. The curriculum's example sentence is: Mutter kocht in der Küche.

### Look at the sentence
Mutter kocht in der Küche.
Mutter = mother (used like a name). kocht = cooks. in der Küche = in the kitchen.

### Reading in chunks
Read sentences in chunks, not word by word.
Mutter kocht | in der Küche.
Vater liest | im Wohnzimmer.
Ich schlafe | im Schlafzimmer.
Die Kinder spielen | im Garten.

Each chunk is a group of words that belong together. Take a very short breath at the line.

### Punctuation reminder
Look for the full stop (der Punkt) at the end. Your voice comes down there. At a comma (das Komma) you pause briefly. At a question mark (das Fragezeichen) your voice goes up.

### Practice
Read the four sentences above three times. First slowly, then at a natural speed, then with a partner.

## PAGE 2: Flash-card reading of home words
Flash cards can help with fluency. The teacher shows a card and you read the word without stopping. Speed grows with practice.

### Cards to read in turns
das Haus, das Zimmer, die Küche, das Wohnzimmer, das Schlafzimmer, das Badezimmer, der Garten, die Tür, das Fenster, der Tisch, der Stuhl, das Bett, das Sofa, die Lampe

### Turn-taking rules
1. One learner reads a card aloud.
2. The others listen quietly.
3. If there is a mistake, the class helps kindly.
4. The next learner takes the next card.

### Speed rounds
Read all 14 cards in one minute. Then read again and try to be faster without losing clarity. Fast but wrong is not fluent. Fluent means smooth and correct.

### Words to watch
Wohnzimmer: two parts, Wohn-zim-mer. Schlafzimmer: Schlaf-zim-mer. Badezimmer: Ba-de-zim-mer. Break long words into small pieces to read them.

## PAGE 3: A short text to read in pairs
Read the text in pairs, taking one sentence each.

### Text: Am Abend
Es ist Abend. Die Familie ist zu Hause. Mutter kocht in der Küche. Vater sitzt im Wohnzimmer. Er liest ein Buch. Der Bruder duscht im Badezimmer. Die Schwester deckt den Tisch. Der Hund schläft im Garten. Dann essen alle zusammen. Das Essen ist gut.

### New words
sitzt = sits. deckt den Tisch = lays the table. duscht = has a shower. der Hund = dog. schläft = sleeps (he/she/it). dann = then. alle = everyone. gut = good.

### Verb endings
Ich kochE, du kochST, er/sie kochT. In this text you see -t often, because the text is about someone else (er, sie, Mutter, Vater): Mutter kocht, Vater liest, der Hund schläft. The verb lesen changes to liest, and schlafen changes to schläft.

### Reading tips
• Do not skip small words like in, im, der, die.
• Look at the end of each verb.
• Read the full stop as a signal to breathe.

## PAGE 4: Reading dialogues aloud
Dialogues are conversations. Read them with two voices.

### Dialogue: Wo ist Mama?
Kind: Wo ist Mama?
Vater: Sie ist in der Küche.
Kind: Was macht sie?
Vater: Sie kocht.
Kind: Und wo ist Oma?
Vater: Sie ist im Garten.

New words: Wo ist ...? = Where is ...? Was macht sie? = What is she doing? Say: vo ist, vas makht zee.

### How to read a dialogue
1. Choose a role: child or father.
2. Read your own lines with feeling.
3. At question marks, raise your voice.
4. Wait for your partner to finish before you read.

### Another dialogue
Kind: Wo ist der Ball?
Mutter: Er ist im Garten.
Kind: Danke!
Mutter: Bitte!

Change roles and read it again.

## PAGE 5: Fluency check and family cooperation
### Reading aloud together
Read the text "Am Abend" again as a class. One row reads each sentence. Then swap rows. This is called choral reading.

### Fluency check
Ask your partner to listen and rate you:
• Speed: not too slow, not too fast.
• Accuracy: most words correct.
• Expression: voice changes at ? and !.
• Phrasing: chunks, not word by word.

### Family and roles
The text shows that everyone at home helps: cooking, laying the table, looking after animals. Talk about how you help at home. Say in English or German: Ich helfe Mama. = I help mum. Ich decke den Tisch. = I lay the table. Ich wasche das Geschirr. = I wash the dishes.

Say: Geschirr = geh-SHEER. Each of us helping makes the home a happy place.

### Remember
• Read in chunks.
• Watch the verb endings: kocht, liest, schläft.
• Take turns and respect readers.
• Practise a little every day.

Common mistake: reading word by word with long stops. Practise chunks.

## QUIZ
1. What does "Mutter kocht in der Küche" mean?
   A) Mother sleeps in the kitchen
   B) Mother cooks in the kitchen
   C) Mother reads in the garden
   D) Mother is at school
   Answer: B
   Why: kocht = cooks, in der Küche = in the kitchen.
2. What does "Wo ist Mama?" mean?
   A) Who is Mama?
   B) Where is Mama?
   C) What is Mama doing?
   D) How old is Mama?
   Answer: B
   Why: wo means where.
3. Which verb form goes with "Vater"?
   A) lese
   B) liest
   C) lesen
   D) lest
   Answer: B
   Why: er/sie/Vater uses liest.
4. What should your voice do at a question mark?
   A) Go down
   B) Go up
   C) Stop
   D) Whisper
   Answer: B
   Why: A question mark tells us to raise our voice.
5. Read: "Der Hund schläft im Garten." Where does the dog sleep? (2 marks)
   Answer: In the garden.
   Why: 1 mark for the answer, 1 mark for showing the meaning (the dog sleeps in the garden).
6. Write two sentences to say where a family member is at home. (3 marks)
   Answer: For example: Mama ist in der Küche. Papa ist im Wohnzimmer.
   Why: 1 mark for each correct sentence and 1 mark for correct articles (in der, im).
7. Explain what "reading in chunks" means and why it helps. (2 marks)
   Answer: It means reading groups of words that belong together at once, so your reading sounds smooth and natural.
   Why: 1 mark for the meaning, 1 mark for the reason.
8. Read the dialogue: "Wo ist der Ball? Er ist im Garten. Danke! Bitte!" Write what the ball question and answer mean. (2 marks)
   Answer: Where is the ball? It is in the garden.
   Why: 1 mark for each.
