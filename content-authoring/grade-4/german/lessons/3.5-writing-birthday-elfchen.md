## PAGE 1: What I would like to do on my birthday
Creative writing means using your own ideas. We start with a very useful German pattern: Ich möchte + a verb. It means "I would like to ...". The verb goes to the end of the sentence and stays in its basic form (the infinitive), which ends in -en.

### Pattern
Ich möchte tanzen. = I would like to dance. Say: ikh MÖKH-te TAN-tsen.
Ich möchte singen. = I would like to sing.
Ich möchte spielen. = I would like to play.
Ich möchte Kuchen essen. = I would like to eat cake.
Ich möchte Freunde einladen. = I would like to invite friends.
Ich möchte schwimmen. = I would like to swim.

### Rules
1. Möchte is the second word.
2. The second verb goes at the end, in the -en form.
3. Other words stay in the middle: Ich möchte Kuchen essen.
4. Write ö carefully: möchte. Without dots it would be a different letter.

### Try it
Write three sentences about your birthday plans. Start each with Ich möchte ... and use a different verb each time.

## PAGE 2: Dates and ordinal numbers
Birthday plans often include a date. In German an ordinal number tells the order: first, second, third. Learn these. They are used with days and months.

### The ordinals 1 to 10
1. der erste  2. der zweite  3. der dritte  4. der vierte  5. der fünfte
6. der sechste  7. der siebte  8. der achte  9. der neunte  10. der zehnte

### Writing a date
In numbers we write a full stop after the digit: 5. Mai. We read it as "am fünften Mai" (on the fifth of May).
Say: Mein Geburtstag ist am fünften Mai. = My birthday is on the fifth of May.
Ich habe am ersten Januar Geburtstag. = My birthday is on the first of January.

### Note
In writing we say am ersten, am zweiten, am dritten, am zehnten. The ending -en is added after am. You do not need to learn all the rules now. Just copy the pattern.

### Practice
Write your own birthday in German. Use the pattern: Mein Geburtstag ist am ... (ordinal) ... (month).

### Link to mathematics
Ordinals also appear in maths: first, second, third. German ordinals help you sort and order numbers.

## PAGE 3: The Elfchen poem
An Elfchen (say: ELF-khen) is a little poem of exactly eleven words. Elf means eleven, and -chen makes it small: "little eleven". It has five lines.

### The structure
Line 1: one word (a thing, colour or feeling)
Line 2: two words (what it is or does)
Line 3: three words (where it is or how it is)
Line 4: four words (you: what you think or feel)
Line 5: one word (an ending word)

## PAGE 4: A model Elfchen and how to write yours
### Model: Geburtstag
Geburtstag
Bunte Ballons
Wir singen laut
Ich bin heute neun
Fest!

### Meaning
Birthday / Colourful balloons / We sing loudly / Today I am nine / Party!

### Word count check
Line 1: Geburtstag (1)
Line 2: Bunte Ballons (2)
Line 3: Wir singen laut (3)
Line 4: Ich bin heute neun (4)
Line 5: Fest! (1)
Total: 11 words.

### Steps to write your own
1. Choose a birthday word: Kuchen, Kerze, Geschenk, Party, Lied.
2. Think about colours, sounds and feelings.
3. Write each line and count the words.
4. Use capital letters for nouns and a punctuation mark at the end.
5. Read your Elfchen aloud and share it with a friend.

### Helpful words
bunt = colourful, süß = sweet, laut = loud, leise = quiet, froh = happy, hell = bright.

## PAGE 5: Famous birthdays and checking your writing
### Finding out about other people
Ask your teacher, a librarian or a trusted book for the birthday of a person you admire, such as a sportsperson, a leader or a scientist. Write the birthday in German: Sein Geburtstag ist am ... (his birthday is on ...) or Ihr Geburtstag ist am ... (her birthday is on ...). Only write dates that you have checked in a reliable source.

### Learning to learn
Research is good practice. Look at more than one source. If two sources disagree, ask your teacher which one to trust.

### Checklist for creative writing
• Did I use capital letters for nouns?
• Did I use the right verb form after möchte (the -en form)?
• Did I write ö, ü and ß correctly?
• Did I end my sentences correctly?
• Does my Elfchen have exactly eleven words?

### Take care of yourself
Writing what you want to do on your birthday teaches you to know yourself. A happy birthday also means being kind to others and thanking your family.

### Remember
• Ich möchte + verb (-en) at the end.
• Elfchen = 1 + 2 + 3 + 4 + 1 words.
• Ordinals: erste, zweite, dritte, vierte, fünfte...

Common mistake: writing "Ich möchte tanze". After möchte, the verb ends in -en: tanzen.

## QUIZ
1. What does "Ich möchte tanzen" mean?
   A) I danced
   B) I would like to dance
   C) I am dancing
   D) I like the dance
   Answer: B
   Why: möchte means would like to.
2. How many words does an Elfchen have?
   A) 5
   B) 10
   C) 11
   D) 15
   Answer: C
   Why: elf means eleven, and the lines have 1, 2, 3, 4 and 1 words.
3. What is the correct verb form after "möchte"?
   A) tanze
   B) tanzt
   C) tanzen
   D) tanzend
   Answer: C
   Why: After möchte we use the -en form of the verb.
4. Which ordinal means "third"?
   A) erste
   B) zweite
   C) dritte
   D) vierte
   Answer: C
   Why: dritte is the third.
5. Write two German sentences about what you would like to do on your birthday. (2 marks)
   Answer: For example: Ich möchte tanzen. Ich möchte Kuchen essen.
   Why: 1 mark for each correct sentence with möchte and a verb at the end.
6. Write the structure of an Elfchen, line by line, with the number of words. (3 marks)
   Answer: Line 1: one word; line 2: two words; line 3: three words; line 4: four words; line 5: one word.
   Why: 1 mark for lines 1 and 2, 1 mark for lines 3 and 4, 1 mark for line 5 and the total 11.
7. Write your own Elfchen about a birthday. Count your words. (4 marks)
   Answer: For example: Kuchen / Süße Kerzen / Wir singen zusammen / Ich bin heute zehn / Danke!
   Why: 1 mark for the pattern 1-2-3-4-1, 1 mark for correct spelling, 1 mark for capital letters on nouns, 1 mark for making sense.
8. Explain why you should use reliable sources when you research a famous person's birthday. (2 marks)
   Answer: Some information may be wrong; reliable sources give facts that are checked.
   Why: 1 mark for wrong information, 1 mark for checked facts.
