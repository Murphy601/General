## PAGE 1: The seven days
A week has seven days, and each day has a German name. In German, the word for week is die Woche and the word for day is der Tag. Say them: dee VOKH-e and dair TAHK.

German weeks usually start on Monday. Here are all seven days with the help of English sounds.

### The days
Montag = Monday. Say: MOHN-tahk.
Dienstag = Tuesday. Say: DEENS-tahk.
Mittwoch = Wednesday. Say: MIT-vokh.
Donnerstag = Thursday. Say: DON-ners-tahk.
Freitag = Friday. Say: FRY-tahk.
Samstag = Saturday. Say: ZAMS-tahk.
Sonntag = Sunday. Say: ZON-tahk.

All the days are der words: der Montag, der Dienstag and so on. They always start with a capital letter in German.

### Listen for the pattern
Six days end in -tag, which means day. Only Mittwoch does not. The word Mittwoch means "middle of the week".

## PAGE 2: Sounds that need care
Pronunciation means saying sounds so that others understand. Some German sounds are new. Study them one by one.

### The ie and ei rule
• ie sounds like the English "ee": Dienstag, Dienst. Say DEENS-tahk.
• ei sounds like the English "eye": Freitag. Say FRY-tahk.
A helpful trick: the second letter tells the sound. In ie it is e, so you say "ee". In ei it is i, so you say "eye".

### The final g
At the end of most syllables, the letter g in -tag sounds like k. So Tag is TAHK and not "tag".

### The ch sound
In Mittwoch the "ch" comes after o, so it sounds like a breathy, scratchy sound at the back of the mouth: VOKH. Practise saying "khhh" softly.

### The s at the start
The letter s before a vowel sounds like z: Samstag = ZAMS-tahk, Sonntag = ZON-tahk.

### The double consonant
The double letters in Mittwoch, Donnerstag, Sonntag make the vowel before them short and quick. Say Sonntag with a short o.

## PAGE 3: A tongue twister for the z sound
The letter z sounds like "ts". This tongue twister is good practice.

Am zehnten zehnten zehn Uhr zehn zogen zehn zahme Ziegen zehn Zentner Zucker zum Zoo.

Meaning: On the tenth of the tenth at ten o'clock, ten tame goats pulled ten hundredweight of sugar to the zoo.

### Word meanings
zehn = ten. zahme = tame. Ziegen = goats. Zucker = sugar. Zoo = zoo.

### How to say it
1. Say only "zehn, zehn, zehn" three times to get the ts sound.
2. Add "zehn Ziegen" and say it four times.
3. Add the rest one phrase at a time.
4. Say it once slowly. Only then try it quickly.

### Feedback game
Say the tongue twister to a partner. Your partner listens and tells you two things: one thing you did well and one sound to improve. Use kind words. Then swap.

Do not worry if you get stuck. Even German children find this one tricky!

## PAGE 4: Matching days and talking about the week
Listening carefully helps you match each day to its position. Use this list to play a matching game.

### Game: Which day comes next?
Your teacher says "Montag" and you say "Dienstag". Then teacher says "Freitag" and you say "Samstag". Continue around the class.

### Order of the week
1. Montag
2. Dienstag
3. Mittwoch
4. Donnerstag
5. Freitag
6. Samstag
7. Sonntag

### Talking about the week
Use "Am" to say "on" with a day.
Am Montag gehe ich zur Schule. = On Monday I go to school.
Am Samstag spiele ich Fußball. = On Saturday I play football.
Am Sonntag gehe ich in die Kirche. = On Sunday I go to church.

Say: gehe = GAY-e; spiele = SHPEE-le; ich = ikh (a soft breath, like a hiss after "i").

## PAGE 5: Time words and review
Some small words tell us about time.

heute = today. Say: HOY-te. The letters eu sound like "oy" in "boy".
morgen = tomorrow (also morning). Say: MOR-gen.
gestern = yesterday. Say: GES-tern.
das Wochenende = the weekend. Say: VOKH-en-en-de.

### Asking about the day
Welcher Tag ist heute? = Which day is it today? Say: VEL-kher tahk ist HOY-te.
Heute ist Mittwoch. = Today is Wednesday.
Morgen ist Donnerstag. = Tomorrow is Thursday.
Gestern war Dienstag. = Yesterday was Tuesday.

### Practice
Use a class calendar. Each day, one learner asks the question and another answers. After a week you will know all seven days.

### Remember
• Days are der words and start with capital letters.
• ie = "ee", ei = "eye", eu = "oy", z = "ts", s before a vowel = "z".
• The g at the end of Tag sounds like k.
• Correct pronunciation helps people understand you.

Common mistake: saying "Frie-tag" with "ee". Freitag has ei, so say FRY-tahk.

## QUIZ
1. Which German day means "Wednesday"?
   A) Dienstag
   B) Donnerstag
   C) Mittwoch
   D) Freitag
   Answer: C
   Why: Mittwoch is Wednesday, "middle of the week".
2. How do you pronounce "ei" in Freitag?
   A) like "ee"
   B) like "eye"
   C) like "oy"
   D) like "oh"
   Answer: B
   Why: ei sounds like the English word "eye", so Freitag is FRY-tahk.
3. What does "Heute ist Montag" mean?
   A) Yesterday was Monday
   B) Tomorrow is Monday
   C) Today is Monday
   D) Monday is over
   Answer: C
   Why: heute means today.
4. Which day comes after Donnerstag?
   A) Mittwoch
   B) Freitag
   C) Samstag
   D) Montag
   Answer: B
   Why: The order is Montag, Dienstag, Mittwoch, Donnerstag, Freitag.
5. Write the seven days of the week in German in the correct order. (4 marks)
   Answer: Montag, Dienstag, Mittwoch, Donnerstag, Freitag, Samstag, Sonntag.
   Why: 4 marks for all seven correct; lose 1 mark for every two mistakes or misspellings or wrong order.
6. Explain how the letters ie and ei sound different. Give one example day for each. (3 marks)
   Answer: ie sounds like "ee", as in Dienstag. ei sounds like "eye", as in Freitag.
   Why: 1 mark for each sound and 1 mark for correct examples.
7. Write in German: "On Saturday I play football." (2 marks)
   Answer: Am Samstag spiele ich Fußball.
   Why: 1 mark for Am Samstag, 1 mark for the rest of the sentence.
8. Say and write what you do on one day of the school week. Use "Am ... gehe ich ...". (3 marks)
   Answer: For example: Am Montag gehe ich zur Schule.
   Why: 1 mark for Am plus a correct day, 1 mark for gehe ich, 1 mark for a sensible ending and capital letters.
