## PAGE 1: Weather words to write
When we write about the weather we use nouns for the weather features and adjectives for how it feels. Learn to spell them correctly.

### Nouns (with capital letters)
die Sonne (sun)   die Wolke (cloud)   der Regen (rain)
der Wind (wind)   der Schnee (snow)   der Nebel (fog)
das Gewitter (thunderstorm)   der Regenbogen (rainbow)
der Himmel (sky)   das Wetter (weather)

### Adjectives (with small letters)
sonnig   windig   wolkig   neblig   regnerisch
kalt   kühl   warm   heiß
trocken (dry)   nass (wet)   feucht (humid)

### Spelling watch
• heiß has ß in Germany and Austria (Swiss writers use ss). The ei says "eye".
• kühl has ü.
• Gewitter has a double t in the middle: Ge-wit-ter.
• Regenbogen is one long word.
• Nebel and neblig: the ending -ig.

### Practise
Write each word three times. Say it as you write. Nouns start with a capital and adjectives with a small letter.

## PAGE 2: Turning nouns into adjectives
Many weather adjectives come from nouns by adding -ig.
Sonne + -ig = sonnig (sunny)
Wind + -ig = windig (windy)
Wolke + -ig = wolkig (cloudy)
Nebel + -ig = neblig (foggy)

### Notice
The last e of the noun drops off before -ig: Sonne becomes sonn-ig, Wolke becomes wolk-ig and Nebel becomes nebl-ig.

### Sentences with es ist
Es ist sonnig. = It is sunny.
Es ist windig. = It is windy.
Es ist wolkig. = It is cloudy.
Es ist kalt. = It is cold.
Es ist heiß. = It is hot.
Es ist warm und trocken. = It is warm and dry.

### Sentences with verbs
Es regnet. = It is raining.
Es schneit. = It is snowing.
Die Sonne scheint. = The sun is shining.
Der Wind weht. = The wind blows.

Say: weht = vayt. scheint = shynt.

### Try it
Write five sentences about the weather. Two must use es ist + an adjective and two must use a verb.

### Word order
A German sentence has its verb in second place.
Heute ist es warm. (Heute is first; ist is second.)
Morgen regnet es. (Morgen is first; regnet is second.)
Am Montag ist es kalt.

Heute = today. Morgen = tomorrow. Gestern = yesterday. Say: GES-tern.
Gestern war es windig. = Yesterday it was windy. (war = was)

### Practice
Write the weather for today, tomorrow and yesterday. Use the pattern above, and remember that the verb is second.

## PAGE 4: Sketching, labelling and puzzles
### Sketch and label
Draw the sky and label the weather features. In pairs, do these steps.
1. Draw a sun, a cloud, rain and wind lines.
2. Write die Sonne, die Wolke, der Regen, der Wind next to them.
3. Add a sentence under each: Die Sonne scheint. Es regnet. Der Wind weht.
4. Show your picture to another pair and check their spelling.

### Unscramble
1. NENSO → Sonne
2. KLOWE → Wolke
3. NEGER → Regen
4. DIWN → Wind
5. EESHCN → Schnee
6. KLAT → kalt (small letter!)

### Crossword clues in English
1. Falls from the clouds: Regen.
2. Cold white flakes: Schnee.
3. The opposite of cold: warm.
4. Shines in the sky: Sonne.

### Environment
Look after nature: plant trees, do not waste water, and keep our rivers and streets clean. The weather affects everyone.

## PAGE 5: Writing a weather report
### Model report
Das Wetter heute
Guten Morgen! Heute ist es sonnig und warm. Der Himmel ist blau. Es ist nicht windig. Morgen regnet es. Der Regen ist gut für die Pflanzen. Tschüss!

Word notes: der Himmel = sky. blau = blue. für = for. die Pflanzen = the plants. nicht = not.

### Steps
1. Start with a greeting.
2. Say today's weather in two or three sentences.
3. Say the weather for tomorrow.
4. Use adjectives such as sonnig, windig, kalt, heiß.
5. End with a goodbye.

### Checklist
• Nouns with capital letters, adjectives with small letters.
• ß in heiß; ü in kühl.
• The verb is in second place.
• Full stops after sentences.

### Remember
• Es ist + adjective: Es ist sonnig.
• Es + verb: Es regnet.
• Adjectives with -ig: sonnig, windig, wolkig.

Common mistake: writing "Es ist Regen". You say Es regnet (it rains), or Es ist regnerisch (it is rainy).

## QUIZ
1. Which word means "windy"?
   A) sonnig
   B) windig
   C) wolkig
   D) neblig
   Answer: B
   Why: windig is windy.
2. Which is correctly spelled?
   A) heis
   B) hais
   C) heiß
   D) haiß
   Answer: C
   Why: heiß has ei and ß.
3. Which sentence has the verb in second place?
   A) Heute ist es warm
   B) Heute es ist warm
   C) Es warm ist heute
   D) Warm heute es ist
   Answer: A
   Why: In Heute ist es warm, ist is the second word.
4. How do you make an adjective from "Sonne"?
   A) sonne
   B) sonnig
   C) sonnen
   D) sonnlich
   Answer: B
   Why: Sonne + -ig, dropping the e, gives sonnig.
5. Write three sentences about the weather today in German. (3 marks)
   Answer: For example: Heute ist es sonnig. Es ist warm. Der Wind weht.
   Why: 1 mark for each correct sentence with capital letters and full stops.
6. Unscramble: KLAT and NEGER. Write the correct words. (2 marks)
   Answer: kalt and Regen.
   Why: 1 mark for each; nouns with a capital letter and adjectives with a small letter.
7. Write a short weather report in German with a greeting, today's weather and a goodbye. (4 marks)
   Answer: For example: Guten Morgen! Heute ist es sonnig und warm. Es ist nicht windig. Tschüss!
   Why: 1 mark for the greeting, 1 mark for weather sentences, 1 mark for correct spelling and 1 mark for a goodbye.
8. Explain the difference between "Es regnet" and "Es ist regnerisch". (2 marks)
   Answer: Es regnet uses a verb and means it is raining. Es ist regnerisch uses an adjective and means it is rainy.
   Why: 1 mark for each correct meaning.
