## PAGE 1: Who is in a nuclear family?
A nuclear family is the small family that lives together: parents and their children. In German, family is die Familie. Say it: dee fah-MEE-lee-eh.

Many Kenyan children also live with a grandmother, an uncle or a cousin. Those people are family too. In this lesson we start with the closest members, and then we add the wider family.

### Family words
die Mutter = mother. Say: MOO-ter.
der Vater = father. Say: FAH-ter. The letter v in Vater sounds like f.
die Eltern = parents. Say: EL-tern.
der Bruder = brother. Say: BROO-der.
die Schwester = sister. Say: SHVES-ter.
die Geschwister = brothers and sisters together.
das Kind = child. Say: kint.

### Small note on der, die, das
Every German noun has a little word in front: der, die or das. They all mean "the". You must learn each noun together with its little word, for example die Mutter, never just Mutter.

## PAGE 2: More family members and friendly names
Now add the wider family.

### The wider family
die Großmutter = grandmother. Children often say die Oma. Say: OH-mah.
der Großvater = grandfather. Children often say der Opa. Say: OH-pah.
der Onkel = uncle. Say: ON-kel.
die Tante = aunt. Say: TAHN-te.
der Sohn = son. Say: zohn.
die Tochter = daughter. Say: TOKH-ter.

### Home names
Mama and Papa are the German friendly names for mum and dad, just like "mum" and "dad" in English. The word Mama stresses the first part: MAH-mah.

### Sounds that are new
• The letter "s" at the start of a word sounds like the English z. So Sohn sounds like "zohn".
• The letters "sch" sound like the English "sh". So Schwester starts with "shv".
• The letter "w" sounds like the English v. So Schwester sounds like SHVES-ter.
• The letter "ö" in Söhne (sons) is made with round lips: say "ay" with your lips in a small circle.

## PAGE 3: Hearing who is who
Now we practise listening for the gist of family texts. Listen for the family words. They tell you who is being talked about.

### Guessing game
The game uses two short sentences.
Wer ist das? = Who is that? Say: vair ist dahs.
Das ist mein Vater. = That is my father.
Das ist meine Mutter. = That is my mother.

Notice mein and meine. Both mean "my". Use mein with der and das nouns (mein Vater, mein Kind). Use meine with die nouns (meine Mutter, meine Schwester) and with plurals (meine Eltern).

### How to play
1. One child thinks of a family member and mimes: for example, a big hand on the head for a tall father.
2. The others ask, "Wer ist das?"
3. The first child answers, "Das ist mein Bruder."
4. The class listens and says if it was right: Ja! or Nein!

### Gist check
Listen to your teacher read: "Das ist meine Schwester. Sie heißt Ann." Who is speaking about whom? A person is speaking about her sister, and the sister is called Ann. The word "Sie" here means she.

## PAGE 4: Songs, poems and a tongue twister
Songs and rhymes help your mouth learn new sounds. Choose a short family song or poem from your teacher and copy it line by line. Copy the melody first, then the words.

### A tongue twister about family
Montags macht mir meine muntere Mutter mittags meistens Mus.

Meaning: On Mondays my lively mother usually makes me mush at midday. Mus is a soft, mashed food.

How to practise it:
1. Say it very slowly and count the m sounds.
2. Clap once for each word.
3. Say it three times, each time a little faster.
4. Say it with a friend, taking turns for each word.

### Tip for the ch and r sounds
This tongue twister has no ch, but it has the German r in Mutter. In German the r is soft and made at the back of the mouth, not rolled at the front like Kiswahili r. At the end of words like Mutter, Vater and Bruder, the -er sounds a little like "uh": MOO-tuh.

### Speaking respectfully
When friends read out their family words, we clap after each one. Everyone's family is special, whether it is big or small.

## PAGE 5: Listening test for gist
Read this short text aloud slowly (your teacher may do it) and then answer the questions with your partner.

### Text
Das ist meine Familie. Das ist mein Vater. Das ist meine Mutter. Das ist mein Bruder. Er heißt Juma. Das ist meine Schwester. Sie heißt Zawadi. Meine Oma ist auch da. Wir sind glücklich.

### Questions
1. Who is speaking about her/his family? A child.
2. Which family members are named? Father, mother, brother (Juma), sister (Zawadi), and grandmother.
3. How does the family feel? Happy (glücklich).

### Vocabulary in the text
er = he. sie = she. auch = also. da = here/there. wir = we. sind = are. glücklich = happy. Say: GLÜK-likh.

### Remember
• Learn each noun with its little word: der Vater, die Mutter, das Kind.
• Use mein for der/das and meine for die.
• Listen for the family words to find the gist.
• Copy the sound and rhythm of your teacher.

Common mistake: saying "Das ist mein Mutter". Mutter is a die word, so say meine Mutter.

## QUIZ
1. What is "die Mutter" in English?
   A) Sister
   B) Mother
   C) Father
   D) Aunt
   Answer: B
   Why: die Mutter means mother.
2. Which word means "brother"?
   A) der Bruder
   B) der Vater
   C) die Tante
   D) der Onkel
   Answer: A
   Why: Bruder is brother. Vater is father, Tante is aunt and Onkel is uncle.
3. Which sentence is correct?
   A) Das ist mein Mutter
   B) Das ist meine Vater
   C) Das ist meine Mutter
   D) Das ist mein Schwester
   Answer: C
   Why: Mutter is a die word, so we use meine.
4. You hear the word "Eltern". What are they talking about?
   A) Brothers and sisters
   B) Parents
   C) Grandparents
   D) Aunts
   Answer: B
   Why: die Eltern means parents.
5. Write "father", "mother", "sister" and "brother" in German, with der or die. (4 marks)
   Answer: der Vater, die Mutter, die Schwester, der Bruder.
   Why: 1 mark for each correct noun with the correct article.
6. How do you ask "Who is that?" and answer "That is my mother"? (2 marks)
   Answer: Wer ist das? Das ist meine Mutter.
   Why: 1 mark for each correct sentence.
7. Explain when we use mein and when we use meine. Give one example of each. (3 marks)
   Answer: Use mein with der and das nouns, for example mein Vater. Use meine with die nouns and plurals, for example meine Mutter.
   Why: 1 mark for each rule and 1 mark for correct examples.
8. Say the tongue twister "Montags macht mir meine muntere Mutter mittags meistens Mus." Explain in English what it says and give one tip for saying it well. (3 marks)
   Answer: It says that on Mondays my lively mother usually makes mush at midday. Say it slowly first and then speed up.
   Why: 1 mark for the general meaning, 1 mark for day and mother, 1 mark for a sensible tip.
