## PAGE 1: Why we imitate
A baby learns to talk by copying the people around. You learned your first language in that way, and you can learn German in the same way. Imitating means listening carefully and repeating the sounds as closely as you can. That is why imitation helps us: our mouth learns new sounds by practising.

In this lesson we learn the German words for our home. A home can be a house, a flat or a room. The German word for house is das Haus. Say it: dahs HOWSS. The sound "au" in Haus is like the "ow" in the English word "cow".

### Two more words
die Wohnung = flat, apartment. Say: VOH-nung.
das Zimmer = room. Say: TSIM-mer. The letter z in German sounds like "ts".

### How to practise
1. Listen to your teacher say a word.
2. Repeat it softly in your mind.
3. Say it aloud together with the class.
4. Say it alone.

## PAGE 2: Rooms in the house
Here are the main rooms. Each room name starts with its little word.

das Wohnzimmer = living room. Say: VOHN-tsim-mer.
das Schlafzimmer = bedroom. Say: SHLAHF-tsim-mer.
die Küche = kitchen. Say: KÜ-khe (ü with round lips).
das Badezimmer = bathroom. Say: BAH-de-tsim-mer. People often say das Bad.
der Garten = garden. Say: GAR-ten.
die Toilette = toilet. Say: toy-LET-te.

### Word-building
German likes to join words together. Schlafen means to sleep and Zimmer means room, so das Schlafzimmer is the "sleep room". Wohnen means to live and das Wohnzimmer is the "living room". The last part of the long word tells you the little word: Zimmer is a das word, so Schlafzimmer and Wohnzimmer are both das words.

## PAGE 3: Furniture and household items
Now let us furnish the rooms. Repeat after your teacher and point to the real item.

der Tisch = table. Say: tish (like "tish" with a soft sh).
der Stuhl = chair. Say: shtool. At the start of a word, st sounds like "sht".
das Bett = bed. Say: bet.
das Sofa = sofa. Say: ZOH-fa.
der Schrank = cupboard, wardrobe. Say: shrank.
die Lampe = lamp. Say: LAM-pe.
die Tür = door. Say: tür, with ü as before.
das Fenster = window. Say: FEN-ster.

### Match the item to the room
• In the kitchen (in der Küche): der Tisch, der Stuhl.
• In the bedroom (im Schlafzimmer): das Bett, der Schrank.
• In the living room (im Wohnzimmer): das Sofa, die Lampe.

The word "im" is short for "in dem". You can say "Das Bett ist im Schlafzimmer." That means: The bed is in the bedroom.

### Speaking practice
Pick a picture card. Say the word with its little word: der Tisch, das Bett. If you say the little word correctly, the class says "Richtig!" which means correct.

## PAGE 4: Poems and songs about home
Rhymes make repeating fun. Read this short poem aloud, line by line, after your teacher. It uses the words you already know.

### Poem: Mein Haus
Das ist mein Haus.
Die Tür ist auf.
Das Fenster ist offen.
Ich bin zu Hause.

Meaning: This is my house. The door is open. The window is open. I am at home.

New words: auf/offen = open. zu Hause = at home. Say: tsoo HOW-ze.

### Tips for reading the poem well
• Stop briefly at each full stop.
• Say the words of each line in one smooth breath.
• Read the last line slowly to show you are happy to be home.

### Group work
Divide into four groups. Each group says one line, then everyone says the last line together. Record a video if your teacher allows. Watch it and check: did you say ü, ei, au and z clearly?

## PAGE 5: Presenting my home
In a short presentation, you can tell the class what you have at home and where things are. Use the words you have learned.

### A model presentation
Das ist mein Haus. Das ist die Küche. Das ist das Wohnzimmer. Das Bett ist im Schlafzimmer. Der Tisch ist in der Küche. Danke!

### Steps
1. Choose four rooms or items.
2. Draw a simple picture and label each item with der, die or das.
3. Practise saying the sentences with a partner.
4. Present to the class, speaking slowly and clearly.

### Responsibility
Keep the class calm when others present. Face the speaker, do not laugh at mistakes, and give a clap at the end. Everyone makes mistakes when they copy a new language, and that is how they improve.

### Remember
• das Haus, die Küche, das Bett, der Tisch, die Tür.
• Learn each noun with its little word.
• Copy sounds slowly and carefully.
• st sounds like sht, z sounds like ts, and ü needs round lips.

Common mistake: saying "Die Bett". Bett is a das word, so say das Bett.

## QUIZ
1. What does "die Küche" mean?
   A) Living room
   B) Bedroom
   C) Kitchen
   D) Garden
   Answer: C
   Why: die Küche is the kitchen.
2. Which is the German word for "bed"?
   A) der Tisch
   B) das Bett
   C) die Tür
   D) das Fenster
   Answer: B
   Why: das Bett is a bed. Tisch is table, Tür is door and Fenster is window.
3. How is the letter "z" pronounced in German, as in Zimmer?
   A) like English z
   B) like English s
   C) like "ts"
   D) like "sh"
   Answer: C
   Why: In German z sounds like "ts", so Zimmer starts with "tsim".
4. Which sentence means "The bed is in the bedroom"?
   A) Der Tisch ist in der Küche
   B) Das Bett ist im Schlafzimmer
   C) Das Sofa ist im Wohnzimmer
   D) Die Lampe ist im Garten
   Answer: B
   Why: das Bett = the bed, im Schlafzimmer = in the bedroom.
5. Write the German words with der, die or das for: kitchen, table, bed, door. (4 marks)
   Answer: die Küche, der Tisch, das Bett, die Tür.
   Why: 1 mark for each correct noun with its correct little word.
6. Explain why we say that learning a language by imitation works. (2 marks)
   Answer: We listen and copy the sounds again and again, so our mouth and ears get used to them, just as when we learned our first language.
   Why: 1 mark for listening and copying, 1 mark for practice.
7. Say and write the German name for two rooms and one item in each. Use the words you learned. (3 marks)
   Answer: For example: das Schlafzimmer with das Bett, and die Küche with der Tisch.
   Why: 1 mark for each correct room and 1 mark for correct items placed in the right rooms.
8. Break the word "Schlafzimmer" into two parts and explain what each part means. (3 marks)
   Answer: Schlaf means sleep and Zimmer means room, so Schlafzimmer is a sleep room, a bedroom.
   Why: 1 mark for each part and 1 mark for the joined meaning.
