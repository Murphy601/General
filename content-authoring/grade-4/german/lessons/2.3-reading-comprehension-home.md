## PAGE 1: What is reading for comprehension?
Comprehension means understanding what you read. When you read a German text, you do not need to know every word. You look for information: names, places, things. Use these three steps.

### Three steps
1. Look at the title and pictures. What is the text about?
2. Read once for the gist. Circle the words you recognise.
3. Read again slowly to find the answers to the questions.

### A first small text
Das ist mein Haus. Es ist groß. Es hat vier Zimmer und eine Küche. Vor dem Haus ist ein Garten.

Words: es = it. hat = has. vier = four. vor = in front of. ein = a (before der/das words). eine = a (before die words).

### Gist
The text says that the house is big, has four rooms and a kitchen, and has a garden in front.

### Try it
Find all the nouns in the text. They start with a capital letter: Haus, Zimmer, Küche, Garten. Now find the words that tell how many: vier, eine, ein.

## PAGE 2: Household items
Here are new household words. Learn them with their little words. Link each word to a picture.

der Kühlschrank = fridge. Say: KÜL-shrank.
der Herd = cooker, stove. Say: hairt.
der Teller = plate. Say: TEL-ler.
das Glas = glass, cup. Say: glahs.
der Löffel = spoon. Say: LÖF-fel.
das Messer = knife. Say: MES-ser.
die Gabel = fork. Say: GAH-bel.
der Topf = pot. Say: topf.
das Radio = radio. Say: RAH-dee-oh.
die Uhr = clock, watch. Say: oor.

### Where do they belong?
• In der Küche: der Kühlschrank, der Herd, der Teller, der Topf, der Löffel.
• Im Wohnzimmer: das Radio, die Uhr, das Sofa.
• Im Schlafzimmer: das Bett, der Schrank.

The plural of Teller is also Teller, and the plural of Löffel is Löffel. The plural of Gabel is Gabeln.

### Reading practice
Read all the words aloud. Cover the English and check if you remember.

## PAGE 3: A longer text and its questions
Read the text slowly. Then use it to answer the questions.

### Text: Unser Haus
Wir wohnen in einem Haus. Das Haus hat ein Wohnzimmer, zwei Schlafzimmer, eine Küche und ein Bad. Im Wohnzimmer ist ein Sofa. Dort ist auch ein Radio. In der Küche ist ein Kühlschrank. Meine Mutter kocht dort. Im Schlafzimmer ist mein Bett. Hinter dem Haus ist der Garten. Dort spiele ich mit meinem Bruder.

New words: wir wohnen = we live. unser = our. dort = there. hinter = behind. mit = with.

### Questions
1. Wie viele Schlafzimmer hat das Haus? = How many bedrooms does the house have? Two (zwei).
2. Was ist im Wohnzimmer? = What is in the living room? A sofa and a radio.
3. Wer kocht in der Küche? = Who cooks in the kitchen? The mother.
4. Wo ist der Garten? = Where is the garden? Behind the house.
5. Wo spielt das Kind? = Where does the child play? In the garden.

Say: wie viele = vee FEE-le (how many). wo = voh (where). was = vahs (what).

## PAGE 4: Reading a short poem and finding hidden words
### Poem: Zu Hause
Zu Hause ist es schön.
Die Lampe brennt.
Das Bett ist weich.
Das Essen ist warm.
Zu Hause bin ich froh.

Meaning: At home it is nice. The lamp is burning (on). The bed is soft. The food is warm. At home I am happy.

New words: schön = nice, beautiful. weich = soft. froh = happy. Say: shurn, vyekh, froh.

### Hidden words puzzle
Here are letters in a row. Can you find five household words? They are written together.
TISCHSTUHLBETTLAMPETÜR

Look for words you know. TISCH, STUHL, BETT, LAMPE, TÜR.

### Tip for the puzzle
1. Look for capital letters in a row and think of words you know.
2. Circle each word you find.
3. Write it out with a capital first letter and add der, die or das.

Answers: der Tisch, der Stuhl, das Bett, die Lampe, die Tür.

### Pair work
Make your own puzzle with four home words and swap with a partner.

## PAGE 5: Safety at home and checking understanding
The curriculum reminds us that some texts are about safety at home. Read this short text and answer.

### Text: Sicher zu Hause
Die Küche ist ein wichtiger Raum. Der Herd ist heiß. Das Messer ist scharf. Kleine Kinder sind nicht allein in der Küche. Die Tür ist immer frei. Wir sind vorsichtig.

New words: wichtig = important. Raum = room, space. scharf = sharp. allein = alone. immer = always. frei = free (not blocked). vorsichtig = careful.

### Gist
The text tells us to be careful in the kitchen. The cooker is hot and the knife is sharp. Children should not be alone there. The door should never be blocked.

### Questions
1. Was ist heiß? The cooker (der Herd).
2. Was ist scharf? The knife.
3. Sind kleine Kinder allein in der Küche? No.

### Remember
• Read for the gist first, then look for details.
• Find answers by looking for key words.
• Read with care so that you understand what is safe at home.

Common mistake: guessing without reading. Always find the words in the text to back up your answer.

## QUIZ
1. What is "der Kühlschrank"?
   A) Cooker
   B) Fridge
   C) Plate
   D) Radio
   Answer: B
   Why: der Kühlschrank is a fridge.
2. What does "Wo ist der Garten?" ask?
   A) Who is in the garden?
   B) Where is the garden?
   C) What is in the garden?
   D) How big is the garden?
   Answer: B
   Why: wo means where.
3. Read: "Im Wohnzimmer ist ein Sofa." Where is the sofa?
   A) In the kitchen
   B) In the living room
   C) In the bedroom
   D) In the garden
   Answer: B
   Why: Wohnzimmer means living room.
4. Which word means "spoon"?
   A) der Löffel
   B) die Gabel
   C) das Messer
   D) der Teller
   Answer: A
   Why: der Löffel is a spoon. Gabel is fork, Messer is knife and Teller is plate.
5. Write the German words with der, die or das for: plate, fork, clock, pot. (4 marks)
   Answer: der Teller, die Gabel, die Uhr, der Topf.
   Why: 1 mark for each correct noun with its article.
6. Read: "Der Herd ist heiß. Das Messer ist scharf." Explain what the text warns about. (2 marks)
   Answer: It warns that the cooker is hot and the knife is sharp, so we must be careful.
   Why: 1 mark for hot cooker, 1 mark for sharp knife.
7. Find five household words hidden in TISCHSTUHLBETTLAMPETÜR and write them with der, die or das. (3 marks)
   Answer: der Tisch, der Stuhl, das Bett, die Lampe, die Tür.
   Why: 3 marks for all five, 2 marks for three or four, 1 mark for one or two.
8. Write three steps you follow to understand a text. (3 marks)
   Answer: Look at the title and pictures; read once for the gist; read again to find details and answer questions.
   Why: 1 mark for each step.
