## PAGE 1: The weather words
Weather is what the sky and air are like today: sunny, rainy, windy, cold or hot. In German, weather is das Wetter. Say: VET-ter.

To ask about it, say: Wie ist das Wetter? = How is the weather? Say: vee ist dahs VET-ter.

### Weather features
die Sonne = sun. Say: ZON-ne.
der Regen = rain. Say: RAY-gen.
der Wind = wind. Say: vint. The w sounds like v and the d at the end sounds like t.
die Wolke = cloud. Say: VOL-ke.
der Himmel = sky. Say: HIM-mel.
der Regenbogen = rainbow. Say: RAY-gen-boh-gen.
das Gewitter = thunderstorm. Say: geh-VIT-ter.
der Nebel = fog, mist. Say: NAY-bel.
der Schnee = snow. Say: shnay.

### Tip
The word Regen is joined to Bogen (bow, arch) to make Regenbogen: a rain-bow! Many German words are made this way.

## PAGE 2: Short sentences about the weather
To talk about weather, German often uses the word es (it), just like English "it is raining".

### Sentences
Es regnet. = It is raining. Say: es RAYG-net.
Es schneit. = It is snowing. Say: es shnite.
Die Sonne scheint. = The sun is shining. Say: dee ZON-ne shine-t.
Es ist windig. = It is windy. Say: es ist VIN-dikh.
Es ist sonnig. = It is sunny. Say: es ist ZON-nikh.
Es ist bewölkt. = It is cloudy. Say: es ist be-VÖLKT.

### Temperature words
kalt = cold. Say: kalt.
warm = warm. Say: varm.
heiß = hot. Say: hice. The ß is a strong s. The ei is "eye" as in Bein.
kühl = cool. Say: kühl.

### Listening tip
When you hear "Es regnet", the gist is rain. When you hear "heiß", think of hot weather, and when you hear "kalt", think of cold. Listen for these key words and ignore the rest at first.

## PAGE 3: Weather in Kenya
Kenya has two rainy seasons and two dry seasons. We can use a few extra German words to talk about them.

die Regenzeit = rainy season. Say: RAY-gen-tsite.
die Trockenzeit = dry season. Say: TROK-en-tsite.
Es ist trocken. = It is dry.
Es ist nass. = It is wet. Say: nas.

### A short model text
In Nairobi ist es oft warm. Im Sommer ist es nicht sehr heiß. Auf dem Mount Kenya ist es kalt. Dort schneit es manchmal. Die Sonne scheint viel.

You need not understand every word. Listen for key words: warm, kalt, schneit, Sonne. Gist: the text describes different weather in Kenya, warm in Nairobi and cold on the mountain.

New words: oft = often. dort = there. manchmal = sometimes. viel = a lot. nicht sehr = not very.

## PAGE 4: Songs, poems and tongue twisters
Poems about weather are fun to say together. Use rhythm: tap softly on your desk with each word.

### A short poem
Die Sonne scheint.
Der Himmel ist blau.
Die Wolken sind weiß.
Es ist warm.

Meaning: The sun is shining. The sky is blue. The clouds are white. It is warm.

New words: blau = blue (say: blow, like "cow"). weiß = white (say: vice).

### Tongue twister
Wenn der Wind weht, weht er weit.
Meaning: When the wind blows, it blows far. Remember that w sounds like English v. Say: ven dair vint vayt, vayt air vite.

### Group recitation
1. Split into two groups.
2. Group A says line one and Group B says line two.
3. Swap and keep a steady beat.
4. Finish together.

### Be fluent
Fluency means speaking smoothly, at a natural speed, with few pauses. Repeat the poem many times and it becomes easier.

## PAGE 5: Matching pictures and reporting the weather
Play the picture game. Your teacher (or a recording) says one sentence. You show the correct picture card.

### Pairs to practise
Es regnet. Card: rain.
Die Sonne scheint. Card: sun.
Es ist windig. Card: wind lines.
Es ist heiß. Card: a hot day.
Es ist kalt. Card: a shivering person.

### Be the weather reporter
Say to the class: Guten Morgen! Heute ist es sonnig und warm. Danke!

### Mini-dialogue
Amani: Wie ist das Wetter heute?
Neema: Es regnet.
Amani: Und morgen?
Neema: Morgen scheint die Sonne.

Words: und = and. morgen = tomorrow.

### Remember
• Es regnet, es schneit, es ist windig, die Sonne scheint.
• Listen for weather words to get the gist.
• w = v, ei = eye, ß = ss.

Common mistake: saying "Es ist regen". Say Es regnet (it rains).

## QUIZ
1. What does "Es regnet" mean?
   A) It is snowing
   B) It is raining
   C) It is windy
   D) It is hot
   Answer: B
   Why: regnen is the verb for rain.
2. Which word means "cloud"?
   A) die Sonne
   B) der Wind
   C) die Wolke
   D) der Regen
   Answer: C
   Why: die Wolke is a cloud.
3. How is "w" said in German, as in "Wind"?
   A) like English w
   B) like English v
   C) like English f
   D) like English b
   Answer: B
   Why: w in German sounds like English v, so Wind is VINT.
4. Which word means "hot"?
   A) kalt
   B) warm
   C) heiß
   D) nass
   Answer: C
   Why: heiß means hot. Kalt is cold, warm is warm and nass is wet.
5. Write the German for: sun, rain, wind, sky. Use der, die or das. (4 marks)
   Answer: die Sonne, der Regen, der Wind, der Himmel.
   Why: 1 mark for each noun with the correct article.
6. Write in German: "The sun is shining." (2 marks)
   Answer: Die Sonne scheint.
   Why: 1 mark for Die Sonne, 1 mark for scheint.
7. You hear "Auf dem Mount Kenya ist es kalt. Dort schneit es manchmal." What is the gist? (2 marks)
   Answer: It is cold on Mount Kenya and sometimes it snows there.
   Why: 1 mark for cold, 1 mark for snow.
8. Explain what fluency means and give two ways to improve it. (3 marks)
   Answer: Fluency means speaking smoothly with few pauses. To improve, practise poems or tongue twisters often, and speak with a friend regularly.
   Why: 1 mark for the meaning and 1 mark for each way.
