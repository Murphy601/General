## PAGE 1: Spelling the days correctly
Orthography means the rules of spelling and writing. German spelling follows clear rules. Learn them and your writing will be neat and correct.

### The seven days written out
Montag
Dienstag
Mittwoch
Donnerstag
Freitag
Samstag
Sonntag

### Rules to notice
1. Every day starts with a capital letter, because a day is a noun.
2. Six days end in -tag. Mittwoch ends in -woch.
3. Dienstag has ie. Freitag has ei.
4. Donnerstag has a double n in the middle: Don-ners-tag.
5. Samstag and Sonntag both start with S but have different vowels: Sa- and So-.

### Trick for ie and ei
Say the word. If you hear "ee", write ie. If you hear "eye", write ei. Dienstag = DEENS-tag → ie. Freitag = FRY-tag → ei.

### Practice
Write each day three times from memory. Then check every letter.

## PAGE 2: Unscrambling and word searches
### Unscramble the days
1. GATMON → Montag
2. STAGDIEN → Dienstag
3. CHOWITTM → Mittwoch
4. TAGFIRE → Freitag
5. GASTAMS → Samstag
6. TAGSONN → Sonntag

Hint: The scrambled words have exactly the same letters as the days.

### Word search idea
Draw a grid 8 by 8. Hide the days in rows and columns, and fill the empty squares with random letters. Swap grids with a partner and find the seven days.

### Crossword idea
Write clues in English and answers in German.
1. The first day of the week: Montag.
2. The day before Saturday: Freitag.
3. The day of rest for many families: Sonntag.

### Tip
Write clearly with every letter in a square. Use capital letters in puzzles.

## PAGE 3: Capital letters, small letters and the special letters
### Capital or small?
• Capital: nouns, names, days, months, the first word of a sentence.
• Small: verbs (spielen), adjectives (groß), and small words (und, ich, in).

Examples
Am Montag spiele ich Fußball.
Heute ist Dienstag.
Morgen ist Mittwoch.

### The letters ä, ö, ü, ß
Use the two dots for ä, ö, ü. When you must type without them use ae, oe, ue. Use ß after a long vowel: Fuß, groß, Straße. After a short vowel you write ss: Wasser, essen.

### Careful with double consonants
Sonntag has nn and Mittwoch has tt. Double letters make the vowel before them short. Write both letters!

### Small words for time
heute = today and gestern = yesterday are written with a small letter. But Morgen in Guten Morgen has a capital M, because there it is the noun der Morgen (the morning).

## PAGE 4: Rückendiktat and sentences
### Rückendiktat (back dictation)
Rücken means back and Diktat means dictation. Work in pairs.
1. Partner A sits behind partner B.
2. A writes a word with a finger on B's back, one letter at a time.
3. B says the letters aloud and then writes the word in a notebook.
4. Change roles.

Start with the days: Montag, Freitag, Sonntag. Then try short sentences such as Heute ist Montag.

### Sentences about the week
Write what you do on each day. Use "Am" plus the day. The verb must be second in the sentence.
Am Montag gehe ich zur Schule.
Am Dienstag lerne ich Deutsch.
Am Mittwoch spiele ich mit Freunden.
Am Samstag helfe ich Mama.
Am Sonntag gehe ich in die Kirche.

### Notice
Am Montag gehe ich: after "Am Montag" comes the verb gehe, then ich. If you begin with ich, you say: Ich gehe am Montag zur Schule.

### Time management
Write a plan for the week. Note when you do homework, help at home and play. A good plan helps you use your time well.

## PAGE 5: Writing a weekly schedule
### Model timetable text
Meine Woche
Am Montag gehe ich zur Schule.
Am Dienstag lese ich ein Buch.
Am Mittwoch spiele ich mit Freunden.
Am Donnerstag helfe ich zu Hause.
Am Freitag singe ich in der Schule.
Am Samstag spiele ich Fußball.
Am Sonntag gehe ich in die Kirche.

### Your turn
Copy the pattern and write your own week. You can use these words:
ich lerne = I study. ich lese = I read. ich schwimme = I swim. ich koche = I cook. ich helfe = I help. ich singe = I sing.

Say: schwimme = SHVIM-me.

### Checklist
• Do all days start with a capital letter?
• Is the verb second?
• Are ie and ei correct?
• Are there full stops?
• Did I write ß correctly in Fußball?

### Remember
• Days are nouns: capital letters.
• ie = ee, ei = eye.
• Write double letters: Sonntag, Mittwoch, Donnerstag (nn, tt).
• Use the pattern: Am + day + verb + ich.

Common mistake: writing "am montag". It should be "Am Montag".

## QUIZ
1. Which is the correct spelling of Tuesday?
   A) Deinstag
   B) Dienstag
   C) Dienstak
   D) Denstag
   Answer: B
   Why: Dienstag has ie and ends with -tag.
2. Which day is spelled with "ei"?
   A) Dienstag
   B) Freitag
   C) Montag
   D) Sonntag
   Answer: B
   Why: Freitag has ei.
3. Which sentence is correctly written?
   A) am Montag gehe ich zur Schule
   B) Am Montag gehe ich zur Schule.
   C) Montag am gehe ich zur Schule.
   D) Am Montag ich gehe zur Schule
   Answer: B
   Why: Capital letter at the start, capital for Montag, verb second, full stop at the end.
4. What is a Rückendiktat?
   A) Writing on the board
   B) Writing a word on a partner's back for them to say and write
   C) A song
   D) A reading test
   Answer: B
   Why: Rücken means back, so the partner traces letters on the back.
5. Unscramble: TAGFIRE. (2 marks)
   Answer: Freitag.
   Why: 1 mark for the correct letters, 1 mark for the capital letter.
6. Write the days of the week in German from Monday to Sunday. (4 marks)
   Answer: Montag, Dienstag, Mittwoch, Donnerstag, Freitag, Samstag, Sonntag.
   Why: 4 marks for all seven correct; lose 1 mark for every two errors.
7. Write three sentences about what you do on three different days. Begin with "Am ...". (3 marks)
   Answer: For example: Am Montag gehe ich zur Schule. Am Samstag spiele ich Fußball. Am Sonntag gehe ich in die Kirche.
   Why: 1 mark for each correct sentence with capitals, verb second, and full stop.
8. Explain the trick to decide between ie and ei when you write. (2 marks)
   Answer: If you hear "ee", write ie; if you hear "eye", write ei.
   Why: 1 mark for each rule.
