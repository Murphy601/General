## PAGE 1: Reading a weather chart
A weather chart shows small pictures with their names. Weather reporters use such charts every day. Read the names aloud and match them to the pictures.

### Weather features
die Sonne = sun
die Wolke = cloud
der Regen = rain
der Wind = wind
der Schnee = snow
der Nebel = fog
das Gewitter = thunderstorm
der Regenbogen = rainbow
der Himmel = sky

### Weather words with -ig
sonnig = sunny
windig = windy
wolkig = cloudy
neblig = foggy
regnerisch = rainy

### Reading tip
Many weather adjectives are made from a noun plus -ig: Sonne + -ig = sonnig, Wind + -ig = windig, Wolke + -ig = wolkig. Read the noun first and then add the ending. Say the ending -ig as "-ikh".

### Sound check
Read aloud: Gewitter (g-e-VIT-ter), Nebel (NAY-bel), Regenbogen (RAY-gen-boh-gen). Which words have a soft g? None; the g in Regen is hard, as in "go".

## PAGE 2: Temperature words and short sentences
Now we read words that say how warm or cold it is.

### Temperature
heiß = hot
warm = warm
kühl = cool
kalt = cold

### Sentences
Es ist heiß. = It is hot.
Es ist warm. = It is warm.
Es ist kalt. = It is cold.
Es ist kalt und windig. = It is cold and windy.
Die Sonne scheint. Es ist warm. = The sun is shining. It is warm.

### Question and answer to read
Wie ist das Wetter in Mombasa? = What is the weather like in Mombasa?
In Mombasa ist es heiß und feucht. = In Mombasa it is hot and humid.

Say: feucht = foykht (eu = oy, ch = breath).

### Spelling: heiß
In the curriculum you may see "heiss". Some people write ss when they cannot type ß. The correct spelling is heiß, because ei is a long sound. Both mean hot.

### Practise
Read every sentence twice, once slowly and once smoothly. Ask a partner to listen and tell you which word you hesitated on.

## PAGE 3: Reading a weather text
Read the text aloud and answer the questions.

### Text: Das Wetter in Kenia
Kenia liegt in Afrika. Am Morgen ist es oft kühl. Am Mittag ist es warm. In Mombasa ist es heiß. In Nairobi ist es warm. In den Bergen ist es kalt. Von März bis Mai ist Regenzeit. Dann regnet es viel. Von Juni bis September ist es trocken. Die Sonne scheint fast jeden Tag.

### Words
liegt = lies, is located. Kenia = Kenya. Afrika = Africa. am Morgen = in the morning. am Mittag = at midday. die Berge = mountains. von ... bis = from ... to. dann = then. fast = almost. jeden Tag = every day.

### Questions
1. Wo liegt Kenia? In Africa.
2. Wie ist das Wetter in Mombasa? Hot.
3. Wie ist das Wetter in den Bergen? Cold.
4. Wann ist Regenzeit? From March to May.
5. Wann ist es trocken? From June to September.

Say: wann = van (when). liegt = leekt.

### Note
Kenya has more than one rainy season. This text gives one main rainy period as an example. Your teacher can tell you about others.

## PAGE 4: Reading with a poem and a weather diary
### Poem: Wetter
Die Sonne scheint, der Himmel ist blau.
Dann kommt der Wind, die Wolke ist grau.
Es regnet ein wenig, dann ist es vorbei.
Der Regenbogen kommt, wir sind alle frei.

Meaning: The sun shines, the sky is blue. Then the wind comes, the cloud is grey. It rains a little, then it is over. The rainbow comes, we are all free.

New words: grau = grey. ein wenig = a little. vorbei = over, past. Say: grow, ine VAY-nikh, for-BY.

### Group reading
Each group reads one line. Use a different voice for the sun, the wind and the rain. Try to keep the same beat.

### A weather diary
Copy this table pattern in your book and fill it for one week. Use German words.
Montag: Es ist sonnig.
Dienstag: Es ist windig.
Mittwoch: Es regnet.
Read your diary aloud to your partner on Friday.

## PAGE 5: Photos, labels and vocabulary building
The curriculum suggests taking photos or drawing pictures of the weather and labelling them. If you cannot take photos, draw.

### Steps
1. Look outside. What is the weather today?
2. Take a photo or draw the sky.
3. Label it with the German word: die Sonne, die Wolke, der Regen.
4. Write one sentence: Heute ist es sonnig.
5. Present it to your class. Read it aloud.

### Building vocabulary
Reading a lot helps you learn new words. A good habit: when you read a text, underline three new words and write them with their meaning. Review them the next day.

### Environmental care
The weather affects how we live: farmers need rain for crops, and people need shade in hot weather. We should also take care of nature: plant trees and do not waste water.

### Remember
• Adjectives: sonnig, windig, wolkig, regnerisch.
• ß in heiß, ei = "eye".
• Read charts, poems and texts to build vocabulary.

Common mistake: writing "die Wetter". Wetter is a das word: das Wetter.

## QUIZ
1. What is "der Regenbogen"?
   A) Thunder
   B) Rainbow
   C) Cloud
   D) Fog
   Answer: B
   Why: der Regenbogen is the rainbow.
2. Which word means "windy"?
   A) sonnig
   B) windig
   C) kalt
   D) warm
   Answer: B
   Why: windig is windy.
3. What does "Es ist kalt und windig" say?
   A) It is hot and dry
   B) It is cold and windy
   C) It is warm and sunny
   D) It is raining
   Answer: B
   Why: kalt = cold, windig = windy.
4. Which article goes with "Wetter"?
   A) der
   B) die
   C) das
   D) den
   Answer: C
   Why: It is das Wetter.
5. Write the German words for: sun, cloud, rain, snow. Use der, die or das. (4 marks)
   Answer: die Sonne, die Wolke, der Regen, der Schnee.
   Why: 1 mark for each correct noun with article.
6. Read: "In Mombasa ist es heiß." What does it say and how do you say heiß? (2 marks)
   Answer: In Mombasa it is hot. Heiß is said like "hice".
   Why: 1 mark for the meaning, 1 mark for the sound.
7. Write two German sentences about the weather today. (3 marks)
   Answer: For example: Heute ist es sonnig. Es ist warm.
   Why: 1 mark for each correct sentence, 1 mark for capital letters and full stops.
8. Explain how "windig" is made from "Wind". Give one more example. (2 marks)
   Answer: Add -ig to the noun: Wind + -ig = windig. Another example: Sonne + -ig = sonnig.
   Why: 1 mark for the rule, 1 mark for the example.
