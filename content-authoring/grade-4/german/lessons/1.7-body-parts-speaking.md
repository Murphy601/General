## PAGE 1: Naming parts of the body
Our body helps us to move, see, hear and taste. In German, the body is der Körper. Say: KÖR-per, with round lips for ö. Now we learn the names of the parts. Copy your teacher and touch each part as you say the word.

### Head and face
der Kopf = head. Say: kopf.
das Gesicht = face. Say: geh-ZIKHT.
das Haar = hair. Say: hahr. The plural die Haare is often used: die Haare.
das Auge = eye. Say: OW-ge. The plural is die Augen.
das Ohr = ear. Say: ohr. The plural is die Ohren.
die Nase = nose. Say: NAH-ze.
der Mund = mouth. Say: moont.
der Zahn = tooth. Say: tsahn. The plural is die Zähne.

### Sound note
The letters au in Auge sound like "ow" in "cow". The letters ie and ei are as before. The letter ä in Zähne is said like the e in "bed", but a bit longer: TSEH-ne.

## PAGE 2: Arms, legs and the rest
### Upper body
der Hals = neck. Say: hals.
die Schulter = shoulder. Say: SHOOL-ter.
der Arm = arm. Say: arm.
die Hand = hand. The plural is die Hände. Say: hand and HEN-de.
der Finger = finger. Say: FIN-ger.
der Bauch = tummy. Say: bowkh.
der Rücken = back. Say: RÜ-ken.

### Lower body
das Bein = leg. Say: bine. The plural is die Beine.
das Knie = knee. Say: k-NEE. Both k and n are said.
der Fuß = foot. Say: foos. The plural is die Füße. Say: FÜ-se.
der Zeh = toe. Say: tsay. The plural is die Zehen.

### The letter ß
The letter ß is called Eszett. It sounds like a strong ss. In Fuß it makes the u long: FOOS. Do not confuse it with the letter B.

## PAGE 3: The naming game
Play in pairs. One asks and the other points to the right body part.

Was ist das? = What is that? Say: vas ist dahs.
Das ist mein Kopf. = That is my head.
Das ist meine Nase. = That is my nose.
Das ist mein Auge. = That is my eye.

Use mein for der and das words (mein Kopf, mein Auge, mein Bein). Use meine for die words (meine Nase, meine Hand, meine Schulter).

### Sentences about how many
Ich habe zwei Augen. = I have two eyes.
Ich habe zwei Ohren. = I have two ears.
Ich habe zehn Finger. = I have ten fingers.
Ich habe eine Nase. = I have one nose.

zwei = two (say tsvy), zehn = ten (say tsayn), eine = one (before a die word).

### Practice
1. Write four body words on paper.
2. Show a card to your partner and ask, "Was ist das?"
3. Your partner answers, "Das ist mein/meine ..."
4. Swap after four cards.

## PAGE 4: The head, shoulders song
There is a famous action song in German too. It starts "Kopf, Schulter, Knie und Fuß, Knie und Fuß". It follows the same tune as the English song "Head, shoulders, knees and toes".

Kopf, Schulter, Knie und Fuß, Knie und Fuß.
Kopf, Schulter, Knie und Fuß, Knie und Fuß.
Augen, Ohren, Mund und Nase.
Kopf, Schulter, Knie und Fuß, Knie und Fuß.

### How to do it
Touch each part as you sing its name. Sing slowly at first. Then go faster and faster!

### Sound practice
Say these words slowly and clearly: Kopf, Schulter, Knie, Fuß. Notice: sch = sh, kn = k+n, ß = ss.

### Making a video
In a group, sing the song and record a video if your school has a device. Watch it and answer: Did we sing each word clearly? Did we all touch the correct body part? Share your video with the class.

Warning: Do not pull or hit anyone while singing. Respect each other's bodies.

## PAGE 5: Pronouncing well: a checklist
Good pronunciation makes your German easy to understand. Use this checklist when you speak body words.

### Checklist
• Did I say the nouns with der, die or das?
• Did I make ö round in Körper?
• Did I say ä in Zähne like a short e?
• Did I say sch as sh in Schulter?
• Did I say z as ts in Zahn and Zeh?
• Did I say the k and the n in Knie?

### Tongue twister for practice
Zehn Zehen zappeln zusammen.
Meaning: Ten toes wriggle together. Use the ts sound at the start of each word and make it crisp. Say it five times slowly.

### Remember
• Ohr, Auge, Nase and Mund tell how we hear, see, smell and eat.
• Use mein or meine: mein Kopf, meine Nase.
• ß sounds like ss, and ä sounds like a short e.

Common mistake: saying "Bein" as "been". The ei sounds like eye: BINE.

## QUIZ
1. What is "die Nase"?
   A) Ear
   B) Nose
   C) Eye
   D) Mouth
   Answer: B
   Why: die Nase is the nose.
2. Which word means "foot"?
   A) das Bein
   B) der Arm
   C) der Fuß
   D) die Hand
   Answer: C
   Why: der Fuß is the foot. Bein is leg.
3. How is "ei" said in "Bein"?
   A) ee
   B) eye
   C) oy
   D) ay
   Answer: B
   Why: ei sounds like the English word eye, so Bein is said BINE.
4. Which sentence is correct?
   A) Das ist meine Kopf
   B) Das ist mein Nase
   C) Das ist meine Nase
   D) Das ist mein Hand
   Answer: C
   Why: Nase is a die word, so we say meine Nase.
5. Write the German words with der, die or das for: head, eye, mouth, hand. (4 marks)
   Answer: der Kopf, das Auge, der Mund, die Hand.
   Why: 1 mark for each correct noun with the correct article.
6. Write in German: "I have two ears." (2 marks)
   Answer: Ich habe zwei Ohren.
   Why: 1 mark for Ich habe zwei, 1 mark for Ohren.
7. Write the first line of the German body song and say what it means. (3 marks)
   Answer: Kopf, Schulter, Knie und Fuß. It means head, shoulders, knees and feet.
   Why: 1 mark for the German words, 1 mark for correct spelling including ß, 1 mark for the meaning.
8. Explain how to pronounce ß, sch and z in German, with one example each. (3 marks)
   Answer: ß is a strong ss, as in Fuß. sch is sh, as in Schulter. z is ts, as in Zahn.
   Why: 1 mark for each correct sound with example.
