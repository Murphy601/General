## PAGE 1: Talking about food
Food is something we all enjoy. In German, food is das Essen and to eat is essen. To drink is trinken. Say: ES-sen and TRIN-ken.

In Kenya we enjoy ugali, sukuma wiki, githeri, chapati, rice, beans and mandazi. Many of the German words for food are easy because you can learn them by comparing with English. Look at Banane, Orange and Tomate. They sound like the English words!

### Fruit (das Obst)
der Apfel = apple. Say: AP-fel.
die Banane = banana. Say: ba-NAH-ne.
die Orange = orange. Say: o-RAHN-zhe.
die Mango = mango. Say: MAN-go.
die Wassermelone = watermelon. Say: VAS-ser-me-LOH-ne.

### Listening tip
When you hear a food text, listen for the food words first. They tell you the topic. Then listen for words like gern (with pleasure) and nicht (not) to hear if the speaker likes or dislikes the food.

## PAGE 2: Vegetables and other foods
### Vegetables (das Gemüse)
die Tomate = tomato. Say: to-MAH-te.
die Kartoffel = potato. Say: kar-TOF-fel.
die Karotte = carrot. Say: ka-ROT-te.
die Zwiebel = onion. Say: TSVEE-bel.
der Kohl = cabbage, kale. Say: kohl.
die Bohne = bean. The plural is die Bohnen. Say: BOH-ne.

### Other foods
das Brot = bread. Say: broht.
der Reis = rice. Say: rice, like the English word.
das Ei = egg. Say: eye. The plural is die Eier.
der Fisch = fish. Say: fish.
das Fleisch = meat. Say: flysh.
der Mais = maize. Say: mice.

### Sound spotting
• z in Zwiebel is ts.
• w in Wassermelone is v.
• ei in Reis, Ei and Fleisch sounds like "eye".
• ie in Zwiebel sounds like "ee".

## PAGE 3: Drinks and meals
### Drinks (die Getränke)
das Wasser = water. Say: VAS-ser.
die Milch = milk. Say: milkh, with a soft ch.
der Tee = tea. Say: tay.
der Saft = juice. Say: zahft.
der Orangensaft = orange juice.
der Kakao = cocoa. Say: ka-KOW.
die Cola = cola. Say: KOH-la.

### Meals (die Mahlzeiten)
das Frühstück = breakfast. Say: FRÜ-shtük.
das Mittagessen = lunch. Say: MIT-tahk-es-sen.
das Abendessen = supper, dinner. Say: AH-bent-es-sen.

### Two useful sentences
Ich esse gern Bananen. = I like eating bananas.
Ich trinke gern Milch. = I like drinking milk.

Say: gern = gairn. It means "gladly, with pleasure". You put it after the verb to say you like doing something. In German we use trinken for drinks and essen for food, just like in English.

## PAGE 4: Likes and dislikes
Listen for these small words to find out if a person likes something.

ich mag = I like. Say: ikh mahk.
ich mag kein / keine = I do not like (a certain thing).
gern = gladly.
lecker = tasty. Say: LEK-ker.
gesund = healthy. Say: geh-ZOONT.
ungesund = unhealthy.

### Mini-dialogue
Aisha: Was isst du gern?
Kamau: Ich esse gern Reis und Bohnen. Und du?
Aisha: Ich esse gern Mais und Kohl. Ich trinke gern Wasser.
Kamau: Lecker!

Words: was = what. isst = eat (you eat). du = you. und = and.

### Listening for gist
1. What are they talking about? Food.
2. What does Kamau like? Rice and beans.
3. What does Aisha like to drink? Water.
4. Which word shows Kamau likes it? Lecker.

Note that isst, the verb form, is the du form of essen: ich esse, du isst.

## PAGE 5: Songs, tongue twisters and the healthy-food chat
### A tongue twister about food
Fischers Fritz fischt frische Fische.
Meaning: Fisher's Fritz fishes fresh fish. Say the f sound like in English, with your top teeth on your lower lip. Say it slowly, then faster.

### Sing and repeat
Choose a food song from your teacher, or a chant like: Ich esse gern Brot, Ich trinke gern Tee. Sing it in a rhythm with your class. If you know a song in Kiswahili about food, sing that too and compare the food words.

### Healthy or unhealthy? (talk in English)
Use the German food words, but explain your ideas in English.
• Healthy: Obst, Gemüse, Wasser, Bohnen.
• Sometimes only: Süßigkeiten (sweets), Cola.
Discuss: Which foods give you energy? Which foods can damage teeth if you eat them too often?

### Remember
• Learn each noun with der, die or das.
• Use gern after the verb to say you like it.
• Listen for the food words to find the gist.

Common mistake: saying "Ich esse gern Wasser". You drink water, so say "Ich trinke gern Wasser".

## QUIZ
1. What is "der Apfel"?
   A) An egg
   B) An apple
   C) A banana
   D) Bread
   Answer: B
   Why: der Apfel is the German word for apple.
2. Which verb do we use with Milch?
   A) essen
   B) trinken
   C) schlafen
   D) spielen
   Answer: B
   Why: Milch is a drink, so we use trinken.
3. What does "Ich esse gern Bananen" mean?
   A) I do not want bananas
   B) I like eating bananas
   C) I am selling bananas
   D) I have one banana
   Answer: B
   Why: gern shows that you like doing something, so the sentence means I like eating bananas.
4. Which word means "breakfast"?
   A) das Abendessen
   B) das Mittagessen
   C) das Frühstück
   D) das Brot
   Answer: C
   Why: das Frühstück is breakfast.
5. Write the German words with der, die or das for: bread, egg, tomato, water. (4 marks)
   Answer: das Brot, das Ei, die Tomate, das Wasser.
   Why: 1 mark for each noun with the correct article.
6. Listen to "Ich esse gern Reis und Bohnen. Lecker!" What is the gist? (2 marks)
   Answer: The speaker likes to eat rice and beans and finds them tasty.
   Why: 1 mark for rice and beans, 1 mark for likes / tasty.
7. Write two sentences in German: one about a food you like to eat and one about a drink you like to drink. (3 marks)
   Answer: For example: Ich esse gern Mais. Ich trinke gern Tee.
   Why: 1 mark for a correct eating sentence, 1 mark for a correct drinking sentence, 1 mark for capital letters and full stops.
8. Give two reasons why fruit and vegetables are good for the body. (2 marks)
   Answer: They give vitamins and help us stay healthy; they help fight sickness and give energy.
   Why: 1 mark for each sensible reason.
