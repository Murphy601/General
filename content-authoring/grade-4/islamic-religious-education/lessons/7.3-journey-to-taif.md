## PAGE 1: Why did the Prophet go to Taif?
About ten years after the first revelation, life in Makkah had become very hard for the Prophet (S.A.W.) and the Muslims. Many of the leaders of Makkah opposed him, and some mocked and hurt the believers. In the same period, the Prophet lost two of his greatest supporters. His wife Khadija (R.A.) passed away, and soon after his uncle Abu Talib also died. Abu Talib had protected him from harm, even though he had not accepted Islam. This period is called the Year of Sorrow.

With Abu Talib gone, the people of Makkah were bolder in troubling him. So the Prophet decided to invite the people of another town, Taif, to Islam. Taif is a town on higher ground south-east of Makkah, known for its cooler weather and its gardens and fruit.

### Facts
• The Prophet travelled with Zayd ibn Haritha (R.A.), his close companion.
• According to the traditional accounts, they travelled the long distance mostly on foot.
• He hoped that the leaders of the Thaqif tribe would listen, help him, and accept Islam.

### Key words
• Taif: a town near Makkah.
• Thaqif: the main tribe of Taif.
• Hardship: difficulty and suffering.

## PAGE 2: What happened in Taif?
In Taif, the Prophet (S.A.W.) spoke to the leaders of Thaqif. He invited them to worship Allah alone and asked for their support. But they refused, and they answered rudely and mockingly. They did not want him to stay, and they told him to leave.

### The stone-throwing
As he left, some of the leaders encouraged the town's slaves and rough boys to insult him and throw stones at him and Zayd. The Prophet's feet were hurt and bled. Zayd tried to protect him and was also wounded. Even so, the Prophet did not curse them.

### Rest in the garden
Finally, the Prophet and Zayd found shelter in a garden owned by two men from Makkah. There, he rested against a wall and turned to Allah in dua, telling Allah about his weakness, and asking for His help and mercy. The owners of the garden felt sorry for him, and, in the traditional accounts, sent a servant with a bunch of grapes. The Prophet began by saying "Bismillah" before eating.

### Think
How would you feel if you were hurt after you tried to do a good thing? What would you do?

## PAGE 3: The angel and the mountains
On his way back to Makkah, the Prophet (S.A.W.) was very sad. Later he told his wife Aisha (R.A.) what happened. He said that the angel Jibril (A.S.) came to him with the angel in charge of the mountains, who said, in meaning: "Allah has heard what your people said to you. I am the angel of the mountains. If you wish, I will bring together the two mountains on them." (Al-Bukhari and Muslim)

### The Prophet's answer
The Prophet said: "No. I hope that Allah will bring from their descendants people who will worship Allah alone and not associate anything with Him."

### What does this show?
• The Prophet could have asked for the people of Taif to be destroyed, but he chose mercy.
• He thought of the future generation, not only of himself.
• He trusted Allah and was patient.

## PAGE 4: Virtues shown by the Prophet
Let us look closely at the virtues that we learn from the journey to Taif.

### Patience (sabr)
The Prophet stayed calm when people insulted him and hurt him. Patience means staying calm and not giving up, even when things are hard.

### Tolerance
He tolerated the rude behaviour of the Thaqif leaders and did not take revenge. Tolerance means bearing with people who behave badly or disagree with you, without hurting them.

### Perseverance
He did not give up his mission. He continued to invite people to Islam, even after being rejected. Perseverance means to keep trying with determination.

### Forgiveness and mercy
He forgave them and hoped good would come from their children. Mercy means being kind to those who have been unkind to us.

### Trust in Allah
He turned to Allah in dua. When we are in trouble, we should turn to Allah.

### Compare
• Revenge says: "You hurt me, so I will hurt you."
• The Prophet's way says: "You hurt me, but I will be patient and wish you well."

## PAGE 5: What happened later?
The people of Taif did not accept Islam at that time. But the Prophet's hope came true. Some years later, in the ninth year after the Hijrah, the people of Taif sent a delegation to the Prophet in Madinah, and they accepted Islam. Over time, many of their descendants became Muslims, and Taif became a Muslim town.

### Returning to Makkah
The Prophet returned towards Makkah. On the way, he rested at a place, and a group of jinn listened to the Qur'an and believed, as the Qur'an (46:29-32) mentions. He entered Makkah under the protection of a leader named Mut'im ibn Adiy, since Makkah was still unsafe for him.

### Lessons for us
• Good may come from difficult times.
• Do not give up when a good idea is rejected at first.
• Be patient, and Allah will help.
• Hope for the best in people.

## PAGE 6: Applying these virtues in daily life
### Situations that need patience, tolerance and perseverance
• A classmate teases you about your name. (Patience and tolerance: stay calm, tell a teacher, do not fight.)
• You fail a test. (Perseverance: study more and try again.)
• A friend does not want to play with you. (Tolerance: be kind and try again later.)
• You are sick and cannot go to play. (Patience: trust Allah, follow the doctor's advice.)
• A neighbour is unkind to you. (Forgiveness: greet them anyway and make dua.)

### Retell the story
In groups, one child tells the story of the Taif journey in their own words, and the others add missing parts. Then write the virtues learned on a chart and put it on the classroom wall.

### Summary
• The Prophet went to Taif after the deaths of Khadija and Abu Talib, to invite people to Islam.
• The leaders rejected him, and he was hurt.
• He turned to Allah, refused to ask for the people to be destroyed, and hoped for good from their descendants.
• The virtues we learn are patience, tolerance, perseverance, mercy and trust in Allah.

### Common mistakes
• Thinking that being patient means being weak. It takes great strength.
• Taking revenge when we are hurt.
• Giving up when the first attempt fails.

## QUIZ
1. Why did the Prophet (S.A.W.) go to Taif?
   A) To trade
   B) To invite the people to Islam
   C) To go on holiday
   D) To visit family only
   Answer: B
   Why: He hoped that the leaders of Taif would listen to and support the message of Islam.
2. Who travelled with the Prophet to Taif?
   A) Zayd ibn Haritha (R.A.)
   B) Ali (R.A.)
   C) Abu Lahab
   D) Khadija (R.A.)
   Answer: A
   Why: Zayd ibn Haritha was his companion on the journey.
3. What did the Prophet say when the angel of the mountains offered to crush the people of Taif?
   A) "Yes, do it"
   B) "I hope Allah will bring from their descendants people who worship Allah alone"
   C) "I do not care"
   D) "Send them away"
   Answer: B
   Why: He chose mercy and hoped for good from their future generations.
4. Which virtue is shown by not giving up his mission even after being rejected?
   A) Laziness
   B) Perseverance
   C) Selfishness
   D) Anger
   Answer: B
   Why: Perseverance means continuing with determination.
5. What is the Year of Sorrow?
   A) The year of Hijrah
   B) The year in which Khadija and Abu Talib died
   C) The year of the first revelation
   D) The year Taif accepted Islam
   Answer: B
   Why: The Prophet lost his wife and his protective uncle in the same period.
6. Narrate briefly what happened to the Prophet in Taif. (4 marks)
   Answer: He invited the leaders to Islam and they rejected him rudely. They made people throw stones at him and Zayd and his feet bled. He took shelter in a garden and made dua to Allah.
   Why: 1 mark for the invitation, 1 mark for the rejection, 1 mark for the stone-throwing, 1 mark for the shelter and dua.
7. State three virtues that the Prophet showed in Taif. (3 marks)
   Answer: Any three: patience, tolerance, perseverance, forgiveness, mercy, trust in Allah.
   Why: 1 mark for each virtue.
8. Explain what we learn from the Prophet's reply to the angel of the mountains. (3 marks)
   Answer: We should show mercy and not take revenge. We should hope for good in people and their future. We should trust Allah.
   Why: 1 mark for each valid lesson.
9. A classmate keeps teasing Musa. Using the lesson, say how Musa should behave. (3 marks)
   Answer: He should stay calm, not fight or insult, tell a teacher and forgive, hoping the classmate will change.
   Why: 1 mark for patience or calmness, 1 mark for no revenge, 1 mark for asking help or forgiving.
