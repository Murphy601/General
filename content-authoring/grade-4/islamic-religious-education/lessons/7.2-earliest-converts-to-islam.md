## PAGE 1: The first people to accept Islam
When the Prophet (S.A.W.) began to receive revelation, he first shared the message with the people closest to him. Some of those who believed early were his wife Khadija, his young cousin Ali and his close friend Abu Bakr. They are called the earliest converts to Islam. A convert is a person who accepts a new faith.

Being among the first to believe was not easy. The people of Makkah were mostly against the new message. Those who accepted it faced mockery and hardship. But these early believers stayed firm and helped Islam to grow.

### The three we will study
• Khadija bint Khuwaylid (R.A.): the first person to believe in the Prophet.
• Ali ibn Abi Talib (R.A.): the first young boy to accept Islam.
• Abu Bakr as-Siddiq (R.A.): the Prophet's close friend, and the first grown man among the free to accept Islam, as is commonly taught.

### About the words "R.A."
After the name of a companion we say "Radiyallahu anhu" (for a man) or "Radiyallahu anha" (for a woman), meaning "may Allah be pleased with him or her". We write it as (R.A.).

### Think
Why do you think the people closest to the Prophet believed first? Because they knew his honesty and character very well.

## PAGE 2: Khadija (R.A.), the first believer
Khadija bint Khuwaylid was a respected and wealthy businesswoman in Makkah. She was known for her honesty and good character. She employed people to trade for her.

### Why did she choose Muhammad?
She hired the young Muhammad (S.A.W.) to take her goods to trade in Syria. She noticed his honesty, trustworthiness and hard work. She was impressed, and later she proposed marriage through a friend. He accepted and they were married. She was older than him, and they lived happily together.

### Khadija's role
• She was the first person to believe in the message of Islam.
• She comforted the Prophet after the first revelation and told him Allah would never disgrace him.
• She supported him with her money and gave her wealth to spread Islam and help the poor.
• She stood with him during years of hardship.
• She was the mother of most of his children. All his children were from her, apart from Ibrahim.

### What we learn
Khadija shows us the value of honesty and hard work in business, and the power of faith and support for others. She is respected by Muslims as one of the best women.

## PAGE 3: Ali ibn Abi Talib (R.A.)
Ali was the son of Abu Talib, the Prophet's uncle. When Abu Talib faced financial hardship, the Prophet took Ali into his own home to raise him, so Ali grew up in the Prophet's household. Ali was a young boy, commonly said to be about ten years old, when the message came.

### How did he accept Islam?
He saw the Prophet and Khadija praying. He asked what they were doing, and the Prophet explained that they worship Allah alone. Ali thought about it and accepted Islam. He is known as the first young boy to believe.

### Ali's role
• He was a loyal young supporter of the Prophet.
• He was brave. On the night the Prophet left Makkah for Madinah, Ali slept in the Prophet's bed so that the enemies would think the Prophet was there, and later returned goods that people had entrusted to the Prophet.
• He grew up to be a scholar and a brave leader, and later he became the fourth Caliph.

### What we learn
Young people can accept the truth and be brave for Allah. Ali's story shows us that we can be role models even as children.

## PAGE 4: Abu Bakr as-Siddiq (R.A.)
Abu Bakr was a respected trader in Makkah and a close friend of Muhammad (S.A.W.) since before the revelation. He knew the Prophet as an honest man who never lied. So when the Prophet told him about the revelation, he believed at once and without doubt. This is why he is called "As-Siddiq", the one who believes and speaks the truth.

### Abu Bakr's role
• He was one of the first adults to accept Islam, and he invited others to Islam. Several respected people of Makkah accepted Islam through him.
• He spent his wealth to help Islam. He bought and freed Muslims who were being tortured for their faith, such as Bilal (R.A.).
• He stayed with the Prophet in times of danger. During the Hijrah, the migration to Madinah, he was the Prophet's companion in the cave of Thawr. The Qur'an (9:40) mentions the two of them in the cave, when the Prophet said to his companion, "Do not grieve, indeed Allah is with us."
• After the Prophet passed away, he became the first Caliph, the leader of the Muslims.

### What we learn
Abu Bakr teaches us loyalty, generosity, truthfulness and courage. He put his wealth and life at the service of Allah and His Messenger.

## PAGE 5: Why the early converts matter
The Muslims of Makkah were few, poor and weak at first. Yet the first believers played a big part in the survival and growth of Islam.

### Their roles
• Moral support: Khadija stood by the Prophet in his fear and difficulty.
• Material support: Khadija and Abu Bakr gave money to help the weak and to spread the message.
• Being first: They believed when it was hard, when few people did. Their faith encouraged others.
• Courage: Ali showed bravery and loyalty even as a young man.
• Sharing the message: Abu Bakr invited others to Islam.
• Sacrifice: They accepted hardship and insults for the sake of Allah.

### How can we honour them?
• Learn about their lives and share their stories.
• Copy their qualities: honesty, loyalty, generosity and courage.
• Support good causes in our community, for example volunteering in mosque clean-up or helping the needy.
• Make dua for them: "Radiyallahu anhum".

### Class activity
Choose one of the three converts. Prepare a two-minute speech about their life and how they helped Islam. Then listen to the speeches of your classmates and say one thing you admired about each person.

## PAGE 6: Summary and remembering
### Summary
• Earliest converts are among the first people who believed in the Prophet's message.
• Khadija (R.A.): first believer, wife, comforter and supporter with wealth.
• Ali (R.A.): first young boy to accept Islam, raised in the Prophet's home, brave and loyal.
• Abu Bakr (R.A.): close friend, truthful, spent wealth freeing believers, companion in the Hijrah and the first Caliph.
• They teach us honesty, loyalty, generosity, courage and sacrifice.

### Match-up game
Match each description to the right person:
• "I comforted the Prophet after the first revelation." (Khadija)
• "I slept in the Prophet's bed on the night of the migration." (Ali)
• "I stayed with the Prophet in the cave of Thawr." (Abu Bakr)

### Common mistakes
• Forgetting that the first person to believe was a woman, Khadija (R.A.).
• Confusing Ali and Abu Bakr. Ali was a young boy, and Abu Bakr was a grown man and a friend.
• Thinking that only rich or strong people helped Islam. Each person gave what they could.

## QUIZ
1. Who was the first person to believe in the Prophet's message?
   A) Ali (R.A.)
   B) Khadija (R.A.)
   C) Abu Bakr (R.A.)
   D) Abu Lahab
   Answer: B
   Why: Khadija was the first to believe and to support the Prophet.
2. Which earliest convert was a young boy raised in the Prophet's home?
   A) Abu Bakr (R.A.)
   B) Ali (R.A.)
   C) Khadija (R.A.)
   D) Bilal (R.A.)
   Answer: B
   Why: Ali was the young cousin who grew up in the Prophet's home and accepted Islam as a boy.
3. Why is Abu Bakr called "As-Siddiq"?
   A) He was a fast runner
   B) He believed and spoke the truth
   C) He was tall
   D) He was a shepherd
   Answer: B
   Why: As-Siddiq means the truthful, the one who believes at once.
4. What did Khadija see in Muhammad (S.A.W.) that made her respect him?
   A) His wealth
   B) His honesty and trustworthiness
   C) His height
   D) His speed
   Answer: B
   Why: She noticed his honesty and trustworthiness when he traded for her.
5. Who accompanied the Prophet in the cave of Thawr during the Hijrah?
   A) Ali (R.A.)
   B) Khadija (R.A.)
   C) Abu Bakr (R.A.)
   D) Waraqa
   Answer: C
   Why: The Qur'an (9:40) mentions the Prophet and his companion in the cave.
6. Name the three earliest converts studied in this lesson. (3 marks)
   Answer: Khadija, Ali and Abu Bakr (may Allah be pleased with them).
   Why: 1 mark for each.
7. State two ways in which Khadija (R.A.) helped the Prophet (S.A.W.). (2 marks)
   Answer: She comforted him after the first revelation and supported him with her wealth.
   Why: 1 mark for each valid way.
8. Explain the role of Abu Bakr (R.A.) in the growth of Islam. (3 marks)
   Answer: He believed at once and invited others to Islam, spent his wealth to free Muslims who were being tortured, and was the Prophet's companion during the Hijrah.
   Why: 1 mark for each valid role.
9. Which quality of the early converts would you like to practise, and how? (3 marks)
   Answer: For example: honesty, by returning a lost item to its owner. Other qualities such as loyalty, generosity or courage are accepted.
   Why: 1 mark for the quality, 1 mark for an action, 1 mark for a link to an early convert.
