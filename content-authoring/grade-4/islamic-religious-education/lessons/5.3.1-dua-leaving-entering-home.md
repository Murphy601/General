## PAGE 1: What is dua?
Dua means calling upon Allah and asking Him for what we need. When a child says "Please help me, my Lord", that is a dua. Allah loves to hear from His servants. The Qur'an (40:60) tells us that our Lord says: "Call upon Me, I will respond to you." The Prophet (S.A.W.) said that dua is worship (Abu Dawud and At-Tirmidhi).

Allah is As-Sami', the All-Hearing. He hears every dua, whether we speak it aloud or only in the heart. He answers in the way that is best for us, whether by giving what we asked, by keeping harm away, or by rewarding us later.

### Manners of dua
1. Begin with praise of Allah and blessings on the Prophet.
2. Face the Qibla if possible and raise the hands.
3. Ask with humility and hope, in a calm voice.
4. Use the beautiful names of Allah.
5. Be patient. Do not say "Allah did not answer me".
6. Say "Amin" at the end.

### Special duas for daily life
The Prophet (S.A.W.) taught us short duas for many daily actions, such as waking up, eating, wearing clothes, travelling and entering or leaving the home. In this lesson we will learn the duas for leaving and entering the house.

## PAGE 2: Dua when leaving the house
Every morning many children leave the house for school. Some go to the shamba, the market or the madrasa. Before leaving, we ask Allah for protection.

### The words
Bismillah, tawakkaltu 'alallah, wa la hawla wa la quwwata illa billah.

### The meaning
In the name of Allah, I place my trust in Allah, and there is no might and no power except with Allah.

### Explanation of the words
• Bismillah: we start in the name of Allah.
• Tawakkaltu 'alallah: I depend on Allah. We do our part, such as looking both ways on the road, and then we rely on Allah.
• La hawla wa la quwwata illa billah: we cannot do anything without Allah's help.

### The reward
The Prophet (S.A.W.) said that when a person says this on leaving home, it is said to him, "You are guided, defended and protected", and Shaytan moves away from him (Abu Dawud and At-Tirmidhi).

### When to say it
Say it as you step out of the door: for school, for the mosque, for a journey, or for a walk.

## PAGE 3: Dua when entering the house
When we come back home, we thank Allah for a safe return and we bring blessings into the home.

### The words
Bismillahi walajna, wa bismillahi kharajna, wa 'ala Rabbina tawakkalna.

### The meaning
In the name of Allah we enter, in the name of Allah we leave, and upon our Lord we depend.

After saying this dua, the Prophet (S.A.W.) taught that we should greet our family with "As-salamu alaykum" (Abu Dawud). The Qur'an (24:61) also says that when we enter homes, we should greet one another with a greeting of peace from Allah, blessed and good.

### Things to do when you enter
1. Say Bismillah and the dua.
2. Greet everyone with salaam. If nobody seems to be at home, you can still say salaam.
3. Remove your shoes and place them neatly.
4. Wash your hands and face if you have come from outside.
5. Speak kindly to your parents and family.

### Note
You should also mention the name of Allah when eating and before sleeping, so that Shaytan does not share your home and food.

## PAGE 4: Why do we say these duas?
### Reasons
• Protection: We ask Allah to keep us safe from harm, accidents and evil whispers.
• Trust in Allah: We do our part and put our trust in Him. This is called tawakkul.
• Remembering Allah: The dua fills our day with the remembrance of Allah.
• Blessings in the home: Greeting family with salaam and saying Bismillah brings peace.
• Following the Sunnah: We copy the Prophet (S.A.W.).
• Building good habits: When we start good habits when we are young, they stay with us.

### Tawakkul in action
Amani's mother tells him, "Take your umbrella because it may rain." Amani says the dua, takes the umbrella and leaves. He did what he could and trusted Allah. That is tawakkul.

### Compare
• Blaming luck: "I hope nothing happens."
• Tawakkul: "I have prepared, and I trust Allah."
Which is better?

## PAGE 5: Practising the duas
Learning by heart takes practice. Try these activities.

### Steps to memorise
1. Listen to your teacher or a recording say the dua slowly.
2. Repeat one line at a time.
3. Say the whole dua with a partner.
4. Test yourself the next day without looking.

### Activity 1: Role-play at the door
Set up a "door" in the classroom with two chairs. One child leaves and says the dua for leaving. Another child enters and says the dua for entering, then greets the class with "As-salamu alaykum". The class replies "Wa alaykumus-salam".

### Activity 2: Match the situation
• Stepping out of the gate to go to school. (Dua for leaving.)
• Arriving home after madrasa. (Dua for entering, then salaam.)
• Going out to fetch water. (Dua for leaving.)
• Visiting a relative and entering their home. (Bismillah and greet with salaam.)

### Activity 3: Make your own reminder card
Write the words and meaning on a card and stick it near your front door at home. Let your family read it too.

### Add your own dua
After saying the dua, you can also ask Allah in your own words: "O Allah, keep me and my family safe." Speak from the heart.

## PAGE 6: Summary
### Summary
• Dua is asking Allah for what we need. It is an act of worship.
• When leaving home we say "Bismillah, tawakkaltu 'alallah, wa la hawla wa la quwwata illa billah", which means "In the name of Allah, I place my trust in Allah, and there is no might and no power except with Allah."
• When entering we say "Bismillahi walajna, wa bismillahi kharajna, wa 'ala Rabbina tawakkalna", which means "In the name of Allah we enter, in the name of Allah we leave, and upon our Lord we depend", and then greet our family.
• These duas bring protection, blessings and peace, and follow the Sunnah.

### Common mistakes
• Rushing out of the door and forgetting the dua.
• Saying the words without thinking about the meaning.
• Trusting Allah but not taking care, such as forgetting to look both ways on the road.
• Entering the house without greeting anyone.

### Try this week
Say both duas every day. At the end of the week, tell a friend or family member what they mean. Teaching others is a good way of remembering.

## QUIZ
1. What is dua?
   A) Asking Allah for what we need
   B) A type of clothing
   C) A meal
   D) A game
   Answer: A
   Why: Dua means calling upon Allah and asking Him; the Prophet said that dua is worship.
2. What does "tawakkaltu 'alallah" mean?
   A) I am hungry
   B) I depend on Allah
   C) I am going to sleep
   D) I am tired
   Answer: B
   Why: Tawakkul means placing trust in Allah, while doing our part.
3. Which greeting should we say to our family when we enter the house, after the dua?
   A) Good luck
   B) As-salamu alaykum
   C) Goodbye
   D) Be quiet
   Answer: B
   Why: The Prophet taught us to greet with salaam when we enter, and the Qur'an (24:61) tells us the same.
4. When do we say "Bismillah, tawakkaltu 'alallah, wa la hawla wa la quwwata illa billah"?
   A) When entering the house
   B) When leaving the house
   C) After eating
   D) Before sleeping only
   Answer: B
   Why: This is the dua for leaving the house.
5. Which shows real tawakkul?
   A) Leaving the door open and hoping nothing bad happens
   B) Taking care, locking the door and trusting Allah
   C) Refusing to take any precaution
   D) Blaming bad luck
   Answer: B
   Why: Tawakkul means doing our part and then relying on Allah.
6. Write the meaning of the dua for entering the house. (3 marks)
   Answer: In the name of Allah we enter, in the name of Allah we leave, and upon our Lord we depend.
   Why: 1 mark for each of the three parts of the meaning.
7. State two benefits of saying the dua when leaving home. (2 marks)
   Answer: It brings Allah's protection and shows that we trust Him. Following the Sunnah is also accepted.
   Why: 1 mark for each benefit.
8. Explain the difference between tawakkul and carelessness. (3 marks)
   Answer: Tawakkul is doing what we can, such as being careful and preparing, then trusting Allah. Carelessness is doing nothing and just hoping. Islam teaches tawakkul.
   Why: 1 mark for the meaning of tawakkul, 1 mark for carelessness, 1 mark for the example or conclusion.
9. List three things to do when you enter your home. (3 marks)
   Answer: Say Bismillah and the dua, greet the family with salaam, and remove and place your shoes neatly. Washing hands is also accepted.
   Why: 1 mark for each correct action.
