## PAGE 1: A strong body is a blessing
Think of a child who runs fast, plays football for a long time and does not get tired quickly. That child has physical fitness. Being fit means that your body is strong, active and healthy, so you can work, learn and worship well.

Islam cares about the body as well as the heart. Our body is a trust from Allah, and we must look after it. The Prophet (S.A.W.) said: "The strong believer is better and more beloved to Allah than the weak believer, while there is good in both." This hadith is narrated by Muslim.

### What this hadith means
• Both strong and weak believers have good in them, because faith is what matters most.
• A believer who takes care of their health can do more good: help others, work hard, and worship for longer.
• So a Muslim should try to be healthy and strong in body and in faith.

### Key words
• Fitness: a healthy, active body.
• Exercise: activities that make the body move and grow stronger.
• Sport: organised games with rules.

## PAGE 2: The hadith on teaching children skills
Your syllabus gives this hadith: "Teach your children swimming, archery and horse riding." Scholars quote it in the books of hadith, and it encourages parents to train children in useful physical skills. The hadith is given here as in the syllabus, and your teacher can guide you on its sources.

### Why these three skills?
• Swimming: keeps a person safe near water, builds strength, and can save lives.
• Archery: builds focus, patience and steady hands.
• Horse riding: builds balance and courage and was a means of travel and protection in the past.

### Skills for today
Today we also enjoy other sports such as running, football, netball, volleyball, cycling and athletics. Kenya is famous for long-distance running. These sports keep the body fit and help us to work in a team. In Islam, sports and games are allowed if they follow Islamic rules.

The Prophet (S.A.W.) was also known to race. It is reported that he once raced with his wife Aisha (R.A.), and he encouraged games that build strength.

## PAGE 3: Benefits of physical fitness
Exercise gives many benefits to our bodies and our lives.

### Benefits to the body
• Strong muscles and bones.
• A healthy heart and lungs.
• A healthy weight and lower risk of diseases such as diabetes.
• Better sleep and more energy.

### Benefits to the mind
• Less stress and more happiness.
• Better concentration at school.

### Benefits to character
• Teamwork: working together to win.
• Sharing: passing the ball and sharing equipment.
• Humility: accepting a win without boasting and a loss without anger.
• Fair play: keeping the rules and not cheating.
• Discipline: coming to practice on time and listening to the coach.
• Leadership: learning to guide your team.

### Benefits to worship
A fit person can stand for prayer, bend and prostrate comfortably. They can fast, walk to the mosque and help others in need. So keeping fit helps us to do our duties to Allah.

## PAGE 4: Games and sports that are permitted
Islam allows sports and games that are healthy, fair and safe. A game is permitted when:
• it does not harm the players or others,
• it does not make you forget your prayers,
• it is not mixed with cheating or lies,
• it does not involve betting or gambling, and
• it does not bring bad words, fighting or disrespect.

### Games that are not allowed
• Any game that involves gambling or betting money.
• Games that hurt animals for fun, such as animal fights.
• Games where people insult or hurt each other.

### Example
Amina's school has a football tournament. The team is winning, but the opponent is playing rough. Amina should not answer with rough play. She should play fairly, control her anger and, after the match, greet the other team and shake hands.

### Think
If there are two teams and one is cheating to win, is the win worth it? A Muslim would rather lose fairly than win by cheating.

## PAGE 5: Islamic etiquette during games and sports
The Muslim who plays sport keeps his or her faith on the field. Here is what a Muslim should observe.

### 1. Dress modestly
Wear clothing that covers the body properly and allows you to move freely. Boys should cover from the navel to the knees at least. Girls should wear modest clothing suited to the sport, and they may wear a tracksuit or long sports clothes. Modest clothes also protect you from injuries and from wandering eyes.

### 2. Keep the times of prayer
Do not let a game make you late for swalah. If the adhan is called, stop the game, make wudhu and pray. Play again afterwards.

### 3. Speak and act well
No insults, no shouting and no cheating. Say Bismillah before you start and Alhamdulillah at the end.

### 4. Respect others
Respect the referee, the coach and the opposing team. Greet everyone with salaam, and help a player who falls.

### 5. Care for your health
Drink water, warm up before you play, and rest when you are tired.

## PAGE 6: Summary and practice
Islam encourages a strong, healthy body because strong believers can do more good. The Prophet taught that the strong believer is more beloved to Allah than the weak believer, and he encouraged skills like swimming and archery for children.

### Remember
• Fitness helps the body, mind, character and worship.
• Games are allowed when they are safe, fair and keep us from wrongdoing.
• We dress modestly, keep prayer times, avoid cheating and respect others.

### Class activity
1. Plan a class fun day with running, skipping and ball games. Make rules that follow Islamic manners.
2. Demonstrate a warm-up: jog on the spot, stretch arms and legs, and bend gently.
3. Make a personal fitness plan for the week: for example 20 minutes of activity a day, and water instead of soda.

### Common mistakes
• Thinking that the Prophet's teaching means only strong people are good. Both have good, but we should try to be strong.
• Playing so long that you miss prayer.
• Winning by cheating, or shouting at other players.

## QUIZ
1. What does physical fitness mean?
   A) Having a lot of money
   B) Having a healthy, active and strong body
   C) Sleeping all day
   D) Eating many sweets
   Answer: B
   Why: Physical fitness means the body is healthy and strong enough to work, learn and play.
2. According to the hadith in Muslim, who is better and more beloved to Allah?
   A) The rich person
   B) The strong believer
   C) The tall person
   D) The person who plays sports only
   Answer: B
   Why: The Prophet said the strong believer is better and more beloved than the weak believer, while there is good in both.
3. Which three skills does the syllabus hadith tell us to teach children?
   A) Cooking, singing and dancing
   B) Swimming, archery and horse riding
   C) Farming, fishing and building
   D) Driving, drawing and typing
   Answer: B
   Why: The hadith says: "Teach your children swimming, archery and horse riding."
4. Which sport activity is NOT allowed in Islam?
   A) Football played fairly
   B) Athletics
   C) A game with betting for money
   D) Swimming
   Answer: C
   Why: Gambling and betting are forbidden, so any game with them is not allowed.
5. What should a Muslim do when the adhan is called during a game?
   A) Continue playing until the game ends
   B) Stop, make wudhu and pray
   C) Switch off the phone and sleep
   D) Ask the referee for extra time
   Answer: B
   Why: Prayer must not be delayed because of play; we can continue the game afterwards.
6. State three values a child can learn from games and sports. (3 marks)
   Answer: Any three: teamwork, sharing, humility, fair play, discipline, leadership.
   Why: 1 mark for each value.
7. Explain how being fit helps a Muslim in worship. (3 marks)
   Answer: A fit person can stand, bow and prostrate easily in prayer, can fast and walk to the mosque, and has strength to help other people. Fitness makes worship and service easier.
   Why: 1 mark for prayer postures, 1 mark for fasting or walking, 1 mark for helping others.
8. List three Islamic manners to observe while playing sports. (3 marks)
   Answer: Dress modestly, keep the times of prayer, avoid cheating and insults, respect others.
   Why: 1 mark for each valid etiquette.
9. Wanjiru is playing netball and wins by cheating when the referee is not looking. Explain what is wrong and what she should do. (3 marks)
   Answer: Cheating breaks the rules of fair play and honesty, which Islam teaches. She should admit it, say sorry, and play fairly in future.
   Why: 1 mark for naming cheating as wrong, 1 mark for honesty or fair play, 1 mark for a correct action.
