## PAGE 1: Makkah before the revelation
Muhammad (S.A.W.) was born in the city of Makkah in Arabia, in about the year 570 of the Common Era. His father, Abdullah, died before he was born, and his mother, Aminah, died when he was still a small child. His grandfather Abdul-Muttalib and then his uncle Abu Talib took care of him.

Makkah was an important city because it had the Kaaba, the House that Prophet Ibrahim and his son Ismail had built to worship Allah alone. But over the years, many people in Makkah had filled it with idols. There was cheating in trade, and the weak and the poor were often treated unfairly.

Muhammad grew up to be honest, kind and respected. People called him Al-Amin, the trustworthy. He worked as a shepherd and a trader. When he was about twenty-five, he married Khadija (R.A.), a respected businesswoman.

### Why did he go to a cave?
As he grew older, Muhammad disliked the wrongs in his society. He often went to be alone, to think and worship, in a cave called Hira on a mountain near Makkah, called Jabal an-Nur. He took some food and water and stayed there for days.

## PAGE 2: The night of the first revelation
When Muhammad (S.A.W.) was about forty years old, in the month of Ramadan, something extraordinary happened while he was in the cave of Hira. The angel Jibril (A.S.) appeared to him and said, "Read!" (Iqra). He replied, "I cannot read." The angel held him firmly and pressed him, and said again, "Read!" He replied again, "I cannot read." This happened a third time. Then Jibril recited the first verses of the Qur'an to him.

This event is told in a hadith recorded by Al-Bukhari and Muslim, from Aisha (R.A.).

### Story steps
1. The Prophet was alone in the cave, worshipping.
2. Jibril came and told him to read.
3. The Prophet said he could not read. He was not a man who had learned to read and write.
4. Jibril repeated the command three times, and then recited the first verses.
5. The Prophet went home shaking with fear.

### The night is called Laylat al-Qadr
The Qur'an (Surah 97) says that the Qur'an was sent down on the Night of Power (Laylat al-Qadr), in the month of Ramadan. This is why Ramadan is a special month.

### Think
Why do you think the Prophet was afraid? It was a new experience, and he did not know what it meant.

## PAGE 3: Khadija comforts the Prophet
The Prophet (S.A.W.) came down from the mountain, went to Khadija (R.A.), and said, "Cover me, cover me!" She wrapped him in a blanket until his fear went away. He told her everything and said that he feared for himself.

Khadija answered wisely and kindly. She said, in meaning, "Never! By Allah, Allah will never disgrace you. You keep good relations with your relatives, you carry the burdens of the weak, you give to the poor, you honour your guests and you help those struck by misfortune" (Al-Bukhari and Muslim).

### Waraqa ibn Nawfal
Khadija then took him to her cousin Waraqa ibn Nawfal, an old man who knew the earlier scriptures. He told the Prophet that this was the same angel who came to Prophet Musa (A.S.), and that his people would one day turn against him. Soon after this, revelation continued.

### Lessons
• A good friend and family member helps us when we are afraid.
• Good character brings Allah's protection and honour.
• The Prophet was chosen because of who he was and the trust of Allah.

## PAGE 4: The first five verses of Surah Al-Alaq
The first words that Allah revealed to the Prophet (S.A.W.) are the first five verses of Surah Al-Alaq (chapter 96).

### The verses
1. Iqra' bismi Rabbikal-ladhi khalaq
2. Khalaqal-insana min 'alaq
3. Iqra' wa Rabbukal-akram
4. Alladhi 'allama bil-qalam
5. 'Allamal-insana ma lam ya'lam

### The meaning
• Read in the name of your Lord who created.
• He created man from a clinging clot.
• Read, and your Lord is the Most Generous.
• Who taught by the pen.
• He taught man what he did not know.

### Understanding the words
• Iqra: read, or recite.
• 'Alaq: something that clings, an early stage of human creation.
• Qalam: the pen, a tool for writing and passing on knowledge.

### Practice
Recite these five verses slowly with a partner. Then close your book and try to say them from memory.

## PAGE 5: Teachings of the first five verses
These first verses of the Qur'an do not begin with a rule about food or clothing. They begin with the command "Read". This shows how important knowledge is in Islam.

### What we learn
1. Read and learn. Islam encourages everyone, boys and girls, to study.
2. Read in the name of Allah. Knowledge should be sought for good, with Allah's name and for His pleasure.
3. Allah is the Creator. He created us and gave us abilities.
4. Allah is Most Generous (Al-Akram). He gives us knowledge, health and ability.
5. Writing matters. Allah mentions the pen, because writing keeps knowledge safe for others.
6. We do not know everything. Allah taught us what we did not know, so we should be humble and keep learning.

### Applying the verses
• Do your homework and read your books every day.
• Ask teachers questions and listen carefully.
• Use the library, radio and safe internet to learn.
• Share what you learn with others.
• Be thankful to Allah for the ability to read and write.

### English link
Reading skills in English and Kiswahili help you to read the Qur'an translation and other books.

## PAGE 6: The Prophet's mission begins
After the first revelation, there was a pause for a period, and then more revelation came. The Prophet (S.A.W.) became the messenger of Allah. At first he called people to Islam quietly, telling his family and close friends. Later he called the people of Makkah openly to worship Allah alone.

### The message
• Worship Allah alone and do not worship idols.
• Be honest, treat the poor fairly and keep promises.
• Believe in the Day of Judgement.
• Pray and do good.

### Some people accepted, some rejected
A few people accepted Islam early, and many others opposed the message. They mocked and harassed the Prophet (S.A.W.) and his followers. But the Prophet stayed patient and truthful.

### Following his Sunnah
We follow the Prophet by being honest, seeking knowledge, worshipping Allah and being kind to others. Muslims also say "Salla Allahu alayhi wa sallam" when his name is mentioned.

### Common mistakes
• Thinking that the Prophet could read and write before revelation. He was unlettered.
• Forgetting that the first word of the Qur'an is "Read".
• Confusing Cave Hira with the Kaaba. Hira is a cave on a mountain near Makkah.

## QUIZ
1. In which cave did the Prophet (S.A.W.) receive the first revelation?
   A) Cave Thawr
   B) Cave Hira
   C) Cave Uhud
   D) Cave Safa
   Answer: B
   Why: He was worshipping in the cave of Hira when Jibril first came to him.
2. Which angel brought the revelation?
   A) Mikail
   B) Israfil
   C) Jibril
   D) Malakul Maut
   Answer: C
   Why: Jibril (A.S.) brings revelation to the prophets.
3. What was the first command in the revelation?
   A) Pray
   B) Fast
   C) Read
   D) Give charity
   Answer: C
   Why: The first word was "Iqra", meaning read.
4. Who comforted the Prophet (S.A.W.) when he returned from the cave?
   A) Abu Lahab
   B) Khadija (R.A.)
   C) Abu Talib
   D) Ali (R.A.)
   Answer: B
   Why: Khadija wrapped him in a blanket and reminded him of his good character.
5. Which surah contains the first five verses revealed?
   A) Al-Fatiha
   B) Al-Ikhlas
   C) Al-Alaq
   D) An-Nasr
   Answer: C
   Why: The first five verses are from Surah Al-Alaq (96).
6. Describe what happened in the cave of Hira when Jibril came. (3 marks)
   Answer: Jibril told the Prophet to read. The Prophet said he could not read. Jibril pressed him and repeated this three times, then recited the first verses.
   Why: 1 mark for the command to read, 1 mark for the reply, 1 mark for repeating three times and the recitation.
7. Write the meaning of the first two verses of Surah Al-Alaq. (2 marks)
   Answer: Read in the name of your Lord who created; He created man from a clinging clot.
   Why: 1 mark for each verse.
8. State three lessons we learn from the first five verses of Surah Al-Alaq. (3 marks)
   Answer: Any three: seek knowledge; read in the name of Allah; Allah is the Creator; Allah is Most Generous; writing is important; Allah teaches what we do not know.
   Why: 1 mark for each lesson.
9. Explain how Khadija (R.A.) supported the Prophet on the night of the first revelation. (3 marks)
   Answer: She comforted him and covered him, told him Allah would never disgrace him because of his good character, and took him to Waraqa for advice.
   Why: 1 mark for comfort, 1 mark for the words about his good character, 1 mark for taking him to Waraqa.
10. Name two ways a Grade 4 child can practise what the first verses teach. (2 marks)
   Answer: Read daily and do homework; ask questions and share knowledge.
   Why: 1 mark for each suitable way.
