## PAGE 1: Allah's creatures around us
Allah created many living things besides people. Look around your home, school and shamba. You will find cows, goats, chickens, dogs, cats, birds, fish in the river, insects like bees and butterflies, and plants such as trees, grass and maize. All of these are Allah's creatures. They praise Him in their own ways, and the Qur'an tells us that everything in the heavens and the earth glorifies Allah.

Humans are given special responsibility. We are told to look after the earth and to be kind to other creatures. Kindness (rahmah) is a good character, and it is an act of ibadah (worship) when we do it to please Allah.

### The Prophet's teaching on mercy
The Prophet (S.A.W.) said: "Those who are merciful will be shown mercy by the Most Merciful. Be merciful to those on the earth, and the One in the heavens will be merciful to you." This hadith is recorded by Abu Dawud and At-Tirmidhi.

### List of creatures
Make a list of creatures you see every day.
• At home: chickens, cats, dogs, cows, goats.
• At school: birds, ants, trees, flowers.
• In the environment: fish, monkeys, wild animals, bees.

## PAGE 2: The story of the thirsty dog
The Prophet (S.A.W.) told the story of a man who was walking on a road and felt very thirsty. He found a well, went down and drank. When he came up, he saw a dog panting and eating wet mud because of thirst. The man said, "This dog has become as thirsty as I was." He went down the well again, filled his leather sock with water, held it in his mouth to climb up, and gave the dog a drink. Allah thanked him and forgave his sins. The companions asked, "Is there a reward for us in serving animals?" He said, "There is a reward in serving every living creature" (Al-Bukhari and Muslim).

### The cat that was not fed
The Prophet (S.A.W.) also told us of a woman who tied a cat and did not feed it or let it search for food. She was punished because of this (Al-Bukhari and Muslim).

### Lessons
• A small act of kindness, like giving water, can bring Allah's forgiveness.
• Cruelty to animals is a sin.
• We must feed and give water to animals in our care.

### Discuss
How would you feel if you were hungry and thirsty and nobody helped? Talk in pairs about how animals feel.

## PAGE 3: Domestic animals used for transport
In many parts of Kenya, animals help people to carry things and travel. Donkeys pull carts in towns and villages. Oxen pull ploughs and carts on farms. Camels carry loads and people in the north. Horses are used in some places.

### Rules for using animals safely
1. Do not put a heavier load than the animal can carry. The Prophet (S.A.W.) told us to fear Allah regarding animals that cannot speak, to ride them when they are fit and to leave them when they are fit (reported by Abu Dawud).
2. Do not beat the animal hard or use sharp objects.
3. Give it rest, shade and time to eat and drink.
4. Keep it clean and take it to an animal health officer when it is sick.
5. Take care on the road, so that the animal, the driver and other people are safe.

## PAGE 4: Kindness to birds, insects, fish and plants
### Birds and small animals
Do not throw stones at birds or destroy nests. Do not keep birds in tiny cages. Put out water in a shallow dish, especially in the dry season.

### Insects
Insects like bees give honey and help crops to grow by carrying pollen. Do not harm them for fun.

### Fish
Do not pollute rivers, lakes or the sea, because fish live there. Take from the water only what you need. Islam teaches us not to waste.

### Plants and trees
The Prophet (S.A.W.) said that when a Muslim plants a tree or sows a crop, and a bird, a person or an animal eats from it, it counts as charity for him (Al-Bukhari and Muslim). So planting trees and caring for plants is worship. Do not cut trees without need. Do not step on flowers for fun or pour dirty water on plants.

### Environment
Keeping our surroundings clean helps all creatures. Put rubbish in the bin. Do not burn plastic or throw it in rivers, because animals may eat it.

## PAGE 5: Why we should be kind to creatures
### Reasons
• Allah made them. They belong to Him, and we are only caretakers.
• Kindness earns Allah's mercy and reward, as the hadith teaches.
• Animals give us food, milk, work, transport and companionship.
• A kind heart makes us kind to people as well.
• It keeps the environment balanced and healthy.

### Kindness is also safety
Being kind does not mean being careless. Wild animals and stray dogs can bite. Do not go near them. Tell an adult, who can take care of them, and wash your hands after touching any animal.

### Think
A boy sees a hungry stray cat near school. He shares a bit of his food and puts water in a small cup. What do you think Allah will give him?

### Role-play ideas
• Scene 1: A child finds a bird with a hurt wing and asks an adult for help.
• Scene 2: A cart driver is asked by a friend to add more sacks to a tired donkey's load. What does the child say?
• Scene 3: Children plant a tree in the school compound and water it.

## PAGE 6: Summary and how to show kindness
### How to show kindness
1. Feed and give water to animals in your care.
2. Use working animals gently and do not overload them.
3. Do not hurt, tease or throw stones at animals.
4. Plant, water and protect trees.
5. Keep water and land clean.
6. Ask an adult for help when an animal is sick or injured.

### Remember
• All creatures belong to Allah and praise Him.
• Kindness to creatures is worship and brings forgiveness and reward.
• The Prophet told the stories of a man given forgiveness for helping a dog, and a woman punished for cruelty to a cat.

### Common mistakes
• Thinking that only people deserve kindness.
• Beating a donkey to make it walk faster.
• Leaving pets or livestock without water.
• Going too near strange animals without care.

## QUIZ
1. The Prophet (S.A.W.) told a story of a man who gave water to a thirsty
   A) horse
   B) camel
   C) dog
   D) goat
   Answer: C
   Why: The man gave a dog water from a well, and Allah forgave him.
2. Which of these is cruelty to an animal?
   A) Giving it water
   B) Overloading a donkey so it can hardly walk
   C) Giving it shade
   D) Taking a sick animal to a health officer
   Answer: B
   Why: Overloading hurts the animal. The Prophet told us to fear Allah regarding animals.
3. According to the hadith, what is the result of planting a tree from which a bird or a person eats?
   A) It is charity for the planter
   B) It is a punishment
   C) It has no reward
   D) It is allowed only in Ramadan
   Answer: A
   Why: The hadith in Al-Bukhari and Muslim says that what is eaten from the crop or tree is counted as charity for the planter.
4. Which is a good way to be kind to birds in the dry season?
   A) Put out clean water in a dish
   B) Throw stones
   C) Take the eggs from the nest
   D) Chase them away
   Answer: A
   Why: Birds need water, especially when it is dry.
5. Who owns all the creatures?
   A) Farmers
   B) Allah
   C) Governments
   D) Shopkeepers
   Answer: B
   Why: Allah created and owns everything. We are only caretakers.
6. State three ways to care for a working donkey. (3 marks)
   Answer: Any three: give it water and food, do not overload it, give it rest and shade, do not beat it, treat its wounds.
   Why: 1 mark for each correct way.
7. Explain the lesson we learn from the story of the man who gave a dog water. (3 marks)
   Answer: A small act of kindness to a creature can earn Allah's forgiveness and reward. There is a reward in serving every living creature.
   Why: 1 mark for kindness to a creature, 1 mark for forgiveness or reward, 1 mark for the point that all creatures are included.
8. How can planting a tree be an act of worship? (2 marks)
   Answer: When people or animals eat from it, it counts as charity for the planter, so we earn reward.
   Why: 1 mark for charity or reward and 1 mark for the link to people or animals.
9. Name two creatures of Allah found in your environment and one way to show kindness to each. (4 marks)
   Answer: For example, chickens: give them clean water and food; bees: do not disturb their hives without need.
   Why: 1 mark for each creature and 1 mark for each suitable way of showing kindness.
