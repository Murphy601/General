## PAGE 1: Why do we wear clothes?
People wear clothes for many reasons. Clothes protect us from the sun, the cold and the rain. Clothes also help us to look neat and to cover our bodies. In Islam, clothing has a special meaning. Allah says in the Qur'an (7:26) that He has given us clothing to cover our private parts and as an adornment, and that the clothing of righteousness is best.

This means that the best "clothing" is not the most expensive dress, but good character and fear of Allah. Clothes are a favour from Allah, and we should use them in the way He likes.

### Uses of clothes
• To cover the body modestly.
• To protect us from heat, cold and injury.
• To look clean and dignified.
• To show respect at school, at the mosque and in public places.

### Key words
• Awrah: the parts of the body that must be covered.
• Haya: modesty or shyness from doing what is wrong.
• Adornment: making oneself look neat and nice.

### Think
The Prophet (S.A.W.) said that modesty is part of faith (Al-Bukhari and Muslim). How can clothes show modesty?

## PAGE 2: Features of the Islamic code of dressing
The Islamic code of dressing gives guidance on how Muslims should dress. It is not meant to make life hard. It is meant to protect dignity, and it is easy to follow.

### Main features
1. Cover the awrah. For boys and men, at least from the navel to the knees, and in public it is better to wear full shirts and trousers or a kanzu. For girls and women, the whole body except the face and hands, once they have reached the age when this is required.
2. Loose and decent. Clothes should not be too tight, and not see-through, so they do not show the shape of the body.
3. Clean and neat. Dirty and torn clothes are not a sign of Islam. The Prophet (S.A.W.) loved cleanliness.
4. Not for showing off. We should not wear clothes to be proud or to show that we are richer than others.
5. Modest. Not wearing clothes with rude pictures, bad words or symbols that go against Islam.
6. Not imitating the opposite sex. Boys should dress as boys, and girls as girls.

### Note for children
Children are still growing, but it is good to build the habit early, so that when the time comes, it is easy.

## PAGE 3: Different Islamic attires
Muslims around the world wear many kinds of clothes. Islam does not have a single uniform. What matters is that clothes follow the rules of modesty.

### Examples in Kenya
• Boys and men: kanzu (a long robe), kofia (a cap), shirts and trousers, and a sarong called kikoi or a similar wrap worn neatly.
• Girls and women: a long dress, a headscarf (hijab), or the buibui, which is a common black cover along the coast.
• Everyone: school uniform, when it is modest. Girls in skirts can wear a headscarf, and longer skirts or long socks may be used.

### Dressing manners
• Start with the right side when wearing clothes and shoes. Aisha (R.A.) said the Prophet loved to begin with the right in purifying himself, combing his hair and putting on shoes (Al-Bukhari and Muslim).
• Say Bismillah when dressing.
• Thank Allah for your clothes.

## PAGE 4: Why the Islamic dress code matters
### Benefits
• Protection: Modest dress protects a person from being looked at in the wrong way and from harm.
• Dignity and respect: People treat a person of modest dress with respect.
• Character: Dressing well trains us in modesty (haya), humility and self-control.
• Unity: Muslims can recognise each other by good manners.
• Obedience to Allah: We follow His command, and He rewards us.
• Health and comfort: Clothes that suit the weather, and that are clean, keep us comfortable.

### Dress is also an act of ibadah
When we dress modestly with the intention of pleasing Allah, even ordinary actions like putting on a shirt earn reward.

### Example
Zainabu enjoys football. At a school match she wears a tracksuit with long trousers and a sports headscarf. She can run freely and still be modest. Her teammates admire her confidence and her skill.

### Assertiveness
Sometimes friends may laugh at you for dressing modestly. Being assertive means calmly and politely saying, "This is how I dress because of my faith", without shouting or being unkind.

## PAGE 5: Dressing trends to avoid
Fashion changes every year. Some trends are harmless, but others go against Islamic teaching. Muslims should avoid a trend if it:

• shows parts of the body that should be covered,
• is tight, see-through or very short,
• carries pictures or writing that is rude or disrespectful to Allah, the Qur'an or religion,
• is worn to show off or to look down on other people,
• copies clothes used for things Islam forbids,
• is worn by boys to look like girls, or by girls to look like boys.

### Other things to avoid
• Wasting money on too many clothes. Allah does not love waste.
• Dirty and untidy dressing.
• Refusing to wear clothes suitable for the weather or the activity.

### What can I do?
1. Ask a parent or a teacher if you are not sure.
2. Choose clothes you like that are also modest.
3. Do not laugh at other children's clothes, whether rich or poor. Everyone has different means.

### Discussion
Is it okay for a child to insist on the newest fashion that the family cannot afford? Talk about how to be content with what we have.

## PAGE 6: Summary and model dressing
### Summary
• Clothes are a gift from Allah for covering, protection and beauty.
• Islamic dressing is modest, clean, neat and not for showing off.
• It differs in style around the world, but the rules of modesty stay the same.
• Dressing well in the right way is an act of ibadah.

### Class activity: Model an ideal outfit
1. In groups, choose an outfit for school, one for the mosque and one for sports.
2. Explain how each meets the rules of modesty.
3. Present to the class and let the others check against the checklist in Figure 1.

### Common mistakes
• Thinking a dress code is only for girls. Boys have rules too.
• Being proud of how modestly we dress and looking down on others.
• Wearing modest clothes but forgetting good manners.
• Copying every trend without thinking.

## QUIZ
1. What does the word "awrah" mean?
   A) A prayer
   B) The parts of the body that must be covered
   C) A cap
   D) A type of food
   Answer: B
   Why: Awrah is the part of the body that must be covered according to Islam.
2. Which quality does the Prophet (S.A.W.) say is part of faith?
   A) Speed
   B) Wealth
   C) Modesty
   D) Loudness
   Answer: C
   Why: The Prophet said that modesty (haya) is part of faith (Al-Bukhari and Muslim).
3. Which of these clothes is NOT suitable according to Islamic rules?
   A) A clean loose kanzu
   B) A see-through, tight shirt
   C) A long school skirt
   D) A neat pair of trousers
   Answer: B
   Why: Clothes should not be tight or see-through, because they do not cover properly.
4. Which side do we start with when putting on shoes?
   A) The left
   B) The right
   C) Either
   D) Whichever is bigger
   Answer: B
   Why: The Prophet loved to start with the right when putting on shoes and in other good actions.
5. What does Allah say is the best clothing (Qur'an 7:26)?
   A) The most expensive
   B) The newest
   C) The clothing of righteousness
   D) The brightest
   Answer: C
   Why: The verse says the clothing of righteousness is best.
6. State four features of the Islamic code of dressing. (4 marks)
   Answer: Any four: covers the awrah, loose and not see-through, clean and neat, not for showing off, modest with no rude pictures or words, not imitating the opposite sex.
   Why: 1 mark for each feature.
7. Give three benefits of dressing modestly. (3 marks)
   Answer: Any three: protection and dignity, respect from others, trains modesty and self-control, obedience to Allah with reward.
   Why: 1 mark for each benefit.
8. A friend laughs at Salma because of her long dress. Explain how she can respond in a good way. (3 marks)
   Answer: She should stay calm and polite, explain that she dresses this way because of her faith, and not insult her friend in return.
   Why: 1 mark for calm and polite, 1 mark for giving the reason, 1 mark for avoiding insults.
9. Name two types of clothes worn by Muslims in Kenya and one thing they should follow. (3 marks)
   Answer: Kanzu and kofia for boys and men; hijab or buibui for girls and women. They should be modest, clean and cover properly.
   Why: 1 mark each for two clothes and 1 mark for the rule.
