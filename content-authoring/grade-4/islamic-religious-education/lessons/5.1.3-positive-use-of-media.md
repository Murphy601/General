## PAGE 1: What is media?
Media are the ways through which information, stories, music and pictures reach many people. The word "media" is the plural of "medium", a way of carrying something. We meet media every day, often without noticing.

### Types of media
• Print media: books, newspapers, magazines, posters and flyers.
• Broadcast media: radio, television and films.
• Internet media: websites, video sites, social media, email and online games on computers, laptops or tablets.
• Mobile phones: used for calls, messages, photos, videos, apps and the internet.
• Video games: games played on phones, computers or consoles such as PlayStation.

### Media are a favour from Allah
Media help us to learn, to talk to family who live far away, to hear the Qur'an and lessons, and to know what is happening in our country. All good things are gifts from Allah, so we must use them in ways that please Him.

### Think about it
Which media do you use at home? Do you listen to the radio, watch TV, or use a parent's phone? Which one do you use most?

## PAGE 2: How media can help us
Used well, media help us to grow in knowledge, faith and character.

### Positive uses
• Learning: watching lessons, educational programmes and documentaries; reading books and articles; using safe learning apps.
• Islamic knowledge: listening to recitation of the Qur'an, learning surahs with the help of recordings, hearing lectures and stories of the prophets, and singing qasidas and anashid.
• News and safety: radio and television give weather reports, health alerts and news.
• Keeping in touch: calling grandparents and relatives, as Islam teaches us to keep family ties.
• Skills and jobs: children can learn computer skills, languages and how to write and design.
• Helping others: sharing correct and helpful information, such as how to prevent diseases.

### Example
Kevin's Qur'an teacher shares a recording of a beautiful recitation of Surah Al-Ikhlas. Kevin listens to it every evening for a week and improves his reading. That is a positive use of media.

## PAGE 3: How media can harm us
Not everything that we find in the media is good. Wrong use may harm our faith, our health and our relationships.

### Negative uses
• Bad content: films, videos or games with violence, rude words, or behaviour that Islam forbids.
• Wasting time: many hours of games or videos steal time from prayer, homework, sleep and helping at home.
• Health harm: sitting too long can affect the eyes, sleep and posture. Games can become an addiction.
• Untrue news and rumours: false news can spread quickly and hurt people.
• Cyberbullying: hurting people with unkind messages or pictures.
• Unsafe contacts: strangers online who may pretend to be friends.
• Privacy risks: giving away passwords, photos or where you live.

### Islam's guidance
The Qur'an (49:6) tells believers that if someone brings news, they should check it first, so they do not harm others by mistake. The Prophet (S.A.W.) said that whoever believes in Allah and the Last Day should say what is good or keep silent (Al-Bukhari and Muslim). This applies to what we type and share, just as it does to what we say.

## PAGE 4: Islamic ethics for using media
A Muslim uses media with good manners. These rules are about how we behave.

### Ethics to follow
1. Truthfulness: do not tell lies or share things you are not sure are true.
2. Modesty and guarding the eyes: avoid pictures, films and games that show shameful or violent things.
3. Respect: never insult, mock or spy on anyone. Do not share people's photos or private information without permission.
4. Time management: set a limit. Prayer, homework, family time, sleep and play come first.
5. Honesty: do not cheat in online games or exams, and do not copy someone's work and say it is yours.
6. Safety: never give your name, address, school, or passwords to strangers, and tell a parent or teacher if something makes you uncomfortable.
7. Permission: ask your parents before using the phone, joining a site or downloading apps.
8. Purpose: use media for what is good: learning, worship, helping others, and healthy fun.

### Integrity
Integrity means doing the right thing even when nobody is watching. Allah sees what we do, even on a screen, and the angels record our deeds.

## PAGE 5: Positive and negative uses side by side
Read the pairs. In each pair, decide which use is positive and which is negative.

### Discussion table
• Listening to Qur'an recitation on a phone. (Positive)
• Watching a violent movie late at night. (Negative)
• Calling grandmother on a video call. (Positive)
• Sending an unkind message about a classmate. (Negative)
• Watching a programme about farming or science. (Positive)
• Playing games for six hours and missing prayer. (Negative)
• Sharing a message that says "the school is closed" without checking. (Negative)
• Using the internet to find information for a school project, with permission. (Positive)

### Personal plan
Create a media plan for your week with a parent. For example:
• Screen time: one hour after homework.
• Content: educational and Islamic programmes, and family-approved shows.
• No screens during prayer times, at meals and one hour before bedtime.
• Whom to tell: a parent if I see something bad.

### Class activity
Invite a resource person, such as a teacher, imam or parent, to speak about how Muslims should use media. Prepare two questions to ask.

## PAGE 6: Summary
Media are a blessing from Allah when used with good manners. They can teach us, connect us and remind us of Allah. They can also waste time and harm us if used wrongly.

### Remember
• Types of media: print, broadcast, internet, mobile phones and video games.
• Positive use: learning, Islamic knowledge, safe communication and useful skills.
• Negative use: bad content, wasting time, false news, cyberbullying, unsafe contacts.
• Ethics: truthfulness, modesty, respect, time management, honesty, safety and permission.

### Common mistakes
• Sharing something without checking if it is true.
• Believing that everyone online is who they say they are.
• Using the phone during prayer time or at night when we should sleep.
• Keeping secrets from parents about what we see online.

### A quick rule
Before you watch, play or share, ask: Is it true? Is it kind? Is it useful? Would Allah be pleased? If the answer to any is no, stop and choose something better.

## QUIZ
1. Which of these is broadcast media?
   A) A newspaper
   B) Radio
   C) A poster
   D) A textbook
   Answer: B
   Why: Radio, television and films are broadcast media. Newspapers, posters and books are print media.
2. Which of these is a positive use of a phone?
   A) Sending unkind messages
   B) Learning a surah with a recording
   C) Playing games during prayer time
   D) Sharing a stranger's photos
   Answer: B
   Why: Learning the Qur'an with a recording uses the phone for good.
3. What does the Qur'an (49:6) advise us to do with news that we are not sure of?
   A) Share it quickly
   B) Check it first
   C) Ignore it forever
   D) Change it
   Answer: B
   Why: The verse tells believers to verify news so they do not harm others by mistake.
4. What should you do if someone online asks for your home address?
   A) Give it
   B) Give it if they are nice
   C) Refuse and tell a parent or teacher
   D) Send it to friends
   Answer: C
   Why: Strangers online may not be who they say they are, so keep private information safe and tell an adult.
5. What is cyberbullying?
   A) Reading a book
   B) Hurting or mocking people through phones or the internet
   C) Learning to type
   D) Watching news
   Answer: B
   Why: It is bullying done with messages, pictures or posts, and Islam forbids harming others.
6. State four types of media. (4 marks)
   Answer: Any four: print (books, newspapers), broadcast (radio, TV), internet media, mobile phones, video games.
   Why: 1 mark for each correct type.
7. Give three positive uses of media. (3 marks)
   Answer: Any three: learning, listening to Qur'an recitation and Islamic lessons, keeping in touch with family, getting news, learning useful skills.
   Why: 1 mark for each correct use.
8. Explain how too much screen time can harm a Muslim child. (3 marks)
   Answer: It wastes time that should be used for prayer, study, sleep and family. It can harm the eyes and posture, and may become an addiction.
   Why: 1 mark for wasting time or missing prayer, 1 mark for health, 1 mark for addiction or another valid harm.
9. Write two rules from your own media plan and say why each is good. (4 marks)
   Answer: For example: no phone during prayer times so I do not delay prayer; one hour of games after homework so I have time to study.
   Why: 1 mark for each rule and 1 mark for each valid reason.
