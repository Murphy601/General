## PAGE 1: Parents deserve our respect
Who carried you as a baby, fed you when you were hungry, stayed awake when you were sick and taught you to walk and speak? Your parents or guardians did. Islam teaches that after worshipping Allah, one of the greatest duties is to be good to our parents.

The Qur'an says (17:23) that your Lord has decreed that you worship none but Him, and that you be kind to parents. Allah puts kindness to parents right after the command to worship Him, to show how important it is.

### A famous hadith
A man asked the Prophet (S.A.W.), "Who among the people deserves the best of my company?" The Prophet said, "Your mother." The man asked, "Then who?" He said, "Your mother." The man asked, "Then who?" He said, "Your mother." The man asked a fourth time, "Then who?" He said, "Your father" (Al-Bukhari and Muslim).

### What does it teach?
• We must honour both parents.
• Mothers have a very special place, because of the hardship of carrying and raising a child.
• Being good to parents is a duty, not a favour.

## PAGE 2: What the Qur'an teaches (17:23-24)
The verses in Surah Al-Isra tell us how to treat our parents, especially when they grow old. In meaning, they say:
• Do not say "uff" (a word of disrespect or annoyance) to them, and do not shout at them.
• Speak to them with respectful and kind words.
• Lower to them the wing of humility out of mercy.
• Say: "My Lord, have mercy on them, as they raised me when I was small."

### The dua for parents
The dua in Arabic is "Rabbir-hamhuma kama rabbayani saghira". The meaning is "My Lord, have mercy on both of them, as they raised me when I was small".

### Understanding the words
• Do not say "uff": even the smallest sign of impatience is disliked.
• Lower the wing: be gentle and humble, as a bird lowers its wing to protect its chicks.
• Kind words: speak politely, with a soft voice.

### Practice
Pair up. One child acts as the parent asking for help. The other answers politely: "Yes, Mama, I am coming", instead of complaining. Discuss how the parent feels.

## PAGE 3: Duties of children to their parents
The syllabus asks us to know how children should treat their parents. Here is a list.

### Duties
1. Obey them in what is good. If a parent asks you to tidy up, do it quickly, without complaining.
2. Respect them. Greet them, do not interrupt, and do not shout at them.
3. Thank them and make dua for them.
4. Help them. Wash dishes, sweep the compound, fetch water, run errands, look after younger children, and help on the shamba.
5. Care for them when they are old or sick.
6. Be honest with them. Do not lie or hide things.
7. Ask permission before going out or using their things.
8. Work hard at school. This makes them happy.
9. Keep good relations with their friends and relatives.

### Limit of obedience
Islam asks us to obey parents in what is good. If they ask us to do something that Allah forbids, we must not obey. Even then, the Qur'an (31:15) tells us to keep treating them kindly in worldly matters.

### Example
Rehema's mother calls her while she is playing. She says "Nakuja, Mama" (I am coming), stops the game and runs to her. She gains her mother's love and Allah's reward.

## PAGE 4: Why we must respect and obey our parents
### Reasons
• Allah commands it in the Qur'an.
• Parents sacrifice their time, energy and money for us.
• Their pleasure brings blessing. A child who honours parents gets peace at home.
• It builds a loving family. Children who obey grow up to be responsible.
• It is a chance to earn reward. The Prophet (S.A.W.) said that among the best deeds are prayer at its time, kindness to parents and striving in the way of Allah (Al-Bukhari and Muslim).

### Signs of good children
• They greet parents with a smile.
• They listen when told something.
• They speak politely, even when they disagree.
• They accept correction.

### Signs of disobedience
• Shouting or slamming doors.
• Refusing to help.
• Lying or making excuses.
• Being rude in front of visitors.

## PAGE 5: Being kind when parents are old or after they pass away
### When parents are old
The Qur'an (17:23) says that if one or both of them reach old age with you, do not show impatience. Old people may be slow, forget things or repeat stories. We must be patient, help them walk, listen and give them food and medicine.

### If parents have passed away
Children continue to do good for them. We can:
• Make dua for them, for example: "Rabbir-hamhuma kama rabbayani saghira".
• Give charity on their behalf.
• Keep good relations with their friends and relatives.
• Repay their debts and fulfil their good wishes.
The Prophet (S.A.W.) taught that when a person dies, their deeds end except for three things, including a righteous child who makes dua for them (Muslim).

### Qasida and nasheed
Listen to a nasheed such as "Ummy", which is about appreciating a mother. Discuss which parts of the song touched you and how you can act on them.

### Class activity
Write a short thank-you note to your parent or guardian. Include one thing you promise to do to help at home this week.

## PAGE 6: Summary
### Summary
• Being good to parents is one of the most important duties after worshipping Allah.
• The Prophet said that the mother deserves the best of our company, then the mother, then the mother, then the father.
• Qur'an 17:23-24: do not say "uff", speak kindly, be humble and make dua for them.
• We obey in what is good, help them, thank them and care for them in old age.
• If they have passed away, we make dua and do good on their behalf.

### Common mistakes
• Obeying only when it is easy.
• Answering back rudely.
• Forgetting to say thank you.
• Preferring friends to parents.
• Thinking that helping at home is a punishment.

### Remember
Allah rewards those who are good to their parents, and the blessing of a happy home begins with a respectful child.

## QUIZ
1. According to the hadith, who deserves the best company from a person?
   A) The neighbour
   B) The mother, then the mother again, then the mother, then the father
   C) Only the father
   D) Friends
   Answer: B
   Why: The Prophet answered "your mother" three times before saying "your father".
2. What does "uff" stand for in Qur'an 17:23?
   A) A prayer
   B) A word of impatience or disrespect
   C) A greeting
   D) A type of dua
   Answer: B
   Why: We are told not to say even "uff" to our parents, meaning any sign of annoyance.
3. Which of these shows respect to parents?
   A) Shouting back
   B) Saying "Yes, Mama" and helping
   C) Ignoring their call
   D) Lying to them
   Answer: B
   Why: A polite reply and quick help are signs of respect.
4. If parents ask a child to do something Allah forbids, what should the child do?
   A) Obey
   B) Not obey, but still be kind to them
   C) Argue loudly
   D) Leave home
   Answer: B
   Why: Obedience is in what is good. Even then, we must treat parents kindly (Qur'an 31:15).
5. Which of these can children do for parents who have passed away?
   A) Make dua for them
   B) Forget them
   C) Complain about them
   D) Laugh at their old photos
   Answer: A
   Why: A righteous child who prays for parents continues to benefit them.
6. State four duties of children to their parents. (4 marks)
   Answer: Any four: obey in what is good, respect them, thank and make dua for them, help at home, care for them in old age, be honest with them.
   Why: 1 mark for each duty.
7. Write the meaning of the dua "Rabbir-hamhuma kama rabbayani saghira". (2 marks)
   Answer: My Lord, have mercy on them both as they raised me when I was small.
   Why: 1 mark for asking mercy for both and 1 mark for the reason.
8. Explain why obeying and respecting parents is a religious obligation. (3 marks)
   Answer: Allah commands it in the Qur'an and places it after worshipping Him. Parents sacrifice for us. It also brings blessing and reward.
   Why: 1 mark for the command of Allah, 1 mark for the sacrifice of parents, 1 mark for the blessing or reward.
9. Describe how a child can show patience to a grandparent who is old. (2 marks)
   Answer: Listen without complaining, help them to walk or bring food, and speak gently.
   Why: 1 mark for each valid action.
