## PAGE 1: Reading weather words and picture cards
Look at the picture on each card first. Then read the word. Reading a word after you see its picture helps you keep the meaning in your mind.

le soleil       the sun
le nuage        the cloud
le vent         the wind
la pluie        the rain
l'orage         the storm
la neige        the snow
le temps        the weather

### Reading tips
The letters ei in neige say eh. The letters ui in pluie say wee. The letters eil in soleil say ay, and the l sounds like y. The letters ent and ant are nasal, as in vent. The letters ge at the end of nuage, orage and neige say zh.

### Word recognition
When you see the same letters again, you can read a new word faster. You know j in jour says zh, and g before e says zh in orage. So you can read orage as o-RAZH.

### Try it
Read the seven words aloud with a partner. Point to the letter groups eil, ei, ui and ge in the words and say their sounds.

## PAGE 2: Reading sentences about the weather
Read these sentences slowly, then at normal speed.

Il fait beau.            It is nice weather.
Il fait chaud.           It is hot.
Il fait froid.           It is cold.
Il pleut.                It is raining.
Il neige.                It is snowing.
Il y a du soleil.        It is sunny.
Il y a du vent.          It is windy.
Il y a des nuages.       It is cloudy.
Il y a un orage.         There is a storm.

### Question and answer
Quel temps fait-il aujourd'hui ?    What is the weather like today?
Aujourd'hui, il pleut.              Today it is raining.

### Read with rhythm
Read the sentences in three groups: Il fait ..., Il y a ..., and Il pleut, Il neige. Take a small breath after each group.

### Try it
Choose three sentences and read them aloud to a partner. Your partner points to the matching weather picture.

## PAGE 3: Friendly and harsh weather
We can sort weather into two groups: friendly weather and harsh weather. Read the text below.

Le temps est agréable quand il y a du soleil et un peu de vent. Le temps est difficile quand il y a un orage, une grande pluie ou un vent très fort.

### New words
agréable = pleasant        (a-gray-A-bluh)
difficile = difficult, harsh
très fort = very strong
un peu de = a little

### Sorting
Friendly: le soleil, un peu de vent, le beau temps.
Harsh: l'orage, la grande pluie, le vent très fort.

### Questions
1. Quand le temps est-il agréable ? When there is sun and a little wind.
2. Quand le temps est-il difficile ? When there is a storm, big rain or very strong wind.

### Reading tip
Read the text once for the general idea. Then read again to find the answers. If you meet a hard word, try to guess from the pictures and the other words around it.

### Try it
Read the text aloud. Then sort five weather words into two lists in your exercise book.

## PAGE 4: Staying safe in harsh weather
Read this safety text aloud with a partner.

Quand il y a un orage, je reste dans la maison. Je ne vais pas sous un grand arbre. Quand il pleut beaucoup, je fais attention à l'eau. Quand il fait très chaud, je bois de l'eau.

### New words
je reste = I stay              (zhuh REST)
je ne vais pas = I do not go
un arbre = a tree              (uhn ARBR)
je fais attention = I am careful
quand = when                   (kon)

### Answer in English
1. What do you do in a storm? Stay in the house.
2. Where should you not go? Under a big tree.
3. What do you drink when it is very hot? Water.

### Why it matters
Some weather can be dangerous. Strong winds may blow off roofs, heavy rain can cause floods and lightning can strike tall trees. Knowing what to do keeps you and your family safe.

### Try it
Read the text once in a chorus, then read one sentence each. Say what you would do in your own words.

## PAGE 5: Summary and reading practice
Reading weather texts is a good way to practise reading with the correct sounds. Use the text and pictures to guess new words.

### Common mistakes
• Saying the g in orage like g in go. It says zh.
• Saying the l of soleil like in English. It sounds like y.
• Reading pluie as ploo-ie. It is plwee.
• Reading too fast and losing the meaning.

### Read together
Read this short weather report:
Bonjour ! Aujourd'hui, il fait beau. Il y a du soleil et un peu de vent. Demain, il pleut. Bonne journée !

Bonne journée means Have a good day (bon zhoor-NAY).

### Summary
You can now read weather words, read sentences with Il fait and Il y a, sort weather into friendly and harsh, and read a short safety text. Your reading with expression gets better with practice.

## QUIZ
1. What does la neige mean?
   A) the rain
   B) the snow
   C) the sun
   D) the storm
   Answer: B
   Why: La neige is snow.
2. What does Il y a des nuages mean?
   A) It is windy.
   B) It is cloudy.
   C) It is hot.
   D) It is raining.
   Answer: B
   Why: Des nuages means some clouds.
3. What does agréable mean?
   A) pleasant
   B) harsh
   C) cold
   D) big
   Answer: A
   Why: Agréable means pleasant.
4. What do you do in a storm, according to the text?
   A) Go under a big tree
   B) Stay in the house
   C) Play outside
   D) Drink water only
   Answer: B
   Why: The text says Je reste dans la maison.
5. How do you say the g in orage?
   A) g as in go
   B) j as in jump
   C) zh
   D) k
   Answer: C
   Why: The letters ge at the end of orage say zh.
6. Translate into English: Aujourd'hui, il pleut. (2 marks)
   Answer: Today it is raining.
   Why: 1 mark for today and 1 mark for it is raining.
7. Read and answer: Quand il fait très chaud, je bois de l'eau. What does the speaker do when it is hot? (2 marks)
   Answer: The speaker drinks water.
   Why: 1 mark for drinks and 1 mark for water.
8. Explain why staying in the house in a storm is a good idea. (2 marks)
   Answer: The house protects you from strong winds, heavy rain and lightning.
   Why: 1 mark for protection and 1 mark for a danger named.
9. Sort into friendly and harsh: le beau temps, l'orage, le soleil, le vent très fort. (3 marks)
   Answer: Friendly: le beau temps, le soleil. Harsh: l'orage, le vent très fort.
   Why: 1 mark for each correct pair and 1 mark for headings.
