## PAGE 1: Family words to write
To write about your family in French, you first need to spell the family words correctly, with their accents. Study this list and copy each word carefully.

le père         the father
la mère         the mother
le frère        the brother
la sœur         the sister
le grand-père   the grandfather
la grand-mère   the grandmother
le bébé         the baby
la famille      the family

### Watch the accents
Père, mère and frère have an accent grave on the è. Bébé has an accent aigu on each é. Sœur has the joined letters œ. Grand-père and grand-mère have a hyphen, a small line joining two words.

### Tip for remembering
Say each word aloud while you write it. Trace the accent last, like a small flick of the pencil. Then check with the list.

### Try it
Cover the list. Write all eight words from memory. Uncover the list and give yourself one point for each correct word, with all accents in place.

### Extracting information
• Le père s'appelle Otieno.
• La mère s'appelle Njeri.
• La fille s'appelle Amina.
• Le garçon s'appelle Kamau.

S'appelle means is called. Write similar sentences for a family tree you know.

## PAGE 3: Writing simple sentences about family
The pattern for writing about your family is easy.

Ma mère s'appelle ...
Mon père s'appelle ...
Mon frère s'appelle ...
Ma sœur s'appelle ...

Remember: mon goes with père, frère and grand-père. Ma goes with mère, sœur and grand-mère.

### Example
Ma mère s'appelle Njeri. Mon père s'appelle Otieno. Mon frère s'appelle Kamau.

### Fill the gaps
1. ____ mère s'appelle Grace. (mon or ma?)
2. ____ père s'appelle David. (mon or ma?)
3. ____ sœur s'appelle Wanjiru. (mon or ma?)

Answers: 1. Ma  2. Mon  3. Ma

### Capital letters and full stops
Every sentence starts with a capital letter and ends with a full stop. Names of people always start with a capital letter, but the words père and mère do not, unless they start the sentence.

### Try it
Write three sentences about your own family members using the pattern above.

## PAGE 4: Word games with family words
Word games help you remember spelling. They are also fun to play in pairs or groups.

### Scrabble letters
Use letter cards to make a word. Take the letters e, r, p, è. You can arrange them into père. Take the letters m, è, r, e. You can arrange them into mère.

### Crossword clues
Here is a small crossword clue list. Write the answers in the boxes.
1. father: père (4 letters)
2. mother: mère (4 letters)
3. sister: sœur (4 letters)
4. brother: frère (5 letters)

Count the letters in your answers. If your word has the wrong number of boxes, check again.

### Word snake
Find and write the words hidden here: mèrepèresœurfrère
The words are: mère, père, sœur, frère.

### Try it
Make your own letter puzzle for a friend using one family word. Jumble the letters and ask your friend to put them in order.

## PAGE 5: Writing a short paragraph
Now you write a short paragraph about your family. Use short, clear sentences. Here is a model.

Bonjour ! Je m'appelle Wanjiru. Voici ma famille. Ma mère s'appelle Grace. Mon père s'appelle David. Mon frère s'appelle Brian. Ma sœur s'appelle Faith. Au revoir !

### Checklist
1. Did I begin with a greeting?
2. Did I write my name after je m'appelle?
3. Did I use mon or ma correctly?
4. Are the accents in père, mère and frère correct?
5. Did I end each sentence with a full stop?

### Common mistakes
• Writing pere and mere without the accent.
• Writing mon mère instead of ma mère.
• Forgetting the capital letter for each name.
• Leaving out the apostrophe in s'appelle.

### Summary
You can now spell family words with accents, read a family tree, write simple sentences using mon and ma, play word games and write a short paragraph. Keep your writing neat so that others can read it easily.

## QUIZ
1. Which spelling is correct?
   A) pere
   B) père
   C) pèré
   D) peré
   Answer: B
   Why: Père has an accent grave on the second letter, è.
2. Which word goes in the gap: ____ sœur s'appelle Njeri.
   A) Mon
   B) Ma
   C) Le
   D) Un
   Answer: B
   Why: Sœur is feminine, so we use ma.
3. What does s'appelle mean?
   A) is going
   B) is called
   C) is big
   D) is here
   Answer: B
   Why: S'appelle means is called.
4. Which sentence is correct?
   A) Mon mère s'appelle Grace.
   B) Ma père s'appelle David.
   C) Mon père s'appelle David.
   D) Ma frère s'appelle David.
   Answer: C
   Why: Père is masculine, so we say mon père.
5. What is la fille?
   A) the girl
   B) the boy
   C) the baby
   D) the father
   Answer: A
   Why: La fille means the girl or daughter.
6. Write in French: My brother is called Kamau. (2 marks)
   Answer: Mon frère s'appelle Kamau.
   Why: 1 mark for mon frère and 1 mark for s'appelle Kamau.
7. Write two sentences in French about a mother and a father. Use the names Grace and David. (4 marks)
   Answer: Ma mère s'appelle Grace. Mon père s'appelle David.
   Why: 2 marks for each correct sentence, with correct mon or ma and accents.
8. Explain the difference between mon and ma. (2 marks)
   Answer: Mon is used with masculine words like père. Ma is used with feminine words like mère.
   Why: 1 mark for each rule.
9. Unscramble the letters to make a family word: r è f e r. (2 marks)
   Answer: frère
   Why: 1 mark for the letters in order and 1 mark for the accent grave.
