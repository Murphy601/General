## PAGE 1: Happy birthday
A birthday is un anniversaire (uhn a-nee-vair-SAIR). It is a special day when we celebrate the day we were born. In French, we say Joyeux anniversaire ! or Bon anniversaire ! to wish someone a happy birthday.

### Birthday words
un anniversaire   a birthday        (uhn a-nee-vair-SAIR)
une fête          a party           (ün FET)
un gâteau         a cake            (uhn ga-TOH)
une bougie        a candle          (ün boo-ZHEE)
un cadeau         a present         (uhn ka-DOH)
un ballon         a balloon         (uhn ba-LON)
une chanson       a song            (ün shon-SON)

### Listen for the sounds
The letter â in gâteau makes a long ah sound. The letters eau in gâteau and cadeau say oh. The letters ch in chanson say sh.

### Try it
Listen to your teacher say each word. Repeat it. Then point to the picture that shows the word. Try to do it in a party game with a partner.

### Sounds
un = uhn, deux = duh, trois = trwa, quatre = KA-truh, cinq = sank, six = seess, sept = set, huit = weet, neuf = nuhf, dix = deess.

### Try it
Count from 1 to 10 in French. Then count only the odd numbers: un, trois, cinq, sept, neuf. Next, count the even numbers: deux, quatre, six, huit, dix. Notice that dix-sept, dix-huit and dix-neuf are built from dix (ten) plus sept, huit and neuf, so they mean ten and seven, ten and eight, ten and nine.

## PAGE 3: How old are you?
To ask a person's age, say Tu as quel âge ? It means How old are you? The answer is J'ai ... ans. Ans means years. The letter â in âge has an accent circonflexe. Say quel âge as kel AHZH.

### Question and answer
Tu as quel âge ?     How old are you?
J'ai neuf ans.       I am nine years old.
J'ai dix ans.        I am ten years old.
J'ai onze ans.       I am eleven years old.

### A birthday dialogue to listen to
Amina: Bonjour, Kamau ! Bon anniversaire !
Kamau: Merci, Amina !
Amina: Tu as quel âge ?
Kamau: J'ai dix ans.
Amina: Voici un cadeau !
Kamau: Oh, merci beaucoup !

Beaucoup means very much (boh-KOO). Oh, merci beaucoup ! means Oh, thank you very much!

### Listen for gist
The gist of this dialogue is that Amina wishes Kamau a happy birthday and gives him a present. Key words are: anniversaire, dix ans, cadeau, merci.

## PAGE 4: Talking about my birthday
When you talk about a birthday, you can say what happens at the party. Use simple sentences.

Il y a un gâteau.        There is a cake.
Il y a des ballons.      There are balloons.
Je chante.               I sing.
Je danse.                I dance.
J'aime la fête !         I like the party!

Des means some. It is said day. Il y a des ballons means There are balloons.

### Miming game
Choose a party action such as singing, dancing or blowing out a candle. Do it as a mime with no words. Your partner guesses it in French: Tu chantes ! Tu danses ! Then you say Oui or Non. Oui means yes and non means no.

### Sing along
Sing the birthday wish to the tune of Happy Birthday: Bon anniversaire, bon anniversaire, bon anniversaire, Amina, bon anniversaire !

### Try it
Do a mime and ask a friend to guess what you are doing.

## PAGE 5: Key words and summary
To identify key words in a text, listen for words that are repeated, words you know, and words that answer the question What is this about? In a birthday text, key words are anniversaire, gâteau, cadeau, fête and ans.

### Practise
Your teacher reads: Aujourd'hui, c'est mon anniversaire. J'ai dix ans. Il y a un gâteau et des ballons. C'est la fête !
Key words: anniversaire, dix ans, gâteau, ballons, fête.
Gist: It is the speaker's birthday and there is a party.

### Common mistakes
• Saying ans with a strong s. The s is silent, and an is a nasal sound.
• Forgetting that gâteau ends in eau and sounds oh.
• Mixing up onze (11) with un (1). Onze is on-z and un is uhn.

### Summary
You can wish someone a happy birthday, count from one to twenty, ask and answer Tu as quel âge ?, talk about a party, and identify key words in a short text. Numbers and greetings make good conversation starters.

## QUIZ
1. What does Bon anniversaire mean?
   A) Good morning
   B) Happy birthday
   C) Goodbye
   D) Thank you
   Answer: B
   Why: Bon anniversaire is a birthday wish.
2. What does un gâteau mean?
   A) a cake
   B) a candle
   C) a present
   D) a song
   Answer: A
   Why: Un gâteau is a cake.
3. What is the number huit?
   A) 6
   B) 7
   C) 8
   D) 9
   Answer: C
   Why: Huit means eight.
4. Which question asks someone's age?
   A) Comment tu t'appelles ?
   B) Tu as quel âge ?
   C) Quel jour est-ce ?
   D) Où est le livre ?
   Answer: B
   Why: Tu as quel âge ? means How old are you?
5. What does J'ai dix ans mean?
   A) I have ten books.
   B) I am ten years old.
   C) I go on ten days.
   D) I have ten friends.
   Answer: B
   Why: J'ai ... ans is used to say your age.
6. Write in French: I am nine years old. (2 marks)
   Answer: J'ai neuf ans.
   Why: 1 mark for j'ai and 1 mark for neuf ans.
7. Write the numbers 1 to 5 in French. (3 marks)
   Answer: un, deux, trois, quatre, cinq
   Why: 1 mark for un and deux, 1 mark for trois and quatre, 1 mark for cinq.
8. Choose the key words in this text: Aujourd'hui, c'est mon anniversaire. Il y a un gâteau. Explain what the text is about. (2 marks)
   Answer: Key words: anniversaire, gâteau. It is about a birthday.
   Why: 1 mark for key words and 1 mark for the gist.
9. Write a short dialogue of three lines to wish a friend a happy birthday. (3 marks)
   Answer: Bon anniversaire, Kamau ! Merci ! Tu as quel âge ? J'ai dix ans.
   Why: 1 mark for the wish, 1 mark for thanks and 1 mark for the age question and answer.
