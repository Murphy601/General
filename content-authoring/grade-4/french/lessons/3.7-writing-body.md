## PAGE 1: Spelling body words
To write about the body, learn to spell the words with their little words. Copy each word carefully.

la tête          the head
les cheveux      the hair
les yeux         the eyes
le nez           the nose
la bouche        the mouth
l'oreille        the ear
la main          the hand
le bras          the arm
la jambe         the leg
le pied          the foot
le doigt         the finger
le dos           the back

### Spelling hints
• Tête has an accent circonflexe on the ê.
• Cheveux ends in eux, and yeux also ends in eux.
• Oreille has ll and a silent e at the end.
• Doigt has a silent g and a silent t: d-o-i-g-t.
• Jambe has mb, not nb.

### Try it
Look at the words for one minute. Cover them and write ten from memory. Check each one and correct your mistakes.

## PAGE 2: Drawing and labelling
Draw a simple figure of a person in your exercise book. Then write the French name of each part next to it. Use straight lines from the label to the part.

### How to label well
• Write neatly, in the same size.
• Put the le, la or les in front of each word.
• Use a ruler to draw the lines.
• Do not let the lines cross each other.

### Example labels
la tête
les yeux
le nez
la bouche
les bras
les mains
les jambes
les pieds

Notice that les bras means the arms. The word bras stays the same in the plural, but les shows there are two. The s in bras is silent.

### Sharing
Show your drawing to a friend. Ask them to read your labels aloud. If they find a mistake, correct it together.

### Try it
Choose the face. Draw a large face and label: les cheveux, les yeux, le nez, la bouche, les oreilles.

## PAGE 3: Filling gaps
Complete each sentence with a word from the list.

Word bank: deux, dix, un, une, mains, tête

1. J'ai ______ yeux.
2. J'ai ______ nez.
3. J'ai ______ bouche.
4. J'ai deux ______ (hands).
5. J'ai ______ doigts.
6. Ma ______ est sur mon corps.

Answers: 1. deux  2. un  3. une  4. mains  5. dix  6. tête

Note: Each word in the bank is used once. Check that each answer agrees with the noun that follows: un goes with nez, une goes with bouche.

### Fill a paragraph
Voici Kamau. Il a dix ans. Il a deux ______ et deux ______. Il a un ______ et une ______.
Possible answers: yeux, oreilles, nez, bouche.

### Try it
Write two gap sentences about the body and give them to a partner to complete.

## PAGE 4: Writing short texts
A short text about the body uses simple sentences. Follow the pattern J'ai + number + part.

J'ai deux mains. J'ai dix doigts. J'ai deux pieds.
J'ai un nez et une bouche.

### Describe a person
Voici mon ami Brian. Il est grand. Il a les cheveux courts et noirs. Il a deux yeux et un grand sourire.

Sourire means smile (soo-REER). Un grand sourire means a big smile.

### Punctuation
Use a full stop at the end of each sentence. Use a comma between two short things, as in Il est grand, il a les cheveux courts. Do not forget the capital letter at the start.

### Putting sentences in order
Sentences should follow each other in order. First say who the person is. Then say how old the person is. Then describe their body.

### Try it
Write four sentences about a friend or a family member. Use il or elle. Read them to a partner and check the order.

## PAGE 5: Charts and safe searching
A chart of the body shows the names of the parts. You can find charts in books or on safe websites. Ask an adult and always turn Safe Search on when you use the internet.

### Make your own body chart
1. Draw a person on a large piece of paper.
2. Write six French labels.
3. Colour the picture.
4. Show the chart to your class.

### Check your writing
• Are all the words spelled correctly?
• Are accents in the right places?
• Is there a capital letter at the start of each sentence?
• Is there a full stop at the end?
• Is my handwriting neat?

### Common mistakes
• Writing tete without the accent.
• Writing doight instead of doigt.
• Writing les mains as le mains. Use les with mains.
• Forgetting the ending of cheveux and yeux.

### Summary
You can now spell body words, draw and label a body, fill gaps, write short texts and a description of a person, and make a body chart. Good writing means careful checking.

## QUIZ
1. Which is the correct spelling of head?
   A) tete
   B) tête
   C) têt
   D) tète
   Answer: B
   Why: Tête has an accent circonflexe on the ê.
2. Which word goes with les?
   A) nez
   B) yeux
   C) bouche
   D) tête
   Answer: B
   Why: Yeux is plural, so we say les yeux.
3. Which sentence is correct?
   A) J'ai deux mains.
   B) J'ai deux main.
   C) Je ai deux mains.
   D) J'ai deux mains
   Answer: A
   Why: It has j'ai with an apostrophe, the plural mains, and a full stop.
4. What is the correct spelling of finger?
   A) doigt
   B) doight
   C) doit
   D) doig
   Answer: A
   Why: Doigt is spelled d-o-i-g-t.
5. What does Il a les cheveux courts mean?
   A) He has short hair.
   B) He has long hair.
   C) He is tall.
   D) He has two eyes.
   Answer: A
   Why: Courts means short and les cheveux means hair.
6. Write in French: I have a mouth. (2 marks)
   Answer: J'ai une bouche.
   Why: 1 mark for j'ai and 1 mark for une bouche.
7. Label these parts in French: head, hand, foot. (3 marks)
   Answer: la tête, la main, le pied
   Why: 1 mark for each label with its le or la and correct spelling.
8. Write three sentences in French to describe a friend. (4 marks)
   Answer: Voici mon ami Brian. Il est grand. Il a les cheveux courts.
   Why: 1 mark for each sentence and 1 mark for correct use of il.
9. Explain why you should turn on Safe Search when searching for a body chart. (2 marks)
   Answer: It only shows pages that are suitable for children, so you stay safe online.
   Why: 1 mark for suitable pages and 1 mark for staying safe.
