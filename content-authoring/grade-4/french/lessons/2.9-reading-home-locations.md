## PAGE 1: Reading room names and objects
Read these words aloud slowly. Look at each little word le, la or l' as well as the noun.

la cuisine      the kitchen
le salon        the living room
la chambre      the bedroom
le canapé       the sofa
la télé         the TV
le frigo        the fridge
le lit          the bed
la porte        the door
le tapis        the rug

### Reading tips
The letters ch in chambre say sh. The letters ui in cuisine say wee. The accent aigu in canapé and télé says ay. The word frigo is short for réfrigérateur, but people say frigo every day.

### Echo reading
In echo reading, your teacher reads a word and you say it back, exactly as you heard it. Try it first with the word list above. Then swap and let a friend be the teacher.

### Try it
Read all nine words to a partner. Point to something in your classroom that is like each thing, or draw a small picture beside each word.

## PAGE 2: Reading sentences about places in the home
Read these sentences. Then say the English meaning.

Il y a un canapé dans le salon.       There is a sofa in the living room.
La télé est sur la table.             The TV is on the table.
Le frigo est dans la cuisine.         The fridge is in the kitchen.
Le lit est dans la chambre.           The bed is in the bedroom.
Le tapis est sous la table.           The rug is under the table.
Le chat est derrière la porte.        The cat is behind the door.

### Questions
Où est le frigo ?  Il est dans la cuisine.
Où est le tapis ?  Il est sous la table.
Où est la télé ?   Elle est sur la table.

Il and elle stand for the noun. Use il for a masculine word and elle for a feminine word.

### Try it
Read the six sentences. Cover the English. Say the meaning in your own words. Check your answers with a partner.

## PAGE 3: A text about my house
Read this text aloud with a partner. Take turns, one sentence each.

Voici ma maison. Dans le salon, il y a un canapé et une télé. Le tapis est devant le canapé. Dans la cuisine, il y a une table, quatre chaises et un frigo. La porte est à côté de la fenêtre. Ma chambre est petite. Mon lit est près de la fenêtre.

### New words
devant = in front of
à côté de = next to        (a ko-TAY duh)
près de = near             (pray duh)
quatre chaises = four chairs

### Questions
1. Where is the sofa? Dans le salon.
2. Where is the rug? In front of the sofa.
3. How many chairs are there in the kitchen? Four.
4. Where is the bed? Near the window.

### Reading with the group
Read the text again in choral reading. Keep the same speed as your classmates and listen for their voices as you read.

### Try it
Draw a small picture of the house described in the text and label the rooms and things in French.

## PAGE 4: Safe search and shared reading
You can find more short texts about houses on the internet, but always ask an adult first and turn Safe Search on. Search for la maison or les pièces de la maison. Read only pages your teacher approves.

### Steps
1. Ask an adult.
2. Turn Safe Search on.
3. Type la maison and look at the pictures.
4. Read the labels and say the words aloud.
5. Write down three words you did not know.

### Match pictures to words
Match the picture to the word: a picture of a bed matches le lit; a picture of a door matches la porte; a picture of a sofa matches le canapé.

### Fluency
Fluent readers read smoothly and at a steady pace. To improve fluency, read the same short text three times. The first time, read slowly. The second time, read faster. The third time, read with expression.

### Try it
Read the text on page 3 three times. Which reading was the smoothest?

## PAGE 5: Summary and practice
Reading well helps you find where things are and tell others about your home.

### Common mistakes
• Saying the s in salon like sh. It is a soft s, as in sa-LON.
• Saying the s in cuisine like s. It is z: kwee-ZEEN.
• Reading près de like pres du. Say pray duh.
• Forgetting the silent letters at the end, as in lit and tapis.

### Read and answer
Read: Le chat est sous le lit. Le livre est sur la chaise. Where is the cat? Under the bed. Where is the book? On the chair.

### Summary
You can read room and object names, read sentences that say where things are, read a short text about a house, and use Safe Search to find more. Keep practising and your reading will become smooth.

## QUIZ
1. What does à côté de mean?
   A) next to
   B) under
   C) behind
   D) between
   Answer: A
   Why: À côté de means next to.
2. What does Le frigo est dans la cuisine mean?
   A) The fridge is in the kitchen.
   B) The fridge is in the bedroom.
   C) The cook is in the kitchen.
   D) The fridge is on the table.
   Answer: A
   Why: Dans means in and la cuisine is the kitchen.
3. How do you say ui in cuisine?
   A) oo
   B) wee
   C) ay
   D) oy
   Answer: B
   Why: The letters ui say wee.
4. Which sentence says the rug is under the table?
   A) Le tapis est sur la table.
   B) Le tapis est sous la table.
   C) Le tapis est dans la table.
   D) Le tapis est devant la table.
   Answer: B
   Why: Sous means under.
5. What does près de mean?
   A) near
   B) far from
   C) in
   D) on
   Answer: A
   Why: Près de means near.
6. Translate into English: Le lit est dans la chambre. (2 marks)
   Answer: The bed is in the bedroom.
   Why: 1 mark for the bed and 1 mark for in the bedroom.
7. Read: Dans le salon, il y a un canapé et une télé. What is in the living room? (2 marks)
   Answer: A sofa and a TV.
   Why: 1 mark for the sofa and 1 mark for the TV.
8. List three safe steps to follow when searching for texts online. (3 marks)
   Answer: Ask an adult; turn Safe Search on; read only approved pages.
   Why: 1 mark for each step.
9. Write two sentences in French about where things are in your classroom. (3 marks)
   Answer: Le livre est sur la table. La porte est à côté de la fenêtre.
   Why: 1 mark for each correct sentence and 1 mark for correct le or la.
