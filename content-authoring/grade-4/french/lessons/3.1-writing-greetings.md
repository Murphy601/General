## PAGE 1: Writing French words correctly
Writing in French means spelling every word correctly, including capital letters, accents and punctuation. A small mistake can change a word. For example, the word Bonjour needs all its letters in the correct order: b, o, n, j, o, u, r.

### Greeting words to write
bonjour     hello, good day
salut       hi, bye
bonsoir     good evening
au revoir   goodbye
merci       thank you

In French, greetings written in the middle of a sentence begin with a small letter. At the start of a sentence, use a capital letter. Names always begin with a capital letter: Amina, Kamau, Wanjiru.

### Write clearly
Clear handwriting helps your reader. Leave a small space between words. Make your letters the same size, and sit on the line. Write each word slowly and check it against the list above.

### Try it
Copy the five greeting words twice in your exercise book. Then cover them and write each one from memory. Check your spelling.

## PAGE 2: Rearranging letters to make words
A fun way to practise spelling is to rearrange mixed letters into a word. The letters are jumbled and you must put them in the right order.

### Puzzle 1
The letters are: r j o u o b n
Put them in order to make the greeting: bonjour.

### Puzzle 2
The letters are: c e m r i
The word is: merci.

### Puzzle 3
The letters are: s a t u l
The word is: salut.

### Your turn
Solve these three puzzles.
1. i r o r v e u a  (hint: goodbye, two words: au revoir)
2. s r o i b n o  (hint: good evening)
3. t l a s u  (hint: hi)

The answers are: 1. au revoir. 2. bonsoir. 3. salut.

### Crossword tip
In a crossword, count the boxes first. The word bonjour has 7 letters, so it needs 7 boxes. The word merci has 5 letters, so it needs 5 boxes. If the number of boxes does not match, the word is not right.

## PAGE 3: Filling gaps in a dialogue
In a gap-fill dialogue, some words are missing. You choose the right word from a list and write it in the gap.

### Word bank
bonjour   merci   je   Comment   au revoir   ça va

### Dialogue with gaps
Amina: ______ ! ______ tu t'appelles ?
Otieno: ______ m'appelle Otieno. Et toi ?
Amina: Je m'appelle Amina. ______ ?
Otieno: Oui, ça va bien, ______ !
Amina: ______ , Otieno !

### Answers
1. Bonjour   2. Comment   3. Je   4. Ça va   5. merci   6. Au revoir

Read the completed dialogue aloud with a friend. Does each sentence make sense? If you are not sure about a gap, read the sentence before and after it. The other lines often give a clue.

### Tip
The word je is short and often missed. In Je m'appelle, the little m' is a short form of me. Word for word, the phrase means I call myself, and we say it as My name is. Always write the apostrophe after the m.

## PAGE 4: Writing short greetings and labels
Now you write your own greetings. A short greeting to a friend has three parts: the greeting word, the name, and a friendly ending.

### Model
Bonjour, Amina !
Ça va ?
Au revoir !

Note the mark ! at the end of a greeting. It is called an exclamation mark. A French question mark is written after a space: Ça va ?

### Labels
Labels are short names written on a card or a desk. In group work, write a label with your friend's name on it as they dictate. For example, your friend says the letters ah, em, ee, en, ah. You write A M I N A. Then you write Amina in a neat name label.

### Writing a card
Write a short card to a friend.
Salut, Kamau !
Je m'appelle Njeri.
Au revoir !

### Try it
Write your own three-line card. Use one greeting, one sentence with je m'appelle, and one goodbye.

## PAGE 5: Dictation, check and summary
Dictation means writing what you hear. Your teacher says a word, and you write it. Listen to the sounds and think about the letters. Say the word quietly to yourself before writing.

### Dictation practice
Ask a friend to read these to you: bonjour, salut, bonsoir, merci, au revoir. Write each word. Then check your spelling against the list.

### Checking your writing
1. Did I start names and sentences with capital letters?
2. Did I write every letter in each word?
3. Did I put a space between words?
4. Did I add ! or ? where needed?
5. Is my handwriting easy to read?

### Common mistakes
• Writing bonjour as bonjor or bonjoure. It ends in our.
• Writing merci as mersi. The c comes before i, and it says s.
• Forgetting to join au and revoir with a space between them.

### Summary
You can now write greeting words, solve letter puzzles, fill gaps in a dialogue, write a short card and take a dictation. Good writing is clear, correct and neat.

## QUIZ
1. Which is the correct spelling of hello in French?
   A) bonjor
   B) bonjour
   C) bonjoure
   D) bonjur
   Answer: B
   Why: Bonjour is spelled b-o-n-j-o-u-r.
2. Which word means thank you?
   A) merci
   B) salut
   C) bonsoir
   D) revoir
   Answer: A
   Why: Merci is thank you.
3. Rearrange the letters s a t u l to make a French word.
   A) lutas
   B) sulat
   C) salut
   D) tulas
   Answer: C
   Why: The letters make salut, which means hi or bye.
4. Which mark ends a greeting such as Bonjour, Amina?
   A) !
   B) ,
   C) :
   D) ;
   Answer: A
   Why: An exclamation mark shows a friendly greeting.
5. How many letters are in the word bonsoir?
   A) 5
   B) 6
   C) 7
   D) 8
   Answer: C
   Why: b-o-n-s-o-i-r has seven letters.
6. Fill the gap: ______ tu t'appelles ? (2 marks)
   Answer: Comment
   Why: 1 mark for the correct word and 1 mark for the capital letter at the start.
7. Write a short card of three lines to a friend called Kamau. (3 marks)
   Answer: Salut, Kamau ! Je m'appelle Njeri. Au revoir !
   Why: 1 mark for the greeting with the name, 1 mark for je m'appelle and 1 mark for a goodbye.
8. Explain why you should check your writing after you finish. (2 marks)
   Answer: To find and correct spelling, capital letter or punctuation mistakes so the reader understands.
   Why: 1 mark for finding mistakes and 1 mark for helping the reader.
9. Write the dialogue line that answers Ça va ? in a positive way. (2 marks)
   Answer: Oui, ça va bien, merci.
   Why: 1 mark for the answer and 1 mark for merci.
