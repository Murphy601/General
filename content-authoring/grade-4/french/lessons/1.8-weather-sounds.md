## PAGE 1: Weather words
Weather is what happens in the sky and the air around us: sun, wind, rain and clouds. In French, the weather is le temps (luh TOM). Listen to the weather words and repeat them carefully.

le soleil       the sun           (luh so-LAY)
le vent         the wind          (luh VON)
le nuage        the cloud         (luh nü-AZH)
la pluie        the rain          (la PLWEE)
l'orage         the storm         (lo-RAZH)
la neige        the snow          (la NEHZH)

### Notice
Many weather words have the zh sound: nuage, orage, neige. This is the soft sound you learned in bonjour and je. The letters ei in neige say eh. The letters ui in pluie say wee.

### Try it
Look out of the window. Say the French word for what you see in the sky. Ask a partner to say the word for what they see.

## PAGE 2: What is the weather like?
To ask about the weather, French speakers ask Quel temps fait-il ? It means What is the weather like? (kel tom feh-TEEL). The answer often begins with Il fait ... or Il y a ...

Beau is said boh, chaud is said shoh, froid is said frwa, and pleut is said pluh. Use Il fait for the general weather, such as beau, chaud and froid. Use Il y a for things you can see or feel in the air, such as du vent, du soleil and des nuages. Use Il pleut and Il neige for rain and snow. Practise each pattern with a different weather word and say it aloud two times.

## PAGE 3: Friendly and harsh weather
Some weather is pleasant. Some weather can be dangerous. Learning the words helps you stay safe.

### Friendly weather
Il fait beau.             It is nice weather.
Il y a du soleil.         It is sunny.
Il fait chaud.            It is hot.

### Harsh weather
Il y a un orage.          There is a storm.
Il y a du vent fort.      There is strong wind.
Il pleut beaucoup.        It is raining a lot.
Il neige.                 It is snowing.
Il gèle.                  It is freezing.

Fort means strong (for). Beaucoup means a lot. Gèle is said zhel.

### Staying safe
In a storm, stay inside a strong building. Do not stand under a tall tree or near water. Heavy rain can cause floods. In French we call a flood une inondation (ün ee-non-da-SYON). Listen to weather reports and follow adults' advice.

### Try it
Sort these into friendly and harsh: beau, orage, soleil, neige.

## PAGE 4: Poems and tongue twisters
Poems help you practise sounds. Say this short poem about weather. Say each line in one breath.

Le soleil, le vent,
la pluie, les nuages.
Il y a un orage,
et puis, le beau temps.

Et puis means and then. Le beau temps means the nice weather.

### The zh sound in a list
nuage, orage, neige, déluge, gèle
Say each word slowly. The zh sound comes at the end of nuage, orage, neige and déluge, and at the start of gèle, where g before è says zh. Déluge means a heavy flood of water (day-LÜZH).

### Chunking
Chunking means saying groups of words together, not one at a time. Say Le soleil // le vent // la pluie // les nuages. Take a tiny pause at each //. Keep the same speed.

### Tongue twister game
Say Le vent, le vent, le vent fort three times, faster each time. This trains your mouth to say the nasal sound von. Make it fun and keep the sounds clear.

## PAGE 5: Summary and games
Games help you learn the words. Try one of these.

### Weather report game
Stand in front of the class and be a weather reporter. Say: Bonjour ! Aujourd'hui, il fait beau. Il y a du soleil. Au revoir ! Look at the class and speak clearly.

### Common mistakes
• Saying the g in nuage like g in go. It is zh.
• Not making the nasal sound in vent.
• Saying pluie as ploo-ee. It is plwee.
• Forgetting to say Il fait ... for hot, cold and nice weather.

### Learning to learn
To tell sounds apart, listen for the small difference: neige (nehzh) and nuage (nü-AZH). Say each pair slowly and ask a friend which one they heard.

### Summary
You can name weather features, ask Quel temps fait-il ?, answer with Il fait ... or Il y a ..., sort friendly and harsh weather, and say weather poems with the zh sound. Stay safe in harsh weather.

## QUIZ
1. What does la pluie mean?
   A) the sun
   B) the rain
   C) the wind
   D) the snow
   Answer: B
   Why: La pluie is the rain.
2. What does Il fait chaud mean?
   A) It is cold.
   B) It is hot.
   C) It is raining.
   D) It is cloudy.
   Answer: B
   Why: Chaud means hot.
3. Which word contains the zh sound?
   A) soleil
   B) vent
   C) nuage
   D) pluie
   Answer: C
   Why: Nuage ends in the zh sound, spelled ge.
4. What does Quel temps fait-il ? ask?
   A) What time is it?
   B) What day is it?
   C) What is the weather like?
   D) What is your name?
   Answer: C
   Why: Le temps means the weather here.
5. Which is harsh weather?
   A) Il fait beau.
   B) Il y a du soleil.
   C) Il y a un orage.
   D) Il fait chaud.
   Answer: C
   Why: A storm can be dangerous.
6. Write in French: It is raining. (2 marks)
   Answer: Il pleut.
   Why: 1 mark for il and 1 mark for pleut.
7. Write in French: The weather is nice. It is sunny. (3 marks)
   Answer: Il fait beau. Il y a du soleil.
   Why: 1 mark for each sentence and 1 mark for accuracy.
8. Explain how to stay safe in a storm. (2 marks)
   Answer: Stay inside a strong building and stay away from tall trees and water.
   Why: 1 mark for staying inside and 1 mark for a second safety point.
9. Put these weather words into friendly and harsh groups: le soleil, l'orage, la neige, le beau temps. (3 marks)
   Answer: Friendly: le soleil, le beau temps. Harsh: l'orage, la neige.
   Why: 1 mark for each correct pair and 1 mark for the headings.
