## PAGE 1: Spelling home vocabulary
Copy each word carefully. Notice the accents and the special letters.

la télé         the TV
la porte        the door
la table        the table
le tapis        the rug
le canapé       the sofa
le frigo        the fridge
le lit          the bed
la chaise       the chair
la cuisine      the kitchen
le salon        the living room
la chambre      the bedroom
le jardin       the garden

### Spelling hints
• Télé and canapé have accents aigus.
• Cuisine has ui: c-u-i-s-i-n-e.
• Chambre has mb: c-h-a-m-b-r-e.
• Jardin ends in in, and the n is nasal.
• Tapis ends in is, but the s is silent.

### Try it
Study the words for two minutes. Cover them. Write ten words from memory and check your work.

### Crossword
Make a small crossword with the words lit, table and porte. Write each word across or down so that they share letters. Give it to a friend to complete.

### Working together
In a group, each person writes one word in a crossword. Read the words aloud when you finish. Check that everyone's spelling is correct. Help each other with kind words.

## PAGE 3: Labelling a picture
Draw a simple house or classroom. Write the French label for each room and object.

### Example
A drawing of a bed               le lit
A drawing of a door              la porte
A drawing of a kitchen           la cuisine
A drawing of a sofa              le canapé

### Draw a plan
A plan is a picture of a room seen from above. Draw a plan of your bedroom. Label the bed, the door and the window. Use a ruler for straight lines.

### Neat labels
Write your labels neatly. Use the same size letters. Put a line from the label to the object it names. Check the accents on télé and canapé.

### Try it
Swap plans with a friend. Read each other's labels aloud and check the spelling.

## PAGE 4: Filling gaps and writing instructions
Complete each sentence with a word from the bank.

Word bank: sur, sous, dans, devant, derrière

1. Le livre est ______ la table. (on)
2. Le chat est ______ la chaise. (under)
3. Le frigo est ______ la cuisine. (in)
4. Le tapis est ______ le canapé. (in front of)
5. Le sac est ______ la porte. (behind)

Answers: 1. sur  2. sous  3. dans  4. devant  5. derrière

### Writing instructions
Instructions tell someone where to put things. Use Mets for one friend.
Mets le livre sur la table.
Mets le sac dans la chambre.
Mets le tapis devant le canapé.

Each instruction has three parts: Mets, the thing, and the place.

### Try it
Write three instructions to a friend. Ask your friend to follow them and do the actions.

## PAGE 5: Check, edit and summarise
When you finish writing, check your work carefully. Read each sentence aloud. Does it make sense? Is it spelled correctly? Would a friend understand it?

### Editing checklist
1. Are the words le, la, un and une correct?
2. Are the accents right, in télé and canapé?
3. Does each sentence start with a capital letter and end with a full stop?
4. Are the position words correct?
5. Is my handwriting clear?

### Common mistakes
• Writing la tapis. Tapis is masculine: le tapis.
• Writing canape and tele without accents.
• Writing sur la table le livre. Say the thing first: Le livre est sur la table.
• Forgetting the full stop.

### A final task
Write four sentences about your bedroom. Say where the bed, the door, the window and a book are. Then read them to a partner.

### Summary
You can spell words for things in the home, unscramble letters, label a plan, complete sentences with position words, and write instructions with Mets. Good writing is careful and clear.

## QUIZ
1. Which spelling is correct?
   A) canape
   B) canapé
   C) cannapé
   D) canappé
   Answer: B
   Why: Canapé has one n, one p and an accent aigu.
2. Which sentence is correct?
   A) La tapis est sous la table.
   B) Le tapis est sous la table.
   C) Le tapis sous est la table.
   D) Sous le tapis est table.
   Answer: B
   Why: Tapis is masculine and the order is thing, est, position word, place.
3. Which word fits: Le frigo est ______ la cuisine.
   A) sur
   B) dans
   C) sous
   D) entre
   Answer: B
   Why: Dans means in.
4. What is a plan?
   A) a picture of a room seen from above
   B) a kind of chair
   C) a sentence
   D) a song
   Answer: A
   Why: A plan shows the room from above.
5. Which instruction means Put the bag in the bedroom?
   A) Mets le sac dans la chambre.
   B) Mets le sac sur la chambre.
   C) Mets la chambre dans le sac.
   D) Le sac est dans la chambre.
   Answer: A
   Why: Mets is put, and dans la chambre is in the bedroom.
6. Write in French: The TV is in the living room. (3 marks)
   Answer: La télé est dans le salon.
   Why: 1 mark for la télé, 1 mark for est and 1 mark for dans le salon.
7. Unscramble the letters: a e l t b. (2 marks)
   Answer: table
   Why: 1 mark for the correct letters and 1 mark for a correct spelling.
8. Write two instructions in French to tell a friend where to put a book and a rug. (4 marks)
   Answer: Mets le livre sur la table. Mets le tapis devant le canapé.
   Why: 2 marks for each correct instruction.
9. Explain why you should read your writing aloud before you finish. (2 marks)
   Answer: It helps you find spelling and sentence mistakes so that the writing is clear.
   Why: 1 mark for finding mistakes and 1 mark for clarity.
