## PAGE 1: Rooms in a house
Listening for gist means listening to understand the main idea. You do not need every word. You listen for key words, and they tell you what the message is about. In this lesson you will listen to a description of a house and find the names of the rooms.

### Rooms
la maison = the house         (la may-ZON)
la chambre = the bedroom      (la SHOM-bruh)
le salon = the living room    (luh sa-LON)
la cuisine = the kitchen      (la kwee-ZEEN)
la salle de bain = the bathroom  (la sal duh BAN)

### Learn the sounds
The word maison has three sounds: may, zon. The s between vowels is said z. The word cuisine has the sound kwee at the beginning, and the s in the middle is z again.

### Try it
Listen as your teacher says each room. Repeat the word after your teacher. Then point to the room in a picture of a house. Say the word with the article le or la.

## PAGE 2: Objects in the house
Inside each room there are many objects. The design of the house helps you to guess where each thing belongs.

le lit = the bed              (luh LEE)
la table = the table          (la TA-bluh)
la chaise = the chair         (la SHEZ)
le canapé = the sofa          (luh ka-na-PAY)
la télé = the TV              (la tay-LAY)
le frigo = the fridge         (luh free-GO)
la porte = the door           (la PORT)
la fenêtre = the window       (la fuh-NEH-truh)

## PAGE 3: Listening for key words
When you listen to a text, do not panic if you do not understand every word. Listen for words you know. Those key words tell you the topic.

### A short description to listen to
Your teacher reads: Voici ma maison. Il y a une cuisine, un salon et deux chambres. Dans le salon, il y a un canapé et une télé.

Key words: maison, cuisine, salon, chambres, canapé, télé.

Il y a means There is or There are. Deux means two. Dans means in.

### Listen and answer
1. What is the text about? A house.
2. How many bedrooms are there? Two.
3. What is in the living room? A sofa and a TV.

### Tips
• Listen first for the topic.
• Listen a second time for details like numbers.
• Do not translate every word. Look for words you know.

### Try it
Listen to a friend describe a room in French. Write down the key words you hear, not whole sentences.

## PAGE 4: Following instructions
Listening is also about doing. You listen to an instruction and put things in the right place. The word dans means in. We use it for something inside a room or a box.

### Instruction words
Mets = Put (to one friend)     (MEH)
le livre = the book            (luh LEEVR)
sur = on                       (sür)
dans = in                      (don)

### Examples
Mets le livre sur la table. = Put the book on the table.
Mets le livre dans la chambre. = Put the book in the bedroom.

Do the actions when you hear each instruction. You may use real objects in the classroom.

### Why tidiness matters
Putting things in the right place keeps a house safe. A bag left on the floor can make someone trip and fall. A clean, ordered house is also easy to live in. Tell a friend one thing you can tidy at home to keep everyone safe.

### Try it
Play a game. One learner says an instruction in French. Others do the action. Use only the words above.

## PAGE 5: Summary, games and practice
Let us gather what you learned. You can name rooms, some household items and follow simple instructions. You also know how to listen for the gist.

### Recap of vocabulary
• la maison, la chambre, le salon, la cuisine, la salle de bain
• le lit, la table, la chaise, le canapé, la télé, le frigo, la porte, la fenêtre

### Hide and seek game
Hide a small object in a room. Say in French where it is: Le livre est sur la table. Your friends listen and find it. Change roles after each round.

### Common mistakes
• Saying the s in maison like s. It is said z.
• Forgetting le or la before a noun.
• Trying to translate every word instead of listening for key words.

### Song idea
Sing the rooms with any tune: la chambre, le salon, la cuisine, la salle de bain. Clap for each syllable and keep a steady beat.

### Reflect
Why is it important to pay attention while listening? Because you may miss the main idea, an instruction or a safety warning. Good listeners look at the speaker and stay quiet.

## QUIZ
1. What does la cuisine mean?
   A) the bedroom
   B) the kitchen
   C) the living room
   D) the door
   Answer: B
   Why: La cuisine is the kitchen.
2. Which word means the sofa?
   A) la table
   B) le lit
   C) le canapé
   D) la fenêtre
   Answer: C
   Why: Le canapé is a sofa.
3. What does Mets le livre sur la table mean?
   A) Take the book from the table
   B) Put the book on the table
   C) Read the book in the room
   D) Where is the table?
   Answer: B
   Why: Mets means put, and sur la table means on the table.
4. How is the s in maison said?
   A) s
   B) sh
   C) z
   D) ks
   Answer: C
   Why: An s between two vowels is said z, so maison is may-ZON.
5. Which sentence has the key words maison, cuisine and salon?
   A) Voici ma maison. Il y a une cuisine et un salon.
   B) Bonjour, je m'appelle Amina.
   C) Au revoir, mes amis.
   D) C'est mon père.
   Answer: A
   Why: It lists rooms of a house.
6. Write the French for the bedroom and the bathroom. (2 marks)
   Answer: la chambre; la salle de bain
   Why: 1 mark for each correct phrase.
7. Write in French: Put the book in the bedroom. (3 marks)
   Answer: Mets le livre dans la chambre.
   Why: 1 mark for mets, 1 mark for le livre and 1 mark for dans la chambre.
8. Give two tips for listening for the gist. (2 marks)
   Answer: Listen for words you know and listen for the main topic first. Do not translate every word.
   Why: 1 mark for each valid tip.
9. Explain why it is good to put things in their right places at home. (2 marks)
   Answer: It keeps the house tidy and safe, and stops people tripping or things breaking.
   Why: 1 mark for tidy and 1 mark for safety.
