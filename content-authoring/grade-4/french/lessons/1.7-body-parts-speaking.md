## PAGE 1: Parts of the body
The body is le corps (luh KOR). Listen to your teacher say each part and repeat it clearly. Copy the sounds and try to keep the same speed.

la tête          the head         (la TET)
les cheveux      the hair         (lay shuh-VUH)
les yeux         the eyes         (lay ZYUH)
le nez           the nose         (luh NAY)
la bouche        the mouth        (la BOOSH)
l'oreille        the ear          (lo-RAY)
la main          the hand         (la MAN)
le bras          the arm          (luh BRA)
la jambe         the leg          (la ZHOMB)
le pied          the foot         (luh PYAY)
le doigt         the finger       (luh DWA)

### Notice
Les yeux means eyes. One eye is un œil (uhn UH-y), but you will use yeux more often. Les cheveux means the hair and is always plural in French. The letter ch in bouche says sh, and the j in jambe says zh.

### Try it
Touch each part of your body as you say the word. Say the word first, then touch.

Le ventre means the stomach or belly (luh VON-truh).

### More body words
le cou         the neck         (luh KOO)
le dos         the back         (luh DOH)
le genou       the knee         (luh zhuh-NOO)
l'épaule       the shoulder     (lay-POHL)
la dent        the tooth        (la DON)

Look at the picture again and find where each of these would be. The neck is between the head and the body. The knee is in the middle of the leg. Say each word, point to that part of your own body, and ask your partner to check.

## PAGE 3: I have ... (J'ai ...)
To say what you have, use J'ai (zhay). Then add a number and a body part.

J'ai deux mains.         I have two hands.
J'ai deux yeux.          I have two eyes.
J'ai un nez.             I have a nose.
J'ai une bouche.         I have a mouth.
J'ai dix doigts.         I have ten fingers.
J'ai deux pieds.         I have two feet.

### Numbers to know
un = 1     deux = 2     trois = 3     quatre = 4     cinq = 5
six = 6    sept = 7     huit = 8      neuf = 9       dix = 10

### Liaison
In J'ai deux yeux, the x of deux is said z before yeux: duh-ZYUH. In J'ai dix ans, the x of dix is said z before ans. This link is called liaison. It makes the words flow together.

### Try it
Say J'ai ... about five parts of your body. Ask a friend to show the part as they listen.

## PAGE 4: Describing people
We can describe how people look. We use il est for he is and elle est for she is.

Il est grand.           He is tall.
Elle est petite.        She is short.
Il a les cheveux courts.  He has short hair.
Elle a les cheveux longs. She has long hair.
Elle a les cheveux noirs. She has black hair.

Grand means tall. Petit or petite means small or short. Court means short and long means long. Noirs means black. For a girl, add e to grand and petit: elle est grande, elle est petite. The e is silent, but the final t is now said.

### Say it
grand   (gron)     grande   (grond)
petit   (puh-TEE)  petite   (puh-TEET)

### Respect
People look different. Some are tall and some are short. Some have long hair, some have short hair. We speak about differences with respect and kindness, and we never make fun of how a person looks.

### Try it
Describe a friend in two short sentences without saying the name. Your partner guesses who it is.

## PAGE 5: Songs, games and summary
Songs help you remember body words. Sing this in any tune and touch each part as you sing: la tête, les yeux, le nez, la bouche, les mains, les pieds.

### Game: Touche !
Your teacher says Touche ton nez ! You touch your nose. Touche ta tête ! Touche ta main ! Touche ton pied ! Ton is used for masculine words and ta for feminine words. Touche means touch.

### Common mistakes
• Saying les cheveux with a strong s. The s is silent.
• Saying jambe with English j. It is zh.
• Forgetting the liaison in J'ai deux yeux.
• Saying grande without the d sound.

### Listening and speaking tips
Say each word clearly and not too fast. Repeat after a good model. Practise the words in short groups of five. Say the sentence with an even rhythm and let your voice fall at the end.

### Summary
You can name parts of the body, say J'ai and a number, describe how a person looks, follow Touche ..., and sing about the body. Practise a little each day.

## QUIZ
1. What does la tête mean?
   A) the hand
   B) the head
   C) the foot
   D) the nose
   Answer: B
   Why: La tête is the head.
2. What does les yeux mean?
   A) the ears
   B) the eyes
   C) the arms
   D) the legs
   Answer: B
   Why: Les yeux means the eyes.
3. What does J'ai deux mains mean?
   A) I have two hands.
   B) I have two feet.
   C) I have two friends.
   D) I have two books.
   Answer: A
   Why: J'ai means I have, deux is two and mains is hands.
4. How do you say the letter j in jambe?
   A) j as in jump
   B) zh
   C) y
   D) g as in go
   Answer: B
   Why: In French the j says zh.
5. What does Elle est petite mean?
   A) She is tall.
   B) She is short.
   C) She has black hair.
   D) She has two eyes.
   Answer: B
   Why: Petite means small or short.
6. Write in French: I have ten fingers. (2 marks)
   Answer: J'ai dix doigts.
   Why: 1 mark for j'ai and 1 mark for dix doigts.
7. Write in French: She has long hair. (3 marks)
   Answer: Elle a les cheveux longs.
   Why: 1 mark for elle a, 1 mark for les cheveux and 1 mark for longs.
8. Explain what liaison is, with one example. (2 marks)
   Answer: Liaison is when a silent final letter is said before a vowel, for example deux yeux, said duh-zyuh.
   Why: 1 mark for the explanation and 1 mark for a correct example.
9. Write two sentences in French about your body, using J'ai and a number. (3 marks)
   Answer: J'ai deux mains. J'ai un nez.
   Why: 1 mark for each correct sentence and 1 mark for correct numbers.
