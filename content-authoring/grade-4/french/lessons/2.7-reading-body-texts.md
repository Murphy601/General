## PAGE 1: Reading body words from pictures
When you read a new word, look at the picture first. The picture helps you understand the word before you say it. Then say the word aloud. In this lesson you will read words and sentences about the body.

### Read and match
Match each French word to its picture in your mind:
la tête       the head
les yeux      the eyes
le nez        the nose
la bouche     the mouth
les oreilles  the ears
la main       the hand
le pied       the foot

### Reading tips
The letters eu say uh, with rounded lips, as in yeux and cheveux. The letters ou say oo, as in bouche. The letters ei and ai often say eh or ay, as in oreille. The ll in oreille sounds like y in the English word yes: o-RAY.

Plural words add an s in writing, so une oreille becomes des oreilles. But this s is silent.

### Try it
Read the seven words aloud twice. Then close your book and try to say them from memory as a partner points to their own head, eyes, nose, mouth, ears, hand and foot.

## PAGE 2: Short sentences about the body
Read these sentences slowly. Read each one two times.

J'ai une tête.          I have a head.
J'ai deux yeux.         I have two eyes.
J'ai un nez.            I have a nose.
J'ai une bouche.        I have a mouth.
J'ai deux oreilles.     I have two ears.
J'ai deux mains.        I have two hands.
J'ai dix doigts.        I have ten fingers.

### Say it with rhythm
Read the sentences in groups. Read the first three, then take a breath. Read the next four. This is called phrasing.

### Repetition
Repeating helps you read fluently. Read the sentences until you no longer stumble. Let your partner listen and clap when you read a line smoothly.

### Try it
Read the seven sentences to a partner. Then swap so that you listen to them read.

## PAGE 3: Reading a description
Read this short text about a girl.

Voici Amina. Elle a dix ans. Elle est grande. Elle a les cheveux noirs et longs. Elle a deux grands yeux. Elle a un petit nez et une belle bouche. Elle aime chanter.

### Words to know
belle = beautiful, pretty      (bel)
grands yeux = big eyes
aime chanter = likes to sing
et = and

### Questions
1. Comment s'appelle la fille ? Amina.
2. Quel âge a Amina ? Dix ans.
3. Est-elle grande ou petite ? Elle est grande.
4. De quelle couleur sont ses cheveux ? Noirs.

De quelle couleur sont ses cheveux ? means What colour is her hair? The answer is Ses cheveux sont noirs.

### Reading tip
Look at the questions first. Then read the text and hunt for the answers. This helps you focus.

### Try it
Read the text to a partner. Then ask them one question in French and listen to the answer.

## PAGE 4: Finding information on the internet
You can find texts about the body on a safe website. Ask a teacher or an adult to help you. Always turn on Safe Search so that you only see pages that are suitable for children.

### Steps to search safely
1. Ask an adult before you go online.
2. Open a search page and turn Safe Search on.
3. Type words such as le corps humain for the human body.
4. Only open pages that a teacher or adult says are safe.
5. Close the page when you finish.

### Reading online texts
Read a short text about the human body aloud. Pick out the words you know, such as tête, main and pied. Note down three new words and ask your teacher what they mean.

### Sing together
Sing a simple song about parts of the body together. Read the words on the board as you sing. Touch each part as you name it. Songs make reading fun and help you remember words.

### Try it
With your teacher's help, look at one picture of the human body with French labels. Read three labels aloud.

## PAGE 5: Summary and practice
Reading well means understanding what you read. Use pictures, key words and questions to help you.

### Common mistakes
• Reading the s at the end of yeux, cheveux or mains. It is silent.
• Reading ll in oreille as in bell. It sounds like y.
• Reading without a pause after each sentence.
• Forgetting to look at the picture for help.

### Read and answer
Read: J'ai deux mains et dix doigts. Combien de mains ai-je ? Deux. Combien de doigts ? Dix. Combien means how many (kom-BYAN).

### Fluency practice
Read the description of Amina again at a smooth pace. Try not to stop at each word. If you get stuck, go back to the start of the sentence.

### Summary
You can read body words with the correct sounds, read sentences with J'ai, read a description of a person, answer questions about a text, and search for texts safely online. Keep reading a little each day and your fluency will grow.

## QUIZ
1. What does la bouche mean?
   A) the nose
   B) the mouth
   C) the hand
   D) the head
   Answer: B
   Why: La bouche is the mouth.
2. How do you say eu in yeux?
   A) ee
   B) uh, with rounded lips
   C) oo
   D) oh
   Answer: B
   Why: The letters eu say uh, with rounded lips.
3. What does Elle a les cheveux noirs mean?
   A) She has black hair.
   B) She has long hair.
   C) She has two eyes.
   D) She is tall.
   Answer: A
   Why: Les cheveux noirs is black hair.
4. What does combien mean?
   A) how many
   B) what colour
   C) where
   D) when
   Answer: A
   Why: Combien means how many.
5. Why should you turn on Safe Search?
   A) To make the page load faster
   B) To see only pages that are suitable for children
   C) To change the language
   D) To print the page
   Answer: B
   Why: Safe Search hides unsuitable pages.
6. Translate into English: J'ai deux oreilles. (2 marks)
   Answer: I have two ears.
   Why: 1 mark for I have and 1 mark for two ears.
7. Read: Elle est grande. Elle a les cheveux longs. Describe the girl in English. (2 marks)
   Answer: She is tall and has long hair.
   Why: 1 mark for tall and 1 mark for long hair.
8. List three steps you take to search safely online. (3 marks)
   Answer: Ask an adult; turn Safe Search on; open only pages a teacher or adult says are safe.
   Why: 1 mark for each step.
9. Write two sentences in French about a friend's face. (3 marks)
   Answer: Il a deux yeux. Il a un nez.
   Why: 1 mark for each correct sentence and 1 mark for correct numbers.
