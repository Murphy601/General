## PAGE 1: Why do French words have accents?
Accents are small marks written above letters. They help you know how to say a letter. In French, an accent is part of the spelling. If you leave it out, the word is spelled wrongly.

Look at these family words: père, mère, frère, bébé. Each one has an accent. Without the accent, a reader would not know how to say the e.

### Reading family words
le père = the father        (luh PAIR)
la mère = the mother        (la MAIR)
le frère = the brother      (luh FRAIR)
la sœur = the sister        (la SUHR)
le bébé = the baby          (luh bay-BAY)

### Try it
Read the five words above aloud three times. First, follow your teacher. Then read with a partner. Last, read alone. Point to the letters that have accents.

## PAGE 2: The three accents on e, and the cedilla
French has several accents. The most common are these.

• accent aigu (acute):        é    said ay, as in bébé
• accent grave:               è    said eh, as in père
• accent circonflexe:         ê    said eh, as in fête (party)
• cédille:                    ç    said s, as in ça and garçon

An accent aigu goes up from left to right. An accent grave goes down from left to right. The circumflex looks like a little roof. The cedilla is a little tail under the letter c. It makes the c say s before a, o or u.

## PAGE 3: Reading short phrases
Now put the words into short phrases. Read them aloud, watching the accents and the silent letters.

C'est mon père.       (say mon PAIR)
C'est ma mère.        (say ma MAIR)
C'est mon frère.      (say mon FRAIR)
C'est ma sœur.        (say ma SUHR)
C'est le bébé.        (say luh bay-BAY)
Ça va ?               (sa va)

Ça va ? is a friendly question. It means How are you? or How is it going? The word ça begins with a ç. Say it as sa. The answer can be Ça va bien, or Ça va, merci.

### Voice
When you read aloud, use a voice that everyone in the room can hear. This is called voice projection. Stand straight and speak from your chest, not too fast and not too loud.

### Pace
Read one phrase at a time. Take a small breath after each phrase. Then move to the next. This is called phrasing.

### Try it
Read all six phrases to a partner. Ask them to tell you which accent word was clear and which needs more practice.

## PAGE 4: Reading a text about a family
Read this short text aloud with a partner. Each person reads one sentence at a time.

Je m'appelle Amina. J'ai un frère et une sœur. Mon frère s'appelle Kamau. Ma sœur s'appelle Njeri. Voici mon père et ma mère. Et voici le bébé. C'est ma famille.

### Words to understand
j'ai = I have
un frère = a brother
une sœur = a sister
s'appelle = is called
et = and

### Questions to answer
1. Comment s'appelle la fille ? Answer: Elle s'appelle Amina.
2. Qui est Kamau ? Answer: C'est le frère d'Amina.
3. Qui est Njeri ? Answer: C'est la sœur d'Amina.

Notice that the answers use Elle s'appelle, which means She is called. For a boy we say Il s'appelle, meaning He is called.

### Tip
If you meet a word you do not know, read the sentence again and look at the pictures. The other words often help you guess.

## PAGE 5: Summary and practice
You have learned how to read family words, phrases and short texts aloud with correct accents. Remember that accents change how you say a letter, so read them carefully.

### Quick guide to accents
é says ay      père has è, not é
è says eh      mère, frère
ê says eh      fête
ç says s       ça, garçon

### Common mistakes
• Reading è like é. Père is PAIR, not pay-ray.
• Leaving out the cedilla and reading ça as ka.
• Reading the final letters that are silent, such as the s in les.
• Reading too quickly and losing the meaning.

### Read-aloud challenge
Read the text on page 4 again, this time with expression. Make your voice happy for the greeting, and gentle for the baby. Ask a friend to listen for your accents.

### Summary
You can name the accents aigu, grave and circonflexe, and the cedilla. You can read the words père, mère, frère, sœur, bébé and ça, and you can read short family sentences with confidence.

## QUIZ
1. Which accent is used in the word père?
   A) aigu
   B) grave
   C) circonflexe
   D) cédille
   Answer: B
   Why: The accent on è in père goes down from left to right, so it is an accent grave.
2. Which letter has a cedilla?
   A) é
   B) è
   C) ç
   D) ê
   Answer: C
   Why: The cedilla is the small tail under the c.
3. How do you say ça?
   A) ka
   B) sa
   C) cha
   D) za
   Answer: B
   Why: The cedilla makes the letter c say s.
4. Which word has an accent aigu?
   A) mère
   B) bébé
   C) frère
   D) fête
   Answer: B
   Why: Bébé has two accents aigus, one on each é.
5. What does Voici mon père mean?
   A) Who is my father?
   B) Here is my father
   C) My father is tall
   D) Goodbye, father
   Answer: B
   Why: Voici means here is.
6. Write the French word for sister and say which special letter it has. (2 marks)
   Answer: sœur; it has the joined letters œ.
   Why: 1 mark for the word and 1 mark for the special letter.
7. Read the sentence J'ai un frère. Write what it means in English. (2 marks)
   Answer: I have a brother.
   Why: 1 mark for I have and 1 mark for a brother.
8. Explain why accents are important in French writing. (3 marks)
   Answer: Accents are part of the spelling. They show how to say the letter. Leaving them out makes the word wrong.
   Why: 1 mark for each point.
9. Copy these words with the correct accents: pere, mere, bebe. (3 marks)
   Answer: père, mère, bébé
   Why: 1 mark for each correctly accented word.
