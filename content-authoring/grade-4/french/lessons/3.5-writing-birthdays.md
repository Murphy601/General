## PAGE 1: Writing the months
To write a date in French, you must know how to spell the months. Copy them in order and check every letter.

janvier, février, mars, avril, mai, juin,
juillet, août, septembre, octobre, novembre, décembre

### Watch the accents
février has an accent aigu on the first é. août has an accent circonflexe on the û. décembre has an accent aigu on the first é.

### Small letters
In French, months are written with small letters. Write mai, not Mai, unless it begins a sentence. This is the same rule as for the days of the week.

### Spelling hints
• septembre, octobre, novembre and décembre all end in bre.
• juillet is spelled j-u-i-l-l-e-t. It has two l letters.
• avril has no e in the middle. It is a-v-r-i-l.

### Try it
Cover the list. Write the twelve months from memory. Check each one and correct mistakes with a different colour pen.

## PAGE 2: Writing my date of birth
To write a date in French, use this pattern: le + number + month. The number is usually written with digits.

le 5 mars
le 12 mai
le 30 juin
le 1er janvier

Le premier janvier is written le 1er janvier. The letters er after the 1 stand for premier.

### A sentence about your birthday
Mon anniversaire, c'est le 12 mai.
Mon anniversaire, c'est le 30 juin.

### Writing the date with words
Sometimes we write the numbers with words: le douze mai, le trente juin. Learn these number words: un, deux, trois, quatre, cinq, six, sept, huit, neuf, dix, onze, douze, treize, quatorze, quinze, seize, dix-sept, dix-huit, dix-neuf, vingt.

### Try it
Write your own birthday as a sentence. Then write the birthday of a friend. Use Mon anniversaire, c'est le ... and Son anniversaire, c'est le ... Son anniversaire means his or her birthday.

## PAGE 3: Birthday wishes
Birthday wishes are short messages. Here are some useful phrases.

Bon anniversaire !               Happy birthday!
Joyeux anniversaire !            Happy birthday!
Meilleurs vœux !                 Best wishes!
Félicitations !                  Congratulations!

Meilleurs vœux is said may-YUR vuh. Vœux has the joined letters œ, like in sœur.

### A birthday card
Cher Kamau,
Bon anniversaire !
Tu as dix ans aujourd'hui.
Voici un cadeau pour toi.
Ton amie, Amina

Cher means dear for a boy, and Chère means dear for a girl. Ton amie means your friend, when you are writing to a boy and you are a girl. Pour toi means for you.

### How to end a card
End with your name. You can write Ton ami before a boy's name, or Ton amie before a girl's name. It means your friend.

### Try it
Write a card to a friend. Start with Cher or Chère, wish them a happy birthday, and end with your name.

## PAGE 4: Copying and composing a party text
Copying is a good way to practise spelling. Read this text and copy it carefully, then write your own version.

Aujourd'hui, c'est mon anniversaire. J'ai dix ans. Il y a un gâteau, des ballons et des cadeaux. Mes amis chantent. Je danse. C'est une belle fête !

Une belle fête means a lovely party. Chantent is the form for they sing.

### Write a dialogue
A dialogue is a conversation. Write a short birthday dialogue in your exercise book.
Amina: Bonjour, Kamau !
Kamau: Salut, Amina !
Amina: Bon anniversaire ! Tu as quel âge ?
Kamau: J'ai dix ans. Merci !

### Try it
Write your own three-line dialogue. Use bon anniversaire, an age and merci. Swap with a friend and read each other's dialogues aloud.

## PAGE 5: Checking and summary
Good handwriting shows care for the reader. Check that your letters are clear and the same size, and that each accent is in the right place.

### Checklist
1. Are the months spelled correctly with small letters?
2. Are the accents on février, août and décembre?
3. Is there a capital letter at the start of each sentence?
4. Do I have the pattern le + number + month?
5. Is my writing neat?

### Common mistakes
• Writing Mai, Juin with capital letters in the middle of a sentence.
• Forgetting the accent on février and décembre.
• Writing the date as mai le 12. The order is le 12 mai.
• Writing septembre as septempre.

### Summary
You can now write the twelve months, write your birthday as a date, write birthday wishes and short cards, and compose a short birthday text and dialogue. Practise by writing the birthdays of your family members in French.

## QUIZ
1. Which spelling is correct?
   A) fevrier
   B) février
   C) febrier
   D) fevier
   Answer: B
   Why: Février has an accent aigu on the first é.
2. How is the first of January written?
   A) le un janvier
   B) le 1er janvier
   C) janvier le 1
   D) le 1 Janvier
   Answer: B
   Why: The first day of a month is written 1er, short for premier.
3. Which wish means Happy birthday?
   A) Bon anniversaire !
   B) Bonne nuit !
   C) Au revoir !
   D) Bon appétit !
   Answer: A
   Why: Bon anniversaire is a birthday wish.
4. How do we write months in French?
   A) always with capital letters
   B) with small letters
   C) all in capitals
   D) with an apostrophe
   Answer: B
   Why: French months are written with small letters inside a sentence.
5. What does Pour toi mean?
   A) With you
   B) For you
   C) Like you
   D) From you
   Answer: B
   Why: Pour toi means for you.
6. Write in French: My birthday is on 30 June. (3 marks)
   Answer: Mon anniversaire, c'est le 30 juin.
   Why: 1 mark for mon anniversaire, 1 mark for c'est le 30 and 1 mark for juin with a small j.
7. Write two birthday wishes in French. (2 marks)
   Answer: Bon anniversaire ! Joyeux anniversaire !
   Why: 1 mark for each correct wish.
8. Write a short birthday card to a friend called Kamau. (4 marks)
   Answer: Cher Kamau, Bon anniversaire ! Voici un cadeau pour toi. Ton ami, Brian
   Why: 1 mark for Cher Kamau, 1 mark for the wish, 1 mark for a correct sentence and 1 mark for the ending.
9. Explain why the order in the date le 12 mai matters. (2 marks)
   Answer: In French the day number comes before the month. If you write mai le 12, the date is wrong.
   Why: 1 mark for the correct order and 1 mark for the explanation.
