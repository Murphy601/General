## PAGE 1: Names of foods and drinks
Food and drink are part of every culture. In Kenya we eat ugali, rice, beans and maize. French speakers eat bread, rice and many fruits as well. Listen to your teacher say each word, then repeat exactly what you hear.

### Foods
le pain        bread          (luh PAN)
le riz         rice           (luh REE)
le maïs        maize          (luh ma-EESS)
les haricots   beans          (lay za-ree-KOH)
la banane      banana         (la ba-NAN)
la mangue      mango          (la MONG)
l'orange       the orange     (lo-RONZH)
la tomate      tomato         (la to-MAT)
le poisson     fish           (luh pwa-SON)
le poulet      chicken        (luh poo-LEH)

### Drinks
l'eau (f)      water          (loh)
le lait        milk           (luh LEH)
le jus         juice          (luh ZHÜ)
le thé         tea            (luh TAY)

### Try it
Repeat each word three times. Point to a picture or a real food item as you say the word.

## PAGE 2: What is it?
To ask what something is, French speakers say Qu'est-ce que c'est ? It means What is it? (kess kuh SAY). The answer is C'est un ... or C'est une ... for one thing, and Ce sont des ... for many things.

Un means a for masculine words and une means a for feminine words. Des means some and is used for more than one thing. Words that name many things usually add s in writing, but the s is not said.

### Try it
Point to an object and ask Qu'est-ce que c'est ? Your partner answers with C'est un ... or C'est une ...

## PAGE 3: Un or une?
Every French noun is masculine or feminine. You must learn the gender with the word. Learn it by saying the little word each time: le pain, la banane.

### Masculine words (le, un)
le pain     un pain
le riz      un riz
le poisson  un poisson
le poulet   un poulet
le gâteau   un gâteau
le jus      un jus

### Feminine words (la, une)
la banane   une banane
la mangue   une mangue
la tomate   une tomate
la carotte  une carotte
la pomme    une pomme

### Before a vowel
Le and la become l' before a vowel, as in l'orange and l'eau. But we still say une orange and une eau, so those words are feminine.

### Say it
C'est un gâteau.    (say uhn ga-TOH)
C'est une mangue.   (say ün MONG)
Ce sont des bananes.  (suh son day ba-NAN)

### Try it
Say C'est un ... or C'est une ... for each food on your table.

## PAGE 4: Likes and healthy choices
You can say what you like with J'aime ... and what you do not like with Je n'aime pas ...

J'aime la mangue.        I like the mango.
J'aime le lait.          I like milk.
Je n'aime pas le poisson.  I do not like fish.

### Healthy or unhealthy?
Some foods help our bodies grow and stay strong. Others should be eaten only a little.

C'est bon pour la santé.        It is good for your health.
Ce n'est pas bon pour la santé. It is not good for your health.

Healthy: les fruits, les légumes, l'eau, le lait, le poisson, les haricots.
Less healthy: les bonbons, les chips, les frites, les gâteaux.

### Group work
Choose a food. Say if it is good for health. For example: La banane, c'est bon pour la santé. Les bonbons, ce n'est pas bon pour la santé.

### Compare places
In your area, people may eat maize, beans or sukuma wiki. In another country they may eat baguette or pasta. Compare your foods with those of other places, and respect each other's choices.

## PAGE 5: Games, summary and practice
Games help you practise sounds. Here are three simple games.

### Guessing game
One learner mimes eating a fruit. The others ask: Qu'est-ce que c'est ? The learner answers: C'est une banane.

### Memory chain
Say: Dans mon panier, il y a une mangue. The next learner says: Dans mon panier, il y a une mangue et un gâteau. Continue adding a food each time. Un panier is a basket.

### Common mistakes
• Saying le eau. Use l'eau.
• Saying C'est des bananes. Say Ce sont des bananes for many.
• Saying the s in haricots and bonbons. It is silent.
• Forgetting to make the r sound at the back of the throat in riz and orange.

### Summary
You can name foods and drinks, ask Qu'est-ce que c'est ?, answer with C'est un, C'est une and Ce sont des, say what you like, and say whether food is healthy. Speak clearly and repeat the model pronunciation carefully.

## QUIZ
1. What does le lait mean?
   A) juice
   B) water
   C) milk
   D) tea
   Answer: C
   Why: Le lait is milk.
2. Which word means bread?
   A) le riz
   B) le pain
   C) le poulet
   D) le poisson
   Answer: B
   Why: Le pain is bread.
3. What is the answer to Qu'est-ce que c'est ? for a picture of a banana?
   A) C'est une banane.
   B) C'est un banane.
   C) Ce sont une banane.
   D) C'est des banane.
   Answer: A
   Why: Banane is feminine and one, so we say C'est une banane.
4. What does Je n'aime pas le poisson mean?
   A) I like fish.
   B) I do not like fish.
   C) I eat fish today.
   D) Fish is good.
   Answer: B
   Why: Je n'aime pas means I do not like.
5. Which food is good for your health?
   A) les bonbons
   B) les chips
   C) l'eau
   D) les frites
   Answer: C
   Why: Water is good for your health.
6. Write in French: It is a mango. (2 marks)
   Answer: C'est une mangue.
   Why: 1 mark for c'est and 1 mark for une mangue.
7. Write in French: These are bananas. (3 marks)
   Answer: Ce sont des bananes.
   Why: 1 mark for ce sont, 1 mark for des and 1 mark for bananes.
8. Say and write one food you like and one you do not like in French. (2 marks)
   Answer: J'aime la mangue. Je n'aime pas le poisson.
   Why: 1 mark for each correct sentence.
9. Group these foods into good for health and not so good: l'eau, les bonbons, la banane, les chips. (3 marks)
   Answer: Good: l'eau, la banane. Not so good: les bonbons, les chips.
   Why: 1 mark for each correct pair and 1 mark for correct headings.
