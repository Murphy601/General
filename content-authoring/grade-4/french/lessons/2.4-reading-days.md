## PAGE 1: Reading the days from flashcards
Flashcards are small cards with one word on each. Your teacher shows a card and you read the word aloud. For the days of the week, the cards say lundi, mardi, mercredi, jeudi, vendredi, samedi and dimanche.

### Reading each day
lundi        (luhn-DEE)
mardi        (mar-DEE)
mercredi     (mair-kruh-DEE)
jeudi        (zhuh-DEE)
vendredi     (von-druh-DEE)
samedi       (sam-DEE)
dimanche     (dee-MONSH)

### Reading tips
The letters eu in jeudi make the sound uh, with rounded lips. The letters en and an in vendredi and dimanche are nasal, so say them softly through the nose. The letters ch in dimanche say sh. The final letters of a French word are often silent, so do not say them.

### Try it
Take turns with a partner. One shows the card, the other reads. If a word is wrong, read it again after your partner. Do not laugh at anyone's mistakes. Be patient and kind.

## PAGE 2: Short texts about the week
Read these short sentences aloud. Each one tells you something about a day.

Le lundi, je vais à l'école. On Monday, I go to school.
Le mardi, je lis.           On Tuesday, I read.
Le mercredi, je joue.       On Wednesday, I play.
Le jeudi, je chante.        On Thursday, I sing.
Le vendredi, je danse.      On Friday, I dance.
Le samedi, j'aide ma mère.  On Saturday, I help my mother.
Le dimanche, je me repose.  On Sunday, I rest.

Le before a day means every, so le lundi means on Mondays, or every Monday. J'aide is said zhed. Je me repose is said zhuh muh ruh-POHZ.

### Verbs
lis = read         chante = sing       danse = dance
joue = play        aide = help         repose = rest

### Try it
Read the seven lines aloud. Then choose a day and say a sentence about what you really do on that day.

## PAGE 3: A weekly timetable
Here is a small text about Kamau's week. Read it with your class.

Salut ! Je m'appelle Kamau. J'ai dix ans. Le lundi, le mardi, le mercredi, le jeudi et le vendredi, je vais à l'école. Le samedi, je joue au football. Le dimanche, je vais à l'église avec ma famille.

### New words
j'ai dix ans = I am ten years old
au football = football
avec = with
ma famille = my family

### Answer the questions
1. Comment s'appelle le garçon ? He is called Kamau.
2. Quel âge a-t-il ? He is ten.
3. Que fait-il le samedi ? He plays football.
4. Où va-t-il le dimanche ? To church.

Comment s'appelle ... ? asks What is ... called? Quel âge a-t-il ? asks How old is he?

### Reading in turns
When reading in turns, do not speak while another person is reading. Wait until they finish. Then it is your turn. This shows respect and helps everyone learn.

### Try it
Read the text to a partner, then ask them one of the questions in French.

## PAGE 4: Reading a poem
Poems are good for practising rhythm. Read this simple poem about the week. Keep the beat steady and read each line in one breath.

Lundi, mardi, mercredi,
jeudi, vendredi,
samedi, dimanche.
Une semaine, sept jours.
Bonne semaine, mes amis !

Bonne semaine means Have a good week. Mes amis means my friends (may za-MEE). Sept jours means seven days (set ZHOOR).

### Reading the poem
1. Listen to your teacher read the poem once.
2. Read it together in choral reading.
3. Read one line each in turn.
4. Try to say the poem from memory.

### Expression
Read the last line with a warm, happy voice, as if you are really wishing your friends a good week. Expression makes a text come alive.

### Try it
Clap the beat while you read the poem. Choose two friends and read the poem in a group of three, each reading one line at a time.

## PAGE 5: Summary and reading practice
Reading aloud is a skill. To improve, read a little each day, listen carefully, and copy good models. Let us review what you can do.

### Summary
• You can read the seven days from flashcards.
• You can read sentences about what happens on each day.
• You can read a short text and answer questions about it.
• You can read a poem with rhythm and expression.

### Common mistakes
• Saying the final s or t when they are silent.
• Skipping the nasal sound in vendredi and dimanche.
• Reading without pauses, so the meaning gets lost.
• Talking while others read.

### Read and choose
Read this sentence: Aujourd'hui, c'est mercredi. Demain, c'est jeudi. What day is it today? Wednesday. What day is it tomorrow? Thursday.

### Challenge
Look at a calendar. Read the days aloud in French. Ask a friend to point to each day. Take turns.

## QUIZ
1. What does le samedi, je joue mean?
   A) On Saturday, I play.
   B) On Sunday, I rest.
   C) On Friday, I sing.
   D) On Monday, I read.
   Answer: A
   Why: Samedi is Saturday and je joue means I play.
2. How is ch said in dimanche?
   A) k
   B) sh
   C) ch as in chair
   D) s
   Answer: B
   Why: In French, ch is said sh.
3. What does semaine mean?
   A) day
   B) week
   C) month
   D) year
   Answer: B
   Why: Une semaine is a week.
4. Which sentence means On Monday, I go to school?
   A) Le lundi, je vais à l'école.
   B) Le dimanche, je me repose.
   C) Le samedi, je joue.
   D) Le jeudi, je chante.
   Answer: A
   Why: Lundi is Monday and école is school, so this is I go to school on Monday.
5. What does mes amis mean?
   A) my sisters
   B) my friends
   C) my parents
   D) my books
   Answer: B
   Why: Amis means friends and mes means my.
6. Translate into English: Le dimanche, je vais à l'église. (2 marks)
   Answer: On Sunday, I go to church.
   Why: 1 mark for on Sunday and 1 mark for I go to church.
7. Read and answer in a full sentence in English: Le samedi, Kamau joue au football. What does Kamau do on Saturday? (2 marks)
   Answer: He plays football on Saturday.
   Why: 1 mark for plays football and 1 mark for a full sentence.
8. Explain why you should not talk when another person is reading aloud. (2 marks)
   Answer: It is disrespectful and stops others from hearing and learning.
   Why: 1 mark for respect and 1 mark for the effect on others.
9. Write two sentences in French about what you do on two different days. (4 marks)
   Answer: Le lundi, je vais à l'école. Le samedi, je joue.
   Why: 2 marks for each correct sentence.
