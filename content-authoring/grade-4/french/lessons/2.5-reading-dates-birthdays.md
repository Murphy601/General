### Pronunciation help
janvier        zhon-vee-AY
février        fay-vree-AY
mars           marss
avril          a-VREEL
mai            meh
juin           zhwan
juillet        zhwee-YEH
août           oot
septembre      sep-TOM-bruh
octobre        ok-TOH-bruh
novembre       no-VOM-bruh
décembre       day-SOM-bruh

Août is a special word. It is said oot. The accent circonflexe on û is part of the spelling.

## PAGE 2: Numbers to thirty-one
To read a date you need the numbers from 1 to 31. You know 1 to 20. Now learn the rest.

21   vingt et un
22   vingt-deux
23   vingt-trois
24   vingt-quatre
25   vingt-cinq
26   vingt-six
27   vingt-sept
28   vingt-huit
29   vingt-neuf
30   trente
31   trente et un

Vingt is said van. Trente is said tront. Notice that 21 and 31 use et, which means and: vingt et un means twenty and one. The other numbers use a hyphen: vingt-deux.

### Special word for the first day
For the first day of a month, French uses le premier (luh pruh-MYAY) instead of un. For example, le premier mai means the first of May.

### Try it
Read the numbers 20 to 31 aloud. Then read these: 24, 27, 30, 31. Ask a friend to check your answers.

## PAGE 3: Reading a date
In French, the date is read in this order: le + number + month. The day of the week can come first.

le 5 mars            the 5th of March
le 12 mai            the 12th of May
le 30 juin           the 30th of June
le premier janvier   the 1st of January

### Reading aloud
le 5 mars = le cinq mars
le 12 mai = le douze mai
le 30 juin = le trente juin
le 25 décembre = le vingt-cinq décembre

Notice that in French, we say the day number before the month, just as we do in Kenya. In writing, we use a small letter for the month.

### Question and answer
Ton anniversaire, c'est quand ?    When is your birthday?
Mon anniversaire, c'est le 12 mai. My birthday is on 12 May.

Ton anniversaire, c'est quand ? is said tohn a-nee-vair-SAIR say KON. Quand means when.

### Try it
Ask three friends for their birthdays and read each date aloud in French.

## PAGE 4: A birthday text
Read this text aloud, first alone and then with a partner.

Aujourd'hui, c'est le 12 mai. C'est l'anniversaire d'Amina. Elle a dix ans. Il y a une fête avec ses amis. Il y a un gâteau et dix bougies. Tout le monde chante : Bon anniversaire, Amina !

### New words
elle = she                 (el)
ses amis = her friends     (say za-MEE)
dix bougies = ten candles
tout le monde = everyone   (too luh MOND)

### Understand the text
1. What is the date? The 12th of May.
2. Whose birthday is it? Amina's.
3. How old is Amina? Ten.
4. What do the guests sing? Bon anniversaire.

### Reading tips
Use a happy voice when reading a birthday text. Read the last line as if you are really singing. Take a short breath at each comma, and a longer pause after each full stop.

### Try it
Read the text aloud again. This time, change the name and the date to your own.

## PAGE 5: Songs, summary and practice
Singing a birthday song in French is a good way to practise reading. Read the lyrics slowly first, then sing.

Bon anniversaire, bon anniversaire,
bon anniversaire, Amina,
bon anniversaire !

You can put any name in place of Amina.

### Summary
• The twelve months: janvier to décembre.
• The numbers 21 to 31: vingt et un, vingt-deux, ..., trente, trente et un.
• A date is read le + number + month.
• Ton anniversaire, c'est quand ? Mon anniversaire, c'est le ...

### Common mistakes
• Writing months with capital letters. Write mars, not Mars, inside a sentence.
• Saying août as ow-oot. It is said oot.
• Saying the silent final t in juillet. It is said zhwee-YEH, with no t at the end.
• Reading trente like trent. The ending is a nasal sound.

### Challenge
Read the following dates aloud: le 3 avril, le 15 août, le 21 septembre, le 31 décembre. Check with a partner.

## QUIZ
1. What does mai mean?
   A) March
   B) May
   C) June
   D) July
   Answer: B
   Why: Mai is May.
2. Which number is trente?
   A) 13
   B) 30
   C) 20
   D) 3
   Answer: B
   Why: Trente is thirty.
3. How do you say the first of a month?
   A) un
   B) le premier
   C) le une
   D) le deux
   Answer: B
   Why: French uses le premier for the first day of a month.
4. What does Mon anniversaire, c'est le 12 mai mean?
   A) My birthday is on 12 May.
   B) My party is in May.
   C) I am twelve years old.
   D) Today is 12 May.
   Answer: A
   Why: Mon anniversaire is my birthday and le 12 mai is 12 May.
5. Which is the correct French for 25?
   A) vingt-cinq
   B) vingt et cinq
   C) vingt-quatre
   D) cinquante
   Answer: A
   Why: Numbers 22 to 29 use vingt and a hyphen. Vingt-cinq is 25.
6. Write in French: 5 March. (2 marks)
   Answer: le 5 mars (le cinq mars)
   Why: 1 mark for le and the number and 1 mark for mars with a small m.
7. Write in French: My birthday is on 12 May. (3 marks)
   Answer: Mon anniversaire, c'est le 12 mai.
   Why: 1 mark for mon anniversaire, 1 mark for c'est le 12 and 1 mark for mai.
8. Read the text: Aujourd'hui, c'est le 12 mai. C'est l'anniversaire d'Amina. Elle a dix ans. How old is Amina and what is the date? (2 marks)
   Answer: She is ten years old, and the date is 12 May.
   Why: 1 mark for the age and 1 mark for the date.
9. Write the months from janvier to juin in French. (3 marks)
   Answer: janvier, février, mars, avril, mai, juin
   Why: 3 marks for all six correct, minus 1 for every two errors.
