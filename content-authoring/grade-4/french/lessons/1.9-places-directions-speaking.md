## PAGE 1: Where is it? Asking about places
To find something, we ask Où est ... ? It means Where is ...? (oo ay). The answer begins with Il est ... for a masculine word or Elle est ... for a feminine word. Il est means it is (or he is) and elle est means it is (or she is).

### Examples
Où est le livre ?       Where is the book?
Il est sur la table.    It is on the table.
Où est la clé ?         Where is the key?
Elle est sous la chaise.  It is under the chair.

La clé means the key (la KLAY).

### Places in and around a home
la cuisine      the kitchen       (la kwee-ZEEN)
le salon        the living room   (luh sa-LON)
la chambre      the bedroom       (la SHOM-bruh)
la salle de bain  the bathroom    (la sal duh BAN)
le jardin       the garden        (luh zhar-DAN)

### Try it
Hide a small object in the classroom. Ask a friend Où est ... ? and let them answer with il est or elle est and a position word.

## PAGE 2: Objects in the home
Learn the names of objects found in a home. Say each word with its little word. Then say where it can usually be found.

le canapé       the sofa          (luh ka-na-PAY)
la télé         the TV            (la tay-LAY)
le frigo        the fridge        (luh free-GO)
le lit          the bed           (luh LEE)
la porte        the door          (la PORT)
la table        the table         (la TA-bluh)
le tapis        the carpet, rug   (luh ta-PEE)

### Sing along
Sing the names of these objects to any rhythm you like. Clap the syllables: ca-na-pé, té-lé, fri-go, lit, porte, ta-ble, ta-pis. Repeat the song until everyone knows it. Songs help you to remember words and the way they sound.

### Poem
Dans la maison, il y a
un lit, une table, un canapé.
Dans la maison, il y a
une télé et un tapis.

Read the poem aloud twice, then try to say it from memory.

## PAGE 3: Location words
You already know sur, sous, dans, devant, derrière and entre. Let us use them to talk about where things are at home.

Le tapis est devant le canapé.    The rug is in front of the sofa.
La télé est dans le salon.        The TV is in the living room.
Le chat est sous la table.        The cat is under the table.
Le sac est derrière la porte.     The bag is behind the door.
Le livre est entre le lit et la table.  The book is between the bed and the table.

### Games to state the location
Play with a partner. Put a book in one of many places. Ask Où est le livre ? and let your partner answer with a full sentence. Swap roles. Try to speak quickly but clearly.

### Safety at home
Store things in the right place to prevent accidents. Keep shoes off the walkway. Put sharp things such as knives in a drawer. Keep the floor clean and dry. Talk with a partner about one thing you can put away at home.

### Try it
Give your partner an instruction, such as Mets le livre sur la table, and check that they do it correctly.

## PAGE 4: Asking for and giving directions
Directions help people find their way. In French, we ask Excusez-moi, où est ... ? It means Excuse me, where is ...? Here are the direction words.

tout droit      straight ahead    (too DRWA)
à gauche        to the left       (a GOHSH)
à droite        to the right      (a DRWAT)
Tournez         turn              (toor-NAY)
Allez           go                (a-LAY)

### A short dialogue
Amina: Excusez-moi, où est l'école ?
Kamau: Allez tout droit. Tournez à gauche.
Amina: Merci !
Kamau: De rien !

De rien means You are welcome (duh RYAN). L'école means the school (lay-KOL).

## PAGE 5: Practice and summary
Now practise asking for and giving directions in a simple role play. Use a map you draw in class. Put a school, a shop and a house on the map.

### Role play
Person 1: Excusez-moi, où est le magasin ?
Person 2: Allez tout droit. Tournez à droite.
Person 1: Merci beaucoup !
Person 2: De rien !

Le magasin means the shop (luh ma-ga-ZAN).

### Speaking with confidence
Look at the person you are speaking to. Speak clearly and keep your voice friendly. Use your hands to show left and right. This is called engaging the listener, and it makes your speech easier to follow.

### Common mistakes
• Mixing up gauche and droite. Practise with your left hand and your right hand.
• Saying tout droit without the nasal sound in droit. It is drwa.
• Forgetting merci at the end.

### Summary
You can ask Où est ... ?, name objects and places in the home, say where things are, and ask and give simple directions with tout droit, à gauche and à droite. Practise with a partner every day.

## QUIZ
1. What does à gauche mean?
   A) to the left
   B) to the right
   C) straight ahead
   D) behind
   Answer: A
   Why: À gauche means to the left.
2. What does Où est la clé ? mean?
   A) Who has the key?
   B) Where is the key?
   C) What is the key?
   D) When is the key?
   Answer: B
   Why: Où means where.
3. Which word means straight ahead?
   A) à droite
   B) à gauche
   C) tout droit
   D) derrière
   Answer: C
   Why: Tout droit means straight ahead.
4. What does De rien mean?
   A) Goodbye
   B) You are welcome
   C) Excuse me
   D) Good morning
   Answer: B
   Why: De rien is the reply to merci.
5. Which answer fits Où est le chat ?
   A) Il est sous la table.
   B) Elle est sous la table.
   C) Ils sont ici.
   D) Elle est petite.
   Answer: A
   Why: Chat is masculine, so we use il est.
6. Write in French: The rug is in front of the sofa. (3 marks)
   Answer: Le tapis est devant le canapé.
   Why: 1 mark for le tapis, 1 mark for est and 1 mark for devant le canapé.
7. Write in French: Excuse me, where is the school? (3 marks)
   Answer: Excusez-moi, où est l'école ?
   Why: 1 mark for excusez-moi, 1 mark for où est and 1 mark for l'école.
8. Give directions in French to someone who wants to go straight and then left. (2 marks)
   Answer: Allez tout droit. Tournez à gauche.
   Why: 1 mark for each correct instruction.
9. Explain one way to keep a home safe by storing things properly. (2 marks)
   Answer: Put sharp things in a drawer and keep walkways clear, so that no one is hurt.
   Why: 1 mark for the action and 1 mark for the reason.
