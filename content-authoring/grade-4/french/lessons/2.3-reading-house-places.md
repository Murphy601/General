## PAGE 1: Reading room and item words
When you read a new language aloud, listen first to your teacher, then copy. In this lesson you read words for the house and the things in it. Read each word slowly, one syllable at a time.

la maison        the house         (la may-ZON)
la chambre       the bedroom       (la SHOM-bruh)
le salon         the living room   (luh sa-LON)
la cuisine       the kitchen       (la kwee-ZEEN)
la table         the table         (la TA-bluh)
la chaise        the chair         (la SHEZ)
le lit           the bed           (luh LEE)
la porte         the door          (la PORT)

### Reading tip
The letter combination ch in French says sh, like in chambre and chaise. The letters ai say eh, as in maison and chaise. The letters ou say oo.

### Try it
Read the list in turns with a partner. One reads the French word, the other says the English meaning. Change roles when you reach the end.

## PAGE 2: Little words that tell us where
Words that tell us where something is are called prepositions. They are very useful for reading and answering questions about a place.

sur        on
sous       under
dans       in
devant     in front of
derrière   behind
entre      between

### Say them
sur = sür (round your lips as for ü)
sous = soo
dans = don (nasal)
devant = duh-VON
derrière = dair-YAIR
entre = ON-truh

## PAGE 3: Reading sentences about places
Now read sentences that use these little words. The pattern is: Le or La + thing + est + preposition + le or la + place.

Le livre est sur la table.        The book is on the table.
Le chat est sous la chaise.       The cat is under the chair.
Le sac est dans la chambre.       The bag is in the bedroom.
Le ballon est derrière la porte.  The ball is behind the door.
La télé est dans le salon.        The TV is in the living room.
Le chat est entre le lit et la porte.  The cat is between the bed and the door.

Est means is. Le chat means the cat (luh SHA). Le sac means the bag (luh SAK). Le ballon means the ball (luh ba-LON).

### Questions to answer
Où est le livre ? means Where is the book? (oo ay luh LEEVR)
Où est le chat ? means Where is the cat?
Answer with a full sentence: Le chat est sous la chaise.

### Try it
Read each sentence aloud. Then close your eyes and ask a friend to test you: Où est la télé ?

## PAGE 4: A short reading text
Read this text aloud, one sentence at a time, then answer the questions.

Voici ma maison. Dans la cuisine, il y a une table et deux chaises. Le frigo est près de la porte. Dans le salon, il y a un canapé et une télé. Ma chambre est petite. Mon lit est sous la fenêtre.

Près de means near. Petite means small. Il y a means there is or there are.

### Comprehension
1. Where is the table? Answer: Dans la cuisine.
2. How many chairs are there in the kitchen? Answer: Two.
3. What is in the living room? Answer: A sofa and a TV.
4. Where is the bed? Answer: Under the window.

### Reading with the group
Choral reading means reading together. Everyone reads at the same speed. Follow the leader and keep the same voice level. Choral reading builds confidence.

### Try it
Read the text with your class in choral reading, then in pairs, and finally alone.

## PAGE 5: Store items safely
Things must be stored in the right place at home. A knife, matches or medicine should be out of reach of small children. A bag should not be left in a walkway.

### Safe storage cards
Le couteau est dans la cuisine, dans un tiroir. (The knife is in the kitchen, in a drawer.)
Les chaussures sont sous le lit. (The shoes are under the bed.)
Le sac est sur la chaise. (The bag is on the chair.)

Les means the for more than one thing. It is said lay. Les chaussures are shoes (lay sho-SÜR).

### Common mistakes
• Saying sous like sue. It is said soo.
• Saying dans with a strong n. It is a nasal sound.
• Putting the preposition after the noun. Say sur la table, not la table sur.

### Summary
You can now read room and item words, use the prepositions sur, sous, dans, devant, derrière and entre, read simple sentences about where things are, and answer questions. Practise reading aloud regularly.

## QUIZ
1. What does sous mean?
   A) on
   B) in
   C) under
   D) behind
   Answer: C
   Why: Sous means under.
2. Which preposition means between?
   A) sur
   B) entre
   C) dans
   D) devant
   Answer: B
   Why: Entre means between.
3. What does Le livre est sur la table mean?
   A) The book is on the table.
   B) The book is under the table.
   C) The book is in the table.
   D) The table is on the book.
   Answer: A
   Why: Sur means on.
4. What does Où est le chat ? ask?
   A) Who is the cat?
   B) Where is the cat?
   C) What is the cat?
   D) When is the cat?
   Answer: B
   Why: Où means where.
5. How is ch said in chambre?
   A) k
   B) ch as in chair
   C) sh
   D) s
   Answer: C
   Why: In French, ch is said sh.
6. Write in French: The bag is in the bedroom. (3 marks)
   Answer: Le sac est dans la chambre.
   Why: 1 mark for le sac, 1 mark for est and 1 mark for dans la chambre.
7. Read and translate: Le chat est sous la chaise. (2 marks)
   Answer: The cat is under the chair.
   Why: 1 mark for the cat and 1 mark for under the chair.
8. Give one reason why we should store things safely at home. (2 marks)
   Answer: To stop accidents, such as tripping or children reaching sharp things.
   Why: 1 mark for a safety reason and 1 mark for a clear explanation.
9. Answer in French with a full sentence: Où est la télé ? Use the text about the house. (2 marks)
   Answer: La télé est dans le salon.
   Why: 1 mark for the sentence pattern and 1 mark for le salon.
