## PAGE 1: Spelling weather words
Copy each weather word carefully. Notice the special spelling patterns.

le soleil       the sun
le vent         the wind
le nuage        the cloud
la pluie        the rain
l'orage         the storm
la neige        the snow
le temps        the weather
il pleut        it is raining
il fait chaud   it is hot
il fait froid   it is cold
il fait beau    the weather is nice

### Spelling hints
• Soleil ends in eil, not ail. Say so-LAY.
• Nuage has ua, not oua: n-u-a-g-e.
• Pluie has ui: p-l-u-i-e.
• Neige has ei: n-e-i-g-e.
• Orage begins with o and ends in age.
• Froid has oi and a silent d.

### Try it
Look at the list for two minutes. Cover it. Write eight words from memory. Check with the list and correct errors.

## PAGE 2: Rearranging letters
A letter puzzle is a fun way to practise spelling. Rearrange the letters to make a weather word.

### Examples
e n t v         Answer: vent
l s o e i l     Answer: soleil
u p l i e       Answer: pluie
n u a g e       Answer: nuage

### Your turn
1. g e n e i     (hint: cold white flakes)
2. r o a g e     (hint: dark clouds and thunder)
3. p m e t s     (hint: this word means weather)

Answers: 1. neige  2. orage  3. temps

### Crossword tip
Count the boxes. Le vent has 4 letters. Pluie has 5. Soleil has 6. If your answer has a different number of letters from the boxes, it is wrong.

### Try it
Make a letter puzzle with two weather words. Give it to a friend and check their answers.

## PAGE 3: Filling gaps in dialogues
Complete this dialogue with words from the bank.

Word bank: temps, pleut, beau, soleil, Bonjour

Amina: ______ ! Quel ______ fait-il ?
Kamau: Il ______ ! Il fait ______ !
Amina: Oui, il y a du ______.

Answers: Bonjour, temps, pleut, beau, soleil.

But wait: the answer for the first gap in Kamau's line must be logical. If Kamau says Il pleut ! then it is not nice weather. Read the dialogue after you complete it. Does every line agree with the others? A better dialogue is this:

Amina: Bonjour ! Quel temps fait-il ?
Kamau: Il fait beau ! Il y a du soleil.
Amina: Super !

Super means great (sü-PAIR).

### Try it
Write a dialogue of three lines about rainy weather. Use il pleut and il fait froid.

## PAGE 4: Labelling pictures and writing short texts
Draw a weather picture and label it. Write the French word beside each part.

A sun with rays              le soleil
Clouds in the sky            les nuages
Falling drops                la pluie
Lines that show air moving   le vent

### A short weather text
Aujourd'hui, il fait beau. Il y a du soleil. Il fait chaud. Demain, il pleut. Il y a des nuages.

### A weather chart for the week
lundi        il fait beau
mardi        il pleut
mercredi     il y a du vent
jeudi        il y a des nuages
vendredi     il fait chaud

### Try it
Look outside for a week. Every day, write one sentence about the weather in French. At the end of the week, read your sentences aloud to a friend.

## PAGE 5: Checking and summary
Careful writers check their spelling, accents and punctuation. In French, a weather sentence often begins with il. Check that each sentence starts with a capital letter.

### Checklist
1. Are all weather words spelled correctly?
2. Did I use Il fait or Il y a correctly?
3. Did I add the apostrophe in l'orage?
4. Did I end every sentence with a full stop?
5. Is my handwriting easy to read?

### Common mistakes
• Writing soleil as soleille.
• Writing nuage as nuague.
• Writing il fait pleut. Say il pleut, with no fait.
• Forgetting the apostrophe in l'orage.

### Summary
You can now spell weather words, unscramble letters, complete dialogues, label a weather picture and write short weather texts. Correct spelling makes your writing clear and helps others to understand you.

## QUIZ
1. Which is the correct spelling of sun?
   A) soleille
   B) soleil
   C) solail
   D) soley
   Answer: B
   Why: Sun is spelled s-o-l-e-i-l.
2. Which sentence is correct?
   A) Il fait pleut.
   B) Il pleut.
   C) Il y a chaud.
   D) Il fait des nuages.
   Answer: B
   Why: We say Il pleut for rain, with no fait.
3. Rearrange n u a g e to make a weather word.
   A) nuage
   B) genau
   C) eguan
   D) ageun
   Answer: A
   Why: The letters spell nuage, which means cloud.
4. What does Super mean?
   A) Great
   B) Cold
   C) Storm
   D) Thank you
   Answer: A
   Why: Super means great.
5. Which is the correct way to write the storm?
   A) l'orage
   B) la orage
   C) le orage
   D) l orage
   Answer: A
   Why: Le or la becomes l' before a vowel, so l'orage.
6. Write in French: It is cold. (2 marks)
   Answer: Il fait froid.
   Why: 1 mark for il fait and 1 mark for froid.
7. Write in French: Today it is sunny. (3 marks)
   Answer: Aujourd'hui, il y a du soleil.
   Why: 1 mark for aujourd'hui, 1 mark for il y a and 1 mark for du soleil.
8. Write a three-line dialogue about the weather in French. (3 marks)
   Answer: Bonjour ! Quel temps fait-il ? Il fait beau.
   Why: 1 mark for a greeting, 1 mark for the question and 1 mark for the answer.
9. Explain why it is important to check your spelling. (2 marks)
   Answer: Correct spelling makes your writing clear so that the reader can understand it.
   Why: 1 mark for clear writing and 1 mark for the reader.
