## PAGE 1: Saying hello in French
Every language has ways to greet people. In Kenya you may say Habari, Hujambo or Hello. In French, the most common greeting is Bonjour. It means Hello or Good day. You can use it in the morning and in the afternoon, to your teacher, to a grown-up or to a friend.

Between friends and children, people often say Salut. It means Hi, and it is also used to say bye in a friendly way. In the evening, French speakers say Bonsoir, which means Good evening.

To say goodbye, use Au revoir. To be polite, add Merci, which means Thank you.

### Words to listen to and repeat
bonjour = hello, good day    (bon-ZHOOR)
salut = hi, bye (friends)    (sa-LÜ)
bonsoir = good evening       (bon-SWAR)
au revoir = goodbye          (oh ruh-VWAR)
merci = thank you            (mair-SEE)

In the respelling, the letters in capitals show the part you say a little longer. The sound ü means you say ee with your lips rounded as if you were saying oo. When you see "on" or "an" in a respelling, say it softly through your nose and do not pronounce a strong n.

### Try it
Greet three friends in French. Use Bonjour first, then Salut. Answer with the same word.

## PAGE 3: The sound zh
French has a sound that is like the s in the English word measure. We write it in respelling as zh. It is spelled with the letter j in many words.

### Listen and repeat
bonjour = hello       (bon-ZHOOR)
je = I                (zhuh)
jour = day            (zhoor)
jeudi = Thursday      (zhuh-DEE)

Notice that j in French is never said like the j in the English word jump. It is softer. Put your teeth close together, push a little air through, and let your voice buzz.

The same zh sound also appears when the letter g comes before e or i, as in the word rouge. You will meet more of these later.

### Meaning of je
Je means I. Before a vowel it becomes j'. For example, Je m'appelle means My name is. It is written as one short sentence: Je m'appelle Amina. Say it slowly: zhuh ma-PEL a-mee-NA.

### Sound game
Say these words one after the other: je, jour, bonjour. Then clap once for each syllable. Bon-jour has two claps. Je has one clap.

## PAGE 4: Spelling names with the French alphabet
In French, when someone asks you to spell your name, you say the French name of each letter. Let us try with the name Amina: A M I N A. You say: ah, em, ee, en, ah.

Now try Kamau: K A M A U. You say: ka, ah, em, ah, ü.

Try Otieno: O T I E N O. You say: oh, tay, ee, uh, en, oh.

### A short conversation
Person 1: Bonjour ! Comment tu t'appelles ?
Person 2: Je m'appelle Amina.
Person 1: Ça s'écrit comment ?
Person 2: A, M, I, N, A.

Comment tu t'appelles ? means What is your name? Je m'appelle means My name is. Ça s'écrit comment ? means How do you spell it?

### Voice matters
When you ask a question, let your voice go up at the end. When you make a statement, let your voice come down. Compare Bonjour ? with a rising voice, and Bonjour. with a falling voice. This up and down is called intonation.

### Activity
Spell your own name in French letters. Then spell the name of the friend next to you. Check each other.

## PAGE 5: Mingle and practise
In a mingle-mingle activity, everyone walks around the room and greets as many friends as possible. Use these lines:

1. Bonjour ! or Salut !
2. Comment tu t'appelles ?
3. Je m'appelle ...
4. Au revoir ! or Salut !

### Why pronunciation matters
A word said the wrong way can be hard to understand. If you say je with a hard j like jump, a French speaker may not follow you. Good pronunciation shows respect for the language and helps you make friends.

### Common mistakes
• Saying the letter j like English j. Say zhee.
• Saying u like oo. In French, u is ü, with rounded lips.
• Forgetting that a question goes up at the end.

### Summary
You can now greet with bonjour, salut, bonsoir and au revoir. You know the letter names, the zh sound in je, jour and bonjour, and how to spell a name aloud. Practise a little every day.

## QUIZ
1. What does Bonjour mean?
   A) Goodbye
   B) Thank you
   C) Hello or good day
   D) Good night
   Answer: C
   Why: Bonjour is the usual French greeting meaning hello or good day.
2. Which word means goodbye?
   A) Merci
   B) Au revoir
   C) Bonsoir
   D) Bonne nuit, merci
   Answer: B
   Why: Au revoir means goodbye. Merci is thank you and bonsoir is good evening.
3. How do you say the French letter J?
   A) jay
   B) zhee
   C) gee
   D) yee
   Answer: B
   Why: The French letter J is named zhee, with the soft zh sound.
4. Which word contains the zh sound?
   A) merci
   B) salut
   C) jour
   D) revoir
   Answer: C
   Why: Jour begins with j, which is said zh.
5. What does Je m'appelle Amina mean?
   A) I am fine, Amina
   B) My name is Amina
   C) Goodbye, Amina
   D) Where is Amina?
   Answer: B
   Why: Je m'appelle means My name is.
6. Write the French question for What is your name? (2 marks)
   Answer: Comment tu t'appelles ?
   Why: 1 mark for Comment and 1 mark for tu t'appelles.
7. Say how you would spell the name Amina using the French letter names. (3 marks)
   Answer: ah, em, ee, en, ah
   Why: 1 mark for A, M, I; 1 mark for N; 1 mark for the final A.
8. Explain why a question in French should rise at the end. (2 marks)
   Answer: The rising voice shows that you are asking, not stating. This is called intonation.
   Why: 1 mark for saying it shows a question and 1 mark for naming intonation or a rising voice.
9. Write a short greeting between two friends meeting in the morning. (3 marks)
   Answer: Bonjour ! Salut ! Comment tu t'appelles ? Je m'appelle Amina.
   Why: 1 mark for a greeting, 1 mark for a question and 1 mark for a correct answer.
