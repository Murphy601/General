## PAGE 1: The days of the week
A week is une semaine (ün suh-MEN) and a day is un jour (uhn ZHOOR). There are seven days in a week. In French, the names of the days start with a small letter, not a capital letter as in English. The week in French begins on Monday.

### The seven days
lundi      Monday       (luhn-DEE)
mardi      Tuesday      (mar-DEE)
mercredi   Wednesday    (mair-kruh-DEE)
jeudi      Thursday     (zhuh-DEE)
vendredi   Friday       (von-druh-DEE)
samedi     Saturday     (sam-DEE)
dimanche   Sunday       (dee-MONSH)

Notice that the first six days all end in di. Only dimanche ends differently. The final letters are said dee for di.

### Try it
Say the days in order three times. Then say them backwards, starting with dimanche. Listen to how each word ends. Six days finish with the sound dee, and this gives the week a steady rhythm that is easy to remember when you sing it.

### Useful little words
aujourd'hui = today      (oh-zhoor-DWEE)
demain = tomorrow        (duh-MAN)
hier = yesterday         (YAIR)

### Question and answer
Quel jour est-ce aujourd'hui ?    What day is it today?
Aujourd'hui, c'est lundi.         Today it is Monday.

Quel jour is said kel ZHOOR. Est-ce is said ess.

### Try it
Ask a partner Quel jour est-ce aujourd'hui ? Your partner answers with the real day.

## PAGE 3: Two new sounds, d and r
Two sounds appear again and again in the days. The letter d in mardi and lundi is said with the tongue touching just behind the top teeth. This is a soft, clear d.

The French r is made at the back of the throat. It is not rolled like a Kiswahili r, and it is not the English r. It is a soft sound like gently clearing your throat. You can hear it in mardi, mercredi and vendredi.

### Words with d
mardi        (mar-DEE)
vendredi     (von-druh-DEE)
dimanche     (dee-MONSH)
demain       (duh-MAN)

### Words with r
rouge = red        (roozh)
riz = rice         (ree)
mercredi = Wednesday
vendredi = Friday

### Try it
Say the four d words and the four r words. Practise the r by whispering a soft ah sound from the back of your throat.

## PAGE 4: Clapping syllables and rhythm
A syllable is one beat in a word. Clapping the beats helps you say each word smoothly. Try this game.

lun-di            2 claps
mar-di            2 claps
mer-cre-di        3 claps
jeu-di            2 claps
ven-dre-di        3 claps
sa-me-di          3 claps
di-manche         2 claps

### Rhyming game
Say a word ending in di and see who can say the next day fastest. Your teacher says lundi, you say mardi, and so on. Keep the rhythm steady.

### Days and activities
Talk about what you do on different days.
Le lundi, je vais à l'école. (On Monday, I go to school.)
Le samedi, je joue. (On Saturday, I play.)
Le dimanche, je vais à l'église ou à la mosquée. (On Sunday, I go to church or to the mosque.)

Je vais means I go. Je joue means I play. À l'école is said a lay-KOL.

### Try it
Tell a friend one thing you do on Saturday, using le samedi, je ...

## PAGE 5: Why time matters
Managing time means using each day well. On school days we study, and on weekends we rest, help at home and play. Use your time wisely so that you finish your homework and still have time to enjoy your friends.

### Intonation
In French, questions go up at the end and statements go down. Say Quel jour est-ce ? with your voice going up. Say Aujourd'hui, c'est lundi. with your voice going down.

### Common mistakes
• Writing Lundi with a capital letter in the middle of a sentence. French days use small letters.
• Saying the d in di as an English d without the soft vowel. Say dee.
• Mixing up mardi and mercredi. Remember mar for the second day and mer for the third.

### Summary
You can say the seven days, ask and answer Quel jour est-ce aujourd'hui ?, use aujourd'hui, demain and hier, clap the syllables, and say what you do on some days. Practise every morning by saying the day in French.

## QUIZ
1. What does jeudi mean?
   A) Tuesday
   B) Wednesday
   C) Thursday
   D) Friday
   Answer: C
   Why: Jeudi is Thursday.
2. Which day is the first day of the French week?
   A) dimanche
   B) samedi
   C) lundi
   D) mardi
   Answer: C
   Why: In French the week starts with lundi, Monday.
3. What does demain mean?
   A) today
   B) yesterday
   C) tomorrow
   D) week
   Answer: C
   Why: Demain means tomorrow.
4. How many claps are there in mer-cre-di?
   A) 2
   B) 3
   C) 4
   D) 1
   Answer: B
   Why: It has three syllables: mer, cre, di.
5. Which answer fits Quel jour est-ce aujourd'hui ?
   A) Aujourd'hui, c'est mardi.
   B) Bonjour, Amina.
   C) Merci beaucoup.
   D) C'est ma mère.
   Answer: A
   Why: The question asks what day it is, so the answer names a day.
6. Write the days Saturday and Sunday in French. (2 marks)
   Answer: samedi, dimanche
   Why: 1 mark for each.
7. Write in French: Today is Friday. (3 marks)
   Answer: Aujourd'hui, c'est vendredi.
   Why: 1 mark for aujourd'hui, 1 mark for c'est and 1 mark for vendredi with a small v.
8. Explain how the voice changes for a question and for a statement in French. (2 marks)
   Answer: For a question the voice goes up at the end. For a statement it goes down.
   Why: 1 mark for each.
9. Write the seven days of the week in French in the correct order. (4 marks)
   Answer: lundi, mardi, mercredi, jeudi, vendredi, samedi, dimanche
   Why: 4 marks for all seven correct in order; lose 1 mark for every two errors.
