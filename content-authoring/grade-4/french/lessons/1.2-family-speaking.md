## PAGE 1: The nuclear family
A nuclear family is a small family made of parents and their children. In French, the family is la famille. Listen to the words and repeat them after your teacher. Try to copy the sounds exactly.

### Family words
le père = the father        (luh PAIR)
la mère = the mother        (la MAIR)
l'enfant = the child        (lon-FON)
le bébé = the baby          (luh bay-BAY)
la famille = the family     (la fa-MEE-yuh)

Notice the little words le and la. They both mean the. French uses le for a masculine word and la for a feminine word. Père is masculine, so we say le père. Mère is feminine, so we say la mère. Before a vowel, le and la become l', as in l'enfant. Enfant can be a boy or a girl, so it stays the same.

### Sounds
The letter è in père and mère is said like the e in the English word bed, but held a little longer. The letter é in bébé is said like the ay in day, but short and pure.

### Try it
Say each word three times: first slowly, then normally, then in a whisper. Point to a picture of a mother, a father and a baby as you say each one.

## PAGE 2: Brothers, sisters and grandparents
Most families have more members than just parents and a baby. Here are more words to listen to and say.

le frère = the brother            (luh FRAIR)
la sœur = the sister              (la SUHR)
le grand-père = the grandfather   (luh gron-PAIR)
la grand-mère = the grandmother   (la gron-MAIR)
le garçon = the boy               (luh gar-SON)
la fille = the girl               (la FEE-yuh)

## PAGE 3: Who is it?
To ask who a person is, French speakers say Qui est-ce ? It means Who is it? The answer starts with C'est, which means It is or This is.

### The pattern
Question: Qui est-ce ?         (kee ess)
Answer: C'est mon père.        (say mon PAIR)

Mon means my for a masculine word, and ma means my for a feminine word. So we say mon père, mon frère and mon grand-père, but ma mère, ma sœur and ma grand-mère.

### Practice lines
Qui est-ce ? C'est mon père.
Qui est-ce ? C'est ma mère.
Qui est-ce ? C'est mon frère.
Qui est-ce ? C'est ma sœur.
Qui est-ce ? C'est le bébé.

The last line shows another useful pattern. For the baby in a picture, we can say C'est le bébé, which means It is the baby.

### Try it
Hold up a family picture. Ask Qui est-ce ? Your partner answers with C'est mon ... or C'est ma ... Swap roles after each picture.

## PAGE 4: Introducing my family
Now we put words together to introduce our family. Read this short introduction by a girl called Wanjiru.

Bonjour ! Je m'appelle Wanjiru. Voici ma famille. C'est ma mère. C'est mon père. C'est mon frère. C'est ma sœur. Au revoir !

Voici means Here is or This is. Use it when you show people something or someone.

### Role play
Work in pairs. One person is the visitor and one is the host.
Visiteur: Bonjour ! Qui est-ce ?
Hôte: C'est ma mère.
Visiteur: Et qui est-ce ?
Hôte: C'est mon père.
Visiteur: Merci ! Au revoir !
Et means and. Merci means thank you.

### Body language
When you speak in a role play, look at your partner, smile, and wait for your turn. Point gently to the picture when you say C'est. Good listening and taking turns are also part of speaking well.

### Try it
Change the names and the family members in the role play and perform it for another pair.

## PAGE 5: Songs, summary and mistakes
Songs help you remember words and their sounds. Make a simple song with the family words by using any tune you know. Sing: le père, la mère, le frère, la sœur, le bébé. Clap for each syllable as you sing.

### What you can now say
• le père, la mère, l'enfant, le bébé, la famille
• le frère, la sœur, le grand-père, la grand-mère
• le garçon, la fille
• Qui est-ce ? C'est mon père. C'est ma mère.

### Common mistakes
• Saying mon ma for the same person. Use mon with père, frère and grand-père. Use ma with mère, sœur and grand-mère.
• Forgetting the accents. In père and mère, the accent goes down from left to right. In bébé, the accent goes up.
• Making the r sound like English r. The French r comes from the back of the throat, a soft rubbing sound.

### Challenge
Point to five people in a family picture and say who they are, using Qui est-ce ? and C'est mon ... or C'est ma ...

## QUIZ
1. What does la sœur mean?
   A) the brother
   B) the sister
   C) the mother
   D) the girl
   Answer: B
   Why: La sœur means the sister.
2. Which word means the father?
   A) la mère
   B) le frère
   C) le père
   D) le bébé
   Answer: C
   Why: Le père is the father. Mère is mother and frère is brother.
3. Which answer fits the question Qui est-ce ?
   A) C'est ma mère.
   B) Au revoir.
   C) Merci beaucoup.
   D) Bonsoir.
   Answer: A
   Why: Qui est-ce ? asks who a person is, and C'est ma mère answers it.
4. Which word do we use with sœur?
   A) mon
   B) ma
   C) le
   D) un
   Answer: B
   Why: Sœur is feminine, so we say ma sœur.
5. What does Voici ma famille mean?
   A) Goodbye, family
   B) Here is my family
   C) Who is the family?
   D) I love my family
   Answer: B
   Why: Voici means here is, and ma famille means my family.
6. Write in French: This is my mother. (2 marks)
   Answer: C'est ma mère.
   Why: 1 mark for C'est and 1 mark for ma mère with the correct accent.
7. Write in French: Who is it? This is my brother. (3 marks)
   Answer: Qui est-ce ? C'est mon frère.
   Why: 1 mark for the question, 1 mark for C'est and 1 mark for mon frère.
8. Explain when we use mon and when we use ma. (2 marks)
   Answer: Use mon before a masculine word such as père. Use ma before a feminine word such as mère.
   Why: 1 mark for each correct rule with an example.
9. Write a short introduction of two members of your family in French. (3 marks)
   Answer: Voici ma famille. C'est ma mère. C'est mon père.
   Why: 1 mark for Voici, 1 mark for each correct family sentence.
