# Grade 4 French - notes for human checking

Structure: 27 lessons, topic numbers 1.1-1.9 (Listening and Speaking), 2.1-2.9 (Reading), 3.1-3.9 (Writing), one per theme (greetings, family, my surroundings, days, birthday, food, body, weather, getting around) in each strand. No retire.json needed: every manifest topicNumber has a lesson.

Decisions
- The KICD design says "sa va"; taught as the correct spelling "ça va".
- Design mentions "Telling Time" but only lists days of the week, so the days lessons also teach aujourd'hui, demain, hier. Months and numbers 1-31 are taught in the birthday theme (dates of birth).
- Theme 9 ("Getting Around") in the design is really about places and objects at home plus directions, so lessons cover objects at home, position words and tout droit / à gauche / à droite.
- Theme 3 lessons cover rooms and simple position words; theme 9 revisits and extends them.

Please double-check
- English respellings of French sounds are approximations (ü = ee with rounded lips; nasal vowels described only loosely).
- Birthday song: sung as "Bon anniversaire" to the Happy Birthday tune; teachers may prefer the traditional "Joyeux anniversaire" lyrics.
- Meilleurs vœux (May-YUR vuh), Félicitations, Chère/Cher cards, Ton ami(e).
- Partitive forms du/de l' appear in "Je mange du pain / Je bois de l'eau" with a short explanation only.
- "déluge" is taught as a heavy flood of water and "il gèle" as it is freezing (both listed in the design).
- Weather lesson uses snow (neige) as a vocabulary item although it is rare in Kenya.
- Bank items are partly generated from vocabulary and sentence lists (definitions, translations, article, spelling) plus hand-written fill-in, comprehension and composition items; all French is limited to what the lessons teach.
