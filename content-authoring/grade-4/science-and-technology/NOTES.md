# Grade 4 Science and Technology: notes

Covered all 9 sub-strands of the KICD design: 1.1 Plants, 1.2 Animals, 1.3 Human Digestive System, 2.1 Properties of Matter, 2.2 Management of Solid Waste, 2.3 Water Conservation, 3.1 Force and its Effects, 3.2 Light, 3.3 Heat. The manifest listed only 2.1, 2.3, 3.2 and 3.3; no retire.json is needed.

Points for a human to check:
- Fire is classed as an ARTIFICIAL light source (made by people), lightning, sun, stars and fireflies as natural. Some textbooks class fire as natural; adjust if the local textbook differs. Bank questions avoid fire in light-source questions.
- Digestive system figure is a simplified schematic, not to scale (large intestine drawn as a frame around the coiled small intestine).
- Deworming: lesson says "when a health worker gives them, for example at school" and gives no frequency.
- Light lesson: "flashing/bright lights scare wild animals" is used for the design's "discourage pests" item.
- Heat lesson mentions temperature in degrees Celsius (water freezes at 0 and boils at 100) as a light introduction only.
- Force lesson names gravity, friction, weight and air pushing on paper simply; no unit (newton) is taught.
- Bank answer positions are rotated by script; explanations and marks were written by hand.
