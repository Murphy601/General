## PAGE 1: Learning by listening and copying
Babies learn to speak by listening and copying the people around them. You can learn Mandarin in the same way. This is called imitation. When you imitate, you listen carefully and then repeat the word with the same sounds, tone and speed.

In this lesson you will imitate words about time: the numbers from one to ten and the days of the week. Good imitation has three steps.
1. Listen to the whole word.
2. Repeat it slowly.
3. Compare your voice with the speaker and try again.

If you can, ask your teacher to play a recording. If there is no recording, your teacher's voice is the model. You can also listen to a friend who speaks Mandarin. Do not worry about mistakes. Every mistake shows you what to practise.

### Why imitation matters
Mandarin has tones, so the way you say a word changes what it means. Copying the tone exactly is the best way to be understood. Different pronunciation can make your listener confused, so take your time with each word.

## PAGE 2: Numbers 1 to 10
Listen and repeat the numbers. Say each one with your hand showing the tone.

一 yī: 1
二 èr: 2
三 sān: 3
四 sì: 4
五 wǔ: 5
六 liù: 6
七 qī: 7
八 bā: 8
九 jiǔ: 9
十 shí: 10

### Tricky pairs
• 四 sì (four) and 十 shí (ten) are easy to mix up. sì is said with the tongue behind the teeth, while shí has a "sh" sound. Practise the pair: sì, shí, sì, shí.
• 七 qī uses the smiling sound q. Spread your lips and puff a little air.
• 三 sān and 四 sì both start with s.

### Counting song
Sing the numbers to a song you know well, for example "One, two, three, four, five, once I caught a fish alive". Replace the English words with yī, èr, sān, sì, wǔ, liù, qī, bā, jiǔ, shí. Sing it twice, once slowly and once quickly. Listen to whether each tone stays clear even when you go faster.

## PAGE 3: Days of the week
In Chinese, the days of the week are easy to make. The word 星期 xīngqī means week. Then we add a number.

星期一 xīngqīyī: Monday
星期二 xīngqī'èr: Tuesday
星期三 xīngqīsān: Wednesday
星期四 xīngqīsì: Thursday
星期五 xīngqīwǔ: Friday
星期六 xīngqīliù: Saturday
星期天 xīngqītiān: Sunday (you may also hear 星期日 xīngqīrì)

So Monday is "week one", Tuesday is "week two", and so on. Sunday is special: it uses 天 (day) or 日 (sun, day) instead of a number.

Three helpful time words:
昨天 zuótiān: yesterday
今天 jīntiān: today
明天 míngtiān: tomorrow

## PAGE 4: Days of the week in Kiswahili and Chinese
The Kiswahili and Chinese ways of naming days are different, and both are clever.

Chinese counts the days starting from Monday:
星期一 is "week one" = Monday.

Kiswahili counts the days starting from Saturday:
Jumamosi = Saturday
Jumapili = Sunday
Jumatatu = Monday
Jumanne = Tuesday
Jumatano = Wednesday
Alhamisi = Thursday
Ijumaa = Friday

Look at the Kiswahili words. Jumatatu has "tatu", which means three. Jumanne has "nne", four. Jumatano has "tano", five. That is because in the Swahili week Saturday is day 1, so Monday is day 3.

### What is the same?
Both languages use numbers to make day names. A learner who knows the numbers can name the days easily.

### What is different?
The number for Monday is one in Chinese (星期一) but three in Kiswahili (Jumatatu). So do not translate the numbers word for word. Remember that each language counts from a different starting day.

### Activity
Work in pairs. One learner says the Kiswahili day and the other says the Chinese day. For example: "Jumatano" and "xīngqīsān". Ask an adult at home how they say the days.

## PAGE 5: Activities during the week
Let us listen to short sentences about what people do on different days.

星期一到星期五,我上学。
xīngqīyī dào xīngqīwǔ, wǒ shàngxué.
From Monday to Friday, I go to school.

星期六和星期天,我在家休息。
xīngqīliù hé xīngqītiān, wǒ zài jiā xiūxi.
On Saturday and Sunday, I rest at home.

New words: 到 dào (to, until), 上学 shàngxué (go to school), 和 hé (and), 在家 zài jiā (at home), 休息 xiūxi (rest).

### Listen and answer
After you hear each sentence, answer these questions in English.
1. On which days does the speaker go to school?
2. What does the speaker do on Saturday and Sunday?
Answers: Monday to Friday. Rest at home.

### Repeat with rhythm
Read the two sentences again and clap on each syllable. The rhythm helps you keep a steady speed. Try saying the days in a rhyme: "星期一,星期二,星期三, 我上学。" (xīngqīyī, xīngqī'èr, xīngqīsān, wǒ shàngxué).

Notice that Mandarin puts the time first: "xīngqīsān wǒ shàngxué".

## PAGE 6: Managing my week
Time is important. If we plan our time well, we can do our homework, help at home and still play. Talk with a friend about how you plan your week.

Some ideas:
• Monday to Friday: school, homework, helping at home.
• Saturday: chores, sports, visiting family.
• Sunday: rest, worship, family time.

### Use Chinese to talk about your week
今天星期几? jīntiān xīngqī jǐ? What day is it today?
今天星期三。jīntiān xīngqīsān. Today is Wednesday.
The question word 几 jǐ means "which number", so 星期几 asks "week which-number", that is, which day of the week.

### Checklist for good imitation
• I listened to the tone before I repeated.
• I copied the speed of the speaker.
• I checked my sì and shí.
• I corrected myself when I made a mistake.

### Summary
You have practised numbers 一 to 十 and the days of the week from 星期一 to 星期天. You also learnt that Chinese counts the week from Monday and Kiswahili counts from Saturday. Keep imitating and your Mandarin will get better each day.

## QUIZ
1. What does 星期三 xīngqīsān mean?
   A) Monday
   B) Tuesday
   C) Wednesday
   D) Friday
   Answer: C
   Why: 三 sān is three, so 星期三 is "week three", Wednesday.
2. Which number is 七?
   A) qī, seven
   B) shí, ten
   C) sì, four
   D) liù, six
   Answer: A
   Why: 七 is pronounced qī and means seven.
3. Which word means "tomorrow"?
   A) 昨天 zuótiān
   B) 今天 jīntiān
   C) 明天 míngtiān
   D) 星期 xīngqī
   Answer: C
   Why: 明天 míngtiān is tomorrow, 昨天 is yesterday and 今天 is today.
4. In Kiswahili, which day is Jumatatu?
   A) Saturday
   B) Monday
   C) Friday
   D) Sunday
   Answer: B
   Why: Jumatatu has tatu, three. Saturday is day 1 in the Swahili week, so Monday is day 3.
5. What does 我在家休息 wǒ zài jiā xiūxi mean?
   A) I go to school
   B) I eat at home
   C) I play outside
   D) I rest at home
   Answer: D
   Why: 在家 is at home and 休息 is rest.
6. Write the days Monday to Friday in Chinese characters with pinyin. (3 marks)
   Answer: 星期一 xīngqīyī, 星期二 xīngqī'èr, 星期三 xīngqīsān, 星期四 xīngqīsì, 星期五 xīngqīwǔ.
   Why: 1 mark for two correct days, 2 marks for three or four, 3 marks for all five.
7. Explain one difference between how Chinese and Kiswahili name Monday. (2 marks)
   Answer: Chinese calls Monday 星期一 which uses the number one, but Kiswahili calls it Jumatatu which uses tatu, three, because the Swahili week starts on Saturday.
   Why: 1 mark for the different numbers and 1 mark for the reason.
8. Give three steps of good imitation when learning a new word. (3 marks)
   Answer: Listen to the whole word, repeat it slowly, then compare and correct.
   Why: 1 mark for each step.
9. Say in Chinese "Today is Wednesday" and write the pinyin. (2 marks)
   Answer: 今天星期三。 jīntiān xīngqīsān.
   Why: 1 mark for the sentence and 1 mark for the pinyin.
