## PAGE 1: Writing position words from dictation
Position words tell us where things are. In this lesson you will write them and use them in sentences that describe a home.

在 zài: is at, is in
上面 shàngmiàn: above
下面 xiàmiàn: below
旁边 pángbiān: beside
右边 yòubian: right side
左边 zuǒbian: left side

### Dictation
Your teacher reads the words aloud in a mixed order. You write the pinyin of each, and the characters if you can. Then check your list against the one above.

### Spelling points
• zài: the mark goes on a (falling tone).
• shàngmiàn: two falling marks, one on the a of shàng and one on the a of miàn. In miàn, the letters i and a are together, and the mark goes on the a.
• pángbiān: rising on a in páng and high on a in biān.
• zuǒbian and yòubian: the second syllable is light, so it has no mark.

### Write the words neatly
Write each word three times in your exercise book. Keep the size of your characters the same.

## PAGE 2: Left and right characters
The characters for left and right look similar, but are written in a different order.

左 zuǒ (left): 5 strokes
右 yòu (right): 5 strokes

### The difference in order
• 左 begins with the horizontal stroke 横, then the falling stroke 撇. The lower part is a shape like 工.
• 右 begins with the falling stroke 撇, then the horizontal 横. The lower part is 口.

### A memory trick
Look at the lower part. 口 means mouth. Most people eat with their right hand, so 右 has 口 in it. Tricks like this help you to remember which is which.

### Practise
Write 左 and 右 five times each. Count the strokes and say the order aloud: "héng, piě, ..." for 左 and "piě, héng, ..." for 右. Ask a partner to check.

## PAGE 3: Matching pictures with sentences
Read these sentences. Each one describes an area or item in a home.

花园在房子旁边。huāyuán zài fángzi pángbiān. The garden is beside the house.
沙发在窗户下面。shāfā zài chuānghu xiàmiàn. The sofa is under the window.
浴室在卧室旁边。yùshì zài wòshì pángbiān. The bathroom is beside the bedroom.
门在左边。mén zài zuǒbian. The door is on the left.
窗户在右边。chuānghu zài yòubian. The window is on the right.

### New characters
花 huā: flower (7 strokes)
园 yuán: garden, park (7 strokes)
门 mén: door (3 strokes)
户 hù: door, household (4 strokes)
旁 páng: side (10 strokes)
边 biān: side, edge (5 strokes)
面 miàn: surface, face (9 strokes)

### Match
Draw simple pictures for each sentence. Or use picture cards. Stick each picture next to its sentence. Read the sentence aloud, and point at the object in the picture.

### Check
Ask your partner to point to the position word in each sentence. For example, in 沙发在窗户下面, the position word is 下面.

## PAGE 4: Filling in the blanks
Fill the blanks with the correct words. Use pictures to help you.

### Example
Picture: a bathroom beside a garden.
浴室在花园______。
Answer: 旁边. So the sentence is 浴室在花园旁边。 The bathroom is beside the garden.

### Practise
1. 沙发在门______。 (Answer: 旁边)
2. 窗户在门______。 (Answer: 上面)
3. 花园在房子______。 (Answer: 右边)
4. 门在______。 (Answer: 左边)

### How to fill a blank
1. Read the whole sentence.
2. Look at the picture.
3. Find the position word that fits.
4. Write it neatly and check your tone marks.
5. Read the finished sentence aloud.

### Try a picture of your own
Draw a room. Put a bed on the left and a window on the right. Write two sentences: 床在左边。 窗户在右边。 chuáng zài zuǒbian. chuānghu zài yòubian. Check whether each one matches the picture.

## PAGE 5: Constructing sentences and describing a model home
You can build a sentence from a set of words. Work with a friend.

### Word sets
Set 1: 沙发 / 在 / 门 / 旁边
Answer: 沙发在门旁边。

Set 2: 浴室 / 花园 / 旁边 / 在
Answer: 浴室在花园旁边。

Set 3: 窗户 / 在 / 沙发 / 上面
Answer: 窗户在沙发上面。

### The rule
Thing + 在 + place + position word.
Check: does your sentence follow the pattern? Does it end with 。?

### Model home
Make a model of a home in your classroom from boxes and paper. Give it a garden, a door, a window, a sofa and a bathroom. Then write three sentences describing where things are in your model. For example:
花园在房子旁边。
沙发在窗户下面。
门在左边。
Swap your writing with another group. Read their sentences and see whether they match their model. Give feedback: say what is clear and what you found hard to follow.

### Take care
Use safe materials. Ask an adult for help with scissors. Share the work so that everyone has a job.

## PAGE 6: Challenges in writing Chinese
Writing in Chinese can be difficult at first. Let us talk about the challenges and find ways to solve them.

### Common challenges
• Remembering stroke order.
• Remembering tone marks.
• Telling similar characters apart, for example 大 and 太.
• Writing neatly in the square.
• Remembering the meaning of a character.

### Possible solutions
• Practise a few characters each day.
• Use flashcards with the strokes numbered.
• Ask a friend to check your work.
• Trace first and write later.
• Say the strokes aloud as you write.

### Identify your own challenge
Write down one thing that you find difficult, and one way you will try to make it easier. Share this with a partner and ask for advice. It is brave to say what you find hard, and it helps others to help you.

### Common mistakes
• Writing 左 and 右 in the wrong order.
• Forgetting to put the position word after the place.
• Leaving out 。 at the end.

### Summary
You have written position words, learnt the stroke order of 左 and 右, matched pictures to sentences, filled blanks, built sentences and described a model home. Keep asking for help when you need it, and keep practising.

## QUIZ
1. Which stroke do you write first in 右?
   A) 横 héng
   B) 撇 piě
   C) 竖 shù
   D) 点 diǎn
   Answer: B
   Why: 右 begins with the falling stroke 撇, and 左 begins with 横.
2. What does 旁边 pángbiān mean?
   A) Above
   B) Below
   C) Beside
   D) Inside
   Answer: C
   Why: 旁边 means beside or next to.
3. Which sentence means "The sofa is under the window"?
   A) 沙发在窗户上面。
   B) 沙发在窗户下面。
   C) 窗户在沙发下面。
   D) 沙发在门旁边。
   Answer: B
   Why: 沙发 is the sofa, 窗户 is the window and 下面 is below, so the sofa is below the window.
4. Fill in the blank: 浴室在花园______。 (beside)
   A) 上面
   B) 下面
   C) 旁边
   D) 左边
   Answer: C
   Why: The bathroom is beside the garden, so the word is 旁边.
5. How many strokes do 左 and 右 each have?
   A) 3
   B) 4
   C) 5
   D) 6
   Answer: C
   Why: Both 左 and 右 have five strokes.
6. Rearrange the words: 在 / 花园 / 房子 / 旁边. Write the sentence and its meaning. (3 marks)
   Answer: 花园在房子旁边。 The garden is beside the house.
   Why: 1 mark for the order, 1 mark for 。, 1 mark for the meaning.
7. Write a sentence in Chinese with pinyin: "The door is on the left." (2 marks)
   Answer: 门在左边。 mén zài zuǒbian.
   Why: 1 mark for the sentence and 1 mark for pinyin.
8. Describe one challenge you may face in writing Chinese and one way to solve it. (2 marks)
   Answer: For example, remembering stroke order; use flashcards with numbered strokes and practise a little every day.
   Why: 1 mark for a challenge and 1 mark for a sensible solution.
9. Explain the memory trick that helps you tell 右 from 左. (2 marks)
   Answer: 右 has 口 (mouth) in it, and most people eat with the right hand.
   Why: 1 mark for the 口 part and 1 mark for the link to the right hand.
