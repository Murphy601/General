## PAGE 1: Matching pictures with words
The weather is part of our environment. Today you will write about it. First, let us make sure we can match the pictures with the words in both pinyin and characters.

太阳 tàiyáng: sun
云 yún: cloud
风 fēng: wind
雨 yǔ: rain
雪 xuě: snow

### Match the pictures
Your teacher gives you picture cards and word cards. Work in a team. Put each picture next to its word. Each word card has two lines: pinyin and characters. Make sure each pair is right. Read your matches aloud.

### Stroke counts
云 has 4 strokes.
风 has 4 strokes.
雨 has 8 strokes.
雪 has 11 strokes.
太 has 4 strokes.
阳 has 6 strokes.
Count as you write each one. 雪 looks tall, and its top part looks like the top of 雨 (rain). Snow and rain both fall from the sky, so it is easy to remember that their tops look alike.

### Write the words
Write each word in pinyin and characters, three times each. Check that the tone marks are right: tàiyáng, yún, fēng, yǔ, xuě.

## PAGE 2: True or false sentences
Read or listen to a text, then decide whether each sentence is true or false.

Text:
今天有太阳,没有雨。风很小。
jīntiān yǒu tàiyáng, méiyǒu yǔ. fēng hěn xiǎo.
Today there is sun, and there is no rain. The wind is very light.

New word: 没有 méiyǒu (there is not, do not have).

### Statements
1. 今天有太阳。 jīntiān yǒu tàiyáng. (Today there is sun.) True.
2. 今天有雨。 jīntiān yǒu yǔ. (Today there is rain.) False.
3. 风很大。 fēng hěn dà. (The wind is strong.) False. The text says the wind is light, 很小.

### Mark them
In Chinese, we can write 对 duì (true) or 错 cuò (wrong). Write 对 or 错 next to each statement.

### Write your own
Write two true sentences and one false sentence about today's weather. Swap with a partner. Your partner marks each one 对 or 错.

### Working together
Compare your answers with another pair. If you disagree, go back to the text and find the words that give the answer. Give reasons, not just opinions.

## PAGE 3: Writing a short text about weather
Look at pictures and write a simple text about them. Use the vocabulary you know.

Example picture: a rainy day.
今天下雨。雨很大。风也很大。
jīntiān xià yǔ. yǔ hěn dà. fēng yě hěn dà.
Today it rains. The rain is heavy. The wind is also strong.

### How to write it
1. Look at the picture carefully.
2. Say what you see in your head: sun, cloud, rain, wind or snow?
3. Write one sentence at a time.
4. Put 。 at the end of each sentence.
5. Read the text aloud to check it.

### Useful words
下雨 xià yǔ: it rains
刮风 guā fēng: the wind blows
有云 yǒu yún: there are clouds
很大 hěn dà: very big or heavy
很小 hěn xiǎo: very small or light
也 yě: also

### A weather diary
Keep a weather diary for a school week. Each day, write the day and the weather.

## PAGE 4: Sentence scrabble
In a sentence scrabble, you rearrange scrambled words to make a correct sentence.

Set 1: 很 / 太阳 / 大
Answer: 太阳很大。 The sun is very big.

Set 2: 风 / 很 / 小
Answer: 风很小。 The wind is very light.

Set 3: 下 / 今天 / 雨
Answer: 今天下雨。 Today it rains.

Set 4: 有 / 云 / 今天
Answer: 今天有云。 Today there are clouds.

### Rules for word order
• Time words such as 今天 come first.
• The weather thing comes next, or the action such as 下.
• Describing words such as 大 or 小 come after 很.
• Put 。 at the end.

### Make your own
Write your own sentence on a card and cut it into words. Give the words to a partner. Can your partner put them in the right order? Check the answer together.

### Give useful feedback
When your partner gets one wrong, say why in a kind way. For example: "Try putting 很 before 大."

## PAGE 5: Draw and label the weather
Draw the five weather features and label them.

### What to draw
• The sun with rays.
• A cloud.
• Wind lines.
• Raindrops.
• Snowflakes.
Colour each picture. Under each picture write the Chinese characters and the pinyin. Make your writing neat.

### Check your labels
• Characters: correct strokes?
• Pinyin: correct spelling and tone marks?
• Neatness: can everyone read it?

### Weather and the environment
Discuss with your group. How does each weather feature affect the environment?
• The sun helps plants to grow, but too much sun makes the land very dry.
• Rain gives water to crops, rivers and animals, but too much rain causes floods.
• Wind carries clouds and seeds, but very strong wind can break trees.
• Clouds bring rain.
Write one sentence in English about how a weather feature affects your area. For example: "The rain helps the maize to grow."

### Looking after the environment
Plant trees, save water and keep the surroundings clean. We can each help to keep the environment healthy.

## PAGE 6: Give feedback and improve
Good writers ask others to read their work and give suggestions.

### How to give feedback
1. Read your partner's text once.
2. Say one thing you liked.
3. Say one suggestion, for example: "Add 也 to join two sentences."
4. Check the spelling, tone marks and punctuation.

### How to use feedback
Listen carefully. Do not argue. Rewrite your text with the good ideas and read it aloud again.

### Checklist for a weather text
• I used weather words correctly.
• My sentences have the right word order.
• I used 。 at the end of each sentence.
• My tone marks and characters are neat.

### Common mistakes
• Leaving out 很 in 太阳很大.
• Confusing 雨 and 雪.
• Writing the characters too small and squashed.

### Being honest
Give honest feedback with kindness. If everybody is truthful, we all improve.

### Summary
You have matched pictures with words, judged true and false sentences, written a short weather text, done a sentence scrabble, drawn and labelled weather features and given feedback.

## QUIZ
1. How many strokes does the character 雨 have?
   A) 4
   B) 6
   C) 8
   D) 11
   Answer: C
   Why: 雨 has 8 strokes. 雪 has 11.
2. What does 没有 méiyǒu mean?
   A) Also
   B) There is not
   C) Very
   D) Today
   Answer: B
   Why: 没有 means there is not or do not have.
3. Which sentence is correct for "Today it rains"?
   A) 下今天雨。
   B) 雨今天下。
   C) 今天下雨。
   D) 今天雨下。
   Answer: C
   Why: The pattern is 今天 + 下 + 雨.
4. What does 风很小 mean?
   A) The wind is very light
   B) The rain is heavy
   C) The sun is big
   D) The cloud is small
   Answer: A
   Why: 风 is wind, 很 is very and 小 is small or light.
5. What does 错 cuò mean in a true-or-false exercise?
   A) Right
   B) Wrong
   C) Rain
   D) Today
   Answer: B
   Why: 错 means wrong. 对 means right.
6. Rearrange and write: 有 / 太阳 / 今天. Give the meaning. (2 marks)
   Answer: 今天有太阳。 Today there is sun.
   Why: 1 mark for the order and 1 mark for the meaning.
7. Write a short two-sentence text about a rainy day in Chinese with pinyin. (3 marks)
   Answer: 今天下雨。雨很大。 jīntiān xià yǔ. yǔ hěn dà.
   Why: 1 mark for each correct sentence and 1 mark for pinyin.
8. Give one way that rain helps the environment and one way that too much rain can harm it. (2 marks)
   Answer: Rain gives water to crops, rivers and animals. Too much rain can cause floods.
   Why: 1 mark for a benefit and 1 mark for a harm.
9. Give two rules for kind, useful feedback on a friend's writing. (2 marks)
   Answer: Say one thing you liked, give one clear suggestion and check spelling and punctuation, and be honest and kind.
   Why: 1 mark for each rule.
