## PAGE 1: Reading a text about the home
When you read a text, you read to understand it. In this lesson you will read about a home and find its main idea and details. You will also practise decoding: working out how to say a word by looking at its letters.

Read this text aloud, slowly at first.

这是我的家。我家有花园。花园旁边是房子。房子里有客厅、厨房、卧室和浴室。客厅里有沙发,沙发在窗户下面。

Pinyin:
Zhè shì wǒ de jiā. Wǒ jiā yǒu huāyuán. Huāyuán pángbiān shì fángzi. Fángzi lǐ yǒu kètīng, chúfáng, wòshì hé yùshì. Kètīng lǐ yǒu shāfā, shāfā zài chuānghu xiàmiàn.

Meaning:
This is my home. My home has a garden. Beside the garden is the house. In the house there is a living room, a kitchen, a bedroom and a bathroom. In the living room there is a sofa. The sofa is under the window.

### Underline the words
Underline the words about the home and its areas: 家, 花园, 房子, 客厅, 厨房, 卧室, 浴室, 沙发, 窗户.

### Read again
Read the text a second time in pairs. Take turns reading a sentence each. Pay attention to full stops and commas.

## PAGE 2: Main idea and details
The main idea is what the whole text is about. The details are the smaller facts that support the main idea.

### Find the main idea
Ask: what is this text mostly about? It is about "my home". The first sentence tells us: 这是我的家。

### Find the details
Ask: what facts does the text give?
• The home has a garden.
• The house is beside the garden.
• There are four rooms: living room, kitchen, bedroom and bathroom.
• There is a sofa in the living room, under the window.

### Match title cards and detail cards
Your teacher prepares cards in a basket. Some are title cards and some are detail cards. Match each detail with its title.
Title: 客厅 (living room). Detail: 客厅里有沙发。 (There is a sofa in the living room.)
Title: 花园 (garden). Detail: 花园旁边是房子。 (The house is beside the garden.)
Title: 房子 (house). Detail: 房子里有四个房间。 (There are four rooms in the house.)
The word 四个 sì gè means four (of something).

### Why is this useful?
When you find the main idea first, the details make more sense. You can then answer questions about a text more quickly, and you can explain it to a friend in your own words.

## PAGE 3: Decoding with sound patterns
Decoding means using what you know about letters and sounds to read a new word. Many Mandarin syllables share patterns. When you know one, you can read many others.

### Pattern 1: the final -ao
好 hǎo (good)
老 lǎo (old)
小 xiǎo (small)
早 zǎo (early, morning)
叫 jiào (to be called)
Each of these has the same final "ao", said like the "ow" in "cow" (in jiào and xiǎo it is "iao"). Read the list aloud.

### Pattern 2: the final -ang
房 fáng (house, room)
旁 páng (side)
上 shàng (on, above)
阳 yáng (sun)
Each of these has "ang", said like "ahng". Read the list aloud.

### Try a new word
Suppose you meet the word 早上 zǎoshang. You know zǎo has the "ao" pattern, and shang has the "ang" pattern. Now you can read it: zǎoshang. It means morning.

### Word hunt
Work with a friend. Look through your class texts and pick words that have the patterns "ao" or "ang". Write them on a chart. Read them aloud together.

### Word wall
Make a word wall on a large sheet. Put one pattern on each column and stick the words under it. Add new words every week.

## PAGE 4: Using clues to work out an unknown word
What if you do not know a word? Look at the words and sentences around it. They can give clues to the meaning.

Read: 客厅里有沙发。
Suppose you do not know 沙发, but you know 客厅 (living room) and 有 (there is). It is something in a living room. If there is a picture, you will see a sofa.

Read: 浴室在花园旁边。
Suppose you do not know 浴室 yùshì, but you know that 花园 is a garden and 旁边 means beside. You also know that a house has rooms. A room beside the garden could be a bathroom.

### Steps
1. Read the whole sentence.
2. Look at words you know.
3. Look at pictures.
4. Guess. Then check with a friend or a dictionary.

### Practise
Read: 窗户在门上面。 If you know 门 (door) and 上面 (above), what do you think 窗户 is? It is above a door. Look at the picture: it is a window. 窗户 chuānghu = window.

### Guess and check
Write your guess for three new words on a slip of paper. Then check your answers together. Be confident. Guessing helps you become a strong reader.

## PAGE 5: Reading about places in the home
Read the sentences aloud. Each one tells where something is. Answer the question after each one.

1. 沙发在门旁边。 shāfā zài mén pángbiān.
Where is the sofa? (beside the door)

2. 花园在房子右边。 huāyuán zài fángzi yòubian.
Where is the garden? (on the right of the house)

3. 浴室在卧室旁边。 yùshì zài wòshì pángbiān.
Where is the bathroom? (beside the bedroom)

4. 窗户在沙发上面。 chuānghu zài shāfā shàngmiàn.
Where is the window? (above the sofa)

### Draw it
Choose one sentence and draw the picture. Label the drawing with characters and pinyin. Show it to a partner and ask whether it matches the sentence.

### Reading with confidence
It is normal to meet words you do not know. Keep calm. Use your patterns, pictures and clues. Confidence grows every time you try.

### Why put things in their place?
When things are put in their proper places, the home is orderly, and people can find them easily and stay safe. A window above a sofa, a bed in a bedroom and a garden outside are all in their proper places.

## PAGE 6: Review and summary
Let us look back at what you have learnt.

### Reading skills
• Read aloud with correct tones and pauses.
• Find the main idea first, then the details.
• Use sound patterns (ao, ang) to decode new words.
• Use clues from words and pictures to work out meaning.

### Vocabulary review
花园 huāyuán garden
沙发 shāfā sofa
浴室 yùshì bathroom
窗户 chuānghu window
门 mén door
旁边 pángbiān beside
左边 zuǒbian left
右边 yòubian right
房子 fángzi house

### Common mistakes
• Reading a new word letter by letter and forgetting its tone.
• Forgetting that the main idea is usually in the first sentence.
• Giving up when you meet a new word instead of using clues.

### A challenge
Write two sentences about your own home, using words from this lesson. Read them aloud to a partner. Ask your partner to find the main idea and one detail.

### Summary
Reading is more than saying words. It means understanding, decoding and thinking about what you read. Practise every day.

## QUIZ
1. What is the main idea of the text "这是我的家..."?
   A) A school
   B) A market
   C) My home
   D) A garden only
   Answer: C
   Why: The first sentence says 这是我的家, so the text is about my home.
2. What does 早上 zǎoshang mean?
   A) Evening
   B) Morning
   C) Afternoon
   D) Night
   Answer: B
   Why: 早 means early and 上 is used here, so 早上 is morning.
3. Which two words share the final "ang"?
   A) 好 and 老
   B) 房 and 旁
   C) 小 and 早
   D) 门 and 手
   Answer: B
   Why: fáng and páng both end in ang.
4. What does 花园旁边是房子 mean?
   A) The house is in the garden
   B) The garden is under the house
   C) Beside the garden is the house
   D) The house has no garden
   Answer: C
   Why: 旁边 is beside, so the house is beside the garden.
5. When you meet a word you do not know, what should you do first?
   A) Skip the whole page
   B) Look for clues in the words and pictures around it
   C) Stop reading
   D) Say it in another tone
   Answer: B
   Why: Nearby words and pictures give clues to the meaning.
6. Read: 客厅里有沙发。 What does it mean? (2 marks)
   Answer: In the living room there is a sofa. 客厅 is the living room, 里 is inside, 有 is there is and 沙发 is a sofa.
   Why: 1 mark for the sentence meaning and 1 mark for correctly linking the words.
7. Write two words with the final "ao" and give pinyin. (2 marks)
   Answer: For example 好 hǎo and 小 xiǎo.
   Why: 1 mark for each correct word with pinyin.
8. Explain the difference between the main idea and the details of a text. (2 marks)
   Answer: The main idea is what the whole text is about. The details are the smaller facts that support it.
   Why: 1 mark for each correct explanation.
9. Describe how you can work out the meaning of an unknown word in a sentence. (3 marks)
   Answer: Read the whole sentence, look at words that you already know, and look at pictures nearby. Then make a guess and check with a friend or a dictionary.
   Why: 1 mark for using known words, 1 mark for pictures, 1 mark for guessing and checking.
