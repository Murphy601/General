## PAGE 1: Key words about birthdays
When you read a text, some words carry the main meaning. These are the key words. In a birthday text, the key words are about the party. Read them aloud.

生日 shēngrì: birthday
快乐 kuàilè: happy
蛋糕 dàngāo: cake
礼物 lǐwù: gift
蜡烛 làzhú: candle
朋友 péngyou: friend
唱歌 chànggē: to sing
切 qiē: to cut
送 sòng: to give (as a gift)
玩 wán: to play

### Circle the key words
Read this line. Circle the birthday words in your exercise book.
今天是小红的生日,朋友们送她礼物,她很快乐。
jīntiān shì Xiǎohóng de shēngrì, péngyoumen sòng tā lǐwù, tā hěn kuàilè.
Today is Xiaohong's birthday. Her friends give her gifts and she is very happy.
Key words: 生日, 朋友, 送, 礼物, 快乐.
The new word 她 tā means she or her.

### Listen and check
Your partner reads the key words aloud. You check that you circled the same words. If you missed one, talk about why it is important. Words that name people, things and actions are usually the key words.

## PAGE 2: Answering questions about birthdays
Reading and answering questions shows that you understand. Read each question aloud, then answer.

你生日是几月几号?
nǐ shēngrì shì jǐ yuè jǐ hào?
When is your birthday?

Sample answer:
我的生日是五月十号。
wǒ de shēngrì shì wǔ yuè shí hào.
My birthday is on 10 May.

过生日的时候会做什么?
guò shēngrì de shíhou huì zuò shénme?
What do you do when you have a birthday?

Sample answer:
我们唱歌,切蛋糕,和朋友一起玩。
wǒmen chànggē, qiē dàngāo, hé péngyou yìqǐ wán.
We sing, cut the cake and play with friends.

### More numbers for dates
To read dates from the 11th onwards, use these patterns.
十一 shíyī: 11
十二 shí'èr: 12
二十 èrshí: 20
二十一 èrshíyī: 21
So 12 December is 十二月十二号 (shí'èr yuè shí'èr hào).

### Practise in groups
Ask each learner in your group when their birthday is. Read the questions and answers aloud together. Who has a birthday in the same month as you?

## PAGE 3: Stress and rhythm
Stress means saying certain syllables or words a little stronger. Rhythm is the beat of a sentence, made by how the words are grouped and spaced. Good stress and rhythm make your reading sound natural.

### Grouping words
Read each phrase in small groups of syllables, with a tiny pause between groups.
祝你 / 生日 / 快乐!
zhù nǐ / shēngrì / kuàilè!
Clap on each group: three claps.

我们 / 唱歌,
wǒmen / chànggē,
切 / 蛋糕。
qiē / dàngāo.

### Stress the important word
In Chinese we stress the word that carries the important information. Try these:
• If you want to say the cake is what you cut, stress 蛋糕: 我们切蛋糕。
• If you want to say we, not they, cut it, stress 我们: 我们切蛋糕。
Say the sentence twice, and let a friend guess which word you stressed.

### Keep the tones
Stress makes a syllable a little louder and longer, but it does not change the tone. Even stressed, nǐ is still dipping and hǎo is still dipping.

### Read with rhythm
Tap the desk once for each group. Keep the beats even. This makes the reading smooth, like a song.

## PAGE 4: Jumbled sentences
Rearranging jumbled words helps you understand how Chinese sentences work. Here are some puzzles.

Puzzle 1: 蛋糕 / 切 / 我们
Answer: 我们切蛋糕。 wǒmen qiē dàngāo. We cut the cake.

Puzzle 2: 礼物 / 送 / 朋友 / 我
Answer: 朋友送我礼物。 péngyou sòng wǒ lǐwù. My friend gives me a gift.

Puzzle 3: 快乐 / 生日 / 祝 / 你
Answer: 祝你生日快乐。 zhù nǐ shēngrì kuàilè. Happy birthday.

### Rules for word order
• The doer comes first (我们, 朋友).
• The action comes next (切, 送).
• The thing follows (蛋糕, 礼物).
This order is: who, what action, what thing.

### Read the puzzles aloud
After you solve a puzzle, read the correct sentence to your partner with good rhythm. Take turns making up a jumbled sentence for your partner.

### Try a harder one
Put these words in order: 唱歌 / 我们 / 朋友 / 和 Answer: 我们和朋友唱歌。 (We sing with friends.) Here 和 hé means "and" or "with".

## PAGE 5: A digital reading journal
A reading journal is a place where you keep the words and sentences you have learnt. A digital journal is one that you make on a computer, tablet or phone. If you cannot use a device, you can keep a paper journal in your exercise book.

### What to put in your journal
1. New words with pinyin and meaning.
2. A sentence you can read fluently.
3. A recording of yourself reading, if you have a device.
4. A tick or a star showing how well you read it.

### Sample journal page
Word: 蛋糕 dàngāo (cake)
Sentence: 我们切蛋糕。
My reading: I can read it well. I need to work on the q in qiē (a smiling sound with a puff of air).
Date: Tuesday

### Self-assessment
Listen to your recording. Ask yourself:
• Did I say all the tones correctly?
• Was my speed steady?
• Did I stress the important word?
Write one thing you did well and one thing you will do better. This honest checking is called self-assessment. It shows that you care about learning.

### Staying safe online
If you use a device, ask an adult for permission first. Do not share your name, school or home address with strangers. Recordings should be shared only with your teacher and classmates.

## PAGE 6: Reading aloud helps us learn
Why do we read aloud? When we read aloud, we hear our own voice, practise pronunciation and share ideas with others. It also builds confidence. The more you read aloud, the easier it becomes.

### Birthday text for the class
Read this text with a friend, taking turns to read sentences.
今天是我朋友的生日。我们唱歌,切蛋糕。我送朋友礼物。大家都很快乐。
jīntiān shì wǒ péngyou de shēngrì. wǒmen chànggē, qiē dàngāo. wǒ sòng péngyou lǐwù. dàjiā dōu hěn kuàilè.
Today is my friend's birthday. We sing and cut the cake. I give my friend a gift. Everybody is very happy.
New words: 大家 dàjiā (everybody), 都 dōu (all).

### Peer assessment
Your friend listens and tells you:
• one word that you read very clearly,
• one word you need to practise.
Then swap roles. Always be gentle and honest.

### Common mistakes
• Stressing every word equally, so the sentence sounds flat.
• Putting the action before the person: for example 切我们蛋糕.
• Forgetting the neutral tone in péngyou.

### Summary
You can find key words, answer birthday questions, read with stress and rhythm, rearrange jumbled sentences and keep a reading journal. Keep reading aloud with a positive attitude.

## QUIZ
1. What does 礼物 lǐwù mean?
   A) Cake
   B) Candle
   C) Friend
   D) Gift
   Answer: D
   Why: 礼物 is gift or present.
2. Which sentence is in the correct order for "We cut the cake"?
   A) 蛋糕切我们。
   B) 切我们蛋糕。
   C) 我们切蛋糕。
   D) 我们蛋糕切。
   Answer: C
   Why: The order is who, action, thing: 我们 + 切 + 蛋糕.
3. What does 十二月十二号 mean?
   A) 12 December
   B) 2 October
   C) 10 December
   D) 12 January
   Answer: A
   Why: 十二月 is December and 十二号 is the twelfth day.
4. What is stress in reading aloud?
   A) Reading without tones
   B) Making an important word a little stronger
   C) Reading in a whisper
   D) Stopping after every word
   Answer: B
   Why: Stress makes an important word or syllable stronger and longer but does not change its tone.
5. What is a good rule for staying safe with a digital reading journal?
   A) Post your address online
   B) Share recordings with strangers
   C) Ask an adult first and share only with your teacher and class
   D) Ignore adults
   Answer: C
   Why: You should ask an adult for permission and keep personal information private.
6. Rearrange to make a sentence and give the meaning: 祝 / 快乐 / 你 / 生日. (2 marks)
   Answer: 祝你生日快乐。 Happy birthday to you.
   Why: 1 mark for the sentence and 1 mark for the meaning.
7. Write your own answer to 你生日是几月几号? Use the pattern 我的生日是...月...号。 Give the pinyin. (3 marks)
   Answer: Any correct date, for example 我的生日是三月五号。 wǒ de shēngrì shì sān yuè wǔ hào.
   Why: 1 mark for the pattern, 1 mark for a correct date, 1 mark for pinyin.
8. Name three things you can put in a reading journal. (3 marks)
   Answer: New words with meaning, a sentence you can read well, a recording of you reading, or a note about how well you read.
   Why: 1 mark for each correct item.
9. Explain why reading aloud is a good way to learn Mandarin. (2 marks)
   Answer: You hear your own pronunciation and tones, you practise, and it builds confidence.
   Why: 1 mark for pronunciation practice and 1 mark for another sensible reason.
