## PAGE 1: Rooms and things in the house
The place we live in is part of our surroundings. In this lesson you will learn Mandarin words for rooms and things in a house. Listen to your teacher and repeat each word.

Rooms
厨房 chúfáng: kitchen
客厅 kètīng: living room
卧室 wòshì: bedroom
厕所 cèsuǒ: toilet

Things
桌子 zhuōzi: table
椅子 yǐzi: chair
床 chuáng: bed
电视 diànshì: television
冰箱 bīngxiāng: fridge

## PAGE 2: Blending syllables into words
Most Mandarin words for things at home have two syllables. A word with two syllables is called a disyllabic word. To make one, you blend two syllables together smoothly with no gap.

Syllable + syllable = word
zhuō + zi = zhuōzi (table)
yǐ + zi = yǐzi (chair)
diàn + shì = diànshì (television)
bīng + xiāng = bīngxiāng (fridge)
chú + fáng = chúfáng (kitchen)
kè + tīng = kètīng (living room)
wò + shì = wòshì (bedroom)
cè + suǒ = cèsuǒ (toilet)

### How to blend
1. Say the first syllable with its tone.
2. Say the second syllable with its tone.
3. Slide them together so they sound like one word.
Notice that in zhuōzi and yǐzi the second syllable zi is short and light. That is the neutral tone again.

### Picture and syllable game
Your teacher shows a picture, such as a chair. You are given two syllable cards, yǐ and zi. Put them side by side, blend them, and say the word aloud. Then your partner tries with a new picture. Try to blend the cards for a fridge, a kitchen and a bed. Careful: 床 chuáng is only one syllable. Some words have one syllable only.

## PAGE 3: Where is it? 在, 上 and 下
Three small words help us say where something is.
在 zài: is at, is in
上 shàng: on, above
下 xià: under, below

The word 在 comes right after the thing we are talking about.
电视在客厅。diànshì zài kètīng. The television is in the living room.
冰箱在厨房。bīngxiāng zài chúfáng. The fridge is in the kitchen.
床在卧室。chuáng zài wòshì. The bed is in the bedroom.

To say "on" or "under" a piece of furniture, put 上 or 下 after the furniture word.
电视在桌子上。diànshì zài zhuōzi shàng. The television is on the table.
猫在椅子下。māo zài yǐzi xià. The cat is under the chair.

### Say the location
Look around your classroom. Say where things are: "书在桌子上" (shū zài zhuōzi shàng: the book is on the table). Here 书 shū means book.

### Sound reminder
zài and xià are falling tones. shàng also falls. Practise the falling pattern in "zài zhuōzi shàng": fall, high, light, fall.

## PAGE 4: Questions about location
When you want to ask where something is, use 哪儿 nǎr. It means "where". Put 哪儿 in the place of the answer.

电视在哪儿? diànshì zài nǎr? Where is the television?
电视在桌子上。diànshì zài zhuōzi shàng. The television is on the table.

冰箱在哪儿? bīngxiāng zài nǎr? Where is the fridge?
冰箱在厨房。bīngxiāng zài chúfáng. The fridge is in the kitchen.

### Word order in a simple sentence
Mandarin word order here is easy.
Thing + 在 + place.
The question keeps the same order. Only the question word 哪儿 appears where the place would be.

### Practise in pairs
Person A asks: 椅子在哪儿? (yǐzi zài nǎr?)
Person B answers: 椅子在桌子下。 (yǐzi zài zhuōzi xià.)
Change the things: bed, television, fridge.

### Notice the tone
nǎr is a third tone. When you ask a question your voice may rise at the end, but keep the third tone in nǎ clear.

## PAGE 5: Building sentences from jumbled words
Sentence building is like solving a puzzle. Here are word cards in a mixed-up order.
在 / 桌子 / 电视 / 上
Put them in order: 电视在桌子上。 The television is on the table.

Another set:
冰箱 / 在 / 厨房
Correct: 冰箱在厨房。

### Rules to check
• Start with the thing you are describing.
• Then 在.
• Then the place.
• If the place is on or under a piece of furniture, put 上 or 下 after the furniture.

### Try these
Put in order:
1. 床 / 卧室 / 在
2. 椅子 / 下 / 桌子 / 在
Answers: 床在卧室。 椅子在桌子下。

### Keeping order at home
Why do we keep things in their places at home? When shoes, toys and books are placed where they belong, the house is neat and safe. Nobody trips over a toy left on the floor. Talk with your classmates about where you put your school bag and shoes at home. Use the words 在, 上 and 下.

## PAGE 6: Say it clearly
Correct pronunciation is important because a small change in tone can change the word. For example 床 chuáng (bed) and 窗 chuāng (window) look and sound close, but they are not the same word.

### Review the vocabulary
厨房 chúfáng kitchen
客厅 kètīng living room
卧室 wòshì bedroom
厕所 cèsuǒ toilet
桌子 zhuōzi table
椅子 yǐzi chair
床 chuáng bed
电视 diànshì television
冰箱 bīngxiāng fridge

### Sing it
Make a short song using a tune you know. Line 1: 厨房有冰箱 (chúfáng yǒu bīngxiāng: the kitchen has a fridge). Line 2: 卧室有床 (wòshì yǒu chuáng: the bedroom has a bed). Line 3: 客厅有电视 (kètīng yǒu diànshì: the living room has a television). The word 有 yǒu means "has, there is".

### Common mistakes
• Saying 在 after the place instead of before it.
• Forgetting that zi in zhuōzi and yǐzi is light.
• Leaving out 哪儿 in a question.

## QUIZ
1. What does 厨房 chúfáng mean?
   A) Bedroom
   B) Toilet
   C) Living room
   D) Kitchen
   Answer: D
   Why: 厨房 is the kitchen. Bedroom is 卧室 and living room is 客厅.
2. Which pair of syllables blends into the word for "chair"?
   A) zhuō + zi
   B) yǐ + zi
   C) diàn + shì
   D) bīng + xiāng
   Answer: B
   Why: yǐ + zi = yǐzi, chair. zhuōzi is table.
3. Choose the correct sentence: "The bed is in the bedroom".
   A) 床在卧室。
   B) 在床卧室。
   C) 卧室在床。
   D) 床卧室在。
   Answer: A
   Why: The order is thing + 在 + place: 床 + 在 + 卧室.
4. What does 哪儿 nǎr mean?
   A) Who
   B) On
   C) Where
   D) Under
   Answer: C
   Why: 哪儿 means where, so 电视在哪儿? asks where the television is.
5. In 电视在桌子上, which word means "on"?
   A) 在
   B) 上
   C) 电
   D) 视
   Answer: B
   Why: 上 shàng means on or above. 在 means is at.
6. Blend the syllables and write the meaning: bīng + xiāng. (2 marks)
   Answer: bīngxiāng 冰箱, fridge.
   Why: 1 mark for the blended word and 1 mark for the meaning.
7. Put these words in the correct order and give the English meaning: 在 / 椅子 / 下 / 桌子. (3 marks)
   Answer: 椅子在桌子下。 The chair is under the table.
   Why: 1 mark for 椅子 first, 1 mark for correct use of 在 and 下, 1 mark for the meaning.
8. Write the Chinese question for "Where is the television?" and answer it with "in the living room". Give pinyin. (3 marks)
   Answer: 电视在哪儿? diànshì zài nǎr? 电视在客厅。 diànshì zài kètīng.
   Why: 1 mark for the question, 1 mark for the answer, 1 mark for correct pinyin.
9. Say why keeping things in their proper places at home is a good habit. (2 marks)
   Answer: The house is tidy and safe, and it is easy to find things.
   Why: 1 mark for safety or tidiness and 1 mark for another sensible reason.
