## PAGE 1: Reading the number characters
Numbers are some of the simplest and most useful characters. Read them aloud from flashcards. Say the pinyin and then show the number with your fingers.

一 yī: 1
二 èr: 2
三 sān: 3
四 sì: 4
五 wǔ: 5
六 liù: 6
七 qī: 7
八 bā: 8
九 jiǔ: 9
十 shí: 10

### Look closely
The first three characters are easy to read because they show the number with lines: 一 has one line, 二 has two and 三 has three. The other numbers must be learnt one by one.

### Flashcard game
Your partner holds up a card. You read it aloud as fast as you can. Then you swap. To make it harder, your partner shows the cards in a mixed order, and you must keep your tones correct. Use a steady voice and do not rush.

### Fluency
Fluency means reading smoothly. You will get there with practice. Read the numbers from 一 to 十 five times. Notice how the second time is smoother than the first.

## PAGE 2: Reading the days of the week
The days of the week in Chinese are made with 星期 xīngqī (week) plus a number. Read them aloud in order.

星期一 xīngqīyī
星期二 xīngqī'èr
星期三 xīngqīsān
星期四 xīngqīsì
星期五 xīngqīwǔ
星期六 xīngqīliù
星期天 xīngqītiān

### A shorter way
People also use 周 zhōu instead of 星期 xīngqī. For example 周一 zhōuyī is Monday, and 周日 zhōurì is Sunday. Both mean the same, but 周 is shorter to write.

### Read in a chain
Sit in a circle. The first learner reads 星期一. The next reads 星期二, and so on around the class. When you reach 星期天, start again at 星期一. Keep the chain going at a good speed, and listen to check that each tone is clear.

### Arrange the cards
Your teacher gives you jumbled cards with the days. Put them in the correct order and read them aloud. Work in a group. Check each other. Let one learner read the answer to the class.

### Tips
• Notice how the pinyin always begins with xīngqī.
• Only the last syllable changes.
• Read the beginning quickly and give a clear tone to the last syllable.

## PAGE 3: Days and activities
People do different things on different days. Read these sentences and match them with the pictures on a chart.

星期一到星期五,去学校。
xīngqīyī dào xīngqīwǔ, qù xuéxiào.
From Monday to Friday, go to school.

星期六和星期天,在家休息。
xīngqīliù hé xīngqītiān, zài jiā xiūxi.
On Saturday and Sunday, rest at home.

## PAGE 4: Different days for worship
In Kenya many families set aside a day for worship. Some people go to a mosque on Friday. Some people go to church on Sunday. Read these words and phrases.

清真寺 qīngzhēnsì: mosque
教堂 jiàotáng: church
星期五,去清真寺。
xīngqīwǔ, qù qīngzhēnsì.
On Friday, go to the mosque.
星期天,去教堂。
xīngqītiān, qù jiàotáng.
On Sunday, go to church.

### Match days with activities
Read each pair and say whether it matches your experience.
1. 星期一, 去学校。
2. 星期五, 去清真寺。
3. 星期天, 去教堂。
4. 星期六, 在家休息。

Some families do other things on these days, such as visiting relatives or working on the shamba. There is no wrong answer. What is important is that you can read the sentences fluently and respectfully.

### Respect for others
Each family follows its own faith and customs. When friends share what they do on their special days, listen kindly. We can learn much about each other and keep the peace in our school.

## PAGE 5: Tone, volume and vocal projection
Reading fluently means using your tone, volume and vocal projection well.

### Tone
Every syllable needs its correct tone. Before reading a new phrase, check the tone marks. For example xīngqīwǔ has high, high and dipping tones.

### Volume
Volume is how loud or soft your voice is. In the classroom, you should read loudly enough for everyone to hear you. In a small group, you can use a softer voice. Do not shout, because shouting spoils the tones and hurts your throat.

### Vocal projection
Projection means sending your voice to the back of the room without shouting. Try this:
1. Stand up straight and breathe in slowly.
2. Open your mouth wide when you speak.
3. Speak from your chest, not from your throat.
4. Look at the person farthest from you and read to them.

### Practise
Read 星期三,去学校。 first in a whisper, then in a normal voice and then in a projecting voice. Ask your friend at the back of the room which was the clearest. It should be the last one.

### Assess your friend
Listen to a classmate reading the days of the week. Say whether the tone, volume and speed were good. Give one kind suggestion.

## PAGE 6: Read a short text about a week
Let us put everything together. Read this short text aloud. First read slowly. Then read fluently.

我星期一到星期五去学校。星期六我在家休息。星期天我去教堂。
wǒ xīngqīyī dào xīngqīwǔ qù xuéxiào. xīngqīliù wǒ zài jiā xiūxi. xīngqītiān wǒ qù jiàotáng.

Meaning: I go to school from Monday to Friday. On Saturday I rest at home. On Sunday I go to church.

### Questions
1. On which days does the speaker go to school? (Monday to Friday)
2. What does the speaker do on Saturday? (rests at home)
3. What does the speaker do on Sunday? (goes to church)

### Improve your text
Change the text to describe your own week. If you go to a mosque on Friday or to a shamba on Saturday, put your own words in. Ask your teacher for extra words if you need them.

### Common mistakes
• Reading 四 sì and 十 shí the same way.
• Speeding up so much that the tones disappear.
• Shouting instead of projecting.

### Summary
You can now read numbers one to ten and all seven days of the week. You have practised reading with the right tone and volume. Keep practising with your friends.

## QUIZ
1. Which character is the number five?
   A) 三
   B) 五
   C) 七
   D) 九
   Answer: B
   Why: 五 wǔ is five. 三 is three, 七 is seven and 九 is nine.
2. What does 星期六 xīngqīliù mean?
   A) Friday
   B) Sunday
   C) Saturday
   D) Thursday
   Answer: C
   Why: 六 liù is six, so 星期六 is Saturday.
3. What does 教堂 jiàotáng mean?
   A) Mosque
   B) School
   C) Home
   D) Church
   Answer: D
   Why: 教堂 is church. 清真寺 is mosque.
4. What is vocal projection?
   A) Reading in a whisper
   B) Sending your voice to the back of the room without shouting
   C) Reading very fast
   D) Reading without tones
   Answer: B
   Why: Projection means your voice reaches everyone clearly, with good breathing and an open mouth.
5. Which word is a short form of 星期?
   A) 周 zhōu
   B) 学 xué
   C) 去 qù
   D) 家 jiā
   Answer: A
   Why: 周 means week and is used in words like 周一 and 周日.
6. Read and give the meaning: 星期五,去清真寺。 (2 marks)
   Answer: xīngqīwǔ, qù qīngzhēnsì. On Friday, go to the mosque.
   Why: 1 mark for pinyin and 1 mark for meaning.
7. Arrange these days in the correct order and write them in Chinese: 星期三, 星期一, 星期二. (2 marks)
   Answer: 星期一, 星期二, 星期三.
   Why: 1 mark for the order and 1 mark for correct characters.
8. Write the numbers 六, 八 and 十 in pinyin and as digits. (3 marks)
   Answer: 六 liù 6, 八 bā 8, 十 shí 10.
   Why: 1 mark for each number with pinyin and digit.
9. Explain the difference between tone, volume and projection when you read aloud. (3 marks)
   Answer: Tone is the rise and fall of each syllable, volume is how loud you speak and projection is sending your voice to the back of the room clearly without shouting.
   Why: 1 mark for each correct meaning.
