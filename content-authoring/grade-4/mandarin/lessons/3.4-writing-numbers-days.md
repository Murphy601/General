## PAGE 1: Tracing the number characters
Numbers are one of the first things to write in any language. Let us trace and write the Chinese characters for 1 to 10.

一 yī: 1
二 èr: 2
三 sān: 3
四 sì: 4
五 wǔ: 5
六 liù: 6
七 qī: 7
八 bā: 8
九 jiǔ: 9
十 shí: 10

### Stroke counts
一 has 1 stroke, 二 has 2 and 三 has 3.
四 has 5 strokes and 五 has 4.
六 has 4 strokes, 七 has 2, 八 has 2, 九 has 2 and 十 has 2.

### How to trace
Put a thin sheet of paper over the characters and trace them with a pencil. Start each stroke at the correct place and follow the order. When you are confident, write them without tracing.

### Check yourself
Count the strokes of each character that you write. The number should match the list above. If it does not, look again at your character. You may have joined two strokes together or missed one out.

### Copy the pinyin
Write the pinyin of each number under the character. Mark the tones: yī, èr, sān, sì, wǔ, liù, qī, bā, jiǔ, shí.

Let us practise the stroke order of some numbers.

### Read the figure
• 一: one horizontal stroke, written from left to right.
• 二: write the top line first, then the longer bottom line.
• 三: write the lines from top to bottom, and the bottom line is the longest.
• 五: horizontal, vertical, turn, then the bottom line.
• 六: dot, horizontal, then the two strokes below.
The drawings are simple guides. Your teacher will show you how the strokes are written on the board.

### Other stroke orders
七: horizontal, then a vertical stroke that bends.
八: the left stroke first, then the right stroke.
九: the left stroke first, then the turning stroke.
十: horizontal, then vertical.

### Practise
Write each number five times, saying the strokes aloud. Rule to remember: top to bottom and left to right.

## PAGE 3: Writing the days of the week
The days of the week are made from 星期 (week) and a number.

星期一 xīngqīyī: Monday
星期二 xīngqī'èr: Tuesday
星期三 xīngqīsān: Wednesday
星期四 xīngqīsì: Thursday
星期五 xīngqīwǔ: Friday
星期六 xīngqīliù: Saturday
星期天 xīngqītiān: Sunday

### Copy the words
Copy each day in pinyin and characters. The characters 星 (9 strokes) and 期 (12 strokes) are in every day, so you will write them many times. That will make them easy to remember.

### Notice the apostrophe
In xīngqī'èr you must write an apostrophe between qī and èr, so that we know the syllables are xīng-qī-èr and not something else.

### Match with English
Match the Chinese days with English.
星期五 - Friday
星期一 - Monday
星期天 - Sunday
星期三 - Wednesday
Write the English word beside each day. Then say the answer aloud to your partner.

### Copy and check
Ask a friend to check whether your pinyin, characters and tone marks are correct.

## PAGE 4: Writing sentences about the week
We can write sentences about what we do on different days. The time word goes first, then who, then what.

我星期三去学校。
wǒ xīngqīsān qù xuéxiào.
On Wednesday I go to school.

我星期六在家休息。
wǒ xīngqīliù zài jiā xiūxi.
On Saturday I rest at home.

Both sentences have the day after 我. You can also put the day first: 星期三,我去学校。

### New characters
去 qù: to go (5 strokes)
校 xiào: school (10 strokes)
学 xué: to learn (8 strokes)
家 jiā: home (10 strokes)

### Use sentence starters
Use these starters to write about your own week.
我星期一 ______。
我星期五 ______。
我星期天 ______。
Fill the blanks with 去学校, 在家休息 or another activity you know.

### Check
Read your sentences aloud. Does each one have a day, an action and a full stop?

## PAGE 5: Jumbled words and phrases
Rearrange jumbled words to make sentences. Try these.

Puzzle 1: 去 / 我 / 学校 / 星期一
Answer: 我星期一去学校。 (I go to school on Monday.)

Puzzle 2: 休息 / 星期天 / 在家 / 我
Answer: 我星期天在家休息。 (On Sunday I rest at home.)

Puzzle 3: 今天 / 星期三 / 是
Answer: 今天是星期三。 (Today is Wednesday.)

### Rules
• The person comes first: 我.
• The day comes next: 星期一.
• The action comes last: 去学校.
The pattern is: who + when + what.
In Puzzle 3, 是 (is) comes between 今天 and 星期三.

### Solve and write
Write the correct sentence in characters and pinyin. Then compare with the person next to you. Did you both find the same order?

### Think about order
Why is order important when writing? If the words are in the wrong order, the sentence does not make sense, and readers may be confused. Order is also important when we plan our week: first school, then homework, then play.

## PAGE 6: Modelling the characters
Make the number characters big with local materials. This is fun, and it helps your memory.

### Materials you can use
• Pebbles or small stones.
• Sand on a tray.
• Grains of maize or beans.
• Plasticine or clay.
• Thread or string.

### Steps
1. Choose a number, for example 三.
2. Lay out each stroke in the right order.
3. Count the strokes.
4. Take a photo or display your work.
5. Look at your friends' work and give kind feedback.

### Working as a team
Share materials fairly. Each group makes numbers 一 to 十 together. One member makes each number, and the group puts them in order on a table. Let the group read the numbers aloud with pride.

### Common mistakes
• Missing a stroke in 四 or 五.
• Writing the days without 星期.
• Forgetting the apostrophe in xīngqī'èr.

### Summary
You have traced number characters, written days of the week, made sentences and modelled characters with local materials. Good writing needs care, order and practice.

## QUIZ
1. How many strokes does the character 三 have?
   A) 2
   B) 3
   C) 4
   D) 5
   Answer: B
   Why: 三 is three horizontal lines, so it has three strokes.
2. Which day is 星期五?
   A) Wednesday
   B) Thursday
   C) Friday
   D) Sunday
   Answer: C
   Why: 五 wǔ is five, so 星期五 is Friday.
3. Which sentence means "On Wednesday I go to school"?
   A) 我星期三去学校。
   B) 我星期三在家休息。
   C) 我星期六去学校。
   D) 星期三是今天。
   Answer: A
   Why: 星期三 is Wednesday and 去学校 is go to school.
4. In writing xīngqī'èr, what is the apostrophe for?
   A) It shows a light tone
   B) It marks a question
   C) It separates the syllables qī and èr
   D) It means "and"
   Answer: C
   Why: The apostrophe shows that the next syllable starts with a vowel and is separate.
5. In the pattern "who + when + what", which part is 星期三 in 我星期三去学校?
   A) Who
   B) When
   C) What
   D) Where
   Answer: B
   Why: 星期三 is the time, or "when".
6. Write the numbers 1, 2 and 3 in Chinese characters and pinyin. (3 marks)
   Answer: 一 yī, 二 èr, 三 sān.
   Why: 1 mark for each number with pinyin.
7. Rearrange: 休息 / 星期六 / 在家 / 我. Write the sentence and its meaning. (3 marks)
   Answer: 我星期六在家休息。 On Saturday I rest at home.
   Why: 1 mark for the order of 我 and 星期六, 1 mark for 在家休息, 1 mark for the meaning.
8. Name two materials you can use to model characters. (2 marks)
   Answer: For example pebbles and sand, or plasticine, maize grains, clay or thread.
   Why: 1 mark for each correct material.
9. Explain why the order of words in a sentence matters. (2 marks)
   Answer: If the words are in the wrong order, the sentence does not make sense and readers are confused.
   Why: 1 mark for the effect on meaning and 1 mark for the effect on the reader.
