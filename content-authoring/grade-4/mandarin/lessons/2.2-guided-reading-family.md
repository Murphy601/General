## PAGE 1: Reading the family tree
A family tree shows who is in a family and how they are connected. In this lesson you will read the family words with the correct tones, and you will answer questions about a family.

Here are the words on our tree.
爸爸 bàba: father
妈妈 māma: mother
哥哥 gēge: older brother
姐姐 jiějie: older sister
弟弟 dìdi: younger brother
妹妹 mèimei: younger sister
儿子 érzi: son
女儿 nǚ'ér: daughter

### Reading each word
Say each word slowly. First read the first syllable with its tone, then the second syllable. In bàba, māma, gēge, jiějie, dìdi and mèimei the second syllable is light. In érzi the zi is also light.

### A note on the apostrophe
In nǚ'ér you see a mark between two letters. It is called an apostrophe. It tells you where one syllable ends and the next one begins: nǚ + ér. Without it, the letters might be read the wrong way.

### Read the tree
Look at a family tree that your teacher draws on the board. The parents are at the top, the children are below. Point to each person and read the word aloud. Which words did you find easy? Which were hard?

## PAGE 2: Reading tones correctly
Tones are the music of Mandarin. Let us read some family words and notice the tone patterns.

Tone pairs in family words:
• bàba: falling + light
• māma: high + light
• gēge: high + light
• jiějie: dipping + light
• dìdi: falling + light
• mèimei: falling + light
• érzi: rising + light
• nǚ'ér: dipping + rising

### Drill
Read each line aloud, tapping the desk on each syllable.
bàba, māma
gēge, jiějie
dìdi, mèimei
érzi, nǚ'ér

### Tones can change the meaning
Look at these pairs and read them carefully.
mā 妈 mother   mǎ 马 horse
bà 爸 dad   bā 八 eight
Which pair did you read correctly? Practise until your voice does not change the tone by mistake.

### Rule of thumb
When two third tones meet, the first is said as a second tone. In jiějie, the first syllable jiě is third tone but the second is neutral, so no change is needed. In nǚ'ér, the tones are third and second, so we say them as written.

## PAGE 3: Words and pictures: true or false?
A good reader checks whether words and pictures match. In Chinese this is called 判断对错 pànduàn duì cuò, which means "judge right or wrong".

Imagine a picture of a mother. Under it are words.
Picture: a woman holding a baby.
Words: 妈妈.
Is this right? Yes. ✓ (对 duì means right.)

Picture: a boy.
Words: 妹妹.
Is this right? No. ✗ (错 cuò means wrong.) A boy is 弟弟 or 哥哥. 妹妹 is a younger sister.

### Practise
Read these pairs. Say whether each is 对 or 错.
1. Picture of a father: 爸爸 (right)
2. Picture of a girl: 儿子 (wrong, 儿子 is a son)
3. Picture of an older sister: 姐姐 (right)
4. Picture of a baby boy: 弟弟 (right if he is a younger brother)

### How to check quickly
• Read the word slowly and say its meaning in your head.
• Look at the picture.
• Decide: right or wrong?
Correct answers show that you understand what you read. Your teacher may ask you to hold up a card: ✓ for right, ✗ for wrong.

## PAGE 4: A family crossword and reading game
A crossword is a good way to practise the words. You read a clue and find the word.

### Reading clues
Read each clue in English and write the pinyin answer.
1. Your father: bàba
2. Your mother: māma
3. Your older sister: jiějie
4. Your younger brother: dìdi
5. Your older brother: gēge

Some families write these as Chinese characters. Match them:
爸爸 = bàba
妈妈 = māma
姐姐 = jiějie
弟弟 = dìdi
哥哥 = gēge

### Sentence reading game
Cut out sentence cards. Each learner reads one aloud, and the class holds up the matching picture.
这是我爸爸。zhè shì wǒ bàba. This is my dad.
这是我妈妈。zhè shì wǒ māma. This is my mum.
这是我姐姐。zhè shì wǒ jiějie. This is my older sister.
那是我弟弟。nà shì wǒ dìdi. That is my younger brother.

### Peer feedback
When a friend reads, listen for the tones. Say one thing that was good, and one thing to improve. For example: "The tone of jiě was good. Try a lighter jie."

## PAGE 5: A family rhyme
Rhymes help us to read with rhythm. This rhyme uses 爱 ài, which means "love".

爸爸,妈妈,我爱你。
哥哥,姐姐,我爱你。
弟弟,妹妹,我爱你。

Pinyin:
Bàba, māma, wǒ ài nǐ.
Gēge, jiějie, wǒ ài nǐ.
Dìdi, mèimei, wǒ ài nǐ.

Meaning: Dad, mum, I love you. Older brother, older sister, I love you. Younger brother, younger sister, I love you.

### Reading it together
Read the rhyme in a group. One group reads the family words. Another group answers with "wǒ ài nǐ". Keep a steady beat.

### Notice the tones
• wǒ ài nǐ has dipping, falling and dipping tones. Two third tones are not together here, because ài is between them.
• Every line ends in nǐ, so the rhyme has a nice pattern.

### Reading with feeling
The rhyme is about love, so read it warmly. Speak softly and smile. The tone of your voice tells your listeners how you feel.

## PAGE 6: Why families matter
Every family is important. The people in our nuclear family look after us, teach us and help us to grow. We also help them.

### Talk about the people in your family
Say one way each person helps. For example:
• 妈妈 cooks and cares for us.
• 爸爸 works hard to provide.
• 哥哥 and 姐姐 help us with homework.
• 弟弟 and 妹妹 make us laugh.
Share your ideas with a partner and listen politely.

### Living together in peace
When family members love and respect each other, the home is peaceful. In a peaceful community, neighbours also help each other. This is called social cohesion.

### Review
爸爸 bàba, 妈妈 māma, 哥哥 gēge, 姐姐 jiějie, 弟弟 dìdi, 妹妹 mèimei, 儿子 érzi, 女儿 nǚ'ér.

### Common mistakes
• Reading gēge and dìdi as if they have the same meaning. One is older and the other is younger.
• Missing the light tone on the second syllable.
• Missing the apostrophe in nǚ'ér.

### Summary
You have read family words with correct tones, checked pictures and words, and read a rhyme about love. Keep reading aloud often.

## QUIZ
1. What does 弟弟 dìdi mean?
   A) Older brother
   B) Younger brother
   C) Son
   D) Father
   Answer: B
   Why: dìdi is a younger brother. gēge is an older brother.
2. Which word means "daughter"?
   A) 女儿 nǚ'ér
   B) 儿子 érzi
   C) 妹妹 mèimei
   D) 姐姐 jiějie
   Answer: A
   Why: 女儿 is daughter and 儿子 is son.
3. What is the job of the apostrophe in nǚ'ér?
   A) It shows a question
   B) It changes the tone
   C) It shows where one syllable ends and the next begins
   D) It means a light tone
   Answer: C
   Why: The apostrophe separates the syllables nǚ and ér.
4. What does 对 duì mean in a true-or-false exercise?
   A) Wrong
   B) Right
   C) Please
   D) Family
   Answer: B
   Why: 对 duì means right or correct. 错 cuò means wrong.
5. Which sentence means "This is my mum"?
   A) 这是我妈妈。
   B) 这是我爸爸。
   C) 那是我弟弟。
   D) 这是我姐姐。
   Answer: A
   Why: 妈妈 is mum.
6. A picture shows a girl. The label says 儿子. Say whether the pair is right or wrong and explain. (2 marks)
   Answer: It is wrong (错). 儿子 means son, but the picture shows a girl.
   Why: 1 mark for saying wrong and 1 mark for the reason.
7. Write the pinyin for these family words: 爸爸, 姐姐, 妹妹. (3 marks)
   Answer: bàba, jiějie, mèimei.
   Why: 1 mark for each correct word with tones.
8. Write the pinyin and meaning of 我爱你. (2 marks)
   Answer: wǒ ài nǐ, I love you.
   Why: 1 mark for pinyin and 1 mark for meaning.
9. Describe one way a person in your family helps you and one way you help them. Say it in English. (3 marks)
   Answer: Any sensible answer, for example: My mother cooks for me and I help by washing plates.
   Why: 1 mark for naming a person, 1 mark for how they help you, 1 mark for how you help them.
