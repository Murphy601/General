## PAGE 1: Sorting foods by meal
In this lesson you will read short texts about food and drinks. You will sort foods by the time they are eaten and work out the meaning of new words.

Read this text.
我早饭吃饼,喝茶。午饭吃米饭和鸡肉。晚饭吃米饭和菠菜。
wǒ zǎofàn chī bǐng, hē chá. wǔfàn chī mǐfàn hé jīròu. wǎnfàn chī mǐfàn hé bōcài.

Meaning: For breakfast I eat bǐng and drink tea. For lunch I eat rice and chicken. For dinner I eat rice and spinach.

### Make a table
Sort the foods in a table like this in your exercise book.
早饭 zǎofàn (breakfast): 饼, 茶
午饭 wǔfàn (lunch): 米饭, 鸡肉
晚饭 wǎnfàn (dinner): 米饭, 菠菜

### Do it for yourself
Write what you eat in the morning, at lunch and in the evening. You do not need to write in Chinese, but try to use a few Chinese words for the foods you know: 茶 chá, 米饭 mǐfàn, 香蕉 xiāngjiāo, 牛肉 niúròu.

### Different foods for different people
Not everybody eats the same things. A friend might have porridge for breakfast and ugali for supper. That is fine. Our meals show our home and culture.

## PAGE 2: Using a dictionary
A dictionary helps you find the meaning of a new word. A Chinese dictionary for learners lists words in pinyin order. That means the order of the alphabet: a, b, c, d, and so on.

### Alphabetical order in pinyin
Let us put some words in order by their first letters.
bǐng 饼
bōcài 菠菜
chá 茶
gǎnlǎncài 橄榄菜
jīròu 鸡肉
kāfēi 咖啡
mángguǒ 芒果
mǐfàn 米饭
niúròu 牛肉
pútao 葡萄
xiāngjiāo 香蕉

### When two words start with the same letter
Look at the second letter. bǐng comes before bōcài because i comes before o. mángguǒ comes before mǐfàn because a comes before i.

### Look up a word
Find kāfēi. Look in the k section, then find the second letter a. Then look at the meaning: coffee.

### Team task
In a group, read the list aloud in order. One learner reads the pinyin and another reads the Chinese characters. Check the meanings together.

## PAGE 3: Guessing the meaning from clues
Sometimes you meet a word that you do not know. Look for clues in the words around it. This is called inferring.

Read this sentence:
我早饭吃饼,喝咖啡。
Suppose you do not know 咖啡. Ask yourself:
• The verb before it is 喝 hē, which means to drink.
• So 咖啡 must be a drink.
• It comes next to 茶 in food lists, so it is a drink like tea.
Good guess! 咖啡 kāfēi means coffee.

Another sentence:
我吃芒果。
If you do not know 芒果, notice the verb 吃 chī (eat). So it is something you eat. Now look at the picture next to it, and you will see that it is a mango.

### Steps for inferring
1. Read the whole sentence.
2. Look at the verb, 吃 for foods and 喝 for drinks.
3. Look at pictures nearby.
4. Ask a friend or check the dictionary.

### Practise
Read: 我喝牛奶。 Here 牛奶 niúnǎi is a drink because of 喝. It is milk.
Read: 我吃苹果。 Here 苹果 píngguǒ is something to eat: an apple.

## PAGE 4: Food groups and favourites
Chinese has words for groups of foods.
水果 shuǐguǒ: fruit (芒果, 香蕉, 葡萄, 苹果)
蔬菜 shūcài: vegetables (菠菜)
肉 ròu: meat (牛肉, 鸡肉)
饮料 yǐnliào: drinks (茶, 咖啡, 牛奶)
零食 língshí: snacks (饼干 bǐnggān, biscuits)

### Say your favourite
我喜欢吃香蕉。
wǒ xǐhuan chī xiāngjiāo.
I like to eat bananas.

我喜欢喝茶。
wǒ xǐhuan hē chá.
I like to drink tea.

The word 喜欢 xǐhuan means to like. Use 吃 with foods and 喝 with drinks.

### Picture-card talk
Take turns. Choose a picture card and say a sentence about it.
• 这是什么? 这是红色的苹果。
zhè shì shénme? zhè shì hóngsè de píngguǒ.
What is this? This is a red apple.
• 我喜欢吃…
Say the sentence smoothly. Your partner listens and says whether it is right.

### Discuss
Which food group is your favourite? Which group do you eat the least? A healthy meal has a mixture from several groups.

## PAGE 5: Reading speed
Reading speed is how fast you read. You should read fast enough to sound natural, but not so fast that you make mistakes. The best pace is steady and clear.

### Three steps to a good speed
1. Read the sentence slowly and correctly.
2. Read it again, a little faster.
3. Read it a third time at the speed you use when you talk.
Compare your speed and clearness in each round.

### Try with a text
我喜欢吃水果。我喜欢吃芒果和香蕉。我不喜欢咖啡。
wǒ xǐhuan chī shuǐguǒ. wǒ xǐhuan chī mángguǒ hé xiāngjiāo. wǒ bù xǐhuan kāfēi.
I like to eat fruit. I like to eat mango and banana. I do not like coffee.
The word 不 bù means not.

### Record and listen
If you can, record yourself reading the text on a phone or a tape recorder, with permission from your teacher. Listen and ask:
• Was my speed too fast or too slow?
• Were the tones clear?
• Did I stop in the wrong place?
Write your notes. Read the text again next week and see if you improve. This is a way of checking your own progress.

### Reading with a partner
Read alternate sentences with a partner. Try to keep the same speed as your partner.

## PAGE 6: Why clear reading matters
When we read food words clearly, our listeners understand exactly what we mean. Look at this: 牛肉 niúròu is beef, but 牛奶 niúnǎi is milk. If you read them carelessly, someone may bring you the wrong thing.

### Clear reading tips
• Say every syllable.
• Keep the tones correct.
• Read at a speed your listener can follow.
• Use expression when you read questions.

### Review
Foods: 饼, 米饭, 芒果, 香蕉, 葡萄, 苹果, 菠菜, 牛肉, 鸡肉.
Drinks: 茶, 咖啡, 牛奶.
Verbs: 吃 (eat), 喝 (drink), 喜欢 (like).
Meals: 早饭, 午饭, 晚饭.

### Respectful discussion
Talk about why we should respect different food choices. Some people cannot eat certain foods for health or religious reasons. When a friend says "I don't eat that", accept it kindly.

### Common mistakes
• Using 喝 with foods or 吃 with drinks.
• Mixing up 牛肉 (beef) and 牛奶 (milk).
• Reading so fast that the meaning gets lost.

### Summary
You have sorted foods by meal, used alphabetical order, guessed word meanings from clues and practised reading speed. Good reading skills help you share information clearly.

## QUIZ
1. Which verb goes with a drink?
   A) 吃
   B) 喝
   C) 是
   D) 去
   Answer: B
   Why: 喝 hē means to drink.
2. What does 牛奶 niúnǎi mean?
   A) Beef
   B) Chicken
   C) Milk
   D) Tea
   Answer: C
   Why: 牛奶 is milk. 牛肉 is beef.
3. Which of these words comes first in pinyin alphabetical order?
   A) chá
   B) bǐng
   C) mángguǒ
   D) xiāngjiāo
   Answer: B
   Why: b comes before c, m and x.
4. What does 我喜欢吃香蕉 mean?
   A) I like to drink bananas
   B) I do not like bananas
   C) I like to eat bananas
   D) I have a banana
   Answer: C
   Why: 喜欢 is like, 吃 is eat and 香蕉 is banana.
5. What is a good clue for working out an unknown food word?
   A) The colour of the page
   B) The verb before it, such as 吃 or 喝
   C) The last page of the book
   D) The number of pages
   Answer: B
   Why: The verb 吃 tells you it is a food and 喝 tells you it is a drink.
6. Sort these into breakfast, lunch or dinner as in the text: 饼, 鸡肉, 菠菜. (3 marks)
   Answer: 饼 is breakfast, 鸡肉 is lunch, 菠菜 is dinner.
   Why: 1 mark for each correct food.
7. Write in Chinese with pinyin: "I like to drink tea." (3 marks)
   Answer: 我喜欢喝茶。 wǒ xǐhuan hē chá.
   Why: 1 mark for 喜欢, 1 mark for 喝茶, 1 mark for pinyin.
8. Give two steps for reading a sentence at a good speed. (2 marks)
   Answer: Read it slowly and correctly first, then read it faster, and finally read at speaking speed.
   Why: 1 mark for each step.
9. Explain how you can guess the meaning of 咖啡 in the sentence 我喝咖啡. (2 marks)
   Answer: The verb 喝 means to drink, so 咖啡 must be a drink.
   Why: 1 mark for using the verb and 1 mark for the conclusion.
