## PAGE 1: Parts of the body
You use your body every minute of the day, so it is a good topic for learning new words. Listen as your teacher says each part of the body in Mandarin. Touch each part as you repeat the word.

头 tóu: head
肩膀 jiānbǎng: shoulder
膝盖 xīgài: knee
脚 jiǎo: foot
眼睛 yǎnjing: eye
耳朵 ěrduo: ear
嘴巴 zuǐba: mouth
鼻子 bízi: nose
手 shǒu: hand

### Listen for the tones
• tóu, bízi and shǒu have rising or dipping tones.
• jiǎo and shǒu are both third tone, so they dip.
• The second syllables in yǎnjing, ěrduo, zuǐba and bízi are light. They are the neutral tone, so they sound short and quiet.

## PAGE 2: A song about the body
Songs help us remember words. This song has the same tune as the English "Head, shoulders, knees and toes". The Mandarin song uses the words:

头、肩膀、膝盖、脚。
tóu, jiānbǎng, xīgài, jiǎo.

眼睛、耳朵、嘴巴、鼻子。
yǎnjing, ěrduo, zuǐba, bízi.

Sing the first line four times, and touch each part as you sing its name. Then sing the second line. Start slowly and speed up every time.

### While you sing
1. Watch the person who leads the song.
2. Listen to how the others say the words.
3. Try to touch the right part before the song moves on.

### Listening for the key ideas
When we listen to a song, we do not have to understand every word. The gist of this song is: "It names parts of the body." The key words are the body parts. If you catch the key words, you understand the gist.

## PAGE 3: Riddles about the body
A riddle describes something, and you guess what it is. Listen to riddles about the body. Here are some in English, with the answers in Chinese.

Riddle 1: I am in the middle of your face and I help you breathe. Who am I?
Answer: 鼻子 bízi (nose).

Riddle 2: I am two round things on your face. I help you see. Who am I?
Answer: 眼睛 yǎnjing (eyes).

Riddle 3: I sit on the sides of your head. I help you hear. Who am I?
Answer: 耳朵 ěrduo (ears).

Riddle 4: I am inside your face. I help you talk and eat. Who am I?
Answer: 嘴巴 zuǐba (mouth).

### How to solve a riddle
1. Listen for the important clue words: middle, see, hear, talk.
2. Think about which part of the body fits all the clues.
3. Say the answer in Chinese.

### Make your own
Write a riddle about your hand or your foot. Read it to a partner. Can your partner guess in Chinese? Try: "I have five fingers." (Answer: 手 shǒu.)

## PAGE 4: What do our body parts do?
Each part of the body has a job. Listen to these Mandarin words for actions and match each to a body part.

看 kàn: to look, to see
听 tīng: to listen, to hear
说 shuō: to speak
吃 chī: to eat
笑 xiào: to laugh, to smile
哭 kū: to cry

Now match them.
眼睛看。yǎnjing kàn. Eyes see.
耳朵听。ěrduo tīng. Ears hear.
嘴巴说。zuǐba shuō. The mouth speaks.
嘴巴吃。zuǐba chī. The mouth eats.

### Guess the part
Your partner says an action word like 吃 chī. You guess which part of the body it is: 嘴巴. Then swap. Try 看 (eyes), 听 (ears), 说 (mouth).

### Thinking about it
Why are these body parts important? Without eyes we could not see the road. Without ears we would not hear a friend call us. Each part helps us in daily life, and we should take care of every part. Some people cannot see or hear, and we respect them and help them.

## PAGE 5: Retelling what we heard
After you listen, you should be able to say the important points again in your own way. This is called rephrasing or retelling.

Listen to this short text.
我有头、手和脚。我用眼睛看,用耳朵听,用嘴巴说。
wǒ yǒu tóu, shǒu hé jiǎo. wǒ yòng yǎnjing kàn, yòng ěrduo tīng, yòng zuǐba shuō.
I have a head, hands and feet. I use my eyes to see, my ears to hear and my mouth to speak.

New word: 用 yòng (to use).

### Capture the key points
1. Which body parts are named? (head, hands, feet, eyes, ears, mouth)
2. What do the eyes do? (see)
3. What do the ears do? (hear)

### Retell it
Now say the key points in English or with Chinese words. For example: "Eyes see, ears hear, mouth speaks." This is a good retelling because it keeps the important ideas and leaves out extra words.

### Peer feedback
Tell your partner the key points. Your partner says whether you remembered every important idea. Say "thank you" to your partner: 谢谢 xièxie.

## PAGE 6: The broken telephone game
This game trains your listening. Sit in a line. The first learner whispers a body word in Chinese, for example "jiānbǎng". Each learner whispers it to the next. The last learner says the word aloud. Is it the same word?

### Rules
• Whisper, so only your neighbour hears you.
• Say it once only. No repeating.
• Do not laugh at mistakes. Wait quietly for your turn.
• Everybody listens carefully.

### What the game teaches
When a word changes on its way, it shows how easy it is to miss a tone or a sound. Sometimes "shǒu" (hand) becomes "shòu" (thin) and the meaning changes. Careful listening protects the message.

### Follow an instruction
Your teacher gives a spoken instruction: 摸头 mō tóu (touch your head). Then: 摸鼻子 mō bízi (touch your nose). Do the action when you hear it. The word 摸 mō means to touch.

### Summary
• Gist is the main idea; key words help you find it.
• Body parts: 头, 手, 脚, 眼睛, 耳朵, 嘴巴, 鼻子, 肩膀, 膝盖.
• We can retell what we have heard by keeping the key points.
• Patience is needed when waiting for your turn.

## QUIZ
1. What does 眼睛 yǎnjing mean?
   A) Ear
   B) Nose
   C) Eye
   D) Mouth
   Answer: C
   Why: 眼睛 is the eye. Ear is 耳朵, nose is 鼻子 and mouth is 嘴巴.
2. Which body part is 脚 jiǎo?
   A) Foot
   B) Hand
   C) Knee
   D) Head
   Answer: A
   Why: 脚 jiǎo is the foot, but 手 shǒu is the hand.
3. "I am in the middle of your face and I help you breathe." Which word is the answer?
   A) 嘴巴 zuǐba
   B) 耳朵 ěrduo
   C) 鼻子 bízi
   D) 头 tóu
   Answer: C
   Why: The nose is in the middle of the face and helps us breathe.
4. What does 耳朵听 ěrduo tīng tell us?
   A) Ears hear
   B) Eyes see
   C) The mouth eats
   D) Hands write
   Answer: A
   Why: 耳朵 is ears and 听 is to listen or hear.
5. Which verb in Chinese means "to see or look"?
   A) 听 tīng
   B) 看 kàn
   C) 说 shuō
   D) 哭 kū
   Answer: B
   Why: 看 kàn means to look or see.
6. Write the Chinese words for head, hand and foot with pinyin. (3 marks)
   Answer: 头 tóu, 手 shǒu, 脚 jiǎo.
   Why: 1 mark for each word with pinyin.
7. Say what the mouth does, using two of the verbs from the lesson. (2 marks)
   Answer: The mouth speaks (说 shuō) and eats (吃 chī).
   Why: 1 mark for each correct verb and use.
8. Write a short riddle in English about one body part and give the answer in Chinese with pinyin. (3 marks)
   Answer: For example: "I have two, I help you hear. Who am I?" Answer: 耳朵 ěrduo.
   Why: 1 mark for a clear clue, 1 mark for the correct body part, 1 mark for the correct Chinese and pinyin.
9. Explain how retelling helps you when you listen for gist. (2 marks)
   Answer: When you retell, you say the key points in your own way. It shows you understood the main idea.
   Why: 1 mark for key points and 1 mark for showing understanding.
