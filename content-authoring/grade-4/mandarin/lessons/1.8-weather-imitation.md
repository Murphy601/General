## PAGE 1: Weather words
The weather is what the sky and air are like outside. Is it sunny, cloudy, windy or rainy? In this lesson you will learn to say five weather words in Mandarin. Then you will build simple sentences with them and practise until they sound smooth.

太阳 tàiyáng: sun
云 yún: cloud
风 fēng: wind
雨 yǔ: rain
雪 xuě: snow

### Listening first
Look at each picture and say what you see. Then listen to your teacher say the word and repeat after your teacher. Match each picture to its word.

### Listen for tones
• tàiyáng has a falling tone, then a rising tone.
• yún rises.
• fēng is high and level.
• yǔ dips down and up (third tone).
• xuě dips too.
Move your hand with each tone as you say the word.

## PAGE 2: This is... 这是...
Now we can make simple sentences using a pattern you know already.

这是太阳。zhè shì tàiyáng. This is the sun.
这是云。zhè shì yún. This is a cloud.
这是风。zhè shì fēng. This is wind.
这是雨。zhè shì yǔ. This is rain.
这是雪。zhè shì xuě. This is snow.

### Question and answer
Ask a question and answer it, with a partner.
这是什么? zhè shì shénme? What is this?
这是太阳。zhè shì tàiyáng. This is the sun.

Point at a picture card. Your partner says the sentence. Then swap.

### Add a describing word
You can say how big something is.
大 dà: big, strong
小 xiǎo: small, light
很 hěn: very

太阳很大。tàiyáng hěn dà. The sun is very big (or hot and strong).
云很小。yún hěn xiǎo. The cloud is very small.
风很大。fēng hěn dà. The wind is very strong.
雨很大。yǔ hěn dà. The rain is heavy.

Notice that 很 comes between the thing and the describing word. It helps the sentence sound natural.

## PAGE 3: Imitate for fluency
Fluency means speaking smoothly, at a natural speed. Here is how to practise by imitation.

### Three rounds
Round 1: Listen. Your teacher says 这是太阳。 You listen only.
Round 2: Repeat slowly. You say 这是太阳。 slowly, together with your class.
Round 3: Repeat at normal speed. You say it again quickly but clearly.

### Build up a sentence
Some sentences are long. Build them from the end.
1. 太阳
2. 是太阳
3. 这是太阳。
4. 太阳很大。

Add one part at a time. This is called back-chaining, and it helps your memory.

### Check your partner
When your partner speaks, listen for three things:
• Is each tone right?
• Is the speed even?
• Are the words in the correct order?
Say kindly what went well and give one hint to improve. For example: "Your yǔ was clear. Make xuě a little longer."

### Useful weather sentences
下雨 xià yǔ: it rains
下雪 xià xuě: it snows
刮风 guā fēng: the wind blows
今天天气很好。jīntiān tiānqì hěn hǎo. The weather is very good today.
The word 天气 tiānqì means weather.

## PAGE 4: Talking about today's weather
Look out of the window. What is the weather today? Use Chinese to say it.

今天下雨。jīntiān xià yǔ. Today it is raining.
今天刮风。jīntiān guā fēng. Today it is windy.
今天有太阳。jīntiān yǒu tàiyáng. Today there is sun.
今天有云。jīntiān yǒu yún. Today there are clouds.

Ask your partner: 今天天气怎么样? jīntiān tiānqì zěnmeyàng? How is the weather today? The word 怎么样 zěnmeyàng means "how is it".

### Role play: the weather reporter
One learner is the weather reporter. The reporter stands in front of the class and tells today's weather in two sentences. For example: "今天有太阳。太阳很大。" The class listens and repeats. Then choose another reporter.

### In Kenya
Kenya has sun, clouds and rain. In some seasons the rain is heavy, and in others there is very little. Snow is rare, but there is snow on the top of Mount Kenya. Which words would you use to talk about the weather where you live?

## PAGE 5: Extreme weather
Sometimes the weather can be dangerous. Too much rain can cause floods. Too little rain can cause a drought, when the land becomes very dry. Very strong wind can break roofs and trees. These are called extreme weather events.

洪水 hóngshuǐ: flood
干旱 gānhàn: drought

### Research and share
Find out about one extreme weather event that happened in Kenya or in another country. Ask an adult, use a newspaper, or search on a computer if you have safe access. Write down:
1. What weather feature caused it? (rain, wind, or too little rain?)
2. What happened to people and to crops?
3. How did people stay safe?

Share your findings in a small group. Speak slowly and clearly, and use the words you learnt: 雨很大 (the rain is heavy), 风很大 (the wind is strong), 太阳很大 (the sun is strong).

### Safety tips
• Do not shelter under a tree in a thunderstorm.
• Stay away from fast rivers when it has rained.
• Drink plenty of water when the sun is strong.
• Listen to adults and follow safety instructions.

### Care for the environment
Planting trees and keeping the environment clean helps to protect us from very bad weather. Everyone can do a small thing to help.

## PAGE 6: Practice and review
Let us review and practise in a fun way.

### Matching game
Match the picture to the sentence.
1. A picture of the sun: 这是太阳。
2. A picture of a cloud: 这是云。
3. Wind lines: 这是风。
4. Raindrops: 这是雨。
5. Snowflakes: 这是雪。

### Sentence chain
Sit in a circle. The first learner says a sentence with a weather word, for example 太阳很大。 The next learner repeats it and adds a new sentence. How long can your class chain grow before someone forgets? Use good pronunciation and be patient with each other.

### Peer evaluation checklist
When your friend speaks, tick these:
• Correct tones
• Clear sounds
• Steady speed
• Smile and eye contact

### Common mistakes
• Saying xuě with a high level tone.
• Leaving out 很 before 大 and 小 in a sentence like 太阳很大.
• Speaking so fast that the tones are lost.

### Summary
You can name five weather features, say 这是..., and describe them with 很大 or 很小. Keep listening and repeating. That is how languages are learned.

## QUIZ
1. What does 雨 yǔ mean?
   A) Rain
   B) Wind
   C) Cloud
   D) Sun
   Answer: A
   Why: 雨 is rain. Wind is 风, cloud is 云 and sun is 太阳.
2. Which sentence means "This is a cloud"?
   A) 这是太阳。
   B) 这是云。
   C) 这是雪。
   D) 这是风。
   Answer: B
   Why: 云 yún is cloud.
3. What does 风很大 fēng hěn dà mean?
   A) The rain is heavy
   B) The sun is small
   C) The cloud is small
   D) The wind is very strong
   Answer: D
   Why: 风 is wind, 很 is very and 大 is big or strong.
4. Which word means "snow"?
   A) 雪 xuě
   B) 雨 yǔ
   C) 云 yún
   D) 风 fēng
   Answer: A
   Why: 雪 xuě is snow.
5. What does 天气 tiānqì mean?
   A) Sky
   B) Weather
   C) Tomorrow
   D) Sun
   Answer: B
   Why: 天气 means weather, as in 今天天气很好.
6. Write in Chinese with pinyin: "This is the sun." (2 marks)
   Answer: 这是太阳。 zhè shì tàiyáng.
   Why: 1 mark for the characters and 1 mark for the pinyin.
7. Translate and give pinyin for "The sun is very big." (3 marks)
   Answer: 太阳很大。 tàiyáng hěn dà.
   Why: 1 mark for 太阳, 1 mark for 很大 in the right order, 1 mark for pinyin.
8. Give three steps for practising fluency by imitation. (3 marks)
   Answer: Listen to the sentence, repeat it slowly, then repeat it at normal speed. (Also accepted: build the sentence from the end.)
   Why: 1 mark for each step.
9. Name one type of extreme weather and one thing people can do to stay safe. (2 marks)
   Answer: For example flood (洪水): stay away from fast rivers. Or drought: drink water and save water.
   Why: 1 mark for a correct extreme event and 1 mark for a sensible safety action.
