## PAGE 1: Labelling the body in pinyin and characters
In this lesson you will write the names of body parts and learn how to write two simple characters with the correct stroke order.

头 tóu: head
肩膀 jiānbǎng: shoulder
膝盖 xīgài: knee
脚 jiǎo: foot
眼睛 yǎnjing: eye
耳朵 ěrduo: ear
嘴巴 zuǐba: mouth
鼻子 bízi: nose
手 shǒu: hand

### Label a chart
Draw a picture of a person on a large sheet of paper. Draw lines to each body part, and write the pinyin and the characters at the end of each line. If you cannot write a character yet, write the pinyin and leave a space for the character.

### Spelling and tone marks
• tóu: the mark is on the o. (When there is no a, we look for o or e.)
• jiǎo: the mark is on the a.
• shǒu: the mark is on the o.
• zuǐba: the mark is on the i, because in ui the second letter takes the mark. The dot of i disappears.
• jiānbǎng: two marks, one on each a.

### Check
Swap charts with a partner. Check each other's spelling and tone marks. Be kind, and thank your partner for checking yours.

## PAGE 2: Types of strokes in body characters
In the last lessons you learnt eight basic strokes. Now find them in the characters for body parts.

口 kǒu: mouth (3 strokes)
目 mù: eye (5 strokes)
耳 ěr: ear (6 strokes)
手 shǒu: hand (4 strokes)
头 tóu: head (5 strokes)

### Look for the strokes
• 口 is made of 竖, 横折 and 横. It looks like an open mouth.
• 目 looks like an eye. It has a box shape with lines inside.
• 手 has 撇, 横, 横 and 竖钩.
• 头 has 点, 点, 横, 撇 and 捺.

### Remember what they mean
These characters began as pictures. 口 was a picture of a mouth, 目 was a picture of an eye and 手 was a picture of a hand. Today they are written with straight strokes, but you can still see the idea.

### Activity
Make flashcards. Write one character on each card, with the pinyin and a small drawing of the body part on the back. Test your friends.

### Part and whole
Many other characters contain these parts. For example, 口 is in 吃 chī (to eat) and 喝 hē (to drink), which are actions of the mouth. The 目 part is in 眼 yǎn (eye). Can you spot 口 in 吃 and 喝?

### Stroke order for 手 shǒu (4 strokes)
1. 撇: a short stroke falling to the left.
2. 横: a horizontal stroke.
3. 横: a second, longer horizontal stroke.
4. 竖钩: a vertical stroke down the middle, ending with a hook.

### Stroke order for 头 tóu (5 strokes)
1. 点: the left dot.
2. 点: the right dot.
3. 横: a horizontal stroke.
4. 撇: falling to the left.
5. 捺: falling to the right.

### Trace and write
Put a sheet of paper over the characters in your book and trace them. Then write each character five times without tracing. Say the strokes aloud as you write: "piě, héng, héng, shùgōu". After writing, count the strokes to check.

### Rules used
Top to bottom, left to right, and 撇 before 捺.

## PAGE 4: Writing characters with local materials
Make the characters 手 and 头 with things you can find near you. Working in groups, you will create large characters.

### Materials
• Small stones or pebbles.
• Sand or soil in a tray.
• Grains of maize or beans.
• Sticks, twigs, string or wool.
• Plasticine or clay.

### Steps
1. Choose a character, for example 手.
2. Lay out the first stroke, then the second, and so on, in the right order.
3. Ask a partner to count the strokes and check the order.
4. Take a photo, or put your character on a display table.
5. Walk around the room, look at other groups' work and give feedback.

### Give helpful feedback
Say what is good. Say what could be improved. Speak politely and honestly. For example: "Your strokes are clear. The second stroke of 手 should be a little longer."

### Share the work
Every member of your group should help. One person can place the strokes, one can check the order, one can count and one can read out the pinyin. Change jobs so that everyone tries each one.

## PAGE 5: Body words in sentences
Write short sentences about body parts. Use words that you know.

我有两只眼睛。
wǒ yǒu liǎng zhī yǎnjing.
I have two eyes.

我用耳朵听。
wǒ yòng ěrduo tīng.
I use my ears to hear.

我用嘴巴说。
wǒ yòng zuǐba shuō.
I use my mouth to speak.

### Fill in the missing words
Complete the lyrics of the body song. Write the missing word from the list: 头, 脚, 耳朵, 鼻子.
头、肩膀、膝盖、____。 (Answer: 脚)
眼睛、____、嘴巴、鼻子。 (Answer: 耳朵)

### A crossword idea
Draw a small crossword grid. Write the clues in English: "I help you hear" (ěrduo), "I help you smell and breathe" (bízi). Ask a partner to fill it in with pinyin.

### Check your sentences
• Do they end with 。?
• Are the tone marks right?
• Are the characters written in the correct stroke order?
Read your sentences aloud to a partner.

## PAGE 6: Taking care of our bodies
Our bodies are precious. We must keep them clean and healthy. Talk with your classmates about hygiene and grooming. Here are some habits.

### Good habits
• Wash your hands with soap: 洗手 xǐshǒu.
• Brush your teeth: 刷牙 shuāyá. The word 牙 yá means tooth.
• Bathe often and keep your hair and nails neat.
• Wear clean clothes.
• Eat healthy food and sleep well.

### Write about it
Write one sentence about how you keep clean. For example, in English: "I wash my hands before I eat." Try to add the Chinese words 洗手 or 刷牙 in your sentence.

### Show love for yourself
When we care for our own bodies, we show that we value ourselves. It also protects others from germs. Respect your body and the bodies of others.

### Common mistakes
• Writing the strokes of 手 or 头 in the wrong order.
• Forgetting the tone marks in pinyin.
• Labelling the wrong body part.

### Summary
You have labelled body parts, learnt to recognise different strokes and written 手 and 头 in the correct order. You have also talked about hygiene and grooming.

## QUIZ
1. How many strokes does 手 have?
   A) 3
   B) 4
   C) 5
   D) 6
   Answer: B
   Why: 手 has four strokes: 撇, 横, 横 and 竖钩.
2. Which character means "mouth"?
   A) 口
   B) 手
   C) 头
   D) 耳
   Answer: A
   Why: 口 kǒu means mouth.
3. In the character 头, which stroke is the last one written?
   A) 横
   B) 点
   C) 撇
   D) 捺
   Answer: D
   Why: The order for 头 is 点, 点, 横, 撇, 捺, so 捺 is last.
4. Which of these is the correct pinyin for "foot"?
   A) jiáo
   B) jiǎo
   C) jiāo
   D) jiào
   Answer: B
   Why: 脚 is jiǎo, with the third (dipping) tone on the a.
5. What does 洗手 xǐshǒu mean?
   A) Brush teeth
   B) Wash hands
   C) Eat food
   D) Clap hands
   Answer: B
   Why: 洗 means to wash and 手 means hand.
6. Write the pinyin for these body parts: 眼睛, 耳朵, 嘴巴. (3 marks)
   Answer: yǎnjing, ěrduo, zuǐba.
   Why: 1 mark for each word with correct tones.
7. Write the stroke order for 手 in Chinese stroke names. (3 marks)
   Answer: 撇, 横, 横, 竖钩.
   Why: 1 mark for two correct strokes in order, 2 marks for three, 3 marks for all four.
8. Describe one way to make the character 手 with local materials. (2 marks)
   Answer: Lay out pebbles, sticks or maize grains in the shape of each stroke in the correct order.
   Why: 1 mark for a material and 1 mark for following the stroke order.
9. Give two ways to keep your body clean and healthy. (2 marks)
   Answer: For example wash hands with soap, brush teeth, bathe often, wear clean clothes.
   Why: 1 mark for each sensible habit.
