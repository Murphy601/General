## PAGE 1: What is listening for gist?
The gist of a message is its main idea. When you listen for gist, you do not need to understand every word. You listen for the most important idea: Who is speaking? What is happening? Is it happy or sad?

Think about a radio announcement at home. Even when there is noise, you catch the key words and you understand what it is about. In Mandarin you can do the same, by listening for the words you already know.

### Active listening
Active listening means you give the speaker your full attention. Here is how.
• Sit still and look at the speaker.
• Do not talk while someone else is talking.
• Listen for the key words.
• Think of one question you can ask afterwards.
• Nod or say "yes" to show you are following.

### Get ready
In this lesson the topic is birthdays. Before you listen, ask yourself: what words would I expect to hear at a birthday party? You might guess cake, gift, song and friends. Guessing in advance helps you listen better. Later in the lesson you will hear a short Chinese text and find its gist.

### Listen and list
Your teacher reads out the items needed for a birthday: candles, a knife, plates and cups. As you listen, tick each one on your list. You are practising to listen for the key words.

Be careful with the knife 刀 dāo. Only an adult should cut with a sharp knife, and children should ask for help.

## PAGE 3: The birthday song and birthday wishes
The most important birthday sentence is:
祝你生日快乐!
zhù nǐ shēngrì kuàilè!
Happy birthday to you! (Literally: I wish you a happy birthday.)

The three key words in the song are 祝 (wish), 生日 (birthday) and 快乐 (happy). You already know 你 (you) from your greeting lesson.

The song has the same tune as "Happy Birthday to You". Sing:
祝你生日快乐,
祝你生日快乐,
祝你生日快乐,
祝你生日快乐。

In Kiswahili we say "Heri ya siku ya kuzaliwa" and in English "Happy birthday". Different languages, same warm wish.

### Sound check
• zhù is a falling tone.
• shēngrì has a high tone then a falling tone.
• kuàilè has two falling tones, so it is said quickly and firmly.
Try it with your hand: down, high-down, down-down.

### Role play
Stand in a circle. One learner is the birthday child. The others sing the song and then say "祝你生日快乐!" one by one. The birthday child says "谢谢" xièxie (thank you). Say it with a big smile.

## PAGE 4: Activities at a birthday party
What do people do at a birthday? Listen and repeat these phrases.

切蛋糕 qiē dàngāo: cut the cake
送礼物 sòng lǐwù: give gifts
唱歌 chànggē: sing
和朋友一起玩 hé péngyou yìqǐ wán: play together with friends

### Ask about a birthday
你生日是几月几号?
nǐ shēngrì shì jǐ yuè jǐ hào?
When is your birthday? (Literally: Your birthday is which month, which day?)

The answer uses numbers.
我的生日是三月五号。
wǒ de shēngrì shì sān yuè wǔ hào.
My birthday is on 5 March.

The word 月 yuè is month, and 号 hào is the date. The month is the number plus 月, for example 一月 (January), 五月 (May), 十二月 (December). Say "sān yuè" for March, "wǔ hào" for the fifth.

### Take turns
Ask five classmates 你生日是几月几号? and write down their month and date in numbers. Then say the answers aloud. You are listening for key numbers, which is exactly what listening for gist needs.

## PAGE 5: A short text to listen to
Listen to this short text about a birthday. Try to catch the gist.

今天是我的生日。朋友们来我家。我们唱歌,切蛋糕。他们送我礼物。我很高兴。

Pinyin:
Jīntiān shì wǒ de shēngrì. Péngyoumen lái wǒ jiā. Wǒmen chànggē, qiē dàngāo. Tāmen sòng wǒ lǐwù. Wǒ hěn gāoxìng.

Meaning:
Today is my birthday. Friends come to my house. We sing and cut the cake. They give me gifts. I am very happy.

New words: 来 lái (come), 我们 wǒmen (we), 他们 tāmen (they), 很 hěn (very), 高兴 gāoxìng (happy, glad).

### Find the gist
Answer these questions.
1. What is the main idea of the text? (A birthday party.)
2. Who came to the house? (Friends.)
3. What did they do? (Sang, cut the cake and gave gifts.)
4. How does the speaker feel? (Very happy.)

### Details and gist
The gist is: "It is a happy birthday party." The details are: who came, what they did and how the speaker felt. First find the gist, then look for a few details.

## PAGE 6: Dramatise and share
Now use your listening skills to act out a birthday party. Work in groups of five or six.

### Roles
• The birthday child.
• Friends who sing and give gifts.
• A helper who sets the table with cups, plates and candles.
• A listener who asks: 过生日的时候会做什么? guò shēngrì de shíhou huì zuò shénme? (What do you do at a birthday?)

The words 会 huì (will) and 做 zuò (do) may be new. Answer: 我们唱歌,切蛋糕。 (We sing and cut the cake.)

### Include everyone
Not every family celebrates in the same way. Some families have a big party, some have a special meal, and some give a small gift or a prayer. All are meaningful. When a friend tells you how they celebrate, listen kindly.

### Give a gift of kindness
In your play, present an imaginary gift and say 送你礼物 (sòng nǐ lǐwù: a gift for you). Giving is a way to show love.

### Summary
• Gist means the main idea.
• Listen actively: face the speaker, be quiet, catch key words.
• Key birthday words: 生日, 快乐, 蛋糕, 礼物.
• You can ask 你生日是几月几号?

## QUIZ
1. What does "gist" mean?
   A) Every word in a message
   B) The main idea of a message
   C) The last word in a message
   D) The speaker's name
   Answer: B
   Why: The gist is the main idea, not every single word.
2. What does 祝你生日快乐 zhù nǐ shēngrì kuàilè mean?
   A) Thank you very much
   B) Where is your birthday
   C) See you tomorrow
   D) Happy birthday to you
   Answer: D
   Why: 祝 is wish, 生日 is birthday and 快乐 is happy.
3. Which word means "cake"?
   A) 礼物 lǐwù
   B) 蛋糕 dàngāo
   C) 蜡烛 làzhú
   D) 盘子 pánzi
   Answer: B
   Why: 蛋糕 dàngāo is cake. 礼物 is gift, 蜡烛 is candle and 盘子 is plate.
4. In 我的生日是三月五号, which date is the birthday?
   A) 3 May
   B) 5 March
   C) 3 March
   D) 5 May
   Answer: B
   Why: 三月 is March (month 3) and 五号 is the fifth day.
5. Which of these is a good active listening habit?
   A) Talk to a friend while the speaker talks
   B) Look away from the speaker
   C) Listen for key words and stay quiet
   D) Only listen to the first sentence
   Answer: C
   Why: Active listeners stay quiet, look at the speaker and listen for key words.
6. Write in Chinese, with pinyin, a question asking a friend when their birthday is. (2 marks)
   Answer: 你生日是几月几号? nǐ shēngrì shì jǐ yuè jǐ hào?
   Why: 1 mark for the correct characters and 1 mark for correct pinyin.
7. Name three activities people do at a birthday party in Chinese or English. (3 marks)
   Answer: Any three of cut the cake (切蛋糕), give gifts (送礼物), sing (唱歌) and play with friends (和朋友一起玩).
   Why: 1 mark for each correct activity.
8. Listen to this: 今天是我的生日。朋友们来我家。我很高兴。 Give the gist in one English sentence. (2 marks)
   Answer: It is the speaker's birthday, friends come to the house and the speaker is very happy.
   Why: 1 mark for saying it is a birthday and 1 mark for friends coming or being happy.
9. Explain why it is respectful to listen kindly when friends describe how they celebrate their birthday. (2 marks)
   Answer: Families celebrate in different ways, and all ways are meaningful, so we should listen without laughing or judging.
   Why: 1 mark for saying families differ and 1 mark for the kind attitude.
