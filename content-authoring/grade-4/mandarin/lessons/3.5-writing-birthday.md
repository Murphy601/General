## PAGE 1: Spelling birthday words
Spelling is writing the letters of a word in the correct order. In pinyin, the tone marks are part of the spelling. Copy these birthday words with great care.

生日 shēngrì: birthday
快乐 kuàilè: happy
蛋糕 dàngāo: cake
礼物 lǐwù: gift
蜡烛 làzhú: candle
朋友 péngyou: friend
盘子 pánzi: plate
杯子 bēizi: cup
刀 dāo: knife

### Points to watch
• shēngrì: the mark is on the e in shēng and on the i in rì (the dot is dropped).
• kuàilè: two falling marks, on the a in kuài and on the e in lè.
• dàngāo: the first mark is a falling mark on a in dàng and the second is a high mark on a in gāo.
• péngyou: the second syllable is light, with no mark.

### Look, cover, write, check
1. Look at the word.
2. Cover it with your hand.
3. Write it from memory.
4. Uncover and check letter by letter.
Do this for each word. Count how many you spelt correctly.

## PAGE 2: Labelling a birthday picture
Draw a birthday table in your exercise book. Draw a cake, candles, gifts, plates and cups. Colour your picture. Then label each thing with the Chinese word, with pinyin.

### Example labels
蛋糕 dàngāo (cake)
蜡烛 làzhú (candles)
礼物 lǐwù (gift)
盘子 pánzi (plate)
杯子 bēizi (cup)

### Draw and label
Point to each thing you drew and say the word. Your partner checks your spelling and tones. The picture and the labels together are a good page for your class book.

### List from dictation
Your teacher reads out words for a birthday party. You write them in a list, neatly. For example: 蛋糕, 蜡烛, 礼物, 朋友, 生日. Check your spelling afterwards.

### Handwriting reminder
Write each letter clearly. Do not squash the letters together. Make the tone marks small and clear above the vowels. Give each word its own space.

### Tips for neat characters
• Keep the size the same for every character.
• Keep the strokes straight and firm. Horizontal strokes go slightly up to the right.
• Do not join strokes that should be separate.
• Leave the same small space between characters.
• Write in the right stroke order.

### Stroke counts
祝 zhù: 9 strokes
你 nǐ: 7 strokes
生 shēng: 5 strokes
日 rì: 4 strokes
快 kuài: 7 strokes
乐 lè: 5 strokes

### Practise
Write each character five times in squares. Compare your best one with the model. Ask a friend which one is your neatest.

## PAGE 4: Planning an imaginary birthday
Planning helps us organise our thoughts. In a group, plan an imaginary birthday party. Make a list of the things you need.

### What you may include
• Food and drinks: 蛋糕, 茶, 水果.
• Decorations and gifts: 礼物.
• Things for the cake: 蜡烛, 刀.
• Plates and cups: 盘子, 杯子.
• Guests: 朋友.

### Write the plan
Write the plan in a list. For example:
1. 蛋糕 dàngāo
2. 蜡烛 làzhú
3. 盘子 pánzi
4. 杯子 bēizi
5. 礼物 lǐwù
6. 朋友 péngyou

### Write a sentence about activities
Write two sentences about what you will do at the party.
我们唱歌。wǒmen chànggē. We sing.
我们切蛋糕。wǒmen qiē dàngāo. We cut the cake.

### Read to the class
Read your plan aloud. Ask your classmates if they have any ideas to add. Listening to ideas from others is a good way to improve a plan.

## PAGE 5: Designing a birthday card
Now make a birthday card for a friend. You can use recycled materials such as old cardboard, paper from newspapers, bottle tops, leaves or scrap cloth. This is a good way to reduce waste.

### Steps
1. Fold the card in half.
2. Decorate the front with drawings or cut-out shapes.
3. Write the greeting neatly on the inside:
祝你生日快乐!
zhù nǐ shēngrì kuàilè!
4. Sign your name at the end.
5. Give the card to your friend, or display it for feedback.

### Check the writing
Before you give the card to your friend, check the following.
• Are the characters in the right order?
• Is the handwriting neat and clear?
• Is the pinyin spelt correctly with tone marks?
• Is there an exclamation mark at the end?

### Share your design
Show your card to the class and explain how you made it. Listen to what your classmates say. Note something you can improve next time.

### Caring for a friend
A birthday card shows that you care. Choose kind words. A card made by hand, with love, is a special gift.

## PAGE 6: Writing clearly matters
Why do we write neatly and legibly? Legible writing is writing that can be read easily. If your handwriting is neat, other people can understand your message. If it is messy, they may misread it.

### Check your writing
• Spelling: are all the letters and tones correct?
• Handwriting: is it neat and easy to read?
• Punctuation: is there a full stop or exclamation mark?
• Meaning: does it say what I want?

### Vocabulary review
生日, 快乐, 蛋糕, 礼物, 蜡烛, 朋友, 盘子, 杯子, 刀, 祝你生日快乐.

### Common mistakes
• Writing the tone mark over the wrong vowel.
• Making some characters too large and others too small.
• Forgetting the exclamation mark in the greeting.

### Summary
You can spell birthday words correctly, label a picture, write neat characters in squares, plan a party and make a birthday card. Writing carefully shows respect for the person who reads your work.

## QUIZ
1. What is a 田字格 used for?
   A) Drawing maps
   B) Writing characters neatly in a square with cross lines
   C) Counting money
   D) Cutting paper
   Answer: B
   Why: It is a square with a cross, which helps keep characters neat and centred.
2. How many strokes does 日 have?
   A) 3
   B) 4
   C) 5
   D) 6
   Answer: B
   Why: 日 has four strokes.
3. What does 祝你生日快乐 say?
   A) Thank you for the gift
   B) Please come to my party
   C) Where is the cake
   D) Happy birthday to you
   Answer: D
   Why: 祝 is wish, 你 is you, 生日 is birthday and 快乐 is happy.
4. Which spelling is right for the word "cake"?
   A) dangao
   B) dàngāo
   C) dāngào
   D) dǎngǎo
   Answer: B
   Why: The tones are falling on dàng and high on gāo.
5. Why should we use recycled materials to make a card?
   A) They are always expensive
   B) They reduce waste
   C) They are not allowed
   D) They make the card heavy
   Answer: B
   Why: Reusing old materials means less rubbish.
6. Write "happy birthday to you" in characters and pinyin. (2 marks)
   Answer: 祝你生日快乐! zhù nǐ shēngrì kuàilè!
   Why: 1 mark for characters and 1 mark for pinyin.
7. Write a list of three things you need for a birthday party, in Chinese, with pinyin. (3 marks)
   Answer: For example 蛋糕 dàngāo, 蜡烛 làzhú, 礼物 lǐwù.
   Why: 1 mark for each correct item with pinyin.
8. Give three tips for neat handwriting of Chinese characters. (3 marks)
   Answer: Keep every character the same size, keep strokes straight and firm, leave equal spaces, do not join separate strokes and write in the right stroke order.
   Why: 1 mark for each correct tip.
9. Explain why writing neatly is important. (2 marks)
   Answer: Others can read and understand your message easily, and it shows respect for the reader.
   Why: 1 mark for understanding and 1 mark for respect or clear communication.
