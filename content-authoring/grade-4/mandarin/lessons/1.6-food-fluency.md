## PAGE 1: Foods and drinks in Mandarin
Food is something every one of us enjoys. In this lesson you will learn to say the names of foods and drinks in Mandarin, and you will learn to speak fluently. Being fluent means speaking smoothly, not stopping after every word.

Fruits 水果 shuǐguǒ
芒果 mángguǒ: mango
香蕉 xiāngjiāo: banana
葡萄 pútao: grapes
苹果 píngguǒ: apple

Vegetables 蔬菜 shūcài
菠菜 bōcài: spinach
橄榄菜 gǎnlǎncài: a salty preserved green vegetable

Meat 肉 ròu
牛肉 niúròu: beef
鸡肉 jīròu: chicken

Drinks 饮料 yǐnliào
茶 chá: tea
咖啡 kāfēi: coffee

Staple foods
米饭 mǐfàn: cooked rice
饼 bǐng: flat bread or cake

### Say it and copy it
Listen to each word in a recording or from your teacher. Repeat each word three times. Try to keep the tones correct even when you speak quickly.

## PAGE 2: Asking and answering
Two simple sentences help you talk about food when you look at pictures.

这是什么? zhè shì shénme? What is this?
这是香蕉。zhè shì xiāngjiāo. This is a banana.

Notice how the question word 什么 shénme (what) takes the place of the answer. This is the same as the question 这是谁?, where 谁 took the place of a person.

### More examples
这是什么? 这是芒果。
zhè shì shénme? zhè shì mángguǒ.
What is this? This is a mango.

这是什么? 这是茶。
zhè shì shénme? zhè shì chá.
What is this? This is tea.

### Adding a colour
This is a red apple: 这是红色的苹果。zhè shì hóngsè de píngguǒ. The word 红色 hóngsè means red, and 的 de connects a describing word to the thing it describes.

### Partner practice
Take turns. Point to a picture card and ask 这是什么? Your partner answers with 这是 and the word. When you have finished, swap roles. Try to keep going without stopping too long between words. That is fluency.

## PAGE 3: Meals of the day
Mandarin has three names for the meals. All three end with 饭 fàn, which means cooked food or meal.

早饭 zǎofàn: breakfast (早 zǎo means morning)
午饭 wǔfàn: lunch (午 wǔ means noon)
晚饭 wǎnfàn: dinner or supper (晚 wǎn means evening)

Two verbs we use with food:
吃 chī: to eat
喝 hē: to drink

We eat food and drink drinks.
我吃早饭。wǒ chī zǎofàn. I eat breakfast.
我喝茶。wǒ hē chá. I drink tea.
我早饭吃饼。wǒ zǎofàn chī bǐng. For breakfast I eat bǐng.

### When do we eat each food?
Look at pictures and decide when each food is usually eaten. Your class may say tea and bread at breakfast, rice and chicken at lunch, and rice with vegetables in the evening. Different families eat different things, and that is fine.

### Word check
• The syllable 饭 fàn is falling, like a short "no".
• 吃 chī is high and level.
• 喝 hē is also high and level.

## PAGE 4: Pace and intonation
Pace is how fast you speak. Intonation is the rise and fall of your voice. To speak fluently in Mandarin, you need a pace that is steady and a voice that clearly shows each tone.

### Steps to good pace
1. Read the sentence once silently.
2. Read it slowly, one word at a time.
3. Read it again, joining words together.
4. Read it at a natural speed without rushing.

### Statements and questions
When you make a statement, your voice comes down a little at the end.
这是香蕉。 (zhè shì xiāngjiāo.)

When you ask a question, your voice may go slightly up at the end.
这是什么? (zhè shì shénme?)
Still, every syllable must keep its own tone.

### Example: reading with pace
zhè / shì / mángguǒ. (slow)
zhè shì mángguǒ. (joined)
Say each version three times.

### Listen to a text
Your teacher reads: 我早饭吃饼,喝茶。午饭吃米饭和鸡肉。 (wǒ zǎofàn chī bǐng, hē chá. wǔfàn chī mǐfàn hé jīròu.) Listen and repeat with the same pace and rise and fall as your teacher. It means: For breakfast I eat bǐng and drink tea. For lunch I eat rice and chicken.

## PAGE 5: A tongue twister
A tongue twister is a sentence that is hard to say fast. Chinese children love this one about grapes.

吃葡萄不吐葡萄皮,
不吃葡萄倒吐葡萄皮。

Pinyin:
Chī pútao bù tǔ pútao pí,
bù chī pútao dào tǔ pútao pí.

Meaning: If you eat grapes, you do not spit out the grape skins. If you do not eat grapes, you spit out the grape skins (instead).

New words: 吐 tǔ (to spit out), 皮 pí (skin), 不 bù (not), 倒 dào (instead, the opposite).

### How to master it
1. Say it very slowly, word by word.
2. Say each half-line separately.
3. Speed up, but keep it clear.
4. Try it with a friend, one line each.

### Why tongue twisters help
Tongue twisters make your mouth practise sounds. The syllables pú, pí, chī and tǔ use the puff of air (p, t, ch). If you can say them clearly, you are getting better at Mandarin.

Do not spit real grape skins in class. Just say the words!

## PAGE 6: Food and health
Which foods help our bodies stay healthy? Talk with a partner about what you eat every day.

• Fruits such as 芒果 and 香蕉 give vitamins.
• Vegetables such as 菠菜 (spinach) help keep us strong.
• Meat such as 牛肉 and 鸡肉 helps us grow.
• Rice, bread and other staple foods give energy.
• Water and tea are drinks. Water is 水 shuǐ. It is the best drink for our bodies.

### A healthy plate
A healthy meal has some staple food, some vegetables, some protein and a fruit. Try describing your favourite plate in Chinese: 我喜欢吃米饭和菠菜。 (wǒ xǐhuan chī mǐfàn hé bōcài: I like to eat rice and spinach.) The word 喜欢 xǐhuan means to like.

### Review
• Fruit: 芒果, 香蕉, 葡萄, 苹果.
• Drinks: 茶, 咖啡, 水.
• Meals: 早饭, 午饭, 晚饭.
• Sentences: 这是什么? 这是…

### Common mistakes
• Saying 吃 for drinks: we use 喝 for drinks.
• Speeding up so much that the tones disappear.
• Forgetting that 什么 replaces the answer word.

## QUIZ
1. What does 香蕉 xiāngjiāo mean?
   A) Mango
   B) Banana
   C) Grapes
   D) Apple
   Answer: B
   Why: 香蕉 is banana. Mango is 芒果, grapes are 葡萄 and apple is 苹果.
2. Which word means "lunch"?
   A) 早饭 zǎofàn
   B) 晚饭 wǎnfàn
   C) 米饭 mǐfàn
   D) 午饭 wǔfàn
   Answer: D
   Why: 午 means noon, so 午饭 is lunch. 早饭 is breakfast and 晚饭 is dinner.
3. Which verb is used with drinks?
   A) 吃 chī
   B) 喝 hē
   C) 是 shì
   D) 有 yǒu
   Answer: B
   Why: 喝 hē means to drink, as in 我喝茶.
4. How do you ask "What is this?" in Mandarin?
   A) 这是谁?
   B) 你好吗?
   C) 这是什么?
   D) 电视在哪儿?
   Answer: C
   Why: 这是什么? uses 什么 for "what".
5. What is a tongue twister useful for?
   A) Writing characters
   B) Practising clear, fluent pronunciation
   C) Counting numbers
   D) Learning days of the week
   Answer: B
   Why: Tongue twisters help the mouth practise hard sounds and speed.
6. Answer in Chinese and pinyin: "What is this?" when you hold a mango. (2 marks)
   Answer: 这是芒果。 zhè shì mángguǒ.
   Why: 1 mark for the correct sentence and 1 mark for the pinyin.
7. Write the three meals of the day in Chinese with pinyin and meaning. (3 marks)
   Answer: 早饭 zǎofàn breakfast, 午饭 wǔfàn lunch, 晚饭 wǎnfàn dinner.
   Why: 1 mark for each meal with pinyin and meaning.
8. Give two steps for reading a sentence at a good pace. (2 marks)
   Answer: For example, read it silently first, then read slowly one word at a time, then join the words at a natural speed.
   Why: 1 mark for each sensible step.
9. Explain why fruits, vegetables and staple foods are all needed in a healthy meal. (3 marks)
   Answer: Fruits and vegetables give vitamins that keep the body healthy. Staple foods like rice or bread give energy. A meal with all of these helps the body grow and stay strong.
   Why: 1 mark for vitamins from fruit and vegetables, 1 mark for energy from staple foods, 1 mark for the link to good health.
