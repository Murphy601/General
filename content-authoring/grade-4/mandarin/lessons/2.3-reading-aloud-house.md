## PAGE 1: Reading the names of rooms and things
When you read aloud, you turn writing into sound. To do this well you must know the words. Here are the words for rooms and things in a house. Read them aloud and say the English meaning after each one.

厨房 chúfáng: kitchen
客厅 kètīng: living room
卧室 wòshì: bedroom
厕所 cèsuǒ: toilet
桌子 zhuōzi: table
椅子 yǐzi: chair
床 chuáng: bed
电视 diànshì: television
冰箱 bīngxiāng: fridge

### Reading with a partner
Work in pairs or small groups. Take a picture card, for example a picture of a bed. Read its name aloud: 床 chuáng. Your partner checks the card and says "对" (right) if the reading is correct.

### How to read a long word
Each word has two syllables, except 床 chuáng which has one. Read one syllable at a time. Then join them.
1. zhuō ... zi
2. zhuōzi
The first syllable carries the main sound. The second syllable is light in zhuōzi and yǐzi.

### Underlining exercise
Read the sentence: 我家有客厅、卧室和厨房。 Underline the words for rooms in your exercise book. There are three: 客厅, 卧室 and 厨房. The character 、 is a special comma. We use it between items in a list.

## PAGE 2: A paragraph to read
Read this paragraph slowly. Then read it again smoothly.

我家有三个房间。客厅里有电视和桌子。卧室里有床。厨房里有冰箱。
wǒ jiā yǒu sān gè fángjiān. kètīng lǐ yǒu diànshì hé zhuōzi. wòshì lǐ yǒu chuáng. chúfáng lǐ yǒu bīngxiāng.

Meaning: My home has three rooms. In the living room there is a television and a table. In the bedroom there is a bed. In the kitchen there is a fridge.

New words: 个 gè (a word used when counting), 房间 fángjiān (room), 里 lǐ (inside), 有 yǒu (there is, has).

### Find the words
Read the paragraph again. Underline every room word and circle every thing in the house. Rooms: 客厅, 卧室, 厨房. Things: 电视, 桌子, 床, 冰箱.

### Answer questions
1. How many rooms are in the house? (three)
2. What is in the kitchen? (a fridge)
3. Where is the bed? (in the bedroom)

### Read to the class
Choose one sentence to read to the class. Speak clearly and loud enough for the back row to hear you. Speak slowly. Pause after each full stop.

## PAGE 3: Dialogues about where things are
Dialogues are conversations. Reading them aloud makes your speech natural. Read the dialogues in pairs.

Dialogue 1
A: 电视在哪儿?
   diànshì zài nǎr?
B: 电视在客厅。
   diànshì zài kètīng.

Dialogue 2
A: 冰箱在哪儿?
   bīngxiāng zài nǎr?
B: 冰箱在厨房。
   bīngxiāng zài chúfáng.

Dialogue 3
A: 椅子在哪儿?
   yǐzi zài nǎr?
B: 椅子在桌子下。
   yǐzi zài zhuōzi xià.

Dialogue 4
A: 电视在桌子上吗?
   diànshì zài zhuōzi shàng ma?
B: 是的,电视在桌子上。
   shì de, diànshì zài zhuōzi shàng.

New words: 吗 ma (a word that turns a statement into a yes-or-no question), 是的 shì de (yes, that is right).

### Practise in pairs
Read the dialogue, then swap parts. When you swap, add your own things and places. Use only the words you know. Listen to your partner's voice as well as your own, and notice whether the question lifts at the end and the answer settles down.

## PAGE 4: Intonation and expression
Intonation is the way your voice rises and falls when you speak. Expression is the feeling you put in your voice. Both help your listener understand what you mean.

### Statements, questions and surprises
• A statement: your voice ends down. 电视在客厅。
• A question: your voice ends a little higher. 电视在哪儿?
• A surprise: your voice is louder and higher. 电视在桌子下!
In Mandarin, every syllable must keep its own tone, so the rise at the end of a question is only a light lift. It never replaces the tone.

### Emphasis
To show which word matters most, say it a little stronger. Read these two sentences.
1. 床在卧室。 (The bed is in the bedroom, not somewhere else.)
2. 床在卧室。 (It is the bed that is in the bedroom, not the table.)
Say the sentence twice and emphasise a different word each time.

### Expression practice
Read the sentence 冰箱在厨房。 in three ways: calmly, as a question (冰箱在厨房吗?) and proudly. Your partner guesses which way you are reading.

### Good reading habit
Read with your eyes ahead of your voice. Look at the next few words while you say the present ones. This helps you keep smooth and steady, with the right expression.

## PAGE 5: A chant about putting things away
A chant is a short poem said with a beat. Clap the beat as you read.

床在卧室,
冰箱在厨房,
电视在客厅,
东西放好,家里真好。

Pinyin:
Chuáng zài wòshì,
bīngxiāng zài chúfáng,
diànshì zài kètīng,
dōngxi fàng hǎo, jiālǐ zhēn hǎo.

Meaning: The bed is in the bedroom. The fridge is in the kitchen. The television is in the living room. When things are put away well, home is really nice.

New words: 东西 dōngxi (things), 放 fàng (to put), 家里 jiālǐ (at home), 真 zhēn (really).

### Chanting steps
1. Read the whole chant silently.
2. Read it aloud slowly.
3. Add a clap on each line.
4. Say it all together with the class.

### Why put things away?
When things are placed where they belong, the house stays clean and safe. You can find your things quickly. A tidy home is also easier for everyone to move around in. Do you tidy your room at home?

## PAGE 6: Giving and receiving feedback
Good readers get better when friends tell them how to improve. This is called peer feedback. Here is how to do it well.

### Steps for kind feedback
1. Say what was good: "Your tones were clear."
2. Say one thing to improve: "Say the second syllable of zhuōzi lighter."
3. Thank your friend for listening.

### Feedback checklist
When your partner reads a dialogue, look at these points.
• Pronunciation: are the sounds right?
• Tones: are they clear?
• Expression: does the voice go up for a question?
• Speed: not too fast, not too slow.
Tick each item as you hear it.

### Receiving feedback
If a friend gives you a hint, listen with an open mind. Do not argue. Say "谢谢" (thank you) and try again. Humility helps you learn.

### Common mistakes
• Reading every syllable in the same tone.
• Saying 在 after the place instead of before it.
• Forgetting that zi is light in zhuōzi and yǐzi.

### Summary
You have read rooms and things, a paragraph, dialogues and a chant. You have also practised intonation and giving peer feedback.

## QUIZ
1. What does 卧室 wòshì mean?
   A) Kitchen
   B) Toilet
   C) Bedroom
   D) Living room
   Answer: C
   Why: 卧室 is the bedroom.
2. Read: 冰箱在厨房。 Where is the fridge?
   A) In the bedroom
   B) In the kitchen
   C) In the living room
   D) On the table
   Answer: B
   Why: 厨房 is kitchen and 在 means is in.
3. What does the word 吗 ma do at the end of a sentence?
   A) It makes a statement
   B) It means "mother"
   C) It makes a yes-or-no question
   D) It means "and"
   Answer: C
   Why: 吗 turns a statement into a yes-or-no question, as in 电视在桌子上吗?
4. Which of these is the best way to show a question when reading aloud?
   A) Let your voice end a little higher
   B) Whisper the whole sentence
   C) Read very slowly
   D) Read without tones
   Answer: A
   Why: In a question the voice lifts a little at the end, but every syllable keeps its tone.
5. What is the meaning of 东西放好?
   A) Things put away well
   B) Please sit down
   C) I am at home
   D) The weather is good
   Answer: A
   Why: 东西 is things and 放好 is put away well.
6. Read this line aloud and write the meaning: 床在卧室。 (2 marks)
   Answer: chuáng zài wòshì. The bed is in the bedroom.
   Why: 1 mark for the pinyin and 1 mark for the meaning.
7. Write the Chinese question "Is the television on the table?" with pinyin. (3 marks)
   Answer: 电视在桌子上吗? diànshì zài zhuōzi shàng ma?
   Why: 1 mark for 电视在桌子上, 1 mark for 吗, 1 mark for pinyin.
8. Explain what intonation is and give one example of how it helps. (2 marks)
   Answer: Intonation is how the voice rises and falls. For example, a question ends a little higher so the listener knows a question is asked.
   Why: 1 mark for the meaning and 1 mark for a correct example.
9. Give two rules for kind peer feedback. (2 marks)
   Answer: Say what was good, say one thing to improve, and thank your friend.
   Why: 1 mark for each rule.
