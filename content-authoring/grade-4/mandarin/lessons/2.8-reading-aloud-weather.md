The weather words to find:
太阳 tàiyáng: sun
云 yún: cloud
风 fēng: wind
雨 yǔ: rain
雪 xuě: snow

### How to find them
1. Read the first row aloud: 太, 阳, 很, 大, 云. Can you see 太阳 at the start and 云 at the end?
2. Read the second row: 风, 雨, 雪, 小, 我. You will find three weather words in a row: 风, 雨 and 雪.
3. Keep going down the grid.
The third row has 天气 tiānqì (weather). The fourth row has 太阳 and 雨 again.

### Read what you circled
Read each word aloud as you circle it. Say the pinyin too. Check with a partner that you found all of them.

## PAGE 2: Reading phrases and sentences
Now read whole phrases. Use the pattern you know: 这是...

这是太阳。zhè shì tàiyáng. This is the sun.
这是云。zhè shì yún. This is a cloud.
太阳很大。tàiyáng hěn dà. The sun is very big.
风很大。fēng hěn dà. The wind is strong.
雨很大。yǔ hěn dà. The rain is heavy.

### The lucky bag game
Write each word or phrase on a small slip of paper and put them all in a bag. Each learner takes turns to pick a slip without looking and reads it aloud. The class repeats after them. Everyone gets an equal chance. If a slip has a word you cannot read, ask a friend for a hint.

### Pointing while reading
Use your finger to follow each character as you read. When you get to the end of the phrase, look up and say it once more without your finger. This helps you remember the phrase.

### A quick check
After each reading, the class tells the reader one thing that was good. For example: "You said yǔ with a very clear dipping tone."

## PAGE 3: Pauses
A pause is a short stop while reading. Pauses give you time to breathe. They help the listener understand where one idea ends and the next begins.

### Punctuation and pauses
• A comma (,) means a short pause.
• A full stop (。) means a longer pause.
• The mark 、 between items in a list also means a very short pause.
Read this line and pause at the marks:
太阳、云、风、雨、雪,这是天气。
tàiyáng, yún, fēng, yǔ, xuě, zhè shì tiānqì.
Sun, cloud, wind, rain, snow: these are the weather.

### Pauses give emphasis
A pause before an important word makes it stand out.
今天 ... 太阳很大。 Today ... the sun is very big.
Try to say it with a short pause after 今天.

### Practise
Read these two lines. Tap the desk at every comma or full stop.
1. 云很小,风很大。 yún hěn xiǎo, fēng hěn dà.
2. 今天有雨,太阳不大。 jīntiān yǒu yǔ, tàiyáng bú dà.
The second line uses 不 bú, which becomes bú before a falling tone. You will learn more about this later. For now, just copy your teacher.

## PAGE 4: A weather chant
A chant is fun and helps you practise pauses. Read it with a partner. Take a breath at every comma and lift your hand at each full stop.

太阳很大,
云很小。
风来了,
雨来了,
雪也来了。

Pinyin:
Tàiyáng hěn dà,
yún hěn xiǎo.
Fēng lái le,
yǔ lái le,
xuě yě lái le.

Meaning: The sun is very big. The clouds are small. The wind has come. The rain has come. The snow has come too.

New words: 来 lái (to come), 了 le (a small word showing that something has happened), 也 yě (also, too).

### Chanting steps
1. One group reads the first two lines.
2. Another group reads the next three lines.
3. Swap.
4. Read the whole chant together.

### Notice the rhythm
The last three lines have the same pattern, "___ lái le". This repetition makes the chant easy to remember. Clap on each syllable and try to keep the beat.

### Think about it
Weather changes during the year. In the real world sun, wind, rain and clouds all come at different times. What kind of weather do you enjoy most?

## PAGE 5: Words that look alike
Some Chinese characters look very similar, but have different meanings. Careful readers can tell them apart.

大 dà: big
太 tài: too, very (太阳 is sun)
天 tiān: sky, day (今天 is today)

Look closely. 大 is the plainest one. 太 has an extra dot. 天 has an extra line on top of 大.

人 rén: person
入 rù: to enter
八 bā: eight

Look closely again. 人 and 入 are almost mirror images. In 人 the long stroke is on the left, and in 入 the long stroke is on the right. 八 is different: its two short strokes lean away from each other and do not touch.

### A game
Your teacher writes one of these characters on the board without saying it. You read it aloud. Your partner points to the correct meaning card. Score a point for each correct answer.

### Why does this matter?
If you read 天 as 大, you will get the wrong word. 今天 (today) is not 今大. The small details in a character change the meaning, just as the tone changes the meaning of a syllable. Read slowly and look at each character with care.

### Weather sentence
Read: 今天天气很好。 jīntiān tiānqì hěn hǎo. The weather is very good today. Which characters look alike in this sentence? Find 天 and 今.

## PAGE 6: Fluent reading with the right pauses
When you read aloud, remember three points about fluency: know your words, pause in the right places, and keep the tones clear.

### Read-aloud checklist
• I read the words without stopping.
• I paused at commas and full stops.
• I kept my tones correct.
• I read at a steady speed.
Give yourself a tick for each one.

### Peer evaluation
Listen to a classmate reading the chant. Say two good things and one thing to try next time. Listen to feedback on your own reading and try to use it.

### Fairness
When picking words from the bag or taking turns in the chant, wait patiently. Everyone should get a chance. That is fair, and it is also how we make friends.

### Common mistakes
• Pausing in the middle of a word, for example tài ... yáng.
• Reading 大 for 太 or 天.
• Forgetting the pause after a comma.

### Summary
You can find weather words in a word square, read phrases and a chant with correct pauses, and tell similar characters apart. Read a little every day to grow your fluency.

## QUIZ
1. What does 云 yún mean?
   A) Wind
   B) Sun
   C) Cloud
   D) Rain
   Answer: C
   Why: 云 is a cloud.
2. How long is the pause at a full stop (。) compared with a comma?
   A) The same
   B) Shorter
   C) Longer
   D) No pause
   Answer: C
   Why: A full stop needs a longer pause than a comma.
3. What does 风来了 fēng lái le mean?
   A) The rain has come
   B) The sun is big
   C) The wind has come
   D) The cloud is small
   Answer: C
   Why: 风 is wind, 来 is come and 了 shows that it has happened.
4. Which character means "big"?
   A) 大
   B) 天
   C) 太
   D) 人
   Answer: A
   Why: 大 dà means big. 天 is sky or day, 太 is too and 人 is person.
5. Why do we pause when we read aloud?
   A) To forget the words
   B) To breathe and show where ideas begin and end
   C) To change the tones
   D) To read faster
   Answer: B
   Why: Pauses let you breathe and help listeners follow the ideas.
6. Read aloud and write the pinyin and meaning: 雨很大。 (2 marks)
   Answer: yǔ hěn dà. The rain is heavy.
   Why: 1 mark for pinyin and 1 mark for meaning.
7. List all five weather words you found in the word square, with pinyin. (3 marks)
   Answer: 太阳 tàiyáng, 云 yún, 风 fēng, 雨 yǔ, 雪 xuě.
   Why: 1 mark for two words, 2 marks for three or four words, 3 marks for all five with pinyin.
8. Say how you can tell 天 tiān from 大 dà when you look at them. (2 marks)
   Answer: 天 has an extra line on top of the shape 大.
   Why: 1 mark for the extra line and 1 mark for correct naming of both.
9. Explain why it is fair to pick words from a bag in turns. (2 marks)
   Answer: Everyone gets an equal chance to read, so nobody is left out.
   Why: 1 mark for equal chance and 1 mark for a fair reason.
