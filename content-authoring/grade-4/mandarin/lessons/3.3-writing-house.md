## PAGE 1: Writing the names of rooms and things
Correct spelling matters. When you write a Mandarin word in pinyin, every letter and every tone mark counts. Let us write the words for the rooms and things at home.

厨房 chúfáng: kitchen
客厅 kètīng: living room
卧室 wòshì: bedroom
厕所 cèsuǒ: toilet
桌子 zhuōzi: table
椅子 yǐzi: chair
床 chuáng: bed
电视 diànshì: television
冰箱 bīngxiāng: fridge

### Copy with care
Copy each word three times. Look at the letters carefully.
• chuáng has the letters c-h-u-a-n-g, and the tone mark is on the a.
• zhuōzi has the tone mark on the o of zhuō, and no mark on zi.
• bīngxiāng is written as one word, with two tone marks, one on each i and a.
• yǐzi has a mark above the i, and the dot disappears.

### Listen and write: dictation
Your teacher reads the words aloud one by one. Write each word in pinyin. You may try to write the characters that you know. Then check your spelling against the list above. Give yourself one point for every word you spelt correctly, including all tone marks.

### Picture labels
Look at pictures of rooms and things. Write the pinyin name of each thing under its picture.

## PAGE 2: Some characters at home
Some of the house characters are simple. Let us look at a few, and count their strokes.

床 chuáng (bed): 7 strokes
门 mén (door): 3 strokes
上 shàng (on): 3 strokes
下 xià (under): 3 strokes
在 zài (is at): 6 strokes
电 diàn (electricity, the first part of 电视): 5 strokes
房 fáng (house, room): 8 strokes

### Look at the meaning
The character 门 looks like a door with two panels. 上 has a short line above a longer line, showing "up". 下 has a short line below, showing "down".

### Write and check
Write each character three times. Count the strokes as you write. Say the stroke names aloud: 横, 竖, 撇, 捺, 点. Check with a partner.

### Write in order
Remember these rules.
• Top to bottom: 下 starts with the long line at the top, and its dot comes last.
• 上 starts with the vertical stroke, then the short line, then the long line at the bottom.
• Horizontal before vertical when they cross.
• Left to right.
Write 上 and 下 slowly. Ask a friend to check that you wrote the strokes in the right order.

### A tip
If the character is too hard, do not give up. Start with pinyin and add characters one by one, until you can write them all.

### The pattern
Thing + 在 + place (+ 上 or 下).

Examples:
床在卧室。chuáng zài wòshì. The bed is in the bedroom.
冰箱在厨房。bīngxiāng zài chúfáng. The fridge is in the kitchen.
椅子在桌子下。yǐzi zài zhuōzi xià. The chair is under the table.
电视在客厅。diànshì zài kètīng. The television is in the living room.

### Another pattern: 有
客厅里有电视。kètīng lǐ yǒu diànshì. In the living room there is a television.
The pattern is place + 里 + 有 + thing.

### Write it
Write three sentences of your own. Use the pattern each time. Read your sentences aloud. Do they make sense?

## PAGE 4: Rearranging word cards
Rearranging jumbled cards helps you understand how sentences are built. Work in pairs.

### Puzzle set
Card set 1: 在 / 冰箱 / 厨房
Correct: 冰箱在厨房。

Card set 2: 桌子 / 电视 / 上 / 在
Correct: 电视在桌子上。

Card set 3: 卧室 / 床 / 在
Correct: 床在卧室。

Card set 4: 下 / 在 / 椅子 / 桌子
Correct: 椅子在桌子下。

### How to do it
1. Look for the thing: it goes at the start.
2. Find 在: it comes next.
3. Find the place.
4. If there is 上 or 下, it goes at the end.
5. Read the sentence and see whether it makes sense.

### Peer review
Swap your sentences with a partner. Check the order of the words, the tone marks and the full stop. Say something kind, then say what to change. For example: "Your sentence is correct. Add a full stop at the end."

### Make your own jumbled cards
Write a sentence on a card, then cut it up into words. Give it to a friend to put in order.

## PAGE 5: Picture prompts and a model house
You can write sentences from pictures. Look at a picture and write what you see. This is called a picture prompt.

### Picture prompt example
Picture: a bed in a bedroom.
Sentence: 床在卧室。
Picture: a chair under a table.
Sentence: 椅子在桌子下。

### Make a model house
Build a model of a house in your classroom. Use cardboard boxes, paper, sticks and bottle tops. Make a kitchen, a living room, a bedroom and a toilet. Place small furniture in each room. Then label everything with pinyin and characters.
• 厨房 chúfáng on the kitchen.
• 客厅 kètīng on the living room.
• 卧室 wòshì on the bedroom.
• 床 chuáng on the bed.

### Write about your model
Write three sentences about your model house. For example: 床在卧室。冰箱在厨房。电视在客厅。

### Be creative and safe
Use clean, safe materials. Do not use sharp objects without an adult. Use recycled boxes and paper, so nothing is wasted. Work with your team and share the work fairly.

## PAGE 6: Spelling matters
Why is correct spelling important when writing? A small mistake can change the meaning. For example, chuáng (bed) and chuāng (window, as in 窗户) are different words with different tones. If you write the wrong tone, the reader may think of the wrong thing.

### Check your spelling
Before you finish writing, check these things.
• Are all the letters correct?
• Are the tone marks above the correct vowels?
• Is there a space between words?
• Is there a full stop at the end?
Read your work aloud once more to catch mistakes.

### Vocabulary review
厨房 chúfáng, 客厅 kètīng, 卧室 wòshì, 厕所 cèsuǒ, 桌子 zhuōzi, 椅子 yǐzi, 床 chuáng, 电视 diànshì, 冰箱 bīngxiāng.

### Common mistakes
• Writing 在 after the place.
• Forgetting the tone mark on chuáng.
• Writing zi with a tone mark in zhuōzi and yǐzi.

### Summary
You have written room and furniture names, counted strokes, used the location pattern, rearranged word cards and made a model house. Neat and correct work helps others read what you write.

## QUIZ
1. How many strokes does the character 上 have?
   A) 2
   B) 3
   C) 4
   D) 5
   Answer: B
   Why: 上 has three strokes.
2. Which sentence is correct for "The bed is in the bedroom"?
   A) 卧室在床。
   B) 在床卧室。
   C) 床在卧室。
   D) 床卧室在。
   Answer: C
   Why: The pattern is thing + 在 + place.
3. How is 冰箱 written in pinyin?
   A) bīngxiāng
   B) bingxiang
   C) bǐngxiǎng
   D) bìngxiàng
   Answer: A
   Why: The tones on both syllables are the first tone, so it is bīngxiāng.
4. In the pattern for "on the table" (桌子上), which word tells us "on"?
   A) 桌
   B) 子
   C) 上
   D) 在
   Answer: C
   Why: 上 shàng means on.
5. What does 客厅里有电视 mean?
   A) The television is in the kitchen
   B) In the living room there is a television
   C) The living room is on the television
   D) The bedroom has a bed
   Answer: B
   Why: 里 is in, 有 is there is, so the living room has a television.
6. Rearrange and write the correct sentence: 在 / 椅子 / 桌子 / 下. (2 marks)
   Answer: 椅子在桌子下。
   Why: 1 mark for the correct order and 1 mark for the full stop.
7. Write the pinyin for 厨房 and 床 with tone marks and say what they mean. (3 marks)
   Answer: chúfáng, kitchen; chuáng, bed.
   Why: 1 mark for each correct pinyin and 1 mark for both meanings.
8. Write one sentence using 有 about a room in your home. Give the pinyin. (3 marks)
   Answer: For example 卧室里有床。 wòshì lǐ yǒu chuáng.
   Why: 1 mark for the pattern, 1 mark for characters, 1 mark for pinyin.
9. Explain why correct spelling and tone marks matter in writing. (2 marks)
   Answer: A wrong spelling or tone can change the meaning, so the reader may misunderstand, for example chuáng (bed) and chuāng (window).
   Why: 1 mark for saying meaning changes and 1 mark for a correct example.
