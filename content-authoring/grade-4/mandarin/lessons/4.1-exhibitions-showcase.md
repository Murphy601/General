### Look back
Read down the list. For each theme, try to say two more words without looking at your notes. Which theme do you know best? Which one needs more practice? Write your answer in your exercise book. This is called self-assessment, and it helps you to choose what to show.

### Skills you have learnt
• Listening for gist and following instructions.
• Speaking clearly with the right tones.
• Reading aloud with good pauses and expression.
• Writing pinyin with tone marks and characters with the right strokes.

## PAGE 2: Planning your showcase
Good showcases need a plan. Work in groups of four or five.

### Choose what to show
Each group chooses one item. Here are some ideas.
1. A dialogue: two learners greet each other and say their names.
2. A song or chant: the counting song or the body song.
3. A play: a birthday party, with cake, gifts and greetings.
4. A poster: a labelled house plan or a weather chart.
5. A display: characters made from pebbles, sand or clay.
6. A tongue twister: the grape rhyme, said fast and clear.
7. A recording: learners reading a short text.

### Fill in a plan
Write in your plan:
• Our item: ______
• Who does what: ______
• Materials we need: ______
• Words we will use: ______
• Practice days: ______

### Give everyone a job
Every learner needs a job. Someone speaks, someone writes, someone draws, someone helps with materials, and someone keeps the time. Rotate the jobs, so that everyone tries different things.

## PAGE 3: Rehearsing a dialogue
Here is a sample dialogue you can perform. Use it or write your own with the words you know.

A: 你好!
   nǐ hǎo!
B: 你好!我叫安娜。你呢?
   nǐ hǎo! wǒ jiào Ānnà. nǐ ne?
A: 我叫小明。这是我妹妹。
   wǒ jiào Xiǎomíng. zhè shì wǒ mèimei.
B: 你好!
   nǐ hǎo!
A: 今天是我的生日。
   jīntiān shì wǒ de shēngrì.
B: 祝你生日快乐!
   zhù nǐ shēngrì kuàilè!
A: 谢谢!
   xièxie!

### Meaning
A: Hello! B: Hello! My name is Anna. And you? A: My name is Xiaoming. This is my younger sister. B: Hello! A: Today is my birthday. B: Happy birthday! A: Thank you!

### Rehearse
1. Read it aloud together.
2. Say it again without looking at the words.
3. Add actions: wave, smile and hold up a gift.
4. Check the tones for nǐ hǎo, xièxie and kuàilè.
5. Speak loudly and clearly, so the back row can hear.

### Change the words
Replace the names and the family word. You may say 我哥哥 (my older brother), 我姐姐 (my older sister) or 我弟弟 (my younger brother) instead of 我妹妹.

## PAGE 4: Preparing a display
Displays let visitors look at your work.

### What to include
• A title in Chinese and pinyin.
• Pictures with labels. For example: 客厅 kètīng, 沙发 shāfā, 花园 huāyuán.
• Models of characters made from local materials.
• Birthday cards made from recycled paper.
• A food album or healthy plate.
• A weather diary.
• A drawing of the body with the parts labelled.

### Check your display
Before the showcase, check the following.
• Are the tone marks in the right place?
• Are the characters written in the correct stroke order?
• Is the handwriting neat and clear?
• Is the spelling correct?
• Is everything labelled?

### Arrange it well
Put the title at the top. Place the pictures in a clear order, so a visitor can follow them from left to right. Use colour, but do not cover the words. Leave space between items.

### Safety and waste
Use safe materials. Reuse boxes, paper and bottle tops. Clean up afterwards, and put the display materials where they belong.

## PAGE 5: Presenting with confidence
On the day of the showcase, you will speak to an audience. Here are steps to help you.

### Before you speak
• Take a deep breath.
• Stand tall and smile.
• Look at your audience, not at the floor.

### While you speak
• Say each word clearly with the correct tone.
• Speak at a steady speed. Do not rush.
• Pause at commas and full stops.
• Use your voice to show a question or a happy greeting.
• Use your hands and face to help explain.

### Useful sentences
大家好! dàjiā hǎo! Hello, everybody!
我叫 ______。 wǒ jiào ______. My name is ______.
谢谢大家! xièxie dàjiā! Thank you, everybody!
The word 大家 dàjiā means everyone.

### If you make a mistake
Everyone makes mistakes. Stay calm, take a breath and say it again. Your audience will be proud that you tried.

### Being a good audience
Listen quietly. Look at the speaker. Clap when they finish. Do not laugh at mistakes. Write one kind comment for each group.

## PAGE 6: Reflecting and assessing
After the showcase, sit down and think about what went well.

### Self-assessment
Ask yourself:
• What did I do well?
• What was hard?
• What Mandarin words can I say now that I could not say before?
• What will I do next to improve?

### Peer assessment
Give feedback to another group.
1. Say two things you liked.
2. Say one thing to improve.
3. Be kind and honest.

### A simple rubric
Use these levels.
4 stars: very clear tones, correct words, confident and creative.
3 stars: mostly clear tones, mostly correct words, good effort.
2 stars: some tones and words need practice.
1 star: needs more practice. Try again!

### Keep learning
Learning a language never stops. Keep practising your tones every day. Greet a friend in Mandarin, sing a song or read your notes. Keep a journal of new words. You can also learn about China: its food, songs and festivals. Language helps us to understand people from different places, and to be friends with them.

### Summary
A showcase brings together your listening, speaking, reading and writing. Plan carefully, practise together, present with confidence and reflect honestly.

## QUIZ
1. What is the first thing a group should do when planning a showcase?
   A) Perform at once
   B) Choose an item and make a plan
   C) Throw away the materials
   D) Skip the practice
   Answer: B
   Why: A plan tells you who does what and what you need.
2. What does 大家好 dàjiā hǎo mean?
   A) Goodbye
   B) Thank you
   C) Hello, everybody
   D) Happy birthday
   Answer: C
   Why: 大家 is everyone and 好 is good, so it is a greeting to everybody.
3. Which of these should you check before a display?
   A) That tone marks and stroke order are correct
   B) That the pictures are all the same colour
   C) That there are no words
   D) That the display is hidden
   Answer: A
   Why: Correct tone marks, strokes, spelling and neat writing make the display clear.
4. What does 祝你生日快乐 say?
   A) Where are you
   B) Happy birthday to you
   C) See you tomorrow
   D) I like cake
   Answer: B
   Why: 祝 is wish, 生日 is birthday and 快乐 is happy.
5. What should you do if you make a mistake while presenting?
   A) Run away
   B) Stop and cry
   C) Stay calm, take a breath and say it again
   D) Blame a friend
   Answer: C
   Why: Everyone makes mistakes. Staying calm and trying again shows courage.
6. Write a short dialogue of two lines in Chinese with pinyin in which two friends greet each other. (3 marks)
   Answer: A: 你好! nǐ hǎo! B: 你好! nǐ hǎo!
   Why: 1 mark for each line and 1 mark for correct pinyin with tone marks.
7. Give three tips for speaking clearly during a presentation. (3 marks)
   Answer: Say each word clearly with the right tone, speak at a steady speed, pause at punctuation, look at the audience.
   Why: 1 mark for each tip.
8. Name three things you could include in a class display and say why you chose them. (3 marks)
   Answer: For example a labelled house plan, birthday cards and models of characters, because they show words and writing skills.
   Why: 1 mark for each item, and marks may be given for a reasonable reason.
9. Describe how you would give kind, useful feedback to another group. (2 marks)
   Answer: Say two things you liked, then one thing to improve, and speak kindly and honestly.
   Why: 1 mark for the positive comments and 1 mark for the improvement suggestion.
