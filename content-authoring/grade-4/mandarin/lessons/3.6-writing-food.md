## PAGE 1: Writing food vocabulary
When we write, we can choose different words to make our sentences interesting. Learn to write two foods for each food group.

水果 shuǐguǒ (fruit): 芒果 mángguǒ, 香蕉 xiāngjiāo
蔬菜 shūcài (vegetables): 菠菜 bōcài, 橄榄菜 gǎnlǎncài
肉 ròu (meat): 牛肉 niúròu, 鸡肉 jīròu
饮料 yǐnliào (drinks): 茶 chá, 咖啡 kāfēi
零食 língshí (snacks): 饼 bǐng, 饼干 bǐnggān

### Listen and write
Your teacher plays a recording or reads the words aloud. Write down two words from each group in pinyin. Try to write the characters for the ones you know, such as 茶 and 肉. Check with the list above.

### Spelling and tones
• mángguǒ: two marks, on the a of máng and the o of guǒ.
• xiāngjiāo: two marks on the a of xiāng and the a of jiāo.
• bǐng: the mark goes on the i, and the dot disappears.
• kāfēi: two high marks, on the a and the e.

### Writing a food list
Write a list of the foods you eat at home. Group them under fruit, vegetables, meat and drinks. Use pinyin and add characters wherever you can.

## PAGE 2: Meals of the day
Write when each food is eaten.

早饭 zǎofàn: breakfast
午饭 wǔfàn: lunch
晚饭 wǎnfàn: dinner

### Sentences about meals
我早饭吃饼,喝茶。
wǒ zǎofàn chī bǐng, hē chá.
For breakfast I eat bǐng and drink tea.

我午饭吃米饭和鸡肉。
wǒ wǔfàn chī mǐfàn hé jīròu.
For lunch I eat rice and chicken.

我晚饭吃米饭和菠菜。
wǒ wǎnfàn chī mǐfàn hé bōcài.
For dinner I eat rice and spinach.

### Work together
Look at the pictures of foods and drinks. Decide together when each is usually eaten. Write a sentence for each picture, for example 我早饭喝茶。 Decide together, and write the answer that the group agrees on.

### Remember
• Use 吃 for foods and 喝 for drinks.
• Put the meal name before the verb.
• End each sentence with 。

## PAGE 3: Punctuation
Punctuation marks are the small signs that show us how to read a text. In Chinese we use these marks.

。 full stop: at the end of a statement.
, comma: a short pause inside a sentence.
、 list comma: between items in a list.
? question mark: at the end of a question.
! exclamation mark: after a greeting or a strong feeling.

### Try this
The following text has no punctuation. Work with a partner and add the correct marks.
我早饭吃饼喝茶你早饭吃什么
Correct: 我早饭吃饼,喝茶。你早饭吃什么?
Explanation: The comma comes after 饼, and the full stop after 茶 finishes the first sentence. The question mark comes at the end of the second sentence.

### Another text
我喜欢水果 我喜欢芒果 香蕉 葡萄 你呢
Correct: 我喜欢水果。我喜欢芒果、香蕉、葡萄。你呢?
Here the list comma 、 separates the three fruits.

### Why punctuate?
Without punctuation, a reader must guess where each idea ends. Punctuation shows how the text should sound when we read aloud. Take turns reading the punctuated texts aloud with a clear pause at each mark.

## PAGE 4: Counting strokes
Counting strokes helps you to write characters carefully, and helps you find them in a dictionary. Here are food characters and their number of strokes.

茶 chá (tea): 9 strokes
米 mǐ (rice): 6 strokes
肉 ròu (meat): 6 strokes
饭 fàn (meal, cooked rice): 7 strokes
牛 niú (cow): 4 strokes
鸡 jī (chicken): 7 strokes
饼 bǐng (flat bread): 9 strokes
果 guǒ (fruit): 8 strokes
吃 chī (to eat): 6 strokes
喝 hē (to drink): 12 strokes

### Do it yourself
Write each character carefully, saying the stroke names as you go. Then count the strokes. Does your count match the list?

### A shortcut
米 looks like a grain of rice with a cross in the middle. 牛 has four simple strokes, and you can see a cow's head and horns in it if you use your imagination. Tricks like this can help you remember.

### Practise
Write 牛肉 and 鸡肉 as words. They share the character 肉. Beef is 牛肉 (cow meat) and chicken is 鸡肉 (chicken meat). Say the words aloud: niúròu, jīròu.

## PAGE 5: A food album and a healthy plate
Make a food album. Cut out or draw pictures of foods and drinks from old newspapers, magazines or photos. Stick each picture on a page, and write a simple sentence under it.

### Example album page
Picture: bananas.
Sentence: 这是香蕉。 zhè shì xiāngjiāo.
Picture: tea.
Sentence: 我喜欢喝茶。 wǒ xǐhuan hē chá.

### Design a healthy plate
With your group, draw a plate like the one in the figure. Add foods you like from each group. Label them in Chinese and pinyin. Explain why each group is important. Vegetables and fruit give vitamins, rice gives energy and meat helps the body grow.

### Discuss
A healthy body needs many kinds of food. Why is it good to eat vegetables and fruits every day? What can you do to make sure your family eats a balanced diet?

## PAGE 6: Review and common mistakes
Let us review what you have learnt about writing about foods.

### Checklist for your writing
• I used the right verb, 吃 or 喝.
• I used punctuation marks in the right places.
• I checked my spelling and tone marks.
• My handwriting is neat.

### Vocabulary review
芒果 mángguǒ, 香蕉 xiāngjiāo, 菠菜 bōcài, 牛肉 niúròu, 鸡肉 jīròu, 茶 chá, 咖啡 kāfēi, 饼 bǐng, 米饭 mǐfàn.

### Common mistakes
• Putting 、 where a comma or a full stop should go.
• Writing 喝 with foods, or 吃 with drinks.
• Leaving off the question mark in 你呢?

### Good to know
Wellness means being healthy in body and mind. A good diet is a big part of it. Writing about foods helps us think about what we eat.

### Summary
You have written food words, meal sentences and food groups. You have punctuated texts, counted strokes and designed a healthy plate. Careful writing helps others understand your ideas.

## QUIZ
1. Which punctuation mark do we use between items in a list?
   A) 。
   B) ?
   C) 、
   D) !
   Answer: C
   Why: 、 is the list comma, used between items.
2. Which verb goes with 茶?
   A) 吃
   B) 是
   C) 有
   D) 喝
   Answer: D
   Why: We drink tea, so we use 喝.
3. How many strokes does 米 have?
   A) 4
   B) 5
   C) 6
   D) 7
   Answer: C
   Why: 米 has six strokes.
4. Which sentence is correctly punctuated?
   A) 我吃米饭。你呢?
   B) 我吃米饭? 你呢。
   C) 我吃米饭、你呢、
   D) 我吃米饭!你呢。
   Answer: A
   Why: A statement ends with 。 and a question ends with ?.
5. Which food group does 菠菜 belong to?
   A) Fruit
   B) Meat
   C) Drinks
   D) Vegetables
   Answer: D
   Why: 菠菜 is spinach, a vegetable.
6. Add punctuation to the text: 我喜欢芒果香蕉葡萄你呢 (3 marks)
   Answer: 我喜欢芒果、香蕉、葡萄。你呢?
   Why: 1 mark for the list commas 、, 1 mark for the full stop, 1 mark for the question mark.
7. Write a sentence in Chinese with pinyin: "For breakfast I drink tea." (2 marks)
   Answer: 我早饭喝茶。 wǒ zǎofàn hē chá.
   Why: 1 mark for the sentence and 1 mark for pinyin.
8. Write two foods from each of these groups in pinyin: fruit and meat. (3 marks)
   Answer: Fruit: mángguǒ, xiāngjiāo. Meat: niúròu, jīròu.
   Why: 1 mark for fruit, 1 mark for meat and 1 mark for correct tones.
9. Explain why a healthy plate should have several food groups. (2 marks)
   Answer: Each group gives the body something different: fruit and vegetables give vitamins, rice gives energy and meat helps the body grow.
   Why: 1 mark for saying the groups do different jobs and 1 mark for an example.
