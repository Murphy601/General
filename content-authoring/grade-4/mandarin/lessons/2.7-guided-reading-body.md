## PAGE 1: Reading body words again and again
Repetition means doing something many times. When you read the same words several times, you become more fluent. Fluent reading is smooth and does not stop after every word.

Here are the body words to read.
头 tóu: head
肩膀 jiānbǎng: shoulder
膝盖 xīgài: knee
脚 jiǎo: foot
眼睛 yǎnjing: eye
耳朵 ěrduo: ear
嘴巴 zuǐba: mouth
鼻子 bízi: nose
手 shǒu: hand

### Read in rounds
Round 1: read the list slowly with a partner.
Round 2: read it faster, touching each part as you say it.
Round 3: read it from bottom to top.
Round 4: cover the pinyin and read from the characters only.
Notice how much easier Round 4 is than Round 1. This is the power of repetition.

### Read with a flashcard
Look at a flashcard with a character on it, for example 手. Say "shǒu" and show your hand. Your partner shows the next card. Do this until you know each character without hesitating.

## PAGE 2: Reading with the right tones
Let us read short phrases and check the tones. Use your hand to draw each tone in the air.

头 tóu: rising
手 shǒu: dipping
脚 jiǎo: dipping
鼻子 bízi: rising + light
耳朵 ěrduo: dipping + light
嘴巴 zuǐba: dipping + light
眼睛 yǎnjing: dipping + light
肩膀 jiānbǎng: high + dipping
膝盖 xīgài: high + falling

### Watch a clip
If your teacher can play a short video or audio clip of a Mandarin body song, listen carefully. Read the words as they are sung. Do the tones sound like the ones you have practised? Read the words together with the singers.

### Correct each other
When your friend reads, listen for the tone of each word. If a tone is wrong, give a kind hint. You might say "Try shǒu again, and let your voice dip."

### Word pairs
tóu (head) and tòu (to go through) are different words. shǒu (hand) and shòu (thin) are different words. Only the tone tells them apart. Read each pair slowly.

## PAGE 3: What do our body parts do?
Read these short texts about body parts and what they do. Read each one two or three times.

我有两只眼睛,我用眼睛看。
wǒ yǒu liǎng zhī yǎnjing, wǒ yòng yǎnjing kàn.
I have two eyes. I use my eyes to see.

我有两个耳朵,我用耳朵听。
wǒ yǒu liǎng gè ěrduo, wǒ yòng ěrduo tīng.
I have two ears. I use my ears to hear.

我有一个嘴巴,我用嘴巴说,也用嘴巴吃。
wǒ yǒu yí gè zuǐba, wǒ yòng zuǐba shuō, yě yòng zuǐba chī.
I have one mouth. I use my mouth to speak and also to eat.

New words: 两 liǎng (two), 只 zhī and 个 gè (words used when counting), 也 yě (also).

### More things we can do
笑 xiào: to laugh, to smile
哭 kū: to cry
We can smile with our mouths and cry with our eyes. Read: 我笑,我哭。 wǒ xiào, wǒ kū. I laugh, I cry.

### Why each body part is important
Talk with a partner. What would happen if you could not hear? What would happen if you could not see? Every part helps us. We must look after our bodies and keep them clean.

## PAGE 4: Skimming
Skimming is reading quickly to get the general idea of a text. You do not read every word. You look at the title, pictures and first words of each part.

### Steps to skim
1. Look at the title or the picture.
2. Run your eyes quickly across the text.
3. Notice words you know, like 眼睛, 耳朵 and 嘴巴.
4. Say to yourself what the text is about.

### Try it
Skim this text for five seconds.
我有头、手和脚。我有眼睛、耳朵和嘴巴。我用眼睛看,用耳朵听,用嘴巴说。
wǒ yǒu tóu, shǒu hé jiǎo. wǒ yǒu yǎnjing, ěrduo hé zuǐba. wǒ yòng yǎnjing kàn, yòng ěrduo tīng, yòng zuǐba shuō.
Ask: What is this text about? Answer: Body parts and what they do.

### When to skim
Skim when you want to know quickly whether a text is useful. For example, you can skim a page in a book to see whether it is about the body or about food. After skimming, you can decide whether to read the text more slowly.

### Skim and share
Your teacher gives each pair a short text. Skim it. Tell the class in one sentence what it is about.

## PAGE 5: Scanning
Scanning is looking for a special word or fact in a text. You do not read every word. You move your eyes over the page until you find the word.

### Steps to scan
1. Decide which word you are looking for.
2. Say it in your head.
3. Move your eyes quickly over the text.
4. Stop when you see it and circle it.

### Word search
Your teacher gives you a grid of characters. Scan it to find the words 手, 头 and 脚. Circle each one. Try to find them as quickly as possible. Time yourself, then try again to beat your time.

### Answer questions from a text
Use scanning to find answers.
Text: 我有两只眼睛,两个耳朵和一个鼻子。
Question 1: How many eyes? (two) You scanned for 眼睛 and saw 两只.
Question 2: What else is named? (ears and a nose)

### Skim or scan?
Skimming: get the general idea. Scanning: find one detail. You will use both when you read.

## PAGE 6: Starting a reading club
A reading club is a group of friends who meet to read together. It can help you read more fluently. Here is how to start one.

### Steps
1. Ask your teacher for a time and a place, for example the classroom at break.
2. Choose a leader to keep time.
3. Bring a short text or a set of flashcards.
4. Read aloud in turns and clap for each reader.
5. Choose a new text each week.

### Club rules
• Be kind. Do not laugh at mistakes.
• Listen while others read.
• Take turns fairly.
• Help friends who find reading hard.

### Review
Read again: 头, 手, 脚, 眼睛, 耳朵, 嘴巴, 鼻子, 肩膀, 膝盖. Which ones can you read without pinyin? Write how many you know.

### Common mistakes
• Reading the pinyin only and never looking at the characters.
• Mixing up skimming and scanning.
• Saying tóu with the wrong tone, so it sounds like tòu.

### Summary
Repetition builds fluency. Skimming gets the main idea, and scanning finds a fact. Reading with friends is fun and helps everyone improve.

## QUIZ
1. What does 手 shǒu mean?
   A) Foot
   B) Hand
   C) Head
   D) Mouth
   Answer: B
   Why: 手 is hand. 脚 is foot and 头 is head.
2. What is skimming?
   A) Reading to find one special word
   B) Writing a text
   C) Reading quickly to get the general idea
   D) Copying a text
   Answer: C
   Why: Skimming means quickly reading to get the main idea.
3. What is scanning?
   A) Looking for a special word or fact
   B) Reading every word slowly
   C) Reading aloud only
   D) Drawing a picture
   Answer: A
   Why: When you scan you move your eyes over a text to find one particular word or fact.
4. What does 我用耳朵听 mean?
   A) I use my eyes to see
   B) I use my mouth to speak
   C) I use my hand to write
   D) I use my ears to hear
   Answer: D
   Why: 耳朵 is ears and 听 is to hear.
5. Why does repeating a text help your reading?
   A) You forget the words
   B) It makes you read more smoothly
   C) It makes the text shorter
   D) It removes the tones
   Answer: B
   Why: Reading a text many times builds fluency.
6. Say which word you would scan for to find how many eyes are in the text, and give the pinyin. (2 marks)
   Answer: 眼睛, yǎnjing.
   Why: 1 mark for the word and 1 mark for the pinyin.
7. Write the Chinese for "I laugh" and "I cry" with pinyin. (3 marks)
   Answer: 我笑 wǒ xiào; 我哭 wǒ kū.
   Why: 1 mark for each correct sentence and 1 mark for correct pinyin.
8. Explain the difference between skimming and scanning. (2 marks)
   Answer: Skimming is reading quickly to get the general idea. Scanning is looking for one particular word or detail.
   Why: 1 mark for each correct meaning.
9. Give three rules for a reading club. (3 marks)
   Answer: Be kind and do not laugh at mistakes, listen when others read, take turns fairly, help others.
   Why: 1 mark for each rule.
