## PAGE 1: Places at home
To get around at home or at school, you need words for places and directions. In this lesson you will listen for key information and learn to give clear spoken instructions in Mandarin.

More words for the home:
花园 huāyuán: garden
沙发 shāfā: sofa
浴室 yùshì: bathroom
窗户 chuānghu: window
门 mén: door

You already know these words:
客厅 kètīng: living room
厨房 chúfáng: kitchen
卧室 wòshì: bedroom

### Listen and write
Your teacher says the five new words in a mixed order. Listen and write down each one in pinyin, for example "huāyuán". Then check your spelling against the list above. Being able to list vocabulary you heard is an important listening skill.

## PAGE 2: Words for position
We use small words to tell where things are. Listen and repeat.

在 zài: is at, is in
旁边 pángbiān: beside, next to
上面 shàngmiàn: above, on top
下面 xiàmiàn: below, under
左边 zuǒbian: left side
右边 yòubian: right side

### How to use them
Thing + 在 + other thing + position word.
沙发在门旁边。shāfā zài mén pángbiān. The sofa is beside the door.
窗户在门上面。chuānghu zài mén shàngmiàn. The window is above the door.
花园在房子旁边。huāyuán zài fángzi pángbiān. The garden is beside the house.
The word 房子 fángzi means house.

### Left and right
Left and right depend on where you stand. Imagine you are facing the front of the room. The side by your left hand is 左边. The side by your right hand is 右边. Hold up both hands and say: 左边, 右边. Practise the tones: zuǒbian starts with a dipping tone, and yòubian starts with a falling tone.

### Listen for the key word
When you hear an instruction, catch the position word. It tells you where to put or look for something. Which word did you hear: pángbiān, shàngmiàn or xiàmiàn?

## PAGE 3: Questions about places
To find out where something is, we use 哪儿 nǎr. You met it in an earlier lesson.

沙发在哪儿? shāfā zài nǎr? Where is the sofa?
沙发在门旁边。shāfā zài mén pángbiān. The sofa is beside the door.

浴室在哪儿? yùshì zài nǎr? Where is the bathroom?
浴室在花园旁边。yùshì zài huāyuán pángbiān. The bathroom is beside the garden.

### Practise in pairs
Look at Figure 1 with your partner. One learner asks where something is. The other answers using the picture. Take turns.

### Check with the picture
Look at Figure 1. Is this true?
沙发在门旁边。 Yes, the sofa is next to the door.
花园在房子右边。 Yes, the garden is on the right of the house.
窗户在沙发下面。 No. The window is at the top, and the sofa is at the bottom.

### Use the listening steps
1. Listen for the thing (沙发, 窗户, 门…).
2. Listen for the position word (旁边, 上面, 下面…).
3. Point to the place on the picture.
Notice that you only need a few key words to understand. This is listening for gist.

## PAGE 4: Follow the instruction
Now you will place things according to spoken instructions. Prepare small cards: 沙发, 门, 窗户, 花园.

### Listening task
Teacher says: 沙发在门旁边。
You place the sofa card next to the door card.

Teacher says: 窗户在门上面。
You place the window card above the door card.

Teacher says: 花园在房子右边。
You place the garden card on the right side of the house.

### Draw what you hear
Work in pairs. Partner A describes a room using the new words. For example: "门在左边。沙发在门旁边。窗户在上面。" (mén zài zuǒbian. shāfā zài mén pángbiān. chuānghu zài shàngmiàn.) Partner B listens and draws. Then they compare the drawing with the description.

### Tips for listening well
• Listen to the whole instruction before you act.
• Ask for a repeat if you are not sure: 请再说一遍。 qǐng zài shuō yí biàn. Please say it once more.
• Show the answer by pointing, placing or drawing.
The new phrase means "please say it again". You may practise it in class.

### What went well?
After the activity talk about it. Did the drawing match the description? Which words were hard to hear?

## PAGE 5: Giving clear instructions
Listening and speaking go together. Now you are the speaker. Good instructions are short and clear.

### Steps to give instructions
1. Name the thing first: 沙发.
2. Say where it is: 在门旁边。
3. Speak slowly and clearly.
4. Watch whether your partner understood.

### Practice sentences
Read these sentences and draw a small map for each.
1. 浴室在卧室旁边。 The bathroom is beside the bedroom.
2. 花园在厨房旁边。 The garden is beside the kitchen.
3. 电视在沙发前面。 The television is in front of the sofa.
The word 前面 qiánmiàn means in front of.

### Take turns
One learner gives an instruction. The other learner follows it by moving an object on the desk. For example: "书在桌子上面。" Put the book on the table. Then swap.

### Why order matters at home
When things are in the right place at home, the house is safe and tidy. If a chair blocks the door, it is dangerous in an emergency. Talk with a friend about where things should be kept: shoes near the door, books on a shelf, the kettle away from small children.

## PAGE 6: Review and summary
Let us put everything together.

### Vocabulary check

门 mén door

左边 zuǒbian left
右边 yòubian right
上面 shàngmiàn above
下面 xiàmiàn below

### A mini text to listen to
我家有花园。花园旁边有房子。房子里有客厅、卧室和浴室。客厅里有沙发。
wǒ jiā yǒu huāyuán. huāyuán pángbiān yǒu fángzi. fángzi lǐ yǒu kètīng, wòshì hé yùshì. kètīng lǐ yǒu shāfā.
My home has a garden. Beside the garden is the house. In the house there is a living room, a bedroom and a bathroom. In the living room there is a sofa.

New word: 里 lǐ means inside.

### Key information
1. Where is the house? (beside the garden)
2. What is in the living room? (a sofa)
3. What three rooms are named? (living room, bedroom and bathroom)

### Common mistakes
• Mixing up 左边 (left) and 右边 (right).
• Forgetting the neutral tone in the second syllable of chuānghu.
• Saying the position word before the thing.

## QUIZ
1. What does 沙发 shāfā mean?
   A) Sofa
   B) Door
   C) Window
   D) Garden
   Answer: A
   Why: 沙发 shāfā is a sofa.
2. Which word means "bathroom"?
   A) 厨房 chúfáng
   B) 浴室 yùshì
   C) 花园 huāyuán
   D) 客厅 kètīng
   Answer: B
   Why: 浴室 yùshì is bathroom.
3. What does 沙发在门旁边 mean?
   A) The sofa is under the door
   B) The sofa is in the garden
   C) The sofa is beside the door
   D) The door is on the sofa
   Answer: C
   Why: 在 is at and 旁边 is beside.
4. Which word means "right side"?
   A) 左边 zuǒbian
   B) 上面 shàngmiàn
   C) 下面 xiàmiàn
   D) 右边 yòubian
   Answer: D
   Why: 右边 yòubian is the right side. 左边 is left.
5. What should you do first when you get a spoken instruction?
   A) Do something at once
   B) Listen to the whole instruction
   C) Walk away
   D) Talk to your neighbour
   Answer: B
   Why: You need to hear all of the instruction to follow it correctly.
6. Ask in Chinese where the sofa is, and write the pinyin. (2 marks)
   Answer: 沙发在哪儿? shāfā zài nǎr?
   Why: 1 mark for the characters and 1 mark for the pinyin.
7. Give the Chinese for "The window is above the door." Include pinyin. (3 marks)
   Answer: 窗户在门上面。 chuānghu zài mén shàngmiàn.
   Why: 1 mark for 窗户 and 门 in the right places, 1 mark for 在 and 上面, 1 mark for pinyin.
8. Give three tips for following spoken instructions well. (3 marks)
   Answer: Listen to the whole instruction first, catch the key words, ask for a repeat if unsure, and show the answer by pointing or drawing.
   Why: 1 mark for each tip, up to three.
9. Say why it is important to keep things in the right place at home. (2 marks)
   Answer: It keeps the house tidy and safe, and it is easy to find things or leave through the door in an emergency.
   Why: 1 mark for safety or tidiness and 1 mark for a second reason.
