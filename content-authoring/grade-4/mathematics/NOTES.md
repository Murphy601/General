# Grade 4 Mathematics: notes for reviewers

Coverage: all 19 sub-strands of the KICD design (1.1 to 1.8, 2.1 to 2.7, 3.1 to 3.3, 4.1) have a lesson. Every manifest topic
has a lesson, so no retire.json is needed. There are 96 SVG figures, drawn from true values by a generator script
(rulers, number lines, fraction bars, hundred square, angles, clocks, cuboids, balances, dial, containers, tally, bar graph).

Bank: 789 MCQ and 240 short items. Numeric items were computed by script, and every distractor was checked to be
different from the correct answer. Hand-written items were re-read.

Please have a teacher check these points:
1. 2.7 Money: text says Kenya has a 40 shilling coin and notes of 50, 100, 200, 500 and 1,000. It also says cent coins are
   rarely seen now but cents still appear in prices. The coin and note pictures are plain drawings, not copies of real money.
   Prices in the shopping lists are examples only.
2. 4.1 Data: the safety text gives 999 and 112 as emergency numbers. Please confirm these are the numbers you want.
   The last page shows a first bar graph. The Grade 4 design covers only tables and tally, so delete that page part if
   you want to keep strictly to the design.
3. 2.6 Time: the shadow page says the Sun is in the east in the morning, so shadows point west, and shadows are shortest
   about midday. It also says sunrise and sunset are about 6 o'clock in most of Kenya. These are simplified statements.
4. 3.1 Position and Direction: taps open anticlockwise and lids tighten clockwise. This is the usual case, not a rule
   for every fitting. Compass turns (N, E, S, W) were added as the way to show clockwise and anticlockwise; the design only
   lists turns.
5. 3.2 Angles: degrees are named once (right angle 90, straight line 180, full turn 360) as a look-ahead only. Names such
   as acute and obtuse are not used.
6. 1.5 Division: dividing by 100 covers exact hundreds (300 divided by 100) and one example with a remainder
   (450 divided by 100 is 4 remainder 50). 1.4: estimated products stay at or below 1,000 as the design says (the largest is 50 times 20 = 1,000).
   The exact answer 48 times 21 = 1,008 is shown once as a check of that estimate.
7. 2.2 Area: square centimetres (cm squared) is mentioned once as the unit. The design only asks for counting squares and
   rows times columns; triangles are not taught (the design title mentions them but its outcomes do not).
8. Quantities in halves and quarters (2.4 Capacity, 2.5 Mass) are written in text as 1/2, 1/4, 3/4 and mixed numbers such as
   2 1/2. Millilitres and grams are not used because the Grade 4 design does not list them.
