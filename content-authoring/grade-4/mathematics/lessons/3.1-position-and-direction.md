## PAGE 1: Clockwise and anticlockwise turns
When you turn your body, you can turn in two ways. You can turn the same way as the hands of a clock, or you can turn the opposite way. The way clock hands move is called clockwise. The opposite way is called anticlockwise.

Look at the hands of a clock on the wall. They start at 12, then move to 1, 2, 3 and so on. That path is clockwise. If a hand went from 12 to 11, then 10, then 9, it would be moving anticlockwise, which is the opposite way.

In the first picture the pointer moves from the top towards the right. That is clockwise. In the second picture the pointer moves from the top towards the left. That is anticlockwise.

### Remember with your hands
Hold out your right hand and look at your palm. Point your finger and draw a circle the same way as a clock. That is clockwise. Do it the other way for anticlockwise.

### Try it
Stand up carefully in an open space. Turn clockwise a little. Now turn anticlockwise. Ask a friend to say which turn you are making.

## PAGE 2: Turns in our surroundings
We see clockwise and anticlockwise turns everywhere in daily life. Once you notice them, you will spot them all the time.

### Clockwise turns
• The hands of a clock move clockwise.
• A screw or a bottle top is usually tightened by turning it clockwise.
• A playground roundabout may turn clockwise if pushed that way.

### Anticlockwise turns
• A bottle top is usually loosened by turning it anticlockwise.
• A water tap is usually opened by turning it anticlockwise, and closed by turning it clockwise.
• Some runners run round a track in an anticlockwise direction.

### The direction can change
A roundabout can turn either way, depending on how you push it. A door handle can turn either way. So the answer depends on which way the object is really moving.

### Activity
Find three things in the classroom or at home that you can turn: a door knob, a tap, a bottle top or a wheel. For each one, say the way it turns to open or to close, and whether the turn is clockwise or anticlockwise.

### Safety
Before you make turns in the classroom, make sure that there is enough space and that the floor is not wet. Do not push or bump into others.

## PAGE 3: Quarter turn, half turn and full turn
A turn can be big or small. We measure a turn by what fraction of a whole circle it makes.

• A full turn is when you turn all the way round and face the same way again.
• A half turn is half of a full turn. You end up facing the opposite way.
• A quarter turn is one quarter of a full turn. You end up facing to your right or your left.

### Putting turns together
• Two quarter turns make one half turn.
• Four quarter turns make one full turn.
• Two half turns make one full turn.
• A half turn and then a quarter turn make a three-quarter turn.

### Example
If you face the door and make a half turn, you face the back wall. If you then make another half turn, you are facing the door again. Two half turns equal one full turn.

## PAGE 4: Turning from a direction
The four main directions are north (N), east (E), south (S) and west (W). They are called the compass directions. If you face north, then east is on your right, west is on your left and south is behind you.

Going clockwise, the order of the directions is N, E, S, W and then back to N. Going anticlockwise the order is N, W, S, E and then back to N.

### Worked examples
• Face north and make a quarter turn clockwise. You now face east.
• Face north and make a half turn. You now face south.
• Face north and make a quarter turn anticlockwise. You now face west.
• Face east and make a half turn. You now face west.
• Face south and make a quarter turn anticlockwise. You now face east.

### A trick
To find where you face after a turn, think of the compass as a clock with N at 12, E at 3, S at 6 and W at 9. A clockwise quarter turn moves you 3 hours on the clock face. An anticlockwise quarter turn moves you back 3 hours.

## PAGE 5: Describing position and direction
We use words to say where things are, and how to reach them. Words such as left, right, in front, behind, next to and between describe position. Words such as forward, backward, turn left and turn right describe direction.

### Left and right turns
A quarter turn to the right is the same as a quarter turn clockwise. A quarter turn to the left is the same as a quarter turn anticlockwise. A half turn to the left or right ends up facing the opposite way.

### Giving instructions
Wafula wants to go from the classroom door to the school gate. He gives the directions:
1. Walk forward 10 steps.
2. Make a quarter turn to the right.
3. Walk forward 5 steps.
4. Make a quarter turn to the left.
5. Walk forward to the gate.

A friend who follows these instructions in the correct order will reach the gate. If they turn the wrong way in step 2, they will end up somewhere else. Position and direction must both be right.

### Playing a game
Make a treasure hunt. Hide an object and write clear instructions with steps and turns. Let another group follow your instructions. The instructions are only correct if they lead to the treasure.

### Remember
Always say the amount of the turn (quarter, half or full) and the direction of the turn (clockwise or anticlockwise, or left or right).

## PAGE 6: Practice and summary
Let us use everything we have learned in some puzzles.

### Puzzle 1
Mercy faces south. She makes a half turn. Which way does she face? A half turn takes her to the opposite direction, so she faces north.

### Puzzle 2
Kip faces west and makes a quarter turn clockwise. West to north is clockwise, since going clockwise the order is N, E, S, W and back to N. So he faces north.

### Puzzle 3
Njeri faces east. She makes a quarter turn clockwise, then another quarter turn clockwise. East then south then west. She faces west. Two quarter turns make a half turn, and a half turn from east is west.

### Puzzle 4
Amina faces north. She makes a full turn. She faces north again, because a full turn brings you back to the start.

### Summary
• Clockwise is the way a clock's hands move. Anticlockwise is the opposite way.
• A full turn goes all the way round. A half turn is half of that. A quarter turn is a quarter of that.
• Two quarter turns make a half turn. Four quarter turns make a full turn.
• N, E, S and W are the compass directions. Going clockwise the order is N, E, S, W.

### Common mistakes
• Mixing up clockwise and anticlockwise. Think of a clock.
• Turning the wrong amount. A quarter turn is a small turn, and a half turn takes you the opposite way.
• Forgetting to say which way to turn.

## QUIZ
1. Which way do the hands of a clock move?
   A) Clockwise
   B) Anticlockwise
   C) Up and down
   D) Sideways
   Answer: A
   Why: The hands of a clock move in the direction we call clockwise.
2. If you face north and make a half turn, which way do you face?
   A) East
   B) West
   C) South
   D) North
   Answer: C
   Why: A half turn takes you to the opposite direction, so north becomes south.
3. Four quarter turns make
   A) a quarter turn
   B) a half turn
   C) a three-quarter turn
   D) a full turn
   Answer: D
   Why: 4 quarters make one whole, so four quarter turns make one full turn.
4. Aisha faces east and makes a quarter turn anticlockwise. Which way does she face?
   A) North
   B) South
   C) West
   D) East
   Answer: A
   Why: Going anticlockwise the order is N, W, S, E, then back to N. From east, one quarter turn anticlockwise gives north.
5. Two half turns together make
   A) a quarter turn
   B) a half turn
   C) a full turn
   D) two full turns
   Answer: C
   Why: Two halves make one whole, so two half turns make one full turn.
6. Name two things in your home or classroom that are turned clockwise to tighten or close them. (2 marks)
   Answer: Any two, for example a bottle top or a screw, and a tap to close it.
   Why: 1 mark for each sensible example where the closing or tightening turn is clockwise.
7. Kip faces west and makes a quarter turn clockwise. Which way does he face now? Explain. (3 marks)
   Answer: North
   Why: Going clockwise the order is N, E, S, W, then back to N (1 mark). West followed by a quarter turn clockwise gives north (1 mark). Answer north (1 mark).
8. Njeri faces east. She makes two quarter turns clockwise, one after the other. Which way does she face? (3 marks)
   Answer: West
   Why: The first quarter turn takes her from east to south (1 mark). The second takes her from south to west (1 mark). Answer west (1 mark). Two quarter turns are one half turn, which also gives west.
9. Write the instructions for a person to move from the classroom door to the school gate using at least two turns. Use words like forward, quarter turn and clockwise or anticlockwise. (4 marks)
   Answer: Any correct set of instructions, for example: Walk forward 10 steps. Make a quarter turn clockwise. Walk forward 5 steps. Make a quarter turn anticlockwise. Walk to the gate.
   Why: 1 mark for the first move with a number of steps, 1 mark for a turn with its amount, 1 mark for the direction of the turn, 1 mark for clear order of instructions.
10. A learner says that a half turn is the same as two quarter turns. Is the learner right? Explain. (2 marks)
   Answer: Yes. A half is made of two quarters, so two quarter turns make one half turn.
   Why: 1 mark for saying yes and 1 mark for the reason 1/4 + 1/4 = 1/2.
