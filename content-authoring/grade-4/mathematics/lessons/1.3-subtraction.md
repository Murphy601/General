## PAGE 1: Subtracting 4-digit numbers without regrouping
Subtraction means taking away, or finding how much bigger one number is than another. The answer to a subtraction is called the difference. As in addition, we write the numbers in columns: thousands under thousands, hundreds under hundreds, tens under tens, ones under ones. The bigger number goes on top. We subtract from the right, starting with the ones.

Work out 7,865 − 3,542.
Step 1: Ones: 5 − 2 = 3.
Step 2: Tens: 6 − 4 = 2.
Step 3: Hundreds: 8 − 5 = 3.
Step 4: Thousands: 7 − 3 = 4.
The difference is 4,323.

### Checking with addition
Addition undoes subtraction. Add the answer to the smaller number: 4,323 + 3,542 = 7,865. We get the top number back, so the answer is correct.

### Example
A cooperative had 8,600 kg of coffee and sold 5,300 kg. It has 8,600 − 5,300 = 3,300 kg left.

## PAGE 2: Subtracting with regrouping
Sometimes the top digit is smaller than the bottom digit. We cannot take 8 from 2, so we regroup. We borrow one from the next column to the left. One ten becomes ten ones, one hundred becomes ten tens, and so on.

Work out 5,432 − 2,718.
Step 1: Ones: 2 is smaller than 8. Borrow 1 ten from the tens column. The 2 becomes 12. Then 12 − 8 = 4. The tens digit 3 becomes 2.
Step 2: Tens: 2 − 1 = 1.
Step 3: Hundreds: 4 is smaller than 7. Borrow 1 thousand. The 4 becomes 14. Then 14 − 7 = 7. The thousands digit 5 becomes 4.
Step 4: Thousands: 4 − 2 = 2.
The difference is 2,714.

### Check
Add back: 2,714 + 2,718 = 5,432. It matches, so the answer is right.

### Tip
Always cross out the digit you borrow from and write its new smaller value above it. Do not forget that the digit you borrowed from now has one less. Forgetting this is the biggest cause of mistakes.

## PAGE 3: Regrouping across zeros
A zero in the top number makes regrouping tricky, because you cannot borrow from a zero. You must go to the next digit that is not zero.

Work out 6,003 − 2,475.
Step 1: Ones: 3 is smaller than 5. The tens and hundreds are both 0, so borrow from the thousands.
Step 2: Take 1 thousand from the 6. Now the thousands digit is 5. That thousand becomes 10 hundreds.
Step 3: Take 1 hundred from the 10 hundreds. Now there are 9 hundreds. That hundred becomes 10 tens.
Step 4: Take 1 ten from the 10 tens. Now there are 9 tens. That ten becomes 10 ones, so the ones become 13.
Step 5: Subtract: 13 − 5 = 8; tens 9 − 7 = 2; hundreds 9 − 4 = 5; thousands 5 − 2 = 3.
The difference is 3,528.

### Check
3,528 + 2,475 = 6,003. It matches.

### Money example
Njeri has Ksh 5,000. She buys a school bag for Ksh 1,850. Her change: 5,000 − 1,850 = 3,150. The answer is Ksh 3,150.
Check: 3,150 + 1,850 = 5,000.

### Try it
Work out 4,000 − 1,236. Borrow from the thousands: 3,999 and 10 more. The answer is 2,764.

## PAGE 4: Subtraction in real-life problems
Subtraction is used to find what is left, how many more, or how much change. Read the problem slowly. Decide which number is bigger, write it on top, and label your answer with the unit.

### Problem 1: The library
A school library had 4,250 books. During the term, 1,875 books were borrowed. How many books remained on the shelves?
Step 1: Subtract 4,250 − 1,875.
Step 2: Ones: 0 is smaller than 5. Borrow from the tens: 10 − 5 = 5. Tens become 4.
Step 3: Tens: 4 is smaller than 7. Borrow from the hundreds: 14 − 7 = 7. Hundreds become 1.
Step 4: Hundreds: 1 is smaller than 8. Borrow from the thousands: 11 − 8 = 3. Thousands become 3.
Step 5: Thousands: 3 − 1 = 2.
Answer: 2,375 books remained.
Check: 2,375 + 1,875 = 4,250.

### Problem 2: The difference between two counties
A county had 9,000 pupils in one year and 6,450 in the next. How many pupils fewer were there? 9,000 − 6,450 = 2,550 pupils.

### Words that tell us to subtract
Look for words like left, remain, difference, fewer, change and how many more. Do not just take away the smaller from the bigger blindly; think about what the question asks.

## PAGE 5: Estimating a difference
An estimate is a close answer that is easy to work out. To estimate a difference, round both numbers to the nearest ten and then subtract.

Estimate 7,483 − 2,196.
Step 1: 7,483 rounds to 7,480 because the ones digit 3 is less than 5.
Step 2: 2,196 rounds to 2,200 because the ones digit 6 is 5 or more (196 rounds up to 200).
Step 3: Subtract: 7,480 − 2,200 = 5,280.
The estimate is 5,280.

If you worked out 6,287 by mistake, the estimate 5,280 would warn you that the answer is about 1,000 too big.

### Rounding rule again
Look at the ones digit. If it is 5 or more, round up to the next ten. If it is less than 5, keep the tens digit and change the ones to 0.

## PAGE 6: Number patterns using subtraction
In a subtraction pattern, we take away the same number each time. The numbers get smaller and smaller. The number we take away is the rule of the pattern.

Start at 5,000 and subtract 250 each time: 5,000, 4,750, 4,500, 4,250, 4,000.

### Finding the rule
Take the second number from the first. In 9,000, 8,000, 7,000, 6,000: 9,000 − 8,000 = 1,000, so the rule is subtract 1,000. The next numbers are 5,000 and 4,000.

### Making your own pattern
Start at 10,000 and subtract 1,500: 10,000, 8,500, 7,000, 5,500, 4,000. Stop before you reach a number below zero.

### Real-life pattern
A water tank holds 1,000 litres. Each day the family uses 125 litres and nobody refills it. After 1 day: 875 litres, after 2 days: 750 litres, after 3 days: 625 litres. The rule is subtract 125.

### Common mistakes
• Taking the smaller digit from the bigger digit, even when the smaller digit is on top.
• Forgetting the digit you borrowed from is now one less.
• Not checking by adding the answer to the smaller number.

## QUIZ
1. What is 7,865 − 3,542?
   A) 4,323
   B) 4,333
   C) 4,423
   D) 11,407
   Answer: A
   Why: Ones 5 − 2 = 3; tens 6 − 4 = 2; hundreds 8 − 5 = 3; thousands 7 − 3 = 4. The difference is 4,323.
2. Work out 5,432 − 2,718.
   A) 3,286
   B) 2,714
   C) 3,714
   D) 2,724
   Answer: B
   Why: Ones 12 − 8 = 4; tens 2 − 1 = 1; hundreds 14 − 7 = 7; thousands 4 − 2 = 2. The difference is 2,714. Check: 2,714 + 2,718 = 5,432.
3. Which sum can be used to check that 6,003 − 2,475 = 3,528?
   A) 3,528 + 2,475
   B) 3,528 − 2,475
   C) 6,003 + 2,475
   D) 6,003 + 3,528
   Answer: A
   Why: Addition undoes subtraction. 3,528 + 2,475 = 6,003, which is the number we started with.
4. Estimate 7,483 − 2,196 by rounding to the nearest ten.
   A) 5,280
   B) 5,290
   C) 5,300
   D) 5,380
   Answer: A
   Why: 7,483 rounds to 7,480 and 2,196 rounds to 2,200. 7,480 − 2,200 = 5,280.
5. The rule of the pattern 9,000, 8,500, 8,000, 7,500 is
   A) subtract 500
   B) subtract 1,000
   C) add 500
   D) subtract 5,000
   Answer: A
   Why: 9,000 − 8,500 = 500 and each step is the same. The rule is subtract 500.
6. Work out 4,000 − 1,236. Show how you regroup. (3 marks)
   Answer: 2,764
   Why: Borrow from the thousands so the number becomes 3 thousands, 9 hundreds, 9 tens and 10 ones (1 mark). Ones 10 − 6 = 4; tens 9 − 3 = 6; hundreds 9 − 2 = 7; thousands 3 − 1 = 2 (1 mark). Answer 2,764 (1 mark).
7. A library had 4,250 books. After the term, 1,875 books had been borrowed. How many books were left on the shelves? Check your answer. (4 marks)
   Answer: 2,375 books
   Why: 4,250 − 1,875 = 2,375 (2 marks for correct working with regrouping, 1 mark for the answer with units). Check: 2,375 + 1,875 = 4,250 (1 mark).
8. Njeri had Ksh 5,000 and bought a bag for Ksh 1,850. How much change did she get? (2 marks)
   Answer: Ksh 3,150
   Why: 5,000 − 1,850 = 3,150. 1 mark for the subtraction and 1 mark for the answer with Ksh.
9. Write the next three numbers in the pattern 5,000, 4,750, 4,500, 4,250. (3 marks)
   Answer: 4,000, 3,750, 3,500
   Why: The rule is subtract 250 (1 mark). 4,250 − 250 = 4,000; 4,000 − 250 = 3,750; 3,750 − 250 = 3,500 (1 mark for each pair of correct numbers, up to 2 marks).
