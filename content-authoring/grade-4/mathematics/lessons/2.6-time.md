## PAGE 1: Reading the clock
A clock helps us know what time it is. An analogue clock has a round face with the numbers 1 to 12, and two hands. The short hand is the hour hand. It tells us the hour. The long hand is the minute hand. It tells us the minutes.

When the minute hand points straight up at 12, it is exactly on the hour. We say the time is o'clock. When the hour hand points at 3 and the minute hand points at 12, the time is 3 o'clock.

When the minute hand points straight down at 6, half an hour has passed. We say it is half past. If the hour hand is between 6 and 7 and the minute hand is at 6, the time is half past 6, or 6:30.

### Digital time
A digital clock shows time with numbers. The first number is the hour and the second number is the minutes, for example 6:30. The two dots separate the hours from the minutes.

### Hours and minutes
There are 60 minutes in one hour. The minute hand takes one hour to go once round the clock face.

## PAGE 2: Minutes past and minutes to the hour
The clock face has 12 numbers, and each number stands for 5 minutes. When the minute hand is on 1, five minutes have passed. On 2, ten minutes. On 3, fifteen minutes, and so on. On 12 it is 0 or 60 minutes.

To count minutes, count in fives: 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 35, 40, 45, 50, 55, 60.

### Minutes past
When the minute hand is on the right side of the clock (between 12 and 6), we say minutes past the hour. If the minute hand is on 4, that is 4 × 5 = 20 minutes past. With the hour hand just after 4, the time is 20 minutes past 4, or 4:20.

### Minutes to
When the minute hand is on the left side (between 6 and 12), we say minutes to the next hour. If it is on 9, the time is 15 minutes to the next hour, which is a quarter to. With the hour hand nearly at 8, the time is a quarter to 8, or 7:45.

### Quarter past and quarter to
• Quarter past means 15 minutes past the hour (minute hand on 3).
• Quarter to means 15 minutes before the next hour (minute hand on 9).

### Try it
What time is it when the minute hand is on 7 and the hour hand is between 2 and 3, past the halfway point? 7 × 5 = 35 minutes past 2, which is 2:35. We can also say 25 minutes to 3.

## PAGE 3: a.m. and p.m.
A day and a night together make 24 hours. The clock face only shows 12 hours, so each time on the clock happens twice in a day: once in the morning and once in the afternoon or evening. To tell the two apart, we add a.m. or p.m.

• a.m. is used from midnight (12 o'clock at night) until noon (12 o'clock midday). This is the time in the morning.
• p.m. is used from noon until midnight. This is the afternoon, evening and night.

• In the early morning the Sun is low in the east. Shadows are long and point towards the west.
• As the morning goes on, shadows become shorter.
• At midday the Sun is high in the sky. Shadows are very short.
• In the afternoon the Sun moves towards the west. Shadows get longer again and point towards the east.

### An experiment
Stand a stick in the ground in a sunny place. Every hour, mark the tip of its shadow with a small stone and measure the length. Write down the times and lengths. You will find the shadow is longest early and late in the day and shortest around midday.

### What can we say?
If a tree's shadow is very short, the time is close to midday. If it is long and points to your right when you face north, it may be late afternoon. A shadow clock gives only a rough estimate, and it does not work at night or when clouds cover the Sun.

### Hours to minutes: multiply by 60
3 hours = 3 × 60 = 180 minutes.

### Minutes to hours: divide by 60
150 minutes: 150 ÷ 60 = 2 remainder 30. So 150 minutes = 2 hours 30 minutes.

### Days to hours: multiply by 24
2 days = 2 × 24 = 48 hours.

### Hours to days: divide by 24
72 hours: 72 ÷ 24 = 3 days.

### Weeks to days: multiply by 7
3 weeks = 3 × 7 = 21 days.

### Days to weeks: divide by 7
35 days: 35 ÷ 7 = 5 weeks. 17 days: 17 ÷ 7 = 2 remainder 3, so 17 days = 2 weeks and 3 days.

### Example
A school trip lasts 2 days. How many hours is that? 2 × 24 = 48 hours.

## PAGE 6: Time duration
Duration is how long an event lasts. It is the time between the start and the end. To find the duration, count from the start time to the end time.

A maths lesson starts at 8:15 a.m. and ends at 10:40 a.m. How long is the lesson?
Step 1: From 8:15 to 10:15 is 2 hours.
Step 2: From 10:15 to 10:40 is 25 minutes.
Step 3: Add: 2 hours + 25 minutes = 2 hours 25 minutes.

## QUIZ
1. When the minute hand points at 6 and the hour hand is between 6 and 7, what is the time?
   A) 6:00
   B) 6:30
   C) 7:30
   D) 6:15
   Answer: B
   Why: The minute hand at 6 means half past. The hour hand between 6 and 7 means it is after 6, so the time is 6:30.
2. What is 3 hours in minutes?
   A) 60 minutes
   B) 120 minutes
   C) 180 minutes
   D) 300 minutes
   Answer: C
   Why: 1 hour = 60 minutes, so 3 × 60 = 180 minutes.
3. How many hours are in 2 days?
   A) 12 hours
   B) 24 hours
   C) 48 hours
   D) 72 hours
   Answer: C
   Why: 1 day = 24 hours, so 2 × 24 = 48 hours.
4. School ends at 3:15 in the afternoon. How do we write this time?
   A) 3:15 a.m.
   B) 3:15 p.m.
   C) 15:3 p.m.
   D) 3:15 noon
   Answer: B
   Why: Afternoon times after noon use p.m., so it is 3:15 p.m.
5. When is your shadow shortest?
   A) Early morning
   B) Around midday
   C) Late afternoon
   D) At night
   Answer: B
   Why: At midday the Sun is highest in the sky, so shadows are very short.
6. A lesson starts at 8:15 a.m. and ends at 10:40 a.m. How long is the lesson? (3 marks)
   Answer: 2 hours 25 minutes
   Why: 8:15 to 10:15 is 2 hours (1 mark). 10:15 to 10:40 is 25 minutes (1 mark). Total 2 hours 25 minutes (1 mark).
7. Add 2 hours 45 minutes and 1 hour 30 minutes. (3 marks)
   Answer: 4 hours 15 minutes
   Why: 45 + 30 = 75 minutes and 2 + 1 = 3 hours (1 mark). 75 minutes = 1 hour 15 minutes (1 mark). 3 h + 1 h 15 min = 4 h 15 min (1 mark).
8. Subtract 2 hours 35 minutes from 5 hours 20 minutes. (3 marks)
   Answer: 2 hours 45 minutes
   Why: Change 5 h 20 min to 4 h 80 min (1 mark). 80 − 35 = 45 minutes (1 mark). 4 − 2 = 2 hours, giving 2 h 45 min (1 mark).
9. A film starts at 4:20 p.m. and lasts 1 hour 40 minutes. At what time does it end? (2 marks)
   Answer: 6:00 p.m.
   Why: 4:20 + 1 hour = 5:20 p.m. (1 mark). 5:20 + 40 minutes = 6:00 p.m. (1 mark).
10. Change 150 minutes to hours and minutes, and 35 days to weeks. (3 marks)
   Answer: 150 minutes = 2 hours 30 minutes. 35 days = 5 weeks.
   Why: 150 ÷ 60 = 2 remainder 30 (1 mark for 2 hours 30 minutes). 35 ÷ 7 = 5 (1 mark for 5 weeks). 1 mark for showing the division steps.
