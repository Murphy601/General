## PAGE 1: Why do we cook food?
Cooking is preparing food using heat. We cook food to make it soft and tasty, to make it easier to eat and digest, and to kill germs that may be in it. Some foods, such as raw dry beans or raw potatoes, are hard or unsafe to eat before cooking.

There are many methods of cooking. Boiling and shallow frying are two methods that you will learn to use.
• Boiling: cooking food in water that is bubbling hot.
• Shallow frying: cooking food in a small amount of hot oil in a flat pan.
Other methods include steaming, roasting, baking and deep frying. In deep frying, food floats in a lot of hot oil, and this is much more dangerous, so children should not do it.

### Before you cook
• Wash your hands with soap and clean water.
• Tie back long hair and wear a clean apron.
• Make sure a teacher or adult is with you. Fire and hot things can burn.
• Gather all the food and tools first. Wash the food.
• Keep the working area clean and dry.

### Think about it
Which foods do you eat that are boiled? Which are shallow fried?

## PAGE 2: Boiling food
Water boils at a high temperature. When it boils, you see many bubbles rising quickly to the surface and steam coming off. Food placed in boiling water cooks through in the hot water.

### Foods that we boil
• Root and tuber foods: potatoes, cassava, arrowroots, sweet potatoes.
• Eggs.
• Maize, beans and githeri.
• Rice.
• Vegetables such as cabbage and carrots, for a short time.
• Tea and drinking water.

### Equipment for boiling
A saucepan or cooking pot with a lid, a wooden spoon, a knife and chopping board, a spoon, a plate and a heat source such as a jiko or cooker.

### Advantages of boiling
• It needs no oil, so it is good for health.
• It is easy and cheap.
• It makes hard foods soft and safe to eat.

### Disadvantages
• If you boil for too long or use a lot of water and throw it away, some vitamins are lost. Boil vegetables only until they are just tender, and use the water in soup or stew when possible.

## PAGE 3: How to boil eggs and potatoes
Follow the steps carefully with an adult.

### Boiling eggs
1. Wash your hands. Put the eggs gently in a pot.
2. Cover the eggs with cold water, so the water is about 2 cm above the eggs.
3. Place the pot on the fire and heat until the water boils.
4. Reduce the heat and let it boil gently for about 10 minutes for hard-boiled eggs.
5. The adult carefully pours off the hot water. Put the eggs in cold water to cool.
6. Tap the egg on a hard surface, peel off the shell and eat or use the egg.

### Boiling potatoes
1. Wash the potatoes. Peel them and cut into pieces of about the same size.
2. Place the pieces in a pot. Add enough clean water to cover them and a pinch of salt.
3. Cover with a lid and bring to the boil. Lower the heat.
4. Cook for about 15 to 20 minutes. Stick a fork in a piece. If it goes in easily, the potatoes are cooked.
5. An adult pours off the water using a lid or sieve to keep the potatoes in the pot.
6. Serve with a protective food such as a vegetable stew.

## PAGE 4: Shallow frying food
In shallow frying, food is cooked in a thin layer of hot oil in a flat frying pan. The oil makes the outside of the food crisp and brown. Only a small amount of oil is needed, about enough to cover the bottom of the pan.

### Foods that can be shallow fried
• Eggs.
• Fish pieces, sausages and chicken pieces.
• Onions and tomatoes for stew.
• Slices of potatoes, cassava or arrowroots.
• Chapati, which is cooked on a flat pan with a small amount of oil.
Mandazi and chips are cooked in deep oil, so they are not shallow fried.

### Equipment
A frying pan with a handle, a spatula (turner), a knife, a chopping board, a plate with paper for draining oil, a fork, cooking oil and a heat source.

### Advantages
• Food cooks quickly.
• It gives a tasty flavour and a golden colour.
• Fat-rich foods such as oil give the body energy.

### Disadvantages
• Too much oil is not good for health, and fried food should not be eaten at every meal.
• Hot oil can splash and burn.
• Oil that is heated too much starts to smoke. Turn the heat down.

## PAGE 5: How to shallow fry an egg and fish
### Frying an egg
1. Wash your hands. Set out the pan, oil, egg, bowl and spatula.
2. Crack the egg into a small bowl first. This checks that it is fresh and keeps out bits of shell.
3. Place the frying pan on medium heat. Add about a tablespoon of oil. Wait a short time until it is warm.
4. Slide the egg gently into the pan away from you.
5. Cook until the white is firm and no longer clear. Turn it over with a spatula if you like it cooked on both sides.
6. Lift onto a plate. Serve with a protective food such as tomato or vegetables.

### Frying fish pieces
1. Wash the fish. Dry the pieces with a clean cloth or paper, because wet food makes hot oil splash.
2. Season with a little salt or lemon juice.
3. Heat the oil in the pan on medium heat.
4. Place the fish pieces in the pan carefully, laying them down away from you.
5. Cook until golden on one side, turn once, and cook the other side until the fish is cooked through, and flakes when pierced with a fork.
6. Lift out and drain on paper. Serve hot.

## PAGE 6: Kitchen safety and first aid
Cooking is safe when we follow the rules. Most accidents happen because someone is careless, is in a hurry or is playing.

### Safety rules
• Cook only with an adult nearby.
• Turn pot and pan handles inward, away from the edge, so they are not knocked.
• Use dry cloths or oven gloves to hold hot pots. Wet cloths let heat through.
• Open lids away from your face. Steam can burn.
• Keep water away from hot oil. If a drop of water falls in hot oil it makes it splatter.
• Never leave cooking food alone.
• Do not carry hot pots around when other children are near.
• If oil catches fire, do not use water. An adult should cover the pan with a lid and turn off the heat.
• Keep flammable things such as paper, cloth and kerosene away from the fire.
• Keep hair tied and sleeves rolled.

### First aid for a small burn or scald
1. Put the burnt part under cool running clean water for at least ten minutes.
2. Do not put butter, oil, toothpaste or other things on the burn.
3. Cover loosely with a clean cloth.
4. Tell an adult at once. A big burn needs a doctor or clinic.

### After cooking
Put out the fire or switch off the gas. Let the pot cool. Wash the dishes with soap and clean water, dry them and store them. Wipe the working area. Wash your hands again.

## PAGE 7: Summary and common mistakes
Boiling cooks food in bubbling water. Shallow frying cooks food in a thin layer of hot oil. Both need care, cleanliness and an adult for safety.

### Remember
• Boiling: eggs, potatoes, cassava, maize, beans, rice.
• Shallow frying: eggs, fish, sausages, onions, chapati.
• Serve cooked food with vegetables and fruit for a balanced meal.
• Safety first: handles inward, dry cloth, lids away, water away from oil.

### Common mistakes
• Putting cold food into oil that is not hot enough. The food soaks up oil.
• Putting wet food in hot oil, which splatters.
• Using too much oil for shallow frying.
• Leaving pot handles sticking out.
• Boiling vegetables for too long, so vitamins are lost.
• Not washing hands and food before cooking.

### Serve and share
When food is ready, serve it neatly on a clean plate. Wash your hands before eating. Thank the people who helped. Share what you learned about cooking with your family.

## QUIZ
1. What is boiling?
   A) Cooking food in hot bubbling water
   B) Cooking food in deep oil
   C) Cooking food in sunlight
   D) Cooking food in a cold pot
   Answer: A
   Why: Boiling means cooking in water that is bubbling at a high heat.
2. Which of these foods is usually shallow fried?
   A) Boiled rice
   B) Mandazi
   C) A fried egg
   D) Tea
   Answer: C
   Why: Eggs are cooked in a thin layer of oil in a pan. Mandazi are deep fried.
3. Why should the handle of a pot be turned inward?
   A) To look neat
   B) To stop someone knocking it and spilling hot food
   C) To make the pot cook faster
   D) To keep the handle cool
   Answer: B
   Why: A handle sticking out can be knocked and cause burns.
4. What should you do first if you burn your hand slightly?
   A) Put cooking oil on it
   B) Run cool clean water over it for at least ten minutes
   C) Rub it with a dry cloth
   D) Cover it with toothpaste
   Answer: B
   Why: Cool running water reduces the heat and damage. Oil and other things can make it worse.
