# Agriculture Grade 4 notes

Topics: 1.1-1.4, 2.1-2.5, 3.1-3.3, 4.1 (all 13 KICD sub-strands). 1.1 Soil Conservation was missing from manifest.json but is a lesson. No retire.json needed.

Please have a teacher check:
- Even tacking stitch length (1 cm) and long-and-short tacking (about 2 cm long, 0.5 cm short, long stitch on top): sizes are teaching approximations; textbooks vary.
- Compost heap size (1.5 m square, chest height), ready in about 2-3 months, turn after 2-3 weeks: typical school-garden guidance.
- Radish ready in 4-6 weeks, carrots 3-4 months, millet 3-4 months: approximate.
- Fruit ripeness signs and how long trees take to bear: general guidance only.
- Planting hole for fruit trees given as about half a metre wide and deep (larger for big trees).
- Water boiled for at least one minute at a rolling boil, hard-boiled eggs about 10 minutes, burns cooled for at least 10 minutes: common health guidance.
- Food groups follow the three-group KICD wording (energy-giving, body-building, protective); no exact proportions are taught.
- Figures for tippy tap, scarecrow and stitches are simplified diagrams.
