# Creative Arts Grade 4 notes for human checking

Topic numbering: the design has two optional sub-strands both numbered 2.6 (Swimming and Indigenous Kenyan Games). Topic numbers must be unique, so Swimming = 2.6 and Indigenous Kenyan Games = 2.7. The second manifest entry "2.6 Indigenous Kenyan Games" is covered by lesson 2.7 (no retire.json needed because 2.6 has a lesson). The build may need to map 2.7 by name.

Please verify:
- Crab balance (2.2): taught as the four-point tabletop "crab" (hands behind, hips lifted, tummy up). Some Kenyan PE books use a different position for "crab balance"; check against the teacher's guide.
- Side roll (2.2): taught as a straight-body log roll on the mat.
- Medium and elongated starts (2.1): rear knee level with the middle of the front foot (medium) and with the heel of the front foot (elongated); front foot about 1.5 foot lengths behind the line. Confirm against the KICD/teacher guide.
- Kenyan flag (2.1): colour meanings (black people, red blood shed for freedom, green land/natural wealth, white peace and honesty) and the figure are simplified, stripe widths not to scale.
- Kenyan instruments (1.1): isukuti (Luhya: Isukha and Idakho), kayamba (Mijikenda/coast), gichandi gourd rattle (Kikuyu). Confirm community links.
- Kodaly hand signs (1.5): fist = d, sloping palm = r, flat palm = m; heights are approximate.
- Netball (1.2): court 30.5 m x 15.25 m, post 3.05 m, 0.9 m defending distance, 3 second rule, position areas. Mini-netball numbers left to the teacher.
- East African Community anthem (2.3): lesson gives no lyrics, title or language, only how to learn and perform it in unison.
- Kenya National Anthem (3.1): title Ee Mungu Nguvu Yetu; verse themes paraphrased; "one verse or all three depending on the occasion" should be checked against official protocol.
- Indigenous animal games (2.7): named games are generic animal-movement games and hawk-and-chicks; community-specific traditional names were not invented, so the teacher should add local examples.
- Recorder (2.5): assumes a baroque-fingering descant; B = thumb+1, A = thumb+1+2, G = thumb+1+2+3; fingering figure shows thumb hole in the same column as front holes.
- Swimming (2.6): only feet-first entries and floats/glide taught; no strokes.
