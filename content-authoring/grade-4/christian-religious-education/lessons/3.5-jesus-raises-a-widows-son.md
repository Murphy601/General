## PAGE 1: A sad day in Nain
The story of the widow's son is found in Luke 7:11-16. Soon after healing a servant in Capernaum, Jesus went to a town called Nain. His disciples and a large crowd went along with Him.

As Jesus approached the town gate, a funeral procession was coming out. A young man had died. He was the only son of his mother, and she was a widow. A widow is a woman whose husband has died. In those days, a widow without a son had no one to provide for her. She was probably very poor and very sad. A large crowd from the town was with her.

### Words to know
• Widow: a woman whose husband has died.
• Compassion: feeling sorry for someone and wanting to help.
• Bier: the frame on which a dead person was carried to be buried.
• Miracle: a wonderful act that shows the power of God.

### Think about it
How do people in your community comfort someone who has lost a loved one?

## PAGE 2: Jesus feels compassion
The Bible says that when the Lord saw her, His heart went out to her. He said, "Don't cry."

This tells us that Jesus noticed the widow's grief. She did not call to Him or ask for help. He saw her need and acted. He was moved by her tears.

### What Jesus did
1. He saw the woman and felt sorry for her.
2. He said, "Don't cry."
3. He went up and touched the bier, and the men carrying it stood still.
4. He said, "Young man, I say to you, get up!"

### Compassion
Compassion is more than feeling sad. It moves us to help. Jesus showed compassion not only by words, but by action.

### Discuss
How did Jesus show that He cared about the widow?

## PAGE 3: The young man lives
Luke 7:15 says, "The dead man sat up and began to talk, and Jesus gave him back to his mother." The mother's sorrow turned to joy. Her only son, who was to provide for her, was alive again.

The crowd were filled with awe and praised God. They said, "A great prophet has appeared among us," and "God has come to help his people." News about Jesus spread throughout Judea and the surrounding country.

### What this shows
• Jesus has power over death. He is not only a healer but is Lord of life.
• Jesus cares about the poor and lonely, such as widows.
• The people recognised the hand of God in what Jesus did.

### A verse to remember
In John 11:25 Jesus says, "I am the resurrection and the life. The one who believes in me will live, even though they die." Write this verse on a flash card and learn it.

## PAGE 4: Showing compassion
We can copy Jesus by showing compassion to people around us.

### Ways of showing compassion
• Visit a neighbour who is sick or grieving and sit with them.
• Share food or clothes with a family that is struggling.
• Speak gentle words to a friend who is sad.
• Help an elderly person to carry water or firewood.
• Include a lonely child in your play.
• Pray for those who have lost someone.

### Example
When Baraka's classmate lost her father, Baraka wrote her a card that said, "I am sorry about your father. I am praying for you." He also offered to share his notes with her. This was a compassionate response.

### Kindness at school
Look around your class. Is there a child who is often alone or sad? A small kind act can make a big difference to that child.

## PAGE 5: Praying when we face challenges
The widow of Nain could do nothing to bring her son back, but Jesus could. This teaches us that God is greater than our biggest problems.

### Challenges children may face
• Losing a family member or pet.
• Illness in the family.
• Lack of school fees or food.
• Fear of exams or bullying.
• Family arguments.

### What we can do
1. Pray and tell God about the problem.
2. Trust that He hears us. Some problems are solved quickly, and some take time.
3. Ask trusted adults for help.
4. Do what we can, and trust God with what we cannot.
5. Thank God for the help He gives.

### Summary
• Jesus raised the only son of a widow at Nain, and He felt compassion for her.
• He has power over life and death.
• We show compassion by helping and comforting others.
• We pray to God when we face challenges.

## QUIZ
1. In which town did Jesus raise the widow's son?
   A) Nain
   B) Nazareth
   C) Cana
   D) Jericho
   Answer: A
   Why: Luke 7:11 says that Jesus went to a town called Nain.
2. Who was the woman in this story?
   A) A teacher
   B) A widow whose only son had died
   C) A queen
   D) A tax collector
   Answer: B
   Why: The young man was the only son of a widow.
3. What did Jesus say to the young man?
   A) Follow me
   B) Go home
   C) Young man, I say to you, get up
   D) Be quiet
   Answer: C
   Why: Luke 7:14 records these words.
4. How did the crowd respond after the miracle?
   A) They ran away
   B) They were filled with awe and praised God
   C) They laughed
   D) They got angry
   Answer: B
   Why: Luke 7:16 says that fear seized them all and they praised God.
5. Which is an act of compassion?
   A) Ignoring a sad classmate
   B) Laughing at a friend who has lost something
   C) Sitting with a sad friend and listening
   D) Telling everyone about a friend's problems
   Answer: C
   Why: Compassion means feeling sorry for someone and helping them.
6. Explain why the widow of Nain was in great need. (2 marks)
   Answer: Her husband had died and now her only son had died as well, so she had nobody to provide for her.
   Why: 1 mark for the loss of husband and 1 mark for the loss of her only son or her lack of support.
7. State three ways in which children can show compassion to others. (3 marks)
   Answer: Comfort a grieving friend, share food or clothes, help an elderly person, include a lonely child.
   Why: 1 mark for each valid way.
8. What does the story teach us about God's power, and what should we do when we face challenges? (3 marks)
   Answer: God has power over life and death. When we face challenges we should pray, trust God and ask trusted adults for help.
   Why: 1 mark for God's power over death, 1 mark for prayer, 1 mark for trust or seeking help.
