## PAGE 1: What is a parable?
A parable is a short story that Jesus told to teach a lesson about God and how we should live. The story uses everyday things, such as coins, seeds, sheep and farming, to explain a deeper meaning. Jesus told many parables because people could remember stories and understand the lesson.

One parable is the parable of the lost coin. It is found in Luke 15:8-10. Jesus told it together with two other stories about things that were lost: a lost sheep and a lost son. Jesus told these stories because some religious leaders complained that He welcomed sinners and ate with them.

### Words to know
• Parable: a story with a meaning that teaches a lesson.
• Sinner: someone who does wrong and has turned away from God.
• Repent: to be sorry for sin and turn back to God.
• Righteous: living in a way that pleases God.

### Think about it
Have you ever lost something that mattered to you? How did you feel?

## PAGE 2: The story of the lost coin
Jesus said, "Suppose a woman has ten silver coins and loses one. Doesn't she light a lamp, sweep the house and search carefully until she finds it?"

Houses then were small and often dark, with few windows and floors of packed earth. A coin could easily be lost in the dust. The woman lit a lamp so she could see better. She swept the floor so that the coin would show up, and she searched carefully.

The coin was valuable to her. Those ten coins may have been all the money she had. She did not say, "I still have nine, so it does not matter." She kept looking until she found the lost one.

### What happened next
When she found it, she called her friends and neighbours together and said, "Rejoice with me; I have found my lost coin." She was so happy that she wanted to share her joy.

## PAGE 3: What the parable means
Jesus explained the meaning: "In the same way, I tell you, there is rejoicing in the presence of the angels of God over one sinner who repents."

### The parts of the story
• The woman stands for God, who loves each person.
• The coin stands for a person who has wandered away from God.
• The search stands for God's love, which goes looking for the lost.
• The joy stands for the joy in heaven when someone turns back to God.

### Lessons from the parable
1. Every person is precious to God. He does not forget anyone.
2. God searches for those who are lost, and He wants them to come back.
3. God is happy when a person repents.
4. We should also care about people who have gone wrong.

What does the story tell us about how God feels about each child?

## PAGE 4: God's love for the sinner
The parable teaches that God loves sinners. This does not mean that God likes sin. He hates sin because it hurts people, but He loves the person and wants to forgive.

### What it means to repent
Repenting is more than saying sorry. It means turning away from wrong and choosing to do right. For example, a child who stole a pencil returns it and promises not to steal again.

### How God helps us
• He forgives us when we are truly sorry.
• He gives us the Bible to show the right way.
• He gives us the Holy Spirit to help us do right.
• He sent Jesus to save us.

### A note about money
This parable also shows that money is valuable and should be used carefully. The woman searched hard for her coin. We should look after our money and not lose it through carelessness.

## PAGE 5: Living a righteous life
Because God loves us, we should try to live in a way that pleases Him. This is called living a righteous or upright life.

### How to live righteously
• Tell the truth.
• Obey your parents and teachers.
• Be kind and share.
• Avoid bad company and harmful things.
• Say sorry quickly when you do wrong.
• Pray and read your Bible.

### Reaching out to others
Like the woman who searched, we can help others who have gone astray. If a friend is doing wrong, we can gently talk to them, pray for them and invite them to church or Sunday school. We should not despise them.

### Summary
• A parable is a story that teaches a lesson.
• A woman lost one of ten silver coins, searched until she found it, and rejoiced.
• God loves each person, searches for the lost, and rejoices when one repents.
• We should repent, live righteously and care for others.

## QUIZ
1. Where is the parable of the lost coin found?
   A) Luke 15:8-10
   B) Genesis 12:1
   C) John 3:16
   D) Acts 2:38
   Answer: A
   Why: The parable is recorded in Luke 15:8-10.
2. How many silver coins did the woman have before she lost one?
   A) Five
   B) Ten
   C) Twelve
   D) Twenty
   Answer: B
   Why: Jesus said that she had ten silver coins.
3. What did the woman do when she found the coin?
   A) Hid it
   B) Called her friends and neighbours to rejoice with her
   C) Threw it away
   D) Ignored it
   Answer: B
   Why: She invited others to celebrate.
4. In the parable, who does the woman stand for?
   A) A sinner
   B) A Pharisee
   C) God
   D) A neighbour
   Answer: C
   Why: The woman's search stands for God's love that seeks the lost.
5. According to Jesus, what causes joy in the presence of the angels of God?
   A) A large crowd
   B) One sinner who repents
   C) A rich person
   D) A new house
   Answer: B
   Why: Luke 15:10 says there is rejoicing over one sinner who repents.
6. What is a parable? (2 marks)
   Answer: A short story used by Jesus to teach a lesson about God and how we should live.
   Why: 1 mark for story and 1 mark for teaching a lesson.
7. State three lessons that we learn from the parable of the lost coin. (3 marks)
   Answer: Every person is precious to God; God searches for those who are lost; heaven rejoices when someone repents; we should care for those who go wrong.
   Why: 1 mark for each valid lesson.
8. Give three ways of living a righteous life as a child. (3 marks)
   Answer: Tell the truth, obey parents, share, avoid bad company, say sorry, pray and read the Bible.
   Why: 1 mark for each valid way.
