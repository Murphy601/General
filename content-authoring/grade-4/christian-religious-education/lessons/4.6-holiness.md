## PAGE 1: What is holiness?
Holy means set apart for God, pure and clean. God is holy, as we learnt earlier. He asks His people to be holy too. 1 Peter 1:16 says, "Be holy, because I am holy."

For a child, holiness means living in a way that pleases God, in what we think, say and do. It means choosing right over wrong, being honest, pure and kind. Holiness is not being perfect. It is trying, with God's help, to live as He wants and saying sorry when we fail.

### Words to know
• Holy: pure and set apart for God.
• Sin: choosing to do what God says is wrong.
• Temple: a holy building where people worship God.
• Glorify: to honour and praise God by the way we live.

### Think about it
What things do you think please God? What things do not?

## PAGE 2: Our bodies are temples
1 Corinthians 6:19-20 says, "Do you not know that your bodies are temples of the Holy Spirit, who is in you, whom you have received from God? You are not your own; you were bought at a price. Therefore honour God with your bodies."

A temple is a holy place where God is honoured. The Bible says that the body of a believer is like a temple because the Holy Spirit lives in those who belong to Jesus.

### Lessons from these verses
• Our bodies are special and holy.
• God made us, and Jesus paid a great price to save us.
• We should honour God with our bodies, by how we treat them and by what we do with them.
• We must not misuse or harm our bodies.

### Discuss
If your body is like a temple, how should you treat it?

## PAGE 3: Ways of glorifying God with our bodies
To glorify God is to bring honour to Him by the way we live.

### Ways to honour God with your body
• Keep your body clean and healthy. Bathe, brush your teeth, and eat well.
• Exercise and rest to keep it strong.
• Use your mouth to speak kindly and truthfully, to pray and sing praise.
• Use your hands to help, share and work, not to hurt, steal or fight.
• Use your feet to walk to safe and good places, such as school and church.
• Use your eyes and ears for what is good. Avoid harmful pictures, videos and songs.
• Do not put harmful things into your body, such as alcohol, cigarettes or drugs.
• Dress neatly and modestly.

### Respecting other people's bodies
Because every person is God's creation, we must respect the bodies of others. Do not hit, bully or touch others in ways they do not want.

### Note
If someone touches you wrongly or tries to harm you, tell a trusted adult at once. It is never your fault.

## PAGE 4: Sins that do not please God
Sin is anything that goes against what God wants. Sin harms us and others.

### Some sins that children may commit
• Lying and cheating.
• Stealing, even something small.
• Disobeying parents and teachers.
• Bullying, fighting and using bad words.
• Being greedy or refusing to share.
• Jealousy and hatred.
• Watching or listening to harmful things.
• Using drugs or taking things that harm the body.

### Results of sin
• It separates us from God.
• It hurts other people.
• It brings guilt and punishment.
• It can lead to bigger wrongs.

### God forgives
The Bible says that if we confess our sins, God is faithful and will forgive us (1 John 1:9). When we sin, we should say sorry to God, say sorry to the person we hurt, put things right, and try not to repeat it.

## PAGE 5: Avoiding situations that lead to sin
It is easier to avoid sin if we stay away from situations that tempt us.

### Ways to avoid situations that lead to sin
1. Choose good friends. Bad company can lead us into wrong actions.
2. Stay away from places where wrong things happen.
3. Do not spend time alone with people in secluded places.
4. Be careful about what you watch, listen to and read. Choose things that build you up.
5. Be busy with good activities: school work, sports, church groups and helping at home.
6. Pray for strength and read God's word.
7. Talk to a trusted adult if you feel tempted or confused.

### Reading Joseph's example
Joseph ran away from a wrong situation. Running away is not cowardly. It is wise.

### Song idea
Make up a short song about holiness. Include the words "clean, pure and strong, God's temple all day long".

### Summary
• We are called to be holy because God is holy.
• Our bodies are temples of the Holy Spirit, and we honour God with them (1 Corinthians 6:19-20).
• We avoid sin and situations that lead to it.
• God forgives us when we are truly sorry.

## QUIZ
1. What does 1 Corinthians 6:19 say our bodies are?
   A) Machines
   B) Temples of the Holy Spirit
   C) Things to be ignored
   D) Property of strangers
   Answer: B
   Why: The verse teaches that the body is a temple of the Holy Spirit.
2. Which action honours God with the body?
   A) Keeping clean, eating well and speaking kindly
   B) Taking harmful drugs
   C) Fighting others
   D) Using bad words
   Answer: A
   Why: Caring for the body and using it well honours God.
3. What does "holy" mean?
   A) Pure and set apart for God
   B) Rich
   C) Famous
   D) Strong
   Answer: A
   Why: Holy means pure and set apart for God.
4. Which is the best way to avoid situations that lead to sin?
   A) Choose good friends and stay away from wrong places
   B) Go to places where wrong things happen
   C) Keep bad secrets
   D) Ignore your parents
   Answer: A
   Why: Good friends and safe places keep us away from temptation.
5. What should a person do after sinning?
   A) Hide it forever
   B) Confess, say sorry, and put things right
   C) Blame another person
   D) Repeat it
   Answer: B
   Why: 1 John 1:9 says that God forgives when we confess.
6. Give three sins that do not please God. (3 marks)
   Answer: Lying, stealing, disobeying, bullying, jealousy.
   Why: 1 mark for each valid sin.
7. Explain why Christians say that the body is a temple of the Holy Spirit. (3 marks)
   Answer: The Holy Spirit lives in believers, so the body is holy like a temple, and it should be treated with respect and kept pure.
   Why: 1 mark for the Holy Spirit living in believers, 1 mark for the body being holy, 1 mark for respect and purity.
8. Describe three ways in which children can glorify God with their bodies. (3 marks)
   Answer: Keep clean and healthy, speak kindly, avoid harmful substances, use hands to help.
   Why: 1 mark for each valid way.
