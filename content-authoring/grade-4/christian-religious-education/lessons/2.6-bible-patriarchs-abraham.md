## PAGE 1: Who was Abraham?
A patriarch is the father and leader of a family or a nation. The Bible calls Abraham, Isaac and Jacob the patriarchs of Israel. Abraham comes first. He is called the father of faith because he trusted God and obeyed Him even when he did not know where God was leading him.

Abraham's first name was Abram. He was born in a city called Ur, in the land of the Chaldeans. His father, Terah, took the family, including Abram, his wife Sarai and his nephew Lot, and set out for the land of Canaan. On the way they settled in a place called Haran, and Terah died there (Genesis 11:31-32).

### Words to know
• Patriarch: a father and leader of a family or nation.
• Call: God's invitation or command to a person.
• Covenant: a solemn promise or agreement.

### Think about it
Have you ever moved to a new place or a new school? How did you feel?

## PAGE 2: The call of Abraham
While Abram was living in Haran, God spoke to him. Genesis 12:1-3 records that God said, "Go from your country, your people and your father's household to the land I will show you."

God made promises to him:
• "I will make you into a great nation."
• "I will bless you."
• "I will make your name great."
• "You will be a blessing."
• "I will bless those who bless you."
• "All peoples on earth will be blessed through you" (Genesis 12:3).

Abram was seventy-five years old when he left Haran. He took his wife Sarai, his nephew Lot, their possessions and the people who had joined them, and set out for the land of Canaan. He did not know exactly where he was going, but he obeyed (Genesis 12:4-5).

### What Abram's obedience shows
Abram trusted God enough to leave his home, his relatives and everything familiar. Obedience means doing what God says, even when it is difficult.

### Reading the sketch
Ur was in the south-east. The family travelled north to Haran. From Haran, Abram went south-west to Canaan. Later, Abram lived in Canaan as a traveller, moving from place to place with his tents and flocks. God told him that this land would belong to his descendants.

### Note
The journey was long and demanded courage and patience. Abram often built altars to worship God on the way, showing that he kept God at the centre of his life.

## PAGE 4: From Abram to Abraham
Many years after God's call, Abram was ninety-nine years old. Genesis 17:1-5 says that God appeared to him and said, "I am God Almighty; walk before me faithfully and be blameless." God made a covenant with him and gave him a new name.

"No longer will you be called Abram; your name will be Abraham, for I have made you a father of many nations."

### What the new name means
The name Abraham is linked to the words "father of many", because God said Abraham would be the father of many nations. Abraham was the name he would carry for the rest of his life.

The new name reminded Abraham of God's promise. He and Sarai had no child, and they were old. But God said many nations would come from him. God also changed the name of Sarai to Sarah (Genesis 17:15). A name shows what someone is and what God plans for them.

### Why the name changed
The name changed because God made a new promise. It marked the start of a new stage in Abraham's life and showed that God was in control.

## PAGE 5: Obeying God and receiving blessings
Abraham teaches us how to obey God. He obeyed even when he did not see the whole plan. He believed God's promise even when it seemed impossible. Later, God gave Abraham and Sarah a son, Isaac, just as promised.

### How can children obey God today?
• By obeying parents and teachers when they give good instructions.
• By reading the Bible and doing what it says.
• By speaking the truth and being kind.
• By praying and trusting God when things seem hard.

### God's blessings
Genesis 12:3 says that all peoples on earth will be blessed through Abraham. Christians understand that Jesus came from Abraham's family, so this blessing reached the whole world. We appreciate God's blessings in our lives, such as family, food, health, school and friends. We thank Him and share His blessings with others.

### Summary
• Abraham, first called Abram, came from Ur and lived in Haran.
• God called him to leave for a land He would show him, and Abram obeyed at age seventy-five.
• God changed his name to Abraham, meaning father of many.
• We should obey God and thank Him for His blessings.

## QUIZ
1. What was Abraham's name before God changed it?
   A) Isaac
   B) Abram
   C) Lot
   D) Terah
   Answer: B
   Why: Genesis 17:5 says that Abram's name was changed to Abraham.
2. What did God tell Abram to do in Genesis 12:1?
   A) Build a boat
   B) Go to the land that God would show him
   C) Stay in his father's house
   D) Fight a giant
   Answer: B
   Why: God told him to leave his country and go to the land He would show him.
3. What does the name Abraham mean?
   A) King of Egypt
   B) Father of many
   C) Wise man
   D) Rich man
   Answer: B
   Why: God said, "I have made you a father of many nations."
4. How old was Abram when he left Haran?
   A) 25
   B) 50
   C) 75
   D) 100
   Answer: C
   Why: Genesis 12:4 says that Abram was seventy-five when he left Haran.
5. Which action shows obedience like Abraham's?
   A) Ignoring your teacher's instructions
   B) Doing what a good instruction says even when it is hard
   C) Waiting for others to do it first
   D) Arguing all the time
   Answer: B
   Why: Abraham obeyed God even when it was hard and he did not know the whole plan.
