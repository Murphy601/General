## PAGE 1: When someone wrongs us
Everyone is wronged at some time. A friend may break your pencil, a classmate may push you in the queue, or someone may say unkind words about you. It is natural to feel angry and to want to get even. But Jesus taught a different way.

Forgiving someone means deciding not to hold their wrong against them and not to pay them back. It does not mean pretending that nothing happened, and it does not mean that what they did was fine. It means letting go of the wish for revenge.

### Words to know
• Forgive: to stop being angry with someone and not to pay them back for the wrong they did.
• Revenge: hurting someone because they hurt you.
• Reconcile: to become friends again after a quarrel.

### Think about it
When someone hurt you, what did you feel like doing? Share with a partner.

## PAGE 2: What Jesus taught
In Matthew 5:38-39 Jesus said, "You have heard that it was said, 'Eye for eye, and tooth for tooth.' But I tell you, do not resist an evil person. If anyone slaps you on the right cheek, turn to them the other cheek also."

People were used to the saying "eye for eye", which meant paying back the same harm. Jesus taught something better. He wanted His followers to break the cycle of revenge.

### What the teaching means
• We should not repay evil with evil.
• We should choose peace instead of fighting.
• We should be ready to forgive, even when it is difficult.

### What the teaching does not mean
• It does not mean we must stay in danger. If someone keeps hurting you, tell a parent, teacher or another trusted adult so they can protect you.
• It does not mean we should let people take advantage of us.

### Discuss
Why does revenge make problems bigger, not smaller?

## PAGE 3: Why we should not take revenge
Suppose Otieno pushes Njeri. Njeri pushes him back harder. Then Otieno hits her. Soon both are fighting, and a teacher must separate them. Both are punished. Revenge did not solve the problem. It made it worse.

### Reasons for not taking revenge
1. Revenge starts a cycle of hurting that does not stop.
2. It brings punishment to us as well.
3. It breaks friendships and makes the class or family unhappy.
4. It does not please God. Jesus teaches us to love even our enemies.
5. Forgiving frees us from bitterness and anger.

### Jesus' own example
When Jesus was nailed to the cross, He prayed, "Father, forgive them, for they do not know what they are doing" (Luke 23:34). Jesus forgave the people who hurt Him.

### Note
Forgiveness is not weakness. It takes courage and self-control.

## PAGE 4: Forgiveness in the Bible
The Bible has other examples of forgiveness.

### Joseph forgives his brothers
Joseph's brothers were jealous of him and sold him as a slave. Years later, Joseph became a ruler in Egypt, and his brothers came to buy grain. Joseph could have punished them, but he forgave them. He told them, "You intended to harm me, but God intended it for good" (Genesis 50:20). He did not take revenge. He looked after his family.

### The Lord's Prayer
In the Lord's Prayer, Jesus taught us to ask, "Forgive us our debts, as we also have forgiven our debtors." This means we ask God to forgive us in the same way as we forgive others.

### What we learn
• God forgives us, so we should forgive others.
• When we forgive, we grow in love and peace.
• Forgiveness can restore friendships.

## PAGE 5: Practising forgiveness
Forgiving is a skill that we can practise. Here are steps to follow.

### Steps to forgive
1. Calm down. Take a deep breath and do not act in anger.
2. Think about what happened and why.
3. Talk to the person kindly, or ask a teacher or parent to help.
4. Say, "I forgive you." If you were also at fault, say sorry.
5. Pray for the person and for peace between you.

### Role-play ideas
• A classmate breaks your ruler by accident. Show a calm response.
• A friend tells a lie about you. Show how you speak to the friend and forgive.
• You wrongly accuse a friend. Show how you say sorry.

### Summary
• Jesus teaches us not to repay evil for evil (Matthew 5:39).
• Revenge makes problems worse.
• Jesus forgave those who hurt Him, and Joseph forgave his brothers.
• We should forgive others, and tell trusted adults if someone keeps hurting us.

## QUIZ
1. What does Matthew 5:39 teach?
   A) Pay back evil with evil
   B) Do not resist an evil person by taking revenge
   C) Hit those who hit you
   D) Ignore your parents
   Answer: B
   Why: Jesus taught that we should not take revenge but respond with peace.
2. Which of these shows forgiveness?
   A) Refusing to talk to a friend forever
   B) Hitting back
   C) Saying "I forgive you" after being wronged
   D) Telling lies about the person
   Answer: C
   Why: Forgiving means letting go of anger and not paying back.
3. Who forgave his brothers who had sold him as a slave?
   A) Samson
   B) Joseph
   C) Balaam
   D) Zacchaeus
   Answer: B
   Why: Joseph forgave his brothers, saying that God intended it for good.
4. What did Jesus pray on the cross about those who hurt Him?
   A) Punish them
   B) Father, forgive them
   C) Send an angel
   D) Let them be afraid
   Answer: B
   Why: Luke 23:34 records that He said, "Father, forgive them."
5. A boy keeps hurting you even after you forgave him. What should you do?
   A) Hit him back
   B) Tell a trusted adult so that you are protected
   C) Say nothing and suffer
   D) Hurt his friends
   Answer: B
   Why: Forgiveness does not mean staying in danger. Trusted adults can protect you.
6. Give two reasons why we should not take revenge. (2 marks)
   Answer: It starts a cycle of hurting; it gets us in trouble; it breaks friendships; it does not please God.
   Why: 1 mark for each valid reason.
7. Describe three steps you can take to forgive someone who wronged you. (3 marks)
   Answer: Calm down; think about what happened; talk kindly to the person; say I forgive you; pray for peace.
   Why: 1 mark for each valid step, up to 3.
8. Explain why forgiving others is important to Christians. (3 marks)
   Answer: God forgives us, so we should forgive others. Forgiving brings peace and restores friendships. Jesus taught it and showed it Himself.
