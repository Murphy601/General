## PAGE 1: Who am I? My name
Your name is one of the first gifts you received. Your parents or guardians chose it for you, and it tells other people who you are. When the teacher calls the register and you answer, "Present," you are using your name to say, "I am here."

Kenyan children have many kinds of names. Some are from the home language, such as Wanjiku, Otieno, Achieng, Kiprono or Mwangi. Some are Kiswahili names, such as Zawadi, which means a gift, Baraka, which means a blessing, and Neema, which means grace. Others are Christian names such as Grace, David or Mary.

### Why names matter
• A name helps people call you and find you.
• A name shows that you are a person, not a thing.
• Knowing your full name helps you stay safe, for example when you are lost and must tell a helper who you are.

### Try it
Say your full name aloud. Then tell a partner one thing you like about your name and who chose it for you. Listen carefully when your partner speaks, and say their name correctly.

## PAGE 2: You are unique
Unique means there is nobody else exactly like you. Look at the tips of your fingers. The lines on them are called fingerprints, and no two people have the same ones. Your voice, your face and the way you laugh or walk also belong only to you. Even twins, who may look alike, are different people with different thoughts and different talents.

Being unique does not mean you are better than others. It means that God made each person different on purpose. Your friend may run fast, and you may sing well. Another child may be good at drawing, sums, farming or telling stories. We need all these different gifts.

### Think about yourself
• What is one thing you are good at?
• What is one thing you enjoy doing?
• What is one thing you would like to learn?

### Compare and respect
When you notice that someone is different from you, do not laugh at them. Different heights, skin colours, tribes, abilities and habits are all part of how God made people. A good Christian respects the differences in others.

## PAGE 3: God is the Creator
The Bible begins with these words: "In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth" (Genesis 1:1). Christians believe that God is the Creator. He made the sun, the moon, the rivers, the mountains, the animals, the plants and people. Nothing was made without Him, and He was not made by anyone. He is the only Creator.

Creation shows how great God is. When you see the sun rising over the shamba, hear birds early in the morning, or watch rain fall after a dry season, you can thank God who made them.

### Two important verses
• Genesis 1:27 says that God created human beings in His own image. This means people are special among all creatures. We can think, speak, love, pray and choose.
• Psalm 139 tells God that He made us in a wonderful way. The writer says that we are fearfully and wonderfully made (Psalm 139:14).

### Remember
You did not make yourself. God planned you and gave you life. That is why every person, young or old, deserves respect.

## PAGE 4: Special because God made me
Because God made you, you are valuable. You do not need to be the tallest, the cleverest or the richest to matter. You matter because God made you and loves you. He knows your name, as Isaiah 43:1 says, "I have called you by name; you are mine."

Sometimes children feel bad about themselves. Someone may call them names or say they are not good enough. When this happens, remember that what God says about you is more important than unkind words. You can say quietly, "God made me, and I am special."

### How to look after God's special creation
• Keep your body clean and eat healthy food.
• Rest and play so that you grow strong.
• Use your talents to help others.
• Do not hurt yourself or others, because everyone is God's creation.

### Song idea
Make up a short song or clap a rhythm with the words, "I am special, God made me." Sing it with your class and add movements.

## PAGE 5: Thanking God and living as His creation
When we understand that God made us, the right response is to thank Him and to live in a way that pleases Him. Christians thank God in prayer. A short prayer of thanks can be as simple as, "Thank you, God, for making me and for my family."

Here are ways to show thanks for being God's creation:
1. Pray each morning and evening.
2. Use your talents, such as singing, sports, reading or helping at home.
3. Take care of the environment, because God also made trees, water and animals.
4. Respect other people, because they too are made in God's image.

### Summary
• A name identifies you and makes you a person to be respected.
• You are unique. No one else is exactly like you.
• God is the sole Creator. He made you in His image.
• Being special is a reason to thank God and to respect others, not to boast.

### Common mistake
Some children think being unique means being better than others. It does not. Every person is unique, so everyone is special.

## QUIZ
1. Who does the Bible say created the heavens and the earth?
   A) The first people
   B) God
   C) The angels
   D) The kings
   Answer: B
   Why: Genesis 1:1 says that in the beginning God created the heavens and the earth.
2. Which sentence best explains what "unique" means?
   A) Nobody else is exactly like you
   B) You are better than everyone
   C) You look the same as your friends
   D) You have no talents
   Answer: A
   Why: Unique means one of a kind. Every person is different, but no one is better in God's eyes because of it.
3. Which verse tells us that people are made in the image of God?
   A) Genesis 1:27
   B) Exodus 20:12
   C) Luke 19:5
   D) Matthew 6:9
   Answer: A
   Why: Genesis 1:27 says that God created human beings in His own image.
4. Why is it important to know your full name?
   A) So you can boast to others
   B) So you can avoid school
   C) So people can identify you and you can stay safe
   D) So you can change it every year
   Answer: C
   Why: A name identifies you, and telling a helper your full name when lost keeps you safe.
5. A classmate teases another child for speaking differently. What is the best Christian response?
   A) Join in the teasing
   B) Ignore that God made people different and respect the child
   C) Tell everyone the child is strange
   D) Laugh quietly
   Answer: B
   Why: God made each person different on purpose, so we should respect differences.
6. Give two things that make you unique. (2 marks)
   Answer: Any two true answers, for example my fingerprints and my voice, or my talent for singing and my favourite food.
   Why: Award 1 mark for each true and sensible answer.
7. Explain why every person deserves respect. Use what you learnt about God as Creator. (3 marks)
   Answer: Every person was created by God in His image, so every person is valuable and special to Him. Because God loves each person, we should treat everyone kindly.
   Why: 1 mark for God as Creator, 1 for made in His image, 1 for the link to respect and kindness.
8. Write a short prayer thanking God for making you. (3 marks)
   Answer: A prayer that begins by addressing God, thanks Him for making the child, and ends with a closing such as Amen. For example: "Dear God, thank you for making me and my family. Help me to be kind. Amen."
   Why: 1 mark for addressing God, 1 for giving thanks for creation or life, 1 for a suitable ending or request.
