## PAGE 1: What is obedience?
Obedience means doing what a person in authority tells you to do, as long as it is good and right. At home, we obey our parents or guardians. At school, we obey our teachers and school rules. In church, we obey God's word. Above all, we obey God.

Obedience is not just doing what we are told when someone is watching. True obedience is doing the right thing willingly, on time and with a good attitude.

### Words to know
• Obey: to do what someone in authority tells you to do.
• Disobey: to refuse to do what you are told.
• Honour: to respect someone and treat them well.
• Authority: the right to give instructions, such as a parent or teacher has.

### Think about it
Who do you obey every day? Make a list of five people or rules that you follow.

## PAGE 2: Honouring parents
Exodus 20:12 says, "Honour your father and your mother, so that you may live long in the land the Lord your God is giving you." This is one of the Ten Commandments that God gave to His people. Ephesians 6:1 says, "Children, obey your parents in the Lord, for this is right."

### Benefits of honouring parents
• It pleases God and brings His blessing.
• It brings peace and happiness in the home.
• Parents trust and help their children more.
• You learn good habits and grow into a responsible person.
• It sets a good example for your younger brothers and sisters.

### Ways of honouring parents
1. Do what they ask cheerfully and quickly.
2. Speak to them politely. Avoid answering back rudely.
3. Help with chores without being reminded.
4. Say thank you for what they do for you.
5. Pray for them.

### Jesus as an example
When Jesus was a boy, He lived in Nazareth with Mary and Joseph. Luke 2:51 tells us that He was obedient to them. Jesus is our example in obeying parents.

## PAGE 3: Obeying teachers and school rules
Teachers guide us so that we learn well and stay safe. When we obey them, we show respect and help the whole school to run well.

### Why we obey teachers
• They know things that we need to learn.
• They care about our safety and progress.
• A class in which everyone obeys can learn in peace.
• It prepares us to obey good laws when we are adults.

### School rules
Most schools have rules such as:
• Arrive on time.
• Wear the school uniform neatly.
• Keep silent when the teacher is teaching.
• Do not fight or bully.
• Take care of school property.
• Do not leave the school compound without permission.
• Keep the school clean.

### Responsibility
Obeying school rules is a way of showing responsibility. If everyone kept the rules, there would be fewer accidents and more time for learning. When you follow the rules, you also set an example.

## PAGE 4: Results of disobedience
Disobedience has consequences. It brings problems to us and to others.

### Examples from the Bible
• Adam and Eve disobeyed God's command not to eat the fruit of one tree. As a result, they had to leave the garden of Eden (Genesis 3).
• Jonah disobeyed God by running away from the task God gave him. He ended up in a great fish before he obeyed (Jonah 1 and 2).

### Results of disobeying parents and teachers today
• Punishment, such as extra work, or losing privileges.
• Danger. A child who ignores a safety rule may be hurt.
• Poor marks, if we ignore instructions in class.
• Broken trust, and parents may not allow us to do things we want.
• Unhappiness and guilt.
• Being unfriendly to others in class, as we spoil their learning.

### Case study
A boy was told not to cross a busy road alone. He ignored his mother and ran across. A motorcycle nearly hit him. Obeying his mother's rule would have kept him safe.

### Discuss
In groups, list five results of disobedience at school and at home.

## PAGE 5: Choosing to obey
Obedience is a choice. We can choose to obey each day.

### How to become obedient
1. Listen carefully when you are given an instruction.
2. Do it at once, not later.
3. Do it well and cheerfully.
4. If you do not understand or if the instruction seems wrong or unsafe, ask politely or tell a trusted adult. Obedience never includes doing something that hurts you or others.
5. Ask God to help you.

### Benefits
Obedient children are trusted, safe and happy. They are also better prepared to lead in the future.

### Summary
• Obedience means doing what those in authority say, when it is good and right.
• The Bible says, "Honour your father and your mother" (Exodus 20:12).
• Obeying teachers and school rules brings order and safety.
• Disobedience leads to trouble, as Adam and Eve and Jonah found out.

## QUIZ
1. Which commandment tells us to honour our father and mother?
   A) Exodus 20:12
   B) Genesis 1:1
   C) John 3:16
   D) Luke 1:37
   Answer: A
   Why: Exodus 20:12 says, "Honour your father and your mother."
2. Who is an example of an obedient child in the Bible lesson?
   A) Jonah
   B) Jesus as a boy in Nazareth
   C) Adam
   D) Balak
   Answer: B
   Why: Luke 2:51 says that Jesus was obedient to Mary and Joseph.
3. Which is a result of disobeying a safety rule?
   A) You may be hurt
   B) You become taller
   C) You win a prize
   D) You get more friends
   Answer: A
   Why: Safety rules protect us from harm.
4. Which action shows obedience at school?
   A) Being late every day
   B) Fighting in class
   C) Wearing uniform and keeping quiet when the teacher teaches
   D) Leaving the compound without permission
   Answer: C
   Why: These follow school rules.
5. What should you do if an adult tells you to do something unsafe or that hurts you?
   A) Do it because they are older
   B) Refuse and tell a trusted adult
   C) Keep it a secret
   D) Do it and forget
   Answer: B
   Why: Obedience is for good and right instructions.
6. State two benefits of obeying parents. (2 marks)
   Answer: It pleases God; brings peace at home; parents trust you; you learn good habits.
   Why: 1 mark for each valid benefit.
7. List three school rules and say why each one is important. (3 marks)
   Answer: Arrive on time, so that lessons are not disturbed; keep silent during teaching, so that all can learn; do not fight, so that everyone is safe.
   Why: 1 mark for each rule with a reason.
8. Outline three results of disobedience. (3 marks)
   Answer: Punishment, danger or harm, broken trust, guilt, poor marks.
   Why: 1 mark for each valid result.
