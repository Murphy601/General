## PAGE 1: Ten sick men
This story is found in Luke 17:11-19. Jesus was on His way to Jerusalem. He travelled along the border between Samaria and Galilee. As He entered a village, ten men who had leprosy met Him.

Leprosy was a serious skin disease. In Bible times people with leprosy were made to live apart from others, because people feared they could pass on the disease. They could not live at home with their families or go to the synagogue. They had to keep a distance and stay away from everyone.

### Words to know
• Leprosy: a disease that affects the skin and nerves. It is also called Hansen's disease. Today it can be cured with medicine.
• Samaritan: a person from the region of Samaria. Many Jews looked down on Samaritans.
• Gratitude: feeling and showing thanks.
• Faith: trusting that God can and will help.

### Think about it
How would you feel if you had to live away from your family because of an illness?

## PAGE 2: The ten men cry for help
The ten men stood at a distance, as they were required to do. They called out in loud voices, "Jesus, Master, have pity on us!" They knew that Jesus could help.

Jesus saw them and said, "Go, show yourselves to the priests." In those days, a priest would examine a person who claimed to be healed and declare them clean. Only then could they go back to their homes and communities.

Notice that Jesus said this before they were healed. The men had to trust His word and start walking. They obeyed, and as they went, they were cleansed. Their skin was made healthy.

### What this shows
• Jesus has the power to heal even serious diseases.
• The men showed faith by obeying His command before they saw any change.
• Jesus cares for those whom society avoids.

## PAGE 3: Only one came back
When one of the ten saw that he was healed, he came back, praising God in a loud voice. He threw himself at Jesus' feet and thanked Him. This man was a Samaritan.

Jesus asked, "Were not all ten cleansed? Where are the other nine? Has no one returned to give praise to God except this foreigner?" Then Jesus said to him, "Rise and go; your faith has made you well."

### Why is this story important?
Nine men received the gift of healing but forgot to say thank you. One man came back. He was a foreigner, yet he was the one who showed gratitude. Jesus noticed and was pleased with his thanks. The story teaches that God notices when people are thankful, and He is sad when they forget.

### Think about it
Why do you think the other nine did not return? What might they have been busy with?

## PAGE 4: Showing gratitude
Gratitude means saying and showing thanks. We should be grateful to God for the gifts He gives us every day.

### Ways to thank God
• Pray each day, thanking Him for life, family, food and health.
• Sing songs of praise.
• Go to church or Sunday school and worship.
• Use the gifts He has given you well.
• Write a thank-you note to God. For example: "Dear God, thank you for my life, my family and my school. Amen."

### Ways to thank people
• Say "Thank you" politely, and look at the person as you say it.
• Write a thank-you card to a teacher or relative.
• Help a person who helped you.
• Do not take kindness for granted.

### Example
Amina's neighbour gave her a lift to school on a rainy day. Amina said thank you, and the next day she carried the neighbour's shopping bag home. This is a good way to show gratitude.

## PAGE 5: Health, faith and gratitude
Leprosy is a communicable disease, which means that it can pass from one person to another, though not easily. Today it is treated with medicine given by health workers, and people who are treated can be cured. In the Bible times, this was not known, but Jesus showed kindness to those with the disease.

### What we can learn about health
• People with a disease should not be rejected or insulted. They should be cared for.
• We should visit clinics and follow the advice of health workers.
• Washing hands with soap, eating well and keeping our environment clean help prevent many illnesses.

### Faith in daily life
We show faith by trusting God in hard times, as the ten men did. We pray, do what is right, and believe that He will help us.

### Summary
• Ten men with leprosy asked Jesus for help, and He healed them as they went to the priests.
• Only one, a Samaritan, came back to thank Jesus.
• We should thank God and people who are kind to us.
• We should care for those who are sick and not reject them.

## QUIZ
1. How many men with leprosy did Jesus heal in this story?
   A) Two
   B) Five
   C) Ten
   D) Twelve
   Answer: C
   Why: Luke 17:12 says that ten men with leprosy met Jesus.
2. What did Jesus tell the ten men to do?
   A) Go and show themselves to the priests
   B) Wash in the river Jordan seven times
   C) Go home and rest
   D) Give money to the poor
   Answer: A
   Why: Luke 17:14 records that He told them to show themselves to the priests.
3. How many of the healed men came back to thank Jesus?
   A) None
   B) One
   C) Five
   D) Nine
   Answer: B
   Why: Only one, a Samaritan, came back.
4. Which best describes gratitude?
   A) Complaining about what you lack
   B) Saying and showing thanks
   C) Forgetting a kind act
   D) Boasting about what you have
   Answer: B
   Why: Gratitude means feeling and showing thanks.
5. How can a child thank God for the gift of life?
   A) By praying, singing and looking after their health
   B) By ignoring their family
   C) By wasting food
   D) By never speaking to anyone
   Answer: A
   Why: Prayer, praise and good use of the gifts God gives show thanks.
6. Why did the ten men have to live apart from other people? (2 marks)
   Answer: They had leprosy, a disease that people feared could spread, so they were kept away from others.
   Why: 1 mark for leprosy or disease and 1 mark for the fear of it spreading.
7. Describe how the ten lepers showed faith in Jesus. (3 marks)
   Answer: They cried out to Jesus for pity; they obeyed His command to go to the priests before they were healed; the one who returned praised God.
   Why: 1 mark for calling out, 1 mark for obeying before healing, 1 mark for the thankfulness of the one.
8. Write a short thank-you note to God for the gift of life. (3 marks)
   Answer: A note that begins by addressing God, gives thanks for life or specific gifts, and ends with a closing.
   Why: 1 mark for addressing God, 1 mark for thanks for life and gifts, 1 mark for a suitable closing.
