## PAGE 1: What is responsibility?
Responsibility means doing the duties that belong to you and taking care of what has been given to you. A responsible person can be trusted to do a job well and to accept the results of their actions.

In the Bible, God gave people responsibility from the beginning. Genesis 2:15 says that God put the first man in the garden of Eden to work it and take care of it. We too have things to take care of, such as our belongings, our school, our home and our environment.

### Words to know
• Responsible: able to be trusted to do what you should do.
• Personal items: things that belong to you, such as your uniform, pens and books.
• School property: things that belong to the school, such as desks, textbooks and the compound.
• Care: to look after something well.

### Think about it
What duties do you have at home and at school? Are you doing them well?

## PAGE 2: Taking care of personal items
Your personal items are things you use every day. Taking good care of them saves money, keeps you tidy and helps you to learn.

### Examples and how to care for them
• Pullover or sweater: fold it neatly, wash it when dirty and write your name inside.
• School uniform: wash it regularly, mend small tears and hang it up.
• Pen and pencils: keep them in a pencil case, sharpen the pencil and do not chew them.
• Eraser (rubber): keep it clean and use it gently.
• Exercise books: cover them, keep the pages clean, do not tear them and write neatly.
• School bag: keep it clean, and do not overload it.
• Shoes: brush off the mud and store them tidily.

### Why we care for personal items
1. Our parents worked hard to buy them.
2. Well-kept items last longer.
3. Clean and neat things show self-respect.
4. Losing items may cost the family money.

### Hygiene
Taking care of our personal items includes keeping ourselves clean. Bathe, brush your teeth, and wash your clothes and hands.

## PAGE 3: Taking care of school property
School property belongs to everyone. If one person spoils it, everybody suffers.

### Examples and how to care for them
• Desks and chairs: do not write on them or kick them.
• Textbooks: cover them, carry them carefully and return them when asked.
• Library books: handle them gently, keep them clean and return them on time.
• Classroom walls and charts: do not draw on them or tear them.
• Toilets and water taps: keep the toilets clean and do not waste water.
• School compound: pick up litter, plant flowers and do not pick or damage plants.
• Sports equipment: return balls and skipping ropes after use.

### Why care for school property?
• It is meant for everyone, including pupils who will come after you.
• Repairing or replacing damaged items costs money, which could be used for other needs.
• A clean and tidy school is a good place to learn.

### Discuss
What would happen if every child wrote on the desks? How would that affect the class?

## PAGE 4: Responsibility and honesty
Luke 16:10 says, "Whoever can be trusted with very little can also be trusted with much." When you are faithful in small duties, you will be trusted with bigger ones later.

### Being responsible when something goes wrong
• If you lose something, look for it and tell the owner or a teacher honestly.
• If you break something, say so, apologise and offer to help fix it.
• If you borrow something, return it in good condition.
• If you find something that belongs to someone else, hand it to the teacher.

### Example
Njeri borrowed a ruler from her friend and broke it. She told her friend, "I am sorry. I broke your ruler. I will buy you a new one." This was responsible and honest.

### Sharing duties
At home, children can share tasks: sweeping, washing plates, fetching water or taking care of younger children. At school, pupils can be class monitors, clean the class or water the school garden. These duties teach us to work together.

## PAGE 5: A poem and a plan
Responsible children make their homes and schools better.

### Making a plan
Write a plan for taking care of your items for one week. For example:
Monday: cover my exercise books.
Tuesday: wash my uniform.
Wednesday: tidy my school bag.
Thursday: help clean the classroom.
Friday: check that I have all my pens and pencils.

### A short poem idea
Compose four lines about taking care of your things, using words such as clean, tidy, care and share.

### Summary
• Responsibility means doing your duties and taking care of what belongs to you and to others.
• We look after personal items, such as books, uniform and pens.
• We take care of school property, such as desks, textbooks and the compound.
• We are honest when something is lost or broken.
• Being faithful in small things prepares us for bigger ones (Luke 16:10).

## QUIZ
1. What does responsibility mean?
   A) Doing your duties and caring for what belongs to you
   B) Leaving jobs for others
   C) Damaging school property
   D) Ignoring rules
   Answer: A
   Why: A responsible person does their duties and cares for things.
2. Which is a good way to care for an exercise book?
   A) Tear pages when bored
   B) Cover it and keep it clean
   C) Sit on it
   D) Leave it in the rain
   Answer: B
   Why: Covering and keeping books clean makes them last longer.
3. What does Luke 16:10 teach?
   A) Whoever can be trusted with little can be trusted with much
   B) Money is everything
   C) Do not work
   D) Take what is not yours
   Answer: A
   Why: Faithfulness in small things leads to bigger responsibilities.
4. A pupil breaks a friend's ruler by accident. What is the responsible action?
   A) Hide the pieces
   B) Blame someone else
   C) Say sorry and offer to replace it
   D) Say nothing
   Answer: C
   Why: Responsible people are honest and try to fix the problem.
5. Which of these is school property?
   A) A pupil's own pencil case
   B) A library book
   C) A pupil's shoes
   D) A pupil's lunch box
   Answer: B
   Why: A library book belongs to the school, not to a pupil.
6. List three personal items and one way of caring for each. (3 marks)
   Answer: Uniform: wash it; exercise book: cover it; pencil: sharpen and keep it in a case.
   Why: 1 mark for each item with a way of caring for it.
7. Give three reasons why we should take care of school property. (3 marks)
   Answer: It belongs to everyone; it costs money to repair or replace; a clean, tidy school helps us to learn; others will use it after us.
   Why: 1 mark for each valid reason.
8. Explain what Genesis 2:15 teaches us about responsibility. (2 marks)
   Answer: God gave people the job of working and taking care of the garden, so caring for what we are given is a duty from God.
   Why: 1 mark for the job of working and caring, 1 mark for a link to our responsibilities.
