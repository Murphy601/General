## PAGE 1: Believers become known as Christians
After the early church began in Jerusalem, many believers were persecuted. Persecution means being treated badly because of what you believe. Some believers left Jerusalem and went to other places, telling people about Jesus as they went.

One of these places was Antioch, a large city in Syria. Acts 11:26 says, "The disciples were called Christians first at Antioch." The word Christian means a follower of Christ. It became the name of all who believe in Jesus.

### Words to know
• Faith: trusting in God and believing in Jesus.
• Persecution: being treated badly for what you believe.
• Christian: a follower of Jesus Christ.
• Martyr: a person who dies because of their faith.

### Think about it
What does it mean to you to be called a Christian?

### Reading the map
The dotted line shows that Antioch lies north of Jerusalem. To travel from Jerusalem to Antioch, you would head north. This is where believers were first called Christians.

## PAGE 3: The story of Stephen
Stephen was one of the first leaders chosen to help in the church in Jerusalem. He was full of faith and the Holy Spirit. He spoke boldly about Jesus, and some people were angry. They brought him before the council and accused him falsely.

Acts 7:54-60 tells what happened. When Stephen spoke, the people were furious. But Stephen, full of the Holy Spirit, looked up to heaven and saw the glory of God and Jesus standing at the right hand of God. The people covered their ears, shouted, dragged him out of the city and stoned him.

### Stephen's last words
• "Lord Jesus, receive my spirit."
• "Lord, do not hold this sin against them."

Stephen then died. He forgave those who were hurting him, just as Jesus had forgiven those who crucified Him. A young man named Saul was there, watching. Later Saul became a follower of Jesus and was called Paul.

## PAGE 4: Lessons from Stephen
Stephen stood firm in his faith even when it cost him his life. Here are three lessons.

### Three lessons
1. Be brave. Stephen did not deny Jesus even when threatened.
2. Forgive. Stephen prayed for those who hurt him.
3. Trust God. Stephen saw heaven open, and he trusted God in his last moments.

### How can we stand firm today?
• Pray every day and read the Bible.
• Say no to wrong even when friends laugh at you.
• Be proud to be a Christian and act like Jesus.
• Join a good church or Bible club.
• Ask God for courage.
• Forgive those who mock you.

### Assertiveness
To be assertive is to say what you believe clearly and politely. You do not need to shout or fight. You can say, "I am a Christian, so I will not do that."

## PAGE 5: Staying away from ungodly groups
Sometimes children are invited to join groups that do wrong. Such groups may be gangs, groups that use drugs, groups that tell you to break the law, or groups that teach you to hate other people or hurt them. Some groups teach that Jesus is not the Saviour, or they pull children away from their families and churches.

### Why we should not join ungodly groups
1. They lead us into wrong actions.
2. They can harm us or others.
3. They may ask us to keep secrets from our parents, which is a warning sign.
4. They can pull us away from God and our families.
5. 1 Corinthians 15:33 warns that bad company spoils good character.

### What to do
• Say no politely and firmly.
• Walk away and do not go back.
• Tell a parent, teacher or pastor about it.
• Choose friends who respect God and others.

### Summary
• Believers were first called Christians in Antioch (Acts 11:26).
• Stephen stood firm in faith and forgave those who stoned him (Acts 7:54-60).
• We should be brave, forgive and trust God.
• We should stay away from ungodly groups.

## QUIZ
1. In which city were believers first called Christians?
   A) Jerusalem
   B) Antioch
   C) Nazareth
   D) Jericho
   Answer: B
   Why: Acts 11:26 says that they were called Christians first at Antioch.
2. On the sketch map, in which direction is Antioch from Jerusalem?
   A) South
   B) East
   C) North
   D) West
   Answer: C
   Why: Antioch is shown at the top of the map, which is north of Jerusalem.
3. What happened to Stephen?
   A) He was stoned because of his faith
   B) He became king
   C) He moved to Antioch
   D) He built the temple
   Answer: A
   Why: Acts 7:54-60 describes how Stephen was stoned.
4. What did Stephen say about those who were stoning him?
   A) Punish them
   B) Lord, do not hold this sin against them
   C) Send fire
   D) They are right
   Answer: B
   Why: Stephen forgave them, as Jesus did.
5. Which action shows standing firm in faith?
   A) Saying no to wrong even when friends laugh
   B) Joining a gang
   C) Hiding your faith
   D) Copying bad behaviour
   Answer: A
   Why: Standing firm means holding to what is right in spite of pressure.
6. State three lessons that we learn from the story of Stephen. (3 marks)
   Answer: Be brave; forgive those who hurt us; trust God.
   Why: 1 mark for each valid lesson.
7. Explain why children should not join ungodly groups. (3 marks)
   Answer: They lead to wrong actions, they can harm us and others, and they can pull us away from God and our families.
   Why: 1 mark for each valid reason.
8. Give two ways in which a child can stand firm in their Christian faith. (2 marks)
   Answer: Pray and read the Bible; say no to wrong; join a good church group.
   Why: 1 mark for each valid way.
