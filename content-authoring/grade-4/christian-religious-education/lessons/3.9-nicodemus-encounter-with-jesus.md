## PAGE 1: Who was Nicodemus?
Nicodemus is found in the Gospel of John. He was a Pharisee, which means he belonged to a group of religious leaders who were careful to keep the Law. He was also a member of the Jewish ruling council, so he was an important and respected man. John 3:1 tells us his story.

Many leaders did not like Jesus, but Nicodemus was curious. He had seen the signs that Jesus performed and wanted to learn more.

### Words to know
• Pharisee: a religious teacher who kept the Law of Moses carefully.
• Born again: to receive new life from God through the Holy Spirit.
• Repentance: being sorry for sins and turning away from them.
• Baptism: the Christian act of being washed with water in the name of the Father, the Son and the Holy Spirit.
• Eternal life: life with God that never ends.

### Think about it
Why do you think Nicodemus wanted to speak to Jesus? Why might he have been careful about it?

## PAGE 2: A visit at night
Nicodemus went to Jesus at night. This may have been because he wanted to talk quietly without other leaders seeing him, or because it was easier to have a long talk when the crowds were gone.

He said, "Rabbi, we know that you are a teacher who has come from God. For no one could perform the signs you are doing if God were not with him."

Jesus answered him with a surprising statement: "Very truly I tell you, no one can see the kingdom of God unless they are born again."

Nicodemus was puzzled. He asked, "How can someone be born when they are old? Surely they cannot enter a second time into their mother's womb to be born!"

### What Jesus meant
Jesus was not talking about being born a second time in the body. He meant a new spiritual birth, a fresh start given by God.

## PAGE 3: Born of water and the Spirit
Jesus explained, "Very truly I tell you, no one can enter the kingdom of God unless they are born of water and the Spirit" (John 3:5). Water reminds us of baptism and being cleansed. The Spirit is the Holy Spirit, who gives new life and helps us follow God.

### Being born again includes
1. Believing in Jesus.
2. Repenting, which means turning away from sin.
3. Being baptised, as Jesus taught.
4. Receiving the Holy Spirit, who helps us to live God's way.

Nicodemus found this hard to understand at first, and Jesus patiently taught him. Jesus then spoke the verse that many Christians know by heart.

### Memory verse: John 3:16
"For God so loved the world that he gave his one and only Son, that whoever believes in him shall not perish but have eternal life."

This verse tells us that God loves everyone, and He gave Jesus so that we can be saved. Learn it and say it aloud with a friend.

## PAGE 4: Baptism and repentance
Baptism is important for Christians. Jesus Himself was baptised by John in the River Jordan (Matthew 3:13-17). Before Jesus went back to heaven, He told His disciples to make disciples of all nations, baptising them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit (Matthew 28:19).

### Why baptism is important
• It shows that a person has decided to follow Jesus.
• It is a sign of being washed from sin.
• It is a way of joining the Christian community, the church.
• It follows the teaching and example of Jesus.

### Repentance draws us near to Christ
Sin separates us from God. When we are sorry and turn away from wrong, God forgives us and we draw near to Him. Nicodemus had to be willing to change and have a new start. So do we.

### Different churches
Churches may baptise at different ages and in different ways. Baptism is important in all of them. Ask your pastor or parents about how your church does it.

## PAGE 5: How Nicodemus changed
The Bible does not tell us everything that Nicodemus did after that night. But it shows us some things that suggest he changed.
• In John 7:50-51, when the other leaders wanted to condemn Jesus without hearing Him, Nicodemus asked, "Does our law condemn a man without first hearing him?"
• In John 19:39-40, after Jesus died, Nicodemus brought a large amount of spices, myrrh and aloes, to prepare Jesus' body for burial. He showed courage and respect.

### What we can learn
• It is good to ask questions and seek the truth.
• God gives a new beginning to everyone who believes.
• Believing in Jesus and repenting brings us closer to Him.
• We can be brave and show respect for Jesus, even when others do not.

### How can I respond to John 3:16?
I can thank God for His love, believe in Jesus, live a good life and tell others about Him.

### Summary
• Nicodemus was a Pharisee who visited Jesus at night.
• Jesus taught that we must be born again, of water and the Spirit.
• John 3:16 says that God loves the world and gave Jesus so that believers have eternal life.
• Repentance and baptism are important for Christians.

## QUIZ
1. Who visited Jesus at night?
   A) Zacchaeus
   B) Nicodemus
   C) Balaam
   D) Joseph
   Answer: B
   Why: John 3:1-2 tells us that Nicodemus came to Jesus by night.
2. What did Jesus say a person must be to see the kingdom of God?
   A) Rich
   B) Old
   C) Born again
   D) A priest
   Answer: C
   Why: Jesus said that no one can see the kingdom of God unless they are born again.
3. Which verse says that God so loved the world that He gave His only Son?
   A) John 3:16
   B) Luke 1:37
   C) Genesis 1:1
   D) Matthew 5:39
   Answer: A
   Why: John 3:16 is the verse about God's love and the gift of His Son.
4. What does baptism show?
   A) A person has decided to follow Jesus
   B) A person is a Pharisee
   C) A person is rich
   D) A person is a king
   Answer: A
   Why: Baptism is a sign of a decision to follow Jesus and to be washed from sin.
5. Which action of Nicodemus shows that he came to respect Jesus?
   A) He brought spices to prepare Jesus' body for burial
   B) He shouted at Jesus
   C) He refused to speak
   D) He ran away
   Answer: A
   Why: John 19:39 says that he brought a large amount of myrrh and aloes.
6. Explain what Jesus meant by being "born again". (2 marks)
   Answer: A new spiritual birth, a fresh start given by God through the Holy Spirit when we believe and repent.
   Why: 1 mark for new spiritual life and 1 mark for the Holy Spirit or repentance.
7. Give three reasons why baptism is important to Christians today. (3 marks)
   Answer: It shows a decision to follow Jesus; it is a sign of being washed from sin; it joins us to the church; it follows Jesus' teaching.
   Why: 1 mark for each valid reason.
8. Write John 3:16 in your own words and say how you can respond to it. (3 marks)
   Answer: God loved the world so much that He gave Jesus, so that everyone who believes in Him will have eternal life. I can respond by believing, thanking God and living a good life.
   Why: 1 mark for the meaning of the verse, 1 mark for eternal life, 1 mark for a response.
