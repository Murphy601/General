## PAGE 1: Why do we pray?
Prayer is talking to God. We can thank Him, praise Him, say sorry for our sins and ask for help for ourselves and others. You do not need special words or a special place. God hears us wherever we are, at home, at school, on the way or in church.

Jesus prayed often. Luke 11:1 tells us that one of His disciples asked Him, "Lord, teach us to pray." In reply, Jesus gave them a prayer that we call the Lord's Prayer or the "Our Father". It is found in Matthew 6:9-13.

### Words to know
• Prayer: talking to God.
• Hallowed: holy, honoured.
• Daily bread: the food and other things we need each day.
• Temptation: something that pulls us to do wrong.

### Think about it
When do you pray? What do you say to God?

## PAGE 2: The words of the prayer
Matthew 6:9-13 says, "This, then, is how you should pray:
Our Father in heaven,
hallowed be your name,
your kingdom come,
your will be done,
on earth as it is in heaven.
Give us today our daily bread.
And forgive us our debts,
as we also have forgiven our debtors.
And lead us not into temptation,
but deliver us from the evil one."

### A note about wording
Churches use slightly different words, such as "trespasses" instead of "debts" and "who art in heaven" instead of "in heaven". The meaning is the same. Use the words that your church uses, and learn them by heart. Many churches also end the prayer with words of praise, saying that the kingdom, the power and the glory belong to God for ever.

### Practise
Say the prayer slowly with a partner, thinking about each line.

## PAGE 3: What each part means
1. "Our Father in heaven" reminds us that God is our loving Father and that He cares for all of us. We say "our", not "my", because we pray together with others.
2. "Hallowed be your name" means that we honour God's name as holy.
3. "Your kingdom come, your will be done" means that we ask that God's rule and His plans be carried out on earth as they are in heaven.
4. "Give us today our daily bread" means that we ask God for the food and things we need each day, and we thank Him for what we have.
5. "Forgive us our debts, as we also forgive our debtors" means that we ask God to forgive our sins, and we promise to forgive others.
6. "Lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from the evil one" means that we ask God to keep us from doing wrong and to protect us from evil.

## PAGE 4: Praying for others and praying always
Jesus taught us to pray not only for ourselves but for others. The Lord's Prayer uses "us" and "our", which shows that we should think of others when we pray.

### People we can pray for
• Parents and guardians.
• Brothers and sisters.
• Friends and classmates.
• Teachers.
• Sick people.
• Leaders of the country.
• People who are hungry or in trouble.

### How to pray
1. Find a quiet moment.
2. Speak to God with respect. Bow your head or close your eyes if you like.
3. Thank Him for good things.
4. Say sorry for what you did wrong.
5. Ask for help for yourself and others.
6. End with "Amen", which means, "Let it be so."

### Praying every day
Christians pray every day: in the morning, before meals, before going to bed and whenever they need help. Regular prayer helps us to know God better and grow spiritually. Jesus also warned us not to pray just to be seen by others, but to pray sincerely (Matthew 6:5-6).

## PAGE 5: Making prayer a habit
Prayer is like a friendship. We talk to God often so that we get to know Him better.

### Ideas for making prayer a habit
• Pray when you wake up and before sleeping.
• Say a thank-you prayer before meals.
• Keep a prayer list of people you want to pray for.
• Recite the Lord's Prayer daily.
• Join in prayer at school and church.
• Write down how often you pray each week, and share with classmates to encourage each other.

### Example of a short prayer for parents
"Dear God, thank you for my parents. Please give them health, strength and joy. Help me to obey them. Amen."

### Summary
• Prayer is talking to God, and Jesus taught us the Lord's Prayer (Matthew 6:9-13).
• The prayer teaches us to honour God, ask for daily needs, forgive and ask for protection.
• We pray for our parents, guardians and friends.
• We should pray every day and grow spiritually.

## QUIZ
1. Where do we find the Lord's Prayer?
   A) Matthew 6:9-13
   B) Genesis 1:1
   C) Acts 2:38
   D) John 3:16
   Answer: A
   Why: The Lord's Prayer is in Matthew 6:9-13.
2. Who taught the disciples the Lord's Prayer?
   A) Peter
   B) Jesus
   C) Moses
   D) John the Baptist
   Answer: B
   Why: When the disciples asked how to pray, Jesus gave them this prayer.
3. What does "daily bread" mean?
   A) Only bread from a shop
   B) The food and things we need each day
   C) A special holiday
   D) Wealth
   Answer: B
   Why: We ask God for the things we need every day, not only bread.
4. What does the word "Amen" mean?
   A) Let it be so
   B) Goodbye
   C) Thank you for the food
   D) I am tired
   Answer: A
   Why: Amen means "let it be so" or "I agree".
5. Which part of the Lord's Prayer asks God to help us not to do wrong?
   A) Hallowed be your name
   B) Give us today our daily bread
   C) Lead us not into temptation
   D) Your kingdom come
   Answer: C
   Why: This line asks God to keep us away from temptation.
6. Write the first two lines of the Lord's Prayer. (2 marks)
   Answer: Our Father in heaven, hallowed be your name. (Wording of "who art in heaven" is also accepted.)
   Why: 1 mark for each line.
7. Give three reasons why it is important to pray every day. (3 marks)
   Answer: It helps us to know God better; we can thank Him and ask for help; it helps us to grow spiritually and to avoid wrong.
   Why: 1 mark for each valid reason.
8. Write a short prayer for your parents or guardians. (3 marks)
   Answer: A prayer that addresses God, asks for a blessing for parents or guardians, and ends with Amen.
   Why: 1 mark for addressing God, 1 mark for the request for parents, 1 mark for the ending.
