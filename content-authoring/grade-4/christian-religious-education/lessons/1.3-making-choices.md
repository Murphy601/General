## PAGE 1: Choices we make every day
A choice is a decision to do one thing instead of another. You make many choices each day, often without noticing. Should you wake up quickly or stay in bed? Should you greet the neighbour or walk past? Should you do your homework first or play first? Should you tell the truth or hide it?

God made human beings able to choose. Animals follow their instincts, but people can think about what is right and decide. This is a great gift, and it also brings responsibility.

### Choices in daily life
• At home: doing your chores, sharing food, obeying your parents.
• At school: paying attention, being kind to classmates, keeping the rules.
• On the road: walking to school safely, not accepting lifts from strangers.
• With money: saving a little, spending on what you need, not stealing.
• With friends: joining good games, saying no to bad behaviour.

### Try it
In pairs, list five choices you made yesterday. Beside each one, write whether it was a good or a bad choice.

## PAGE 2: Results of our choices
Every choice has a result, which we call a consequence. Good choices bring good results. Bad choices bring bad results, for you and for other people.

### Examples
• Choice: Revising your work before a test. Result: You feel confident and may do well.
• Choice: Telling the truth even when you broke a cup. Result: People trust you, and you feel free inside.
• Choice: Fighting with a classmate. Result: You may get hurt, be punished, and lose a friend.
• Choice: Taking a sweet from a shop without paying. Result: It is stealing. You may be caught and feel guilty, and it hurts the shop owner.
• Choice: Sharing your lunch. Result: You make a friend happy and you feel joy.

### Think about others
Some choices affect only you, but many affect your family, your school and your friends. A child who litters makes the whole compound dirty. A child who helps keeps the compound clean for everyone.

### Discuss
Why should we think before we choose? Talk with a partner about a time when you did not think and the result was bad.

## PAGE 3: Choosing right with God's word
How do we know what is right? Christians look to God's word, the Bible. Psalm 119:105 says, "Your word is a lamp for my feet, a light on my path." A lamp helps you see where to step in the dark. The Bible helps us see which choices are safe and good.

### Steps for making a right choice
1. Stop and think. Do not rush.
2. Ask, "Does this agree with what God teaches?"
3. Ask, "Who will be hurt or helped?"
4. Ask a trusted adult if you are not sure.
5. Pray for wisdom and courage.
6. Choose the right thing, even if others laugh.

### Some things the Bible teaches
• Honour your father and mother (Exodus 20:12).
• Speak the truth (Ephesians 4:25).
• Help those in need (Matthew 5:42).
• Do not repay evil with evil (Matthew 5:39).

When your choice matches these teachings, you can be sure you are on the right path.

## PAGE 4: Examples from the Bible: Daniel and Joshua
The Bible shows people who made hard choices. Daniel was a young man taken away from his home to live in Babylon. The king wanted the young men to eat the royal food and drink the royal wine. Daniel decided not to defile himself with that food (Daniel 1:8). He asked politely for permission to eat differently, and God blessed him. Daniel chose to stay faithful to God even when he was far from home and nobody could see him.

Joshua told the people of Israel to choose whom they would serve. He said, "As for me and my household, we will serve the Lord" (Joshua 24:15). He made his choice clear and led his family by example.

### What we learn
• A right choice may not be popular, but God is pleased with it.
• Being polite and respectful helps us stand for what is right.
• Our choices can lead others to follow us.

### Ask yourself
What would Daniel do if a friend said, "Come, let us take fruits from the neighbour's tree without asking"?

## PAGE 5: Making right choices in practice
Making right choices takes practice. Here are some situations. Think about the best choice in each.

### Situation A
You find a Ksh 50 coin on the school field. Choice: keep it quietly, or give it to the teacher to find the owner. The honest choice is to hand it in.

### Situation B
Friends invite you to skip class and play. Choice: join them or say no. The right choice is to say no politely and go to class.

### Situation C
Your younger brother is crying because he lost his toy. Choice: laugh at him or help look for it. The loving choice is to help.

### Choosing and being responsible
When you choose well, you show responsibility. When you make a wrong choice, be brave: admit it, say sorry, and try to put things right. God forgives those who are sorry.

### Summary
• Everyone makes choices daily, and choices have results.
• God's word guides us to right choices.
• Daniel and Joshua chose to follow God.
• Stop, think, pray, and then choose.

## QUIZ
1. What is a choice?
   A) A decision to do one thing instead of another
   B) Something that happens by accident
   C) A kind of punishment
   D) A school rule
   Answer: A
   Why: A choice means deciding between different actions.
2. Which choice is likely to bring a good result?
   A) Copying a friend's answers
   B) Revising before a test
   C) Skipping class
   D) Hiding the truth
   Answer: B
   Why: Preparing well helps you learn and do well in a test. The other choices lead to trouble.
3. Which verse says that God's word is a lamp for our feet?
   A) Psalm 119:105
   B) Genesis 1:1
   C) Luke 15:8
   D) Acts 2:38
   Answer: A
   Why: Psalm 119:105 says, "Your word is a lamp for my feet, a light on my path."
4. Why did Daniel refuse to eat the royal food?
   A) He was not hungry
   B) He wished to be honest and faithful to God
   C) The king ordered him to fast
   D) He disliked the cooks
   Answer: B
   Why: Daniel 1:8 says that he decided not to defile himself, so he stayed faithful to God.
5. You find money on the school field. What is the right choice?
   A) Keep it quietly
   B) Buy sweets and share them
   C) Give it to the teacher to find the owner
   D) Hide it in your bag until Friday
   Answer: C
   Why: Honesty means returning what is not ours.
6. List three choices you make in daily life at school. (3 marks)
   Answer: Examples: to pay attention or not, to obey the rules or break them, to share or be selfish, to be kind or unkind to classmates.
   Why: 1 mark for each valid choice given.
7. Explain one good result and one bad result of choices that children make. (2 marks)
   Answer: A good result: telling the truth makes people trust you. A bad result: fighting can lead to injury and punishment.
   Why: 1 mark for a suitable good result and 1 mark for a suitable bad result.
8. Describe three steps you can follow to make a right choice. (3 marks)
   Answer: Stop and think; ask whether it agrees with God's word; ask a trusted adult or pray; then choose the right thing.
   Why: 1 mark for each correct step, up to 3.
