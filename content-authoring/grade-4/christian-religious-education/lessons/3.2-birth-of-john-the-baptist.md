## PAGE 1: Zechariah and Elizabeth
Luke 1:5-25 tells about a priest named Zechariah and his wife Elizabeth. The Bible says that both of them were righteous in God's sight. They obeyed God's commandments faithfully. But they had no children, and both of them were very old. In those days, many people thought that a couple without children was not blessed. Zechariah and Elizabeth had probably prayed for a child for many years.

### Words to know
• Priest: a man who served God in the temple.
• Temple: the holy house of God in Jerusalem, where people worshipped.
• Incense: a sweet-smelling smoke burned as an offering to God in the temple.
• Righteous: living in a way that pleases God.

### Think about it
Have you ever prayed for something for a long time? How did you feel while you waited?

## PAGE 2: The angel appears in the temple
One day it was Zechariah's turn to serve as priest in the temple. He was chosen to enter the sanctuary of the Lord to burn incense. A large crowd was praying outside.

Suddenly, an angel of the Lord appeared, standing at the right side of the altar of incense. Zechariah was startled and gripped with fear. The angel said, "Do not be afraid, Zechariah; your prayer has been heard. Your wife Elizabeth will bear you a son, and you are to call him John."

### The angel's message about John
• He will bring you joy and delight, and many will rejoice at his birth.
• He will be great in the sight of the Lord.
• He must never take wine or other strong drink.
• He will be filled with the Holy Spirit even before he is born.
• He will bring many people of Israel back to the Lord their God.
• He will go before the Lord to prepare the people for Him.

### Role play
Act out the angel's appearance to Zechariah. Show Zechariah's fear and the angel's calm voice.

## PAGE 3: Zechariah doubts
Zechariah asked the angel, "How can I be sure of this? I am an old man, and my wife is well along in years." The angel answered, "I am Gabriel. I stand in the presence of God, and I have been sent to speak to you and to tell you this good news. And now you will be silent and unable to speak until the day this happens, because you did not believe my words, which will come true at their appointed time."

Outside, the people waited and wondered why Zechariah was taking so long. When he came out, he could not speak. He made signs to them, and they realised that he had seen a vision in the temple.

### What we learn
Even a good man like Zechariah found it hard to believe. But God's word came true. When we find God's promises hard to believe, we should ask Him for more faith.

### After the temple
When his days of service were over, Zechariah went home. Soon after, his wife Elizabeth became pregnant. She said, "The Lord has done this for me. In these days he has shown his favour and taken away my disgrace among the people."

## PAGE 4: The birth of John
When the time came, Elizabeth gave birth to a son. Her neighbours and relatives heard that the Lord had shown her great mercy, and they shared her joy (Luke 1:57-58).

On the eighth day, they came to name the child. They wanted to call him Zechariah, after his father. But Elizabeth said, "No! He is to be called John." The people said that nobody in the family had that name, and they asked Zechariah by signs. He asked for a writing tablet and wrote, "His name is John." Immediately his mouth was opened, his tongue was set free, and he began to speak, praising God.

The people were amazed, and all these things were talked about through the hill country of Judea. They asked, "What then is this child going to be?" For the Lord's hand was with him.

### Note
John was obedient to the name that God chose. Zechariah also showed obedience. When he obeyed God's instruction on the name, his speech returned.

## PAGE 5: Preparing the way for Jesus
Zechariah was filled with the Holy Spirit and praised God. He spoke about his son John. Luke 1:76-79 says, "And you, my child, will be called a prophet of the Most High; for you will go on before the Lord to prepare the way for him."

A prophet is someone who speaks God's message. John grew up and preached in the wilderness near the River Jordan. He called people to repent, or turn away from their sins, and he baptised them in the river. This prepared people's hearts to receive Jesus, who was coming after him. That is why we call John the Baptist the forerunner of Jesus, meaning the one who goes ahead to prepare the way.

### How can we live righteously?
• Turn away from wrongdoing and say sorry to God.
• Be honest and kind.
• Pray and read the Bible.
• Tell others about Jesus with your good example.

### Summary
• Zechariah and Elizabeth were old and had no child, but God answered their prayer.
• The angel Gabriel said their son should be called John.
• Zechariah could not speak until the baby was named.
• John prepared the way for Jesus by calling people to repent.

## QUIZ
1. What was Zechariah's job?
   A) Fisherman
   B) Priest
   C) Soldier
   D) Tax collector
   Answer: B
   Why: Luke 1:5 says that Zechariah was a priest.
2. Who was the mother of John the Baptist?
   A) Mary
   B) Sarah
   C) Elizabeth
   D) Anna
   Answer: C
   Why: Elizabeth was Zechariah's wife and John's mother.
3. Why could Zechariah not speak after the angel's visit?
   A) He was tired
   B) He did not believe the angel's words
   C) He had a cold
   D) He was afraid of the people
   Answer: B
   Why: The angel said he would be silent because he did not believe the message.
4. What name did Elizabeth and Zechariah give the baby?
   A) Zechariah
   B) Jesus
   C) John
   D) Gabriel
   Answer: C
   Why: The angel said to call him John, and Zechariah wrote, "His name is John."
5. What was John's work?
   A) To build the temple
   B) To prepare the way for the Lord
   C) To lead an army
   D) To rule Israel
   Answer: B
   Why: Luke 1:76 says that John would go before the Lord to prepare the way for Him.
6. Explain what it means to say that John was the forerunner of Jesus. (2 marks)
   Answer: John went ahead of Jesus and called people to repent so they were ready to receive Him.
   Why: 1 mark for going before Jesus, 1 mark for preparing people through repentance.
7. Describe how Zechariah got his speech back. (3 marks)
   Answer: When the baby was born, the people wanted to name him after his father. Elizabeth said his name was John, and Zechariah wrote on a tablet, "His name is John." Then his mouth was opened and he began to praise God.
   Why: 1 mark for the naming, 1 mark for writing John, 1 mark for speech returning and praising God.
8. Give three ways in which a child can turn away from sin and live righteously. (3 marks)
   Answer: Say sorry to God, be honest, be kind, read the Bible and pray, obey parents.
   Why: 1 mark for each valid way.
