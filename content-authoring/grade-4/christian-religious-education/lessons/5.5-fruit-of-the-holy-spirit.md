## PAGE 1: Who is the Holy Spirit?
The Holy Spirit is God. Christians believe in one God who is Father, Son and Holy Spirit. Jesus promised that the Holy Spirit would come to help, teach and guide His followers. The Holy Spirit came on the disciples at Pentecost. He lives in those who believe in Jesus and helps them to do what is right.

Think of a mango tree. When a tree is healthy, it grows sweet fruits, and we know the kind of tree by its fruit. In the same way, when a person allows the Holy Spirit to guide them, good qualities grow in that person's life. These qualities are called the fruit of the Holy Spirit.

### Words to know
• Fruit of the Spirit: the good qualities that the Holy Spirit grows in believers.
• Patience: waiting calmly without getting angry.
• Gentleness: being kind and careful with others.
• Faithfulness: being loyal and dependable.

### Think about it
How can you tell that a tree is healthy? How can you tell that a person follows God?

## PAGE 2: The nine fruits
Galatians 5:22-23 says, "But the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, forbearance, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness and self-control. Against such things there is no law."

### How to read the tree
Each fruit hangs on the same tree, which stands for a life that is guided by the Holy Spirit. A person does not choose only the easy fruits. The Spirit wants to grow all nine, so that our love, joy, peace and the rest can be seen at home, at school and in church. Read the fruits aloud in order and try to say one example of each from your own life.

### Note
Some Bibles use "patience" instead of "forbearance". Both mean staying calm and putting up with others. Some use "faith" or "faithfulness".

## PAGE 3: What the fruits mean
1. Love: caring for others and wanting the best for them.
2. Joy: deep gladness that comes from knowing God.
3. Peace: calm in the heart, and living in harmony with others.
4. Patience: waiting calmly and not losing your temper.
5. Kindness: being gentle and helpful.
6. Goodness: doing what is right and good.
7. Faithfulness: being loyal and keeping promises.
8. Gentleness: being humble and careful with people's feelings.
9. Self-control: controlling what we say, do and want.

### One fruit, many parts
Notice that the Bible says "fruit", not "fruits". The nine qualities belong together, like parts of one fruit. A person led by the Spirit grows in all of them, step by step.

### Activity
Write each fruit on a flash card. Display the cards in the classroom. Choose one fruit that you want to work on this week.

## PAGE 4: Using the fruit in daily life
The fruit of the Spirit shows in our behaviour. Here are examples for a child.

### Examples of the fruit in action
• Love: you share your lunch with a friend who forgot theirs.
• Joy: you thank God for the day and smile at others.
• Peace: you stop an argument and help friends make up.
• Patience: you wait your turn in the queue at the water tap.
• Kindness: you help your younger brother with his homework.
• Goodness: you return the extra change to the shopkeeper.
• Faithfulness: you keep your promise to help a classmate.
• Gentleness: you speak softly to a crying child.
• Self-control: you walk away instead of hitting back.

### Discuss
In groups, list good things that you should do to others as Christians. Match each one with a fruit of the Spirit.

## PAGE 5: Being led by the Spirit
Why should we have the fruit of the Spirit? Because it pleases God, helps us to live well with others and makes us more like Jesus.

### How to grow the fruit
1. Pray and ask the Holy Spirit to help you.
2. Read the Bible to learn what God wants.
3. Practise one fruit at a time.
4. Ask a friend or parent to encourage you.
5. When you fail, say sorry and start again.

### Being led by the Spirit in daily activities
Before you act, ask, "Would this show love? Would it be kind and good? Would it bring peace?" This helps us to make decisions the way God wants.

### Song idea
Compose a song naming the nine fruits. Make it rhyme and add clapping.

### Summary
• The Holy Spirit helps believers to do good.
• The fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness and self-control (Galatians 5:22-23).
• We can practise these in daily life.
• We grow the fruit through prayer, Bible reading and practice.

## QUIZ
1. Which Bible verses list the fruit of the Spirit?
   A) Galatians 5:22-23
   B) Genesis 1:1
   C) John 3:16
   D) Luke 1:37
   Answer: A
   Why: Galatians 5:22-23 gives the list.
2. How many fruits of the Spirit are listed?
   A) Five
   B) Seven
   C) Nine
   D) Twelve
   Answer: C
   Why: There are nine fruits.
3. Which of these is a fruit of the Spirit?
   A) Anger
   B) Self-control
   C) Jealousy
   D) Greed
   Answer: B
   Why: Self-control is listed in Galatians 5:23.
4. A child helps two friends who were quarrelling to make peace. Which fruit is shown?
   A) Peace
   B) Greed
   C) Pride
   D) Laziness
   Answer: A
   Why: Making peace shows the fruit of peace.
5. What does patience mean?
   A) Waiting calmly without losing your temper
   B) Always rushing
   C) Being angry quickly
   D) Refusing to wait
   Answer: A
   Why: Patience is calm waiting.
6. List any five fruits of the Holy Spirit. (3 marks)
   Answer: Love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, self-control. Any five.
   Why: 3 marks for five correct, 2 marks for three or four, 1 mark for one or two.
7. Describe how a child can show kindness and self-control at school. (3 marks)
   Answer: Kindness: help a classmate or share; self-control: walk away instead of fighting, or wait for your turn.
   Why: 1 mark for an example of kindness, 1 mark for an example of self-control, 1 mark for clear explanation.
8. Explain why Christians should show the fruit of the Holy Spirit. (2 marks)
   Answer: It pleases God and makes us more like Jesus; it helps us to live peacefully with others.
   Why: 1 mark for each valid reason.
