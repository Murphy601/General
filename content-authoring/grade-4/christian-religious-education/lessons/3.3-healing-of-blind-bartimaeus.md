## PAGE 1: A blind man by the road
Jesus did many miracles. A miracle is something wonderful that only God's power can do. One of them is the healing of a blind man named Bartimaeus. It is told in Mark 10:46-52.

Bartimaeus was the son of Timaeus, and his name means "son of Timaeus". He lived near the city of Jericho. He was blind, so he could not go to school or work in the usual way. He spent his days sitting by the roadside begging. People who passed by gave him coins or food.

### Words to know
• Miracle: an amazing act that shows God's power.
• Beggar: a person who asks others for money or food because they cannot work.
• Faith: trusting God completely.
• Mercy: kindness shown to someone who is suffering.

### Think about it
What would be hard about life if you could not see? How can we help people who are blind?

## PAGE 2: Bartimaeus calls out
One day, Jesus was leaving Jericho with His disciples and a large crowd. Bartimaeus heard the noise of the crowd and asked what was happening. Someone told him that Jesus of Nazareth was passing by.

Bartimaeus had heard about Jesus. He shouted, "Jesus, Son of David, have mercy on me!" Many people told him sternly to be quiet. But he shouted all the louder, "Son of David, have mercy on me!"

### Why is this important?
• Bartimaeus believed Jesus could help him.
• He did not give up when people told him to keep quiet.
• He asked for mercy, meaning he asked for kind help.
• Calling Jesus "Son of David" showed that he believed Jesus was the promised King, the Messiah.

### Discuss
What would you have done if people told you to be quiet? Why did Bartimaeus keep calling?

## PAGE 3: Jesus calls him
Jesus stopped and said, "Call him." The people called to the blind man, "Cheer up! On your feet! He is calling you."

Bartimaeus threw his cloak aside, jumped to his feet and came to Jesus. The cloak was probably his only warm covering, but he left it behind. This shows how much he wanted to reach Jesus quickly.

Jesus asked him, "What do you want me to do for you?" The blind man said, "Rabbi, I want to see."

"Go," said Jesus, "your faith has healed you." Immediately he received his sight and followed Jesus along the road (Mark 10:52).

### What we see in the story
1. Jesus stops for one person, even in a large crowd.
2. Jesus asks what Bartimaeus wants, though He already knows.
3. Jesus heals him because of his faith.
4. Bartimaeus follows Jesus and praises God.

### Role play
In groups, act out the story with roles for Bartimaeus, Jesus, a disciple and the crowd. Some can be narrators.

## PAGE 4: Faith and the healing power of Jesus
Bartimaeus showed faith in several ways.
• He called out to Jesus in public, even though people told him to stop.
• He believed that Jesus could heal him.
• He came at once when Jesus called.
• He asked clearly for what he needed: "I want to see."
• After the healing, he followed Jesus.

### Jesus has power over sickness
The healing shows that Jesus has power over sickness and disease. He cared for people who were suffering. He did not ignore Bartimaeus because he was poor and blind.

Christians believe that Jesus still cares for the sick today. We can pray for those who are ill, and God can heal in different ways, sometimes through doctors and medicine, sometimes in other ways. When we are sick, we should pray and also go to a clinic or hospital for treatment.

### Share
Tell a partner about a time when you or someone in your family was sick and you prayed. How did you feel?

## PAGE 5: Caring for people who cannot see
The story teaches us to care for people with disabilities. Some Kenyans are blind or have poor eyesight. They need our help and respect.

### How to help someone who is blind
• Greet them by name so that they know who you are.
• Offer your arm and walk slowly if they need guidance.
• Do not move things in their home or classroom without telling them.
• Do not tease or laugh at them.
• Include them in games and lessons.

### Caring for our own eyes
• Do not rub your eyes with dirty hands.
• Do not read in very dim light.
• Go to the clinic if your eyes hurt or you cannot see the board clearly.
• Eat foods such as vegetables and fruits that keep the body healthy.

### Summary
• Bartimaeus was a blind beggar near Jericho who cried out to Jesus for mercy.
• Jesus called him and healed him because of his faith.
• Jesus has power over sickness, and He cares about people who suffer.
• We should have faith, pray and care for people with disabilities.

## QUIZ
1. Where was Bartimaeus sitting when Jesus passed by?
   A) At the market
   B) By the roadside near Jericho
   C) In the temple
   D) On a mountain
   Answer: B
   Why: Mark 10:46 says he was sitting by the roadside as Jesus left Jericho.
2. What did Bartimaeus shout as Jesus passed?
   A) "Jesus, Son of David, have mercy on me!"
   B) "Give me money!"
   C) "Leave me alone!"
   D) "Heal my friend!"
   Answer: A
   Why: He called for mercy, showing he believed in Jesus.
3. What did Bartimaeus ask Jesus for?
   A) Food
   B) Gold
   C) To see
   D) A house
   Answer: C
   Why: He said, "Rabbi, I want to see."
4. What did Jesus say made Bartimaeus well?
   A) His money
   B) His faith
   C) His cloak
   D) His shouting
   Answer: B
   Why: Jesus said, "Your faith has healed you."
5. Which is a kind way to help a blind classmate?
   A) Move their things without telling them
   B) Greet them by name and offer to guide them
   C) Laugh at them
   D) Leave them out of games
   Answer: B
   Why: Greeting and guiding kindly shows respect and care.
6. Give two ways in which Bartimaeus showed faith in Jesus. (2 marks)
   Answer: He called out to Jesus; he kept calling when people told him to be quiet; he came quickly when called; he asked for sight; he followed Jesus.
   Why: 1 mark for each valid way.
7. What does the story teach us about the power of Jesus? (3 marks)
   Answer: Jesus has power over sickness and disease. He cares for people who suffer, even the poor. He can help us when we have faith.
   Why: 1 mark for power over sickness, 1 mark for His care for the poor and suffering, 1 mark for faith.
8. Describe two ways of caring for our eyes and two ways of helping people who cannot see. (4 marks)
   Answer: Caring for eyes: keep hands clean, do not read in dim light, go to the clinic. Helping others: greet them by name, guide them, include them, do not tease. Any two of each.
   Why: 1 mark for each valid point, up to four.
