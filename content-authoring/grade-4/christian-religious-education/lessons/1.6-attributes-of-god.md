## PAGE 1: What are attributes?
An attribute is a quality that describes someone. When we say that Wanjiku is kind, honest and hardworking, we are naming her attributes. In the same way, the Bible tells us about the attributes of God, which help us to know what God is like.

Why should we know God's attributes? Because when we know what God is like, we can trust Him, pray to Him, thank Him and try to become more like Him. You will learn three main attributes in this lesson.

### The three attributes
• God is the sole Creator.
• God is a loving Father.
• God is holy.

### Sole means only
When we say God is the sole Creator, we mean He is the only Creator. Nobody helped Him make the world, and nobody made Him.

### Picture-reading
When you read a Bible text with a partner, look for the words that describe God. Underline them in your notebook.

## PAGE 2: God the sole Creator
The first chapter of the Bible tells us that God made the world. Genesis 1:1 says, "In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth." In Genesis 1 we read that God made light, sky, land, seas, plants, the sun, the moon and the stars, sea creatures, birds, land animals, and finally human beings.

### What creation teaches us
• God is powerful. He made everything by His word.
• God is wise. Everything He made works together, like day and night, and seasons.
• God is good. Genesis 1 says over and over that what He made was good.

### Our response
Because God is the only Creator, we should worship only Him. We should also look after His creation. Use water carefully, plant trees, do not waste food and be kind to animals.

### Activity
Make a list of ten things in your home area that God created. Tell your partner which one you like best and why.

## PAGE 3: God the loving Father
The Bible says that God loves us. 1 John 4:8 says, "God is love." A loving father feeds, protects, guides and forgives his children. God does all these things and more. He cares for us even when we forget to thank Him.

### How God shows love
• He gives us life, air, water and food.
• He gives us families and friends.
• He forgives us when we are truly sorry.
• He sent Jesus Christ to save us (John 3:16).

### God knows you by name
Isaiah 43:1 says, "I have called you by name; you are mine." This means God knows each child personally. He knows your name, your worries and your needs. You are never alone or forgotten.

### Showing love back
When we know that God loves us, we can love Him and love others. 1 John 4:7 says, "Dear friends, let us love one another, for love comes from God." We show God's love when we are kind, forgiving and helpful.

### Poem idea
Write a four-line poem called "God our loving Father". Use words like care, love, name and forgive.

## PAGE 4: God the holy Father
Holy means completely pure, perfect and set apart. God never does wrong. There is nobody like Him. 1 Samuel 2:2 says, "There is no one holy like the Lord; there is no one besides you; there is no Rock like our God."

### What holiness means for us
• God hates sin, but He loves the sinner and forgives those who repent.
• We must respect and honour God. We call this reverence.
• We should try to be holy, too, by choosing good things, telling the truth and avoiding evil.

### How we show reverence
1. We pray with respect and speak of God with honour.
2. We handle the Bible with care.
3. We behave well in church.
4. We obey His commandments.

### A word about "revere"
To revere God means to respect Him deeply and to be in awe of His greatness. It does not mean being afraid of Him in a way that makes us run away. It means we love Him and want to please Him.

## PAGE 5: Putting it together
Now you can list three attributes of God: sole Creator, loving Father and holy Father. There are others in the Bible, but these three are enough to start with. They show that God is great, good and pure.

### How the attributes help in daily life
• When you see a beautiful sunrise, remember God the Creator, and say thank you.
• When you feel lonely or scared, remember God the loving Father, who knows your name.
• When you are tempted to do wrong, remember God the holy Father, and choose what is right.

### Matching game
Match each attribute with its meaning:
Sole Creator: God alone made everything.
Loving Father: God cares for us and knows us by name.
Holy Father: God is pure and perfect and never does wrong.

### Summary
• Attributes are qualities that describe a person, and the Bible tells us about God's attributes.
• God is the sole Creator (Genesis 1:1), a loving Father (1 John 4:8) and holy (1 Samuel 2:2).
• Knowing God's attributes helps us to trust, thank and revere Him.
• God knows each of us by name (Isaiah 43:1).

## QUIZ
1. What does "sole Creator" mean?
   A) God is the only one who made everything
   B) God made only some things
   C) God had many helpers
   D) God was made by someone
   Answer: A
   Why: Sole means only. God alone created all things.
2. Which verse says that God is love?
   A) 1 John 4:8
   B) Exodus 20:12
   C) Luke 19:5
   D) Acts 2:38
   Answer: A
   Why: 1 John 4:8 says, "God is love."
3. Which verse from the lesson says that there is no one holy like the Lord?
   A) Genesis 1:1
   B) 1 Samuel 2:2
   C) Matthew 5:39
   D) Psalm 125:1
   Answer: B
   Why: 1 Samuel 2:2 says, "There is no one holy like the Lord."
4. What does it mean to say that God knows you by name?
   A) He knows only famous people
   B) He has forgotten you
   C) He knows each person personally and cares about them
   D) He only knows adults
   Answer: C
   Why: Isaiah 43:1 shows that God calls each person by name.
5. Which action shows reverence for God?
   A) Talking loudly in church
   B) Handling the Bible with care and behaving well in worship
   C) Laughing at prayers
   D) Throwing the Bible on the floor
   Answer: B
   Why: Reverence means honouring God, which includes respecting His word and His house.
6. List three attributes of God. (3 marks)
   Answer: God is the sole Creator, a loving Father and holy.
   Why: 1 mark for each attribute.
7. Explain how the attribute "loving Father" can comfort a child who is scared. (3 marks)
   Answer: God loves and cares for each child and knows them by name. He is always with them, so they are not alone. They can pray and ask Him for help.
   Why: 1 mark for God's love and care, 1 mark for knowing us by name or being with us, 1 mark for prayer or help.
8. Give two ways in which we can show respect for God as Creator. (2 marks)
   Answer: Look after the environment by planting trees, saving water or being kind to animals; thank God in prayer; worship only Him.
   Why: 1 mark for each valid answer.
