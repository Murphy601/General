## PAGE 1: The Bible is God's word
The Holy Bible is the special book of Christians. It is not just one book. It is a library of 66 books written by many different people over a very long time. Christians believe that God inspired these writers. Inspired means that God guided the writers so that what they wrote is His message to people. 2 Timothy 3:16 says that all Scripture is inspired by God and is useful for teaching us what is right.

The Bible tells us who God is, how He made the world, how Jesus came to save us, and how we should live. That is why we call it the Word of God.

### Respecting the Bible
Because it is God's word, we handle the Bible with respect.
• Wash and dry your hands before you hold it.
• Carry it carefully. Do not throw it or drop it.
• Keep it in a clean, safe and high place, not on the floor.
• Do not write or draw in it, or tear the pages.
• Read it regularly and listen when it is read.

### Song idea
Compose a short song about respecting the Bible, such as "This is God's word, I will keep it clean."

## PAGE 2: Two parts of the Bible
The Bible has two main parts. The Old Testament tells about God's dealings with people before Jesus was born. The New Testament tells about Jesus, His teachings and the early church.

### Remember
Testament means a covenant or agreement between God and people. Old comes first in the Bible, and New comes after. The Old Testament was first written mostly in Hebrew, and the New Testament in Greek, and today the Bible has been translated into Kiswahili, English and hundreds of other languages so that people can read God's word in their own language.

## PAGE 3: Books of the Old Testament
The Old Testament has 39 books. They are grouped by the kind of writing they contain.

### Law (5 books)
Genesis, Exodus, Leviticus, Numbers, Deuteronomy.

### History (12 books)
Joshua, Judges, Ruth, 1 Samuel, 2 Samuel, 1 Kings, 2 Kings, 1 Chronicles, 2 Chronicles, Ezra, Nehemiah, Esther.

### Poetry and wisdom (5 books)
Job, Psalms, Proverbs, Ecclesiastes, Song of Songs.

### Prophets (17 books)
Isaiah, Jeremiah, Lamentations, Ezekiel, Daniel, Hosea, Joel, Amos, Obadiah, Jonah, Micah, Nahum, Habakkuk, Zephaniah, Haggai, Zechariah, Malachi.

### Tip for remembering
Start by learning the first five: Genesis, Exodus, Leviticus, Numbers, Deuteronomy. Say them in a rhythm or sing them. Then add the history books. Practise with a friend in a game where one names a book and the other names the next.

## PAGE 4: Books of the New Testament
The New Testament has 27 books.

### The Gospels (4 books)
Matthew, Mark, Luke, John. These tell the story of Jesus' life, teachings, death and resurrection.

### History (1 book)
Acts, which tells how the early church began and grew.

### Letters (21 books)
Romans, 1 Corinthians, 2 Corinthians, Galatians, Ephesians, Philippians, Colossians, 1 Thessalonians, 2 Thessalonians, 1 Timothy, 2 Timothy, Titus, Philemon, Hebrews, James, 1 Peter, 2 Peter, 1 John, 2 John, 3 John, Jude.

### Prophecy (1 book)
Revelation.

### Count them
4 + 1 + 21 + 1 = 27 books.

### Role play
Stand in a line with your classmates. Each child holds a card with a book name. Arrange yourselves in the right order. Then say the names aloud from first to last.

## PAGE 5: Finding a verse and learning Isaiah 49:16
The Bible is divided into books, chapters and verses. To find a text such as John 3:16, look for the book John, then chapter 3, then verse 16. Use the contents page at the start of your Bible to find the page of the book.

### Reading a reference
• John 3:16 means the book of John, chapter 3, verse 16.
• Luke 19:1-10 means Luke, chapter 19, verses 1 to 10.
• Genesis 1:27 means Genesis, chapter 1, verse 27.

### A memory verse
Isaiah 49:16 says that God has engraved His people on the palms of His hands. This is a picture to show that God does not forget us. Copy this verse in your notebook and try to say it from memory.

### Why read the Bible?
• It teaches us right from wrong.
• It comforts us when we are sad or afraid.
• It helps us to know God and to trust Him.
• It gives us stories of people who followed God.

Try to read a little of the Bible each day, with a parent or an older sibling if you need help.

## QUIZ
1. How many books are in the Bible altogether?
   A) 39
   B) 27
   C) 66
   D) 100
   Answer: C
   Why: The Old Testament has 39 books and the New Testament has 27, and 39 + 27 = 66.
2. Which is the first book of the Bible?
   A) Genesis
   B) Matthew
   C) Exodus
   D) Psalms
   Answer: A
   Why: Genesis is the first book of the Old Testament and of the whole Bible.
3. Which group of New Testament books tells the story of Jesus' life?
   A) The letters
   B) The Gospels
   C) The Law
   D) The Prophets
   Answer: B
   Why: Matthew, Mark, Luke and John are the four Gospels.
4. In the reference Luke 19:1-10, what does 19 stand for?
   A) The verse
   B) The chapter
   C) The book
   D) The page
   Answer: B
   Why: The number after the book name is the chapter, and the numbers after the colon are the verses.
5. Which is the correct way to handle the Bible?
   A) Throw it on the floor
   B) Draw pictures on its pages
   C) Keep it clean and carry it carefully
   D) Use it as a seat
   Answer: C
   Why: The Bible is God's word, so we handle it with respect.
6. Write the first five books of the Bible in order. (3 marks)
   Answer: Genesis, Exodus, Leviticus, Numbers, Deuteronomy.
   Why: 3 marks for all five in the correct order, 2 marks for four correct, 1 mark for two or three correct.
7. Explain what it means to say that the Bible is inspired by God. (2 marks)
   Answer: God guided the writers of the Bible so that their writing is His message to people.
   Why: 1 mark for God guiding the writers, 1 mark for the writing being God's message.
8. Give three ways of showing respect for the Bible. (3 marks)
   Answer: Keep it clean, carry it carefully, wash your hands before reading, keep it in a safe place, do not write or tear it, read it often.
   Why: 1 mark for each valid way, up to 3.
