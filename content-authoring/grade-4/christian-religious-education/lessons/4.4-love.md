## PAGE 1: What is love?
Love is caring about others and wanting what is good for them. It is shown in what we do, not only in what we say. A mother who wakes up early to prepare breakfast shows love. A friend who shares a pencil shows love. A neighbour who visits a sick person shows love.

The Bible says that love comes from God. 1 John 4:8 says, "God is love." 1 John 4:7 says, "Dear friends, let us love one another, for love comes from God." God shows us love first, and we can show love to others.

### Words to know
• Love: caring for others and doing good to them.
• Kindness: being gentle, helpful and thoughtful.
• Bless: to ask God to do good to someone or to pray for them.

### Think about it
Name three people who show love to you. What do they do?

## PAGE 2: Jesus blesses the children
Matthew 19:13-15 tells how people brought little children to Jesus so that He would place His hands on them and pray. The disciples told the people off. They probably thought that Jesus was too busy and important to be troubled by children.

But Jesus said, "Let the little children come to me, and do not hinder them, for the kingdom of heaven belongs to such as these." Then He placed His hands on them and blessed them, and went on from there.

### What this story shows
• Jesus loves children. He makes time for them.
• Children are welcome in God's kingdom.
• Children are important, not a bother.
• The disciples were wrong to send children away, and Jesus corrected them.

### Role play
In groups, act out this story. Choose children, parents, the disciples and Jesus. Show how Jesus welcomes and blesses the children.

### Discuss
How does it make you feel to know that Jesus loves children?

## PAGE 3: Showing love by kindness
Jesus loves us, and we can share His love with others. John 13:34 records His words, "A new command I give you: Love one another. As I have loved you, so you must love one another."

### Ways of showing love
• Greet people warmly.
• Share what you have, such as food, pencils or books.
• Help those who are sick or weak.
• Comfort a friend who is sad.
• Forgive those who hurt you.
• Speak kindly. Avoid insults and name-calling.
• Include children who are left out.
• Take care of animals and the environment.

### Love at home
At home you show love by obeying your parents, helping with chores, being kind to brothers and sisters and looking after younger children.

### Love at school
At school you show love by being polite to teachers, helping classmates and welcoming new pupils.

### Love in the community
In the community you show love by greeting neighbours, helping the elderly, and taking part in good activities.

## PAGE 4: Love and obeying God's teachings
Jesus said that if we love Him we will obey His commands (John 14:15). Obeying God's word is a way of showing love for Him.

### Love of God and love of neighbour
Jesus said that the two greatest commandments are to love God with all our heart, soul and mind, and to love our neighbour as ourselves (Matthew 22:37-39). Your neighbour is anyone near you or in need, not only people who live close to you.

### Ways of obeying God's teachings
• Pray and thank God for His love.
• Read the Bible and follow its guidance.
• Go to church or Sunday school.
• Treat others as you would like to be treated.
• Forgive, tell the truth and be honest.

### Love is not only a feeling
Sometimes we do not feel like loving a person who annoyed us. Love is a choice. We can choose to be kind even when we do not feel like it.

### Illustration
Kamau does not like Juma because Juma once teased him. But when Juma falls and hurts his knee, Kamau helps him up. This is love in action.

## PAGE 5: Growing in love
Love grows when we practise it every day.

### Activities to practise love
1. Do one kind thing for someone each day.
2. Say something encouraging to a classmate.
3. Pray for a person who is unkind to you.
4. Draw a picture of Jesus blessing children, and share it with a friend.
5. Thank someone who has shown you love.

### Love and safety
Love is never hurtful. If someone says they love you but hurts you or asks you to keep frightening secrets, tell a trusted adult.

### Summary
• God is love, and His love is for everyone.
• Jesus welcomed and blessed children (Matthew 19:13-15).
• We show love by kindness, sharing, helping and forgiving.
• We show love for God by obeying His teachings.

## QUIZ
1. Which verse says that God is love?
   A) 1 John 4:8
   B) Genesis 12:1
   C) Luke 19:5
   D) Acts 2:38
   Answer: A
   Why: 1 John 4:8 says, "God is love."
2. What did Jesus say when the disciples tried to send the children away?
   A) Go away, children
   B) Let the little children come to me
   C) I am too busy
   D) Come back tomorrow
   Answer: B
   Why: Matthew 19:14 records Jesus' welcome.
3. What did Jesus do to the children?
   A) He ignored them
   B) He placed His hands on them and blessed them
   C) He gave them coins
   D) He sent them to school
   Answer: B
   Why: Matthew 19:15 says that He placed His hands on them.
4. Which action shows love?
   A) Teasing a new pupil
   B) Sharing your lunch with a hungry classmate
   C) Refusing to greet neighbours
   D) Ignoring a sick person
   Answer: B
   Why: Sharing is a practical act of love.
5. How can we show love for God?
   A) By obeying His teachings
   B) By ignoring the Bible
   C) By being rude
   D) By being greedy
   Answer: A
   Why: Jesus said that those who love Him obey His commands.
6. Give two ways of showing love at school. (2 marks)
   Answer: Help classmates, welcome new pupils, be polite to teachers, share things.
   Why: 1 mark for each valid way.
7. What does the story of Jesus blessing children teach us about how Jesus sees children? (3 marks)
   Answer: Jesus loves children; children are important; they are welcome in God's kingdom.
   Why: 1 mark for each valid point.
8. Describe how Kamau shows love to Juma even though Juma teased him. (2 marks)
   Answer: Kamau helps Juma up when he falls and hurts his knee, even though he does not like him.
   Why: 1 mark for helping and 1 mark for showing love in spite of dislike.
