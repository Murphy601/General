## PAGE 1: What is the annunciation?
Annunciation means an announcement. The Annunciation is the time when God sent an angel to announce to Mary that she would give birth to Jesus. The story is in Luke 1:26-38.

Many stories about Jesus begin with His birth. The Bible tells us that His birth was different from every other birth. He was born by the power of God, and that is why we say the birth of Jesus was unique.

### Words to know
• Angel: a messenger sent by God.
• Annunciation: the announcement of Jesus' birth to Mary.
• Virgin: a young woman who has not been married.
• Messiah or Christ: God's chosen Saviour.

### The main people
• Gabriel, the angel.
• Mary, a young woman living in Nazareth, a town in Galilee.
• Joseph, a man of the family of King David, to whom Mary was engaged to be married.

### Think about it
How would you feel if an angel suddenly appeared to you?

## PAGE 2: The angel visits Mary
Luke 1:26-27 says that in the sixth month of Elizabeth's pregnancy, God sent the angel Gabriel to Nazareth, a town in Galilee. He came to Mary, a virgin engaged to Joseph, who was a descendant of David.

The angel said, "Greetings, you who are highly favoured! The Lord is with you." Mary was troubled by these words and wondered what they meant. The angel told her, "Do not be afraid, Mary, you have found favour with God."

### The angel's message
1. You will conceive and give birth to a son.
2. You are to call Him Jesus.
3. He will be great and will be called the Son of the Most High.
4. The Lord God will give Him the throne of His ancestor David.
5. He will reign over Israel for ever, and His kingdom will never end.

### Jesus means
Jesus is a name that means "the Lord saves". The name shows that He came to save people from their sins.

## PAGE 3: Mary asks a question
Mary asked the angel, "How will this be, since I am a virgin?" She was not doubting. She wanted to understand how it could happen. The angel answered, "The Holy Spirit will come on you, and the power of the Most High will overshadow you. So the holy one to be born will be called the Son of God."

### The angel gives a sign
The angel told Mary about her relative Elizabeth. She was old and had never had a child, but she was now expecting a baby, in her sixth month. The angel then said, "For no word from God will ever fail," or in some translations, "Nothing is impossible with God" (Luke 1:37).

### Mary's answer
Mary said, "I am the Lord's servant. May your word to me be fulfilled." Then the angel left her.

Mary trusted God and was willing to obey Him, though she did not understand everything. This was very brave. She was young, and she knew that people might not understand.

What does Mary's answer tell us about her?

## PAGE 4: Why the birth of Jesus is unique
The birth of Jesus was unique for several reasons.
• He was born to a virgin. The Holy Spirit came upon Mary, and Jesus was conceived by the power of God.
• An angel announced His birth before it happened.
• He is called the Son of God, the Saviour.
• His kingdom will never end.
• He was named by God before birth.

### What this means for us
It means that Jesus is not an ordinary person. He came from God to save us. Christians celebrate His birth at Christmas each year with church services, songs and prayers.

### Joseph
The Bible also tells us that an angel appeared to Joseph in a dream and told him not to be afraid to take Mary as his wife. The angel said the baby was from the Holy Spirit and that He would be named Jesus, because He would save His people from their sins (Matthew 1:20-21). Joseph obeyed.

### Role play
In groups, act out the visit of Gabriel to Mary. One child is Gabriel, one is Mary and one is the narrator.

## PAGE 5: Learning from Mary
Mary was a young woman who loved God and lived a holy life. She was pure in heart and body, humble and obedient. Christians look up to her as an example.

### How we can copy Mary
• Trust God's power. Luke 1:37 reminds us that God can do what seems impossible.
• Obey God's word, even when it is not easy.
• Be humble. Mary called herself the Lord's servant.
• Keep your body and mind pure. Sexual purity is important because our bodies are gifts of God. Children should learn to respect their bodies, and adults should wait for marriage.

### Memory verse
Learn Luke 1:37 by heart and repeat it when you feel afraid or when a problem seems too big.

### Summary
• The angel Gabriel announced to Mary that she would bear Jesus.
• Jesus' birth was unique because He was conceived by the Holy Spirit.
• Mary trusted God and said, "I am the Lord's servant."
• We can copy Mary by trusting God, being humble and living holy lives.

## QUIZ
1. Which angel announced the birth of Jesus to Mary?
   A) Michael
   B) Gabriel
   C) Raphael
   D) Peter
   Answer: B
   Why: Luke 1:26 says that God sent the angel Gabriel to Mary.
2. In which town did Mary live?
   A) Bethlehem
   B) Jerusalem
   C) Nazareth
   D) Jericho
   Answer: C
   Why: Luke 1:26 says that Gabriel was sent to Nazareth in Galilee.
3. What name did the angel tell Mary to give her son?
   A) John
   B) Jesus
   C) David
   D) Joseph
   Answer: B
   Why: The angel said, "You are to call him Jesus."
4. Who would come upon Mary so that she could conceive?
   A) An angel
   B) King David
   C) Joseph
   D) The Holy Spirit
   Answer: D
   Why: The angel said that the Holy Spirit would come upon her.
5. What did Mary say when she agreed to God's plan?
   A) I do not believe you
   B) I am the Lord's servant, may your word to me be fulfilled
   C) Go away
   D) I must ask Joseph first
   Answer: B
   Why: Luke 1:38 records Mary's humble and trusting answer.
6. Give two reasons why the birth of Jesus is unique. (2 marks)
   Answer: He was conceived by the Holy Spirit; His birth was announced by an angel; He is the Son of God and Saviour.
   Why: 1 mark for each valid reason.
7. State three qualities of Mary that we can copy. (3 marks)
   Answer: Trust in God, obedience, humility, purity.
   Why: 1 mark for each valid quality.
8. Write out Luke 1:37 in your own words and say when it can comfort us. (3 marks)
   Answer: Nothing is impossible with God, or God's word never fails. It comforts us when we are afraid or when a problem seems too big.
   Why: 1 mark for the meaning of the verse, 1 mark for the situation, 1 mark for clear expression.
