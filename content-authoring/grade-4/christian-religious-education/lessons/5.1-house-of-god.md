## PAGE 1: What is a church?
The word church has two meanings. It can mean the building where Christians meet to worship, such as a cathedral, a chapel or a small church under an iron roof. It also means the people of God, the community of all who believe in Jesus.

The church building is called the house of God, because it is a place set apart where believers meet to pray, sing, listen to God's word and worship Him. God is not limited to buildings, as He is present everywhere, but a church is a special place where His people gather. Psalm 122:1 says, "I rejoiced with those who said to me, 'Let us go to the house of the Lord.'"

### Words to know
• Worship: to honour and praise God.
• Temple: the house of God in Jerusalem in Bible times.
• Sacred: holy and set apart for God.
• Congregation: the group of people who worship together.

### Think about it
What do you like about going to church? What do you see and hear there?

## PAGE 2: Jesus cleans the temple
In Bible times, the temple in Jerusalem was the house of God. Matthew 21:12-13 tells us that Jesus went into the temple courts and drove out all who were buying and selling there. He overturned the tables of the money changers and the benches of those selling doves.

"It is written," He said to them, "'My house will be called a house of prayer,' but you are making it 'a den of robbers.'"

### Why was Jesus so upset?
• The temple was meant for prayer and worship, but people were using it to make money and were cheating others.
• It was a place that should have been respected as holy.
• Jesus cared deeply about the honour of His Father's house.

### After the clearing
Matthew 21:14 tells us that then the blind and the lame came to Jesus in the temple, and He healed them. The temple was once again a place of help and healing.

## PAGE 3: Lessons from Matthew 21:12-14
What can we learn?
1. The house of God is holy and should be treated with respect.
2. It is a place for prayer, not for business, arguing or showing off.
3. God is angry when people take advantage of others, especially in His house.
4. It is a place where people find help and healing.
5. Jesus cares about what happens in God's house.

### Respecting the church today
• Enter quietly and behave well.
• Do not run or shout.
• Switch off phones or keep them silent.
• Dress neatly.
• Listen during prayers, songs and the sermon.
• Keep the church compound clean.
• Take good care of the Bibles, hymn books and chairs.

### Activity
In pairs, write down three ways of respecting the church. Share them with the class.

## PAGE 4: What Christians do in church
The Bible describes what believers do when they meet. In 1 Corinthians 14:26 Paul wrote, "When you come together, each of you has a hymn, or a word of instruction, a revelation, a tongue or an interpretation. Everything must be done so that the church may be built up."

### Activities in church
• Praying to God.
• Singing hymns and praise songs.
• Reading the Bible and listening to the sermon.
• Giving offerings and tithes.
• Sunday school and youth groups.
• Fellowship, meeting to encourage one another.
• Sharing communion, in many churches.
• Helping the poor, visiting the sick and doing service.

### Children in church
Children take part in different ways. They can sing in the choir, read a verse, take the offering, help to clean the church, help in Sunday school or greet visitors.

### Why participate?
Taking part helps us to grow spiritually, to learn about God and to serve others. It also builds friendships among believers.

## PAGE 5: Serving God in His house
The church belongs to God and to His people. We take care of it by taking part and giving our gifts.

### Ways in which children can serve
1. Attend regularly and arrive on time.
2. Sing and pray with the congregation.
3. Join the choir, or a children's group.
4. Help to keep the church clean.
5. Welcome visitors and new children.
6. Give offerings, however small.

### Poem idea
Write a four-line poem about respecting the church. Use words such as house, God, prayer and peace.

### Summary
• The church is the people of God and also the building where they worship.
• Jesus cleared the temple because it should be a house of prayer (Matthew 21:12-13).
• We respect the church and take part in its activities.
• We serve God in His house by praying, singing, giving and helping.

## QUIZ
1. What did Jesus say the temple should be called?
   A) A market
   B) A house of prayer
   C) A palace
   D) A school
   Answer: B
   Why: Matthew 21:13 says, "My house will be called a house of prayer."
2. Why did Jesus drive out the buyers and sellers?
   A) They were too noisy
   B) They were misusing God's house for making money
   C) They were late
   D) They were children
   Answer: B
   Why: The temple was meant for prayer and worship, not for business.
3. Which is an activity that Christians do in church?
   A) Praying and singing
   B) Selling goods
   C) Playing loud games
   D) Fighting
   Answer: A
   Why: Prayer and singing are acts of worship.
4. Which behaviour shows respect for the church?
   A) Shouting in the aisles
   B) Entering quietly and listening
   C) Littering the compound
   D) Using a phone during prayers
   Answer: B
   Why: Quiet behaviour shows respect for God's house.
5. What happened after Jesus cleared the temple, according to Matthew 21:14?
   A) The blind and lame came to Him and He healed them
   B) He left Jerusalem at once
   C) Soldiers arrested Him
   D) The temple was closed
   Answer: A
   Why: Matthew 21:14 says the blind and lame came to Him and He healed them.
6. Give two meanings of the word "church". (2 marks)
   Answer: The building where Christians worship; the people of God who believe in Jesus.
   Why: 1 mark for each meaning.
7. State three lessons that we learn from Matthew 21:12-14. (3 marks)
   Answer: The house of God is holy; it is a place of prayer; we must not cheat or misuse it; it is a place of healing and help.
   Why: 1 mark for each valid lesson.
8. Describe three ways in which children can serve in church. (3 marks)
   Answer: Sing in the choir, help clean, welcome visitors, read a verse, take the offering.
   Why: 1 mark for each valid way.
