## PAGE 1: Who was Zacchaeus?
The story of Zacchaeus is found in Luke 19:1-10. Zacchaeus lived in the town of Jericho. He was a chief tax collector, and he was rich. In the days of Jesus, Israel was ruled by the Romans. Tax collectors collected money from the people for the Roman rulers. Many tax collectors took more money than they were supposed to and kept the extra for themselves. Because of this, people disliked tax collectors and called them sinners.

Zacchaeus was also a short man. This mattered on the day that Jesus came to Jericho. A large crowd gathered to see Jesus, and Zacchaeus could not see over the people in front of him.

### Words to know
• Tax: money that people pay to the government.
• Tax collector: a person who collects taxes.
• Sinner: a person who does wrong things against God.
• Repent: to be truly sorry for wrong and to turn away from it.

## PAGE 2: Zacchaeus meets Jesus
Zacchaeus wanted to see who Jesus was. Since he was short and the crowd was thick, he ran ahead and climbed a sycamore-fig tree along the road where Jesus would pass. Zacchaeus did not mind what people thought. He wanted to see Jesus.

When Jesus reached the spot, He looked up and said, "Zacchaeus, come down immediately. I must stay at your house today." Jesus called him by his name. Zacchaeus hurried down and welcomed Jesus gladly.

The crowd began to grumble. They said, "He has gone to be the guest of a sinner." They were shocked that a good teacher would visit such a person. But Jesus was showing that He came for people who need to change.

## PAGE 3: How Zacchaeus changed
Zacchaeus stood up and said to the Lord, "Look, Lord! Here and now I give half of my possessions to the poor, and if I have cheated anybody out of anything, I will pay back four times the amount."

This shows real repentance. Zacchaeus did not only say sorry. He changed how he lived and made things right.

### What Zacchaeus decided
1. To give half of what he owned to the poor.
2. To pay back four times the amount to anyone he had cheated.

Paying back four times is a very large amount, which shows how sorry he was. Zacchaeus was ready to lose money in order to be honest and to help others.

### What Jesus said
Jesus said, "Today salvation has come to this house." Salvation means being saved from sin and being made right with God. Jesus also said, "The Son of Man came to seek and to save the lost." Zacchaeus had been lost in sin, but Jesus found him.

### Note
Nobody forced Zacchaeus to change. He did so because Jesus showed him love and he wanted to be different.

## PAGE 4: Honesty with money
Zacchaeus teaches us to be honest when we use money. Children use money too, for example, when they buy at the school shop, or when they are given change by a shopkeeper.

### Ways of using money wisely and honestly
• Do not steal money or take what is not yours.
• If a shopkeeper gives you too much change, give it back.
• Do not lie to your parents about how you spent your money.
• Save a little for the future and for helping others.
• Give some to those in need, like Zacchaeus did.
• Spend on needs before wants.

### Example
Kamau is sent to the shop with Ksh 100 to buy bread that costs Ksh 60. The shopkeeper gives him back Ksh 60 instead of Ksh 40. What should Kamau do? The honest choice is to tell the shopkeeper and give back the extra Ksh 20.

### Integrity
Integrity means doing the right thing even when nobody is watching. It is a lesson that we learn from the change in Zacchaeus.

## PAGE 5: Repenting and following Jesus
To repent means to turn away from sin and to be sorry in a way that changes our behaviour. Jesus wants each of us to be like Zacchaeus in this way. We may not be tax collectors, but children can also do wrong things, such as lying, cheating, stealing or being unkind.

### Steps of turning away from sin
1. Admit that you did wrong.
2. Tell God that you are sorry.
3. Say sorry to the person you hurt.
4. Put right what you can, for example, return what you took.
5. Decide not to do it again, and ask God to help you.

### Three lessons from Zacchaeus
• Jesus loves everyone, even people that others reject.
• Real change means turning from wrong and putting things right.
• We should use money honestly and share with the poor.

### Summary
• Zacchaeus was a rich chief tax collector in Jericho who climbed a sycamore-fig tree to see Jesus.
• Jesus visited his house, and Zacchaeus repented.
• He gave half of what he had to the poor and repaid four times anyone he cheated.
• Jesus came to seek and save the lost.

## QUIZ
1. In which town did Zacchaeus live?
   A) Nazareth
   B) Bethlehem
   C) Jericho
   D) Nain
   Answer: C
   Why: Luke 19:1 says Jesus was passing through Jericho, where Zacchaeus lived.
2. Why did Zacchaeus climb a tree?
   A) He wanted to hide from the police
   B) He was short and wanted to see Jesus over the crowd
   C) He was picking fruit
   D) He was playing a game
   Answer: B
   Why: The crowd was large and Zacchaeus was short, so he climbed a tree to see Jesus.
3. What did Jesus say to Zacchaeus under the tree?
   A) Go away
   B) Sell your house
   C) Come down immediately, I must stay at your house today
   D) Follow me to Jerusalem
   Answer: C
   Why: Luke 19:5 records that Jesus asked to stay at Zacchaeus's house.
4. How much did Zacchaeus promise to pay back to those he had cheated?
   A) Double
   B) Four times the amount
   C) Half
   D) Ten times
   Answer: B
   Why: Zacchaeus said he would pay back four times the amount.
5. Kamau receives Ksh 20 too much in change. What should he do, following Zacchaeus' example?
   A) Keep it quietly
   B) Spend it on sweets
   C) Tell the shopkeeper and return it
   D) Give it to a friend
   Answer: C
   Why: Zacchaeus put right what was wrong. Returning the extra money is honest.
6. Why did people dislike tax collectors in Zacchaeus' time? (2 marks)
   Answer: Many collected more money than they should and kept the extra, and they worked for the Roman rulers.
   Why: 1 mark for taking too much money and 1 mark for working for the foreign rulers, or for cheating.
7. State three lessons that we learn from the story of Zacchaeus. (3 marks)
   Answer: Jesus loves everybody, even people others reject; repentance means changing behaviour and putting things right; be honest with money and share with the poor.
   Why: 1 mark for each valid lesson.
8. Describe three steps a child can take to turn away from wrong behaviour. (3 marks)
   Answer: Admit the wrong, tell God and the person hurt that you are sorry, put right what you can, and decide with God's help not to do it again.
   Why: 1 mark for each correct step, up to 3.
