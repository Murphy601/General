## PAGE 1: What is self-control?
Self-control means controlling what we say, do, want and feel, so that we do what is right. It is like a brake on a bicycle. A bike without brakes is dangerous on a steep road. In the same way, a person without self-control can get into trouble.

Self-control is one of the fruits of the Holy Spirit. Galatians 5:22-23 lists it, and Galatians 5:25 says, "Since we live by the Spirit, let us keep in step with the Spirit." When we let the Holy Spirit guide us, He helps us to control ourselves.

### Words to know
• Self-control: being able to control your words, actions and wishes.
• Decision: a choice that you make.
• Defile: to make something dirty or unclean, or to harm it.
• Addiction: when a person cannot stop using something harmful.

### Think about it
Describe a time when it was hard to control yourself. What happened?

## PAGE 2: Signs of self-control
A person with self-control shows it in many ways.

### Examples
• Waiting for your turn without pushing.
• Not shouting or hitting when you are angry.
• Not eating all the food, but sharing with others.
• Doing your homework before playing.
• Not spending all your money at once.
• Not spending too many hours on a phone, TV or games.
• Saying no to things that you know are wrong.

### What happens without self-control?
• You may hurt others or get hurt.
• You may waste time and money.
• You may become unhealthy.
• You may get into trouble at school and at home.
• You may fall into bad habits.

### Proverbs 25:28
Proverbs 25:28 says, "Like a city whose walls are broken through is a person who lacks self-control." In the old days, a city without walls was easy for enemies to attack. Self-control is like a wall that protects us.

## PAGE 3: Making right decisions
Every day we make decisions. Some are small, such as what to wear. Others are big, such as whether to obey or disobey. The Holy Spirit helps us to make right decisions.

### Steps for right decisions
1. Stop and think before you act.
2. Ask, "Is this pleasing to God?"
3. Think about the results for you and others.
4. Pray, and ask for help from the Holy Spirit.
5. Ask advice from a parent, teacher or pastor.
6. Choose the right thing, and stick to your choice.

### Role play
In groups, act out a scene where a child is tempted to do wrong, such as taking a classmate's mandazi. Show the child stopping, thinking and making the right decision. Then act out what would happen if the child had no self-control.

### Listing choices
In pairs, list choices that you make every day at home and at school. Mark those that need self-control.

## PAGE 4: Things that can defile the body
God wants us to keep our bodies healthy and pure. Here are some things that harm us. Avoid them.

### Harmful things
• Alcohol, cigarettes and other drugs. These harm the brain and body and can cause addiction. Some children are tricked into trying them. Say no and tell a trusted adult.
• Sniffing glue or petrol and similar harmful substances.
• Watching or listening to things that are bad for young people, such as violent or ungodly movies and songs, and harmful pictures or videos on phones or the internet.
• Sex outside marriage. God wants sex to be kept for marriage between adults. Children should not be touched sexually by anyone, and no child should be led into it.
• Overeating or eating unclean food, which can harm our health.

### Remember
If someone offers you something harmful or asks you to do something wrong, say no, walk away and tell a trusted adult. If someone has hurt you, it is not your fault.

## PAGE 5: Living guided by the Spirit
Galatians 5:23-25 says that those who belong to Christ Jesus have put away their old sinful desires. We live by the Spirit and keep in step with the Spirit. This means we ask for the Spirit's help each day.

### Ways to build self-control
1. Pray each morning and ask for help.
2. Set small goals, such as not shouting today.
3. Keep busy with good things: school work, sports, church activities and helping at home.
4. Choose friends who encourage self-control.
5. Keep away from tempting situations.
6. Reward yourself for success, and if you fail, try again.

### Song idea
Compose a song on self-control. Use Galatians 5:23: "The fruit of the Spirit is self-control."

### Summary
• Self-control means controlling our words, actions and wishes.
• It is a fruit of the Holy Spirit (Galatians 5:22-25).
• We avoid things that defile our bodies, such as drugs, alcohol and harmful media.
• The Holy Spirit helps us to make right decisions.

## QUIZ
1. Which verses in Galatians speak about self-control as a fruit of the Spirit?
   A) Galatians 5:22-25
   B) Genesis 1:1
   C) Luke 19:1
   D) Acts 2:1
   Answer: A
   Why: Galatians 5:22-25 lists self-control and speaks about keeping in step with the Spirit.
2. Which of these shows self-control?
   A) Hitting a classmate who annoyed you
   B) Waiting for your turn in a queue
   C) Eating all the food without sharing
   D) Shouting at teachers
   Answer: B
   Why: Waiting calmly for your turn is self-control.
3. Which of these can harm the body?
   A) Drugs and alcohol
   B) Clean water
   C) Sleep
   D) Vegetables
   Answer: A
   Why: Drugs and alcohol damage the body and mind.
4. What should you do if someone offers you a harmful drug?
   A) Accept it
   B) Say no, walk away and tell a trusted adult
   C) Try a little
   D) Take it home
   Answer: B
   Why: Saying no and telling a trusted adult keeps you safe.
5. According to Proverbs 25:28, a person without self-control is like what?
   A) A tree by a river
   B) A city whose walls are broken through
   C) A mountain
   D) A shepherd
   Answer: B
   Why: Self-control protects a person like walls protect a city.
6. State three signs of a person with self-control. (3 marks)
   Answer: Waits their turn, does not hit when angry, shares food, saves money, does homework first.
   Why: 1 mark for each valid sign.
7. Describe three steps for making a right decision. (3 marks)
   Answer: Stop and think; ask if it pleases God; think of results; pray; ask advice; choose the right thing.
   Why: 1 mark for each valid step.
8. Name two things that can defile the body and explain how to avoid them. (4 marks)
   Answer: Drugs and alcohol, harmful videos. Avoid them by saying no, walking away, choosing good friends and telling trusted adults.
   Why: 1 mark for each of two things and 1 mark for each way of avoiding them.
