## PAGE 1: What is telling the truth?
Telling the truth means saying what really happened and not changing it to make yourself look better or to avoid trouble. A truthful person is honest in what they say and do.

Suppose the teacher asks who broke the window. If you broke it, the truthful answer is, "I did." It is hard to say, but it is right. A lie is saying something that is not true in order to deceive someone.

### Words to know
• Truth: what is real and correct.
• Lie: a false statement told to deceive.
• Honesty: being truthful and fair in words and actions.
• Integrity: doing what is right even when nobody is watching.

### Think about it
Have you ever been tempted to lie? What made it hard to tell the truth?

## PAGE 2: What the Bible says about truth
The Bible teaches us to tell the truth.
• Ephesians 4:25 says, "Therefore each of you must put off falsehood and speak truthfully to your neighbour, for we are all members of one body."
• Proverbs 12:19 says, "Truthful lips endure for ever, but a lying tongue lasts only a moment."
• Exodus 20:16, one of the Ten Commandments, says, "You shall not give false testimony against your neighbour."

### What these verses mean
1. Put off falsehood. We should get rid of lying as we take off dirty clothes.
2. Speak truthfully to your neighbour. We should tell the truth to our friends, family and classmates.
3. Truth lasts. A truthful word will still be true tomorrow, but lies are found out sooner or later.
4. We are members of one body. When we lie, we damage the trust that holds us together.

### Discuss
Why does Proverbs 12:19 say that a lying tongue lasts only a moment?

## PAGE 3: Why we should tell the truth
There are many good reasons to tell the truth.

### Reasons
• God commands it, and He loves the truth.
• People trust you when you are honest.
• You feel free and peaceful inside. Lies make you worry that you will be found out.
• You do not need to remember what you said, because the truth is always the same.
• It keeps friendships strong.
• It helps people solve problems, because they know what really happened.

### A well-known story
A shepherd boy shouted "Wolf!" as a joke when there was no wolf. The villagers rushed to help and found he was joking. He did it again. Later, a real wolf came, and when he shouted for help, no one believed him. This story is a fable, not from the Bible, but it shows what lying can do. People stop believing a person who lies.

### Results of lying
• Loss of trust.
• Punishment and more trouble than the truth would have caused.
• Guilt and shame.
• Broken friendships.

## PAGE 4: Different ways of not telling the truth
Lying is not only saying words that are false.

### Other forms of dishonesty
• Cheating in a test by copying.
• Blaming someone else for what you did.
• Leaving out important parts of a story to give a false picture.
• Exaggerating, which means making things sound bigger or better than they are.
• Making a promise that you do not plan to keep.
• Gossiping and spreading rumours.

### Example
Wanjiku did not do her homework. She told the teacher that her little brother tore her book. This was a lie, and it also blamed someone who was innocent. The truthful thing would have been to say, "I did not do my homework, and I am sorry."

### Telling the truth kindly
Being truthful does not mean being unkind. We should tell the truth with love and respect. We can say things gently and choose the right time.

### Practise
In pairs, take turns to tell about a time when you did not tell the truth and how you could have done better.

## PAGE 5: Becoming a truthful person
Truthfulness is a habit that we build by practising every day.

### How to overcome lying
1. Ask God for help to be honest.
2. Stop and think before you speak.
3. Admit your mistakes quickly and say sorry.
4. Accept the results, even if there is punishment. Most teachers and parents forgive quickly when you tell the truth.
5. Choose friends who are honest.
6. Return anything you borrowed or found.

### Being honest when nobody is watching
Integrity means doing right even when nobody sees. Do not cheat at games or copy homework in secret. God sees everything.

### Summary
• Truthfulness means saying what is real and being honest.
• The Bible teaches us to put off falsehood (Ephesians 4:25) and says truthful lips endure for ever (Proverbs 12:19).
• Lies lead to loss of trust and more trouble.
• We can build the habit of telling the truth with God's help.

## QUIZ
1. Which verse tells us to speak truthfully to our neighbour?
   A) Ephesians 4:25
   B) Genesis 1:1
   C) Luke 19:5
   D) Acts 2:38
   Answer: A
   Why: Ephesians 4:25 says to put off falsehood and speak truthfully.
2. According to Proverbs 12:19, what lasts for ever?
   A) Money
   B) Truthful lips
   C) Lying tongues
   D) Strong buildings
   Answer: B
   Why: The verse says "Truthful lips endure for ever."
3. Which action is dishonest?
   A) Returning a lost pencil
   B) Copying answers in a test
   C) Admitting you broke something
   D) Saying sorry
   Answer: B
   Why: Cheating in a test is a form of deceiving others.
4. What is one result of lying?
   A) People trust you more
   B) People stop trusting you
   C) You become famous
   D) You feel peaceful
   Answer: B
   Why: Lies break trust.
5. A pupil broke a cup and says she did not. What should she do?
   A) Keep lying
   B) Blame a friend
   C) Tell the truth and say sorry
   D) Run away
   Answer: C
   Why: Telling the truth and saying sorry is right and helps to restore trust.
6. Give two reasons why we should always tell the truth. (2 marks)
   Answer: God commands it; people trust us; we feel peaceful; friendships stay strong.
   Why: 1 mark for each valid reason.
7. State three results of telling lies. (3 marks)
   Answer: Loss of trust, punishment, guilt, broken friendships.
   Why: 1 mark for each valid result.
8. Write a four-line poem on the importance of telling the truth. (3 marks)
   Answer: A poem of about four lines that expresses that truth is good and lies are harmful.
   Why: 1 mark for four lines, 1 mark for a message about truth, 1 mark for rhythm or rhyme.
